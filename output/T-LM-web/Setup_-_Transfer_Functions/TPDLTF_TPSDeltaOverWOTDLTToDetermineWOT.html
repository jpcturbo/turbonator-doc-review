1. Overview & Purpose

TPDLTF (TPSDeltaOverWOTDLTToDetermineWOT) is a high-speed modifier that slightly reduces the required throttle opening threshold (WOTDLT) at elevated engine speeds inside HandleO2Processing_MM.

At high RPM, engine cylinder air ingestion is vastly greater than at low speeds. A throttle plate cracked 65% open flows virtually as much air mass as 100% full throttle. TPDLTF subtracts a small delta from the required WOT tripwire at high speeds, ensuring that closed-loop O2 fuel trimming disengages earlier at high engine RPM to safeguard against lean detonation.
