1. Overview & Purpose

WOTDLT (DeltaThrottleFrom_MINTHR_ToDetermineFullThrottle) establishes the throttle threshold for Wide Open Throttle (WOT) power enrichment and open-loop operation.

Rather than looking for a fixed absolute voltage (e.g. 4.0V), the ECU adds WOTDLT (stock calibration = 133 counts / ~2.60V) to the learned closed-throttle baseline MINTHR. For example, if learned idle is 0.85V, WOT activates at 0.85V + 2.60V = 3.45V (approximately 70% to 75% accelerator pedal travel). Upon crossing this threshold: - Closed-loop oxygen sensor fuel feedback is instantly suspended. - Fueling transitions to the high-output WOTTBL fuel curve. - Full electronic boost control targets (WSGRPM / RPMBST) are unleashed.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Earlier Power Enrichment: If you want WOT fueling and max boost to engage earlier in the pedal travel (e.g. at 60% throttle for a more responsive feel), reduce WOTDLT to 0x70 (~2.2V above idle).
  • Highway Cruise Economy: If high-speed cruising with a heavy load or headwind accidentally dips into WOT enrichment (wasting fuel), increase WOTDLT to 0x95 (~2.9V above idle) so WOT only triggers when the pedal is pressed firmly toward the floor.
