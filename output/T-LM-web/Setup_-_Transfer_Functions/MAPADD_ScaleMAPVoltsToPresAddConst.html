1. Overview & Purpose

MAPADD (ScaleMAPVoltsToPresAddConst) is the 16-bit zero-offset / intercept constant added to the scaled product of MAPVolts * MAPMULT inside ConvertMAPVoltsToValue.

Pressure sensors output a small non-zero offset voltage at absolute zero vacuum (typically 0.2V to 0.4V). MAPADD provides the mathematical intercept calibration that aligns zero manifold absolute pressure to the baseline of the ECU's internal lookup tables: - Stock Mopar 2-Bar: .word 0x0361 (865 decimal). - Mopar Super 60 / 3-Bar: .word 0x0700 (1792 decimal). Together with MAPMULT, MAPADD forms the classic linear equation y = mx + b for manifold pressure sensing.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Fine-Tuning MAP Accuracy: When verifying MAP sensor calibration with a hand vacuum pump and MPTune live data display:
  • If atmospheric pressure reads 29.9 inHg but live scan data reads 28.5 inHg (offset error across all points), trim MAPADD upward or downward by a few counts to zero the sensor perfectly.
  • If the slope is incorrect between atmospheric and full boost, adjust MAPMULT.
