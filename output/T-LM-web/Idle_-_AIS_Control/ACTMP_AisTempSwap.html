1. Overview & Purpose

ACTMP (AisTempSwap) defines the coolant temperature crossover point separating cold A/C compensation from warm A/C compensation.

Below ACTMP, the ECU considers the engine cold and applies the static step offset defined by ACPOS_ACPositionWhenTempToCold. At or above ACTMP, the ECU switches to the dynamic, vehicle-speed-indexed table ACCTBL_ACOffIdleDelta_FromSpeed. This allows a seamless transition from high-effort cold anti-stall compensation to refined warm cruising idle regulation.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated in CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at line 5249.
  • Compares CoolantTemp against ACTMP.
  • If CoolantTemp < ACTMP, uses ACPOS. If CoolantTemp >= ACTMP, executes ACCTBL lookup.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0xA6 (166 decimal, corresponding to approximately 166°F / 74°C).
  • Thermostat Changes: If running a 160°F low-temperature racing thermostat, the engine may operate continuously right around or slightly below 166°F, causing the ECU to remain permanently locked into the cold ACPOS adder mode. Tuners running 160°F thermostats should lower ACTMP to 0x90 (~145°F) so the ECU transitions into normal warm A/C compensation.
