1. Overview & Purpose

PNPOS (ParkNeutralPosition) is a single-byte calibration constant that adds a fixed step offset to the desired AIS motor position whenever the transmission is in Park or Neutral.

When shifting out of gear into Park or Neutral, the torque converter load vanishes, and engine RPM tends to rise. In stock calibrations, PNPOS is set to 0x00 (zero added steps) because the ECU handles the load transition via separate kick tables (DRPNK and PNDRK) and target curves (AISPN). However, the calibration location exists to provide an immediate static step adjustment if required by specific driveline configurations.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated in CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at line 5269.
  • Applied only when bit 3 of BitFlags_4f is clear (signifying Park or Neutral).
  • Added directly to the cumulative desired AIS step accumulator (adda PNPOS_ParkNeutralPosition).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x00.
  • Tuning Usage: Generally left at zero. If custom automatic transmissions exhibit an immediate RPM drop upon shifting into neutral before the closed-loop integrator responds, a small positive value (2 - 5 steps) can be entered here.
