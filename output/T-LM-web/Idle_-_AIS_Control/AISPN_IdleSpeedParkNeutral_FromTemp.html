1. Overview & Purpose

AISPN (IdleSpeedParkNeutral_FromTemp) is the foundational 2D calibration curve defining the target engine idle speed across operating coolant temperatures whenever the drivetrain is unloaded (Park/Neutral switch closed, or clutch switch / neutral switch grounded on manual vehicles).

On manual transmission vehicles (such as this T_LM_22_MTX_A171 calibration), the transmission park/neutral sense input on Port C (Z83_PortCdata, bit 3) is hard-tied to the unloaded state, meaning AISPN is the primary running idle target table used during all normal idle conditions. Cold engine temperatures command elevated idle speeds (~1,100 to 1,200 RPM) to accelerate catalytic converter light-off and prevent stall, smoothly declining to ~850-900 RPM at full operating temperature (180°F - 200°F).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated within DetermineTargetIdleSpeed_MM at line 5361.
  • Selected when bit 3 of Z83_PortCdata is cleared (signifying Park, Neutral, or Manual trans configuration).
  • Indexed continuously by CoolantTemp.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Setting Target Idle RPM: To raise your hot idle speed (e.g. for a lumpy aftermarket camshaft or higher alternator output), adjust the hot end of this table (right-side coordinate).
  • Big Cam Tuning: Large overlap camshafts (e.g. F2, F3, or custom grinds) produce weak manifold vacuum and misfire tendencies at stock 800-850 RPM idle. Raising the warm idle target in AISPN to 950 - 1,050 RPM significantly improves idle vacuum, oil pressure, and cabin vibration.
  • Lightweight Flywheels: Light aluminum flywheels lose rotational inertia quickly. Raising cold and warm points in AISPN by 100-150 RPM prevents sudden stalling when dipping the clutch during parking maneuvers.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Manual vs Automatic Transmissions: In manual calibrations (MTX), the hardware wiring pin is typically jumpered or configured so that bit 3 is zero, meaning AISPN is always the active table and AISCLD is unused.
