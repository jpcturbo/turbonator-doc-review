1. Overview & Purpose

FWDAIS (ForwardAisSteps_NoSlope) is a stepped lookup table (without linear interpolation) that dictates how many forward (opening) steps the ECU commands the AIS stepper motor to take when the engine is running slower than target idle speed.

When engine load increases (such as power steering pump lock, alternator charging surge, or clutch drag), engine speed sags below TargetIdleSpeed_HB. The ECU calculates the underspeed error (Target - Actual) / 4. Based on the magnitude of the error, FWDAIS assigns an immediate step increase (1, 2, 4, or 6 steps). Small RPM errors command single-step adjustments to avoid overshooting, while severe RPM drops command aggressive multi-step corrections to prevent an imminent stall.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stalling Under Sudden Load: If turning the steering wheel to full lock or turning on headlights causes the engine to dip and stall, increase the step values in FWDAIS (especially for mid-to-high RPM error brackets).
  • Idle Oscillating / Hunting: If the engine surges up and down rhythmically at idle ('hunting' between 750 and 1,100 RPM), FWDAIS is over-correcting. Reduce the step counts at low error brackets (e.g. keep small errors at 1 step).
  • Large Aftermarket Throttle Bodies: Larger throttle bodies flow much more bypass air per step. Reduce FWDAIS values by 30% to 50% to maintain smooth, non-oscillating idle recovery.
