1. Overview & Purpose

ACPOS (ACPositionWhenTempToCold) provides a fixed step addition for the idle air bypass whenever the Air Conditioning compressor clutch is engaged and engine coolant temperature is below the warm threshold defined by ACTMP_AisTempSwap.

When the engine is cold or warming up, refrigerant head pressures and compressor pumping displacement place a massive parasitic torque load on the cold engine. Because the vehicle speed-dependent table ACCTBL is tuned for warm operating temperatures, ACPOS overrides that table during cold running with a generous static step addition (stock calibration = 23 steps / 0x17) to keep the cold engine from lugging or stalling when the A/C clutch engages.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated in CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at line 5245.
  • Active when A/C clutch engagement is commanded (Bit 2 of BitFlags_85 is 0) and CoolantTemp < ACTMP_AisTempSwap.
  • Replaced by ACCTBL once engine coolant warms up past ACTMP.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • A/C Cold Stall: If turning on the air conditioning or windshield defroster while the engine is cold causes a severe RPM dip or engine stall, increase ACPOS (e.g., from 23 steps to 30-35 steps).
  • A/C Cold High Idle: If engaging the A/C while cold causes engine speed to surge excessively high (e.g. > 1,400 RPM), reduce ACPOS.
