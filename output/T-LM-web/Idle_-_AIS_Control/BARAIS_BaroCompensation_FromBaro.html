1. Overview & Purpose

BARAIS (AISBaroCompensation) scales the cold off-idle AIS step position (CLDPOS) as a function of atmospheric barometric pressure (BaroPressure).

At high altitudes (such as Denver, Colorado or mountain passes), ambient air density drops significantly. A given physical bypass orifice area passes substantially less air mass at 8,000 feet than at sea level. BARAIS provides a multiplicative correction factor to increase the stepper motor opening at low barometric pressures, ensuring consistent cold-engine throttle-lift recovery regardless of altitude.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated in CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at line 5217.
  • Indexed by BaroPressure (sampled at key-on and during deep manifold vacuum updates).
  • Multiplied directly by CLDPOS via 6803 hardware mul instruction.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Behavior: Most tuners will never need to modify BARAIS unless validating high-altitude vehicle behavior.
  • High Altitude Tuning: If vehicles driven at high elevation suffer from cold-engine dipping on decel, elevate the low-baro end of the BARAIS curve to command more open steps.
