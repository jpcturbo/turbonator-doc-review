1. Overview & Purpose

SFDCAY_FuelStartDecayIntoRun defines the fuel decay rate during the immediate start-to-run transition.

When the engine catches and crosses the start-to-run RPM threshold (RNFPRD), the fueling requirement drops immediately from heavy cranking pulse widths down to base speed-density running pulse widths. An abrupt, step-change drop in fuel delivery at the exact instant the starter disengages can cause the engine to stumble, sag, or stall. SFDCAY provides an after-start enrichment buffer that decays smoothly into normal running fuel over the first few seconds of operation, with the decay rate tailored to engine temperature.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

SFDCAY is engaged at the instant of start-to-run transfer: * Trigger: Bit 3 of BitFlags_82 flags the active decay transition. * Lookup Input: Current CoolantTemp. * Action: Looked-up value is stored into FuelSubtractedFromStartToRun and deducted incrementally per engine revolution.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Table Values (Faster Fuel Decay):
  • Effect: Decays after-start enrichment fuel more rapidly.
  • Real-World Feel: Cleans up post-start black exhaust smoke and soot on warm restarts, but can cause a cold engine to sag or die 2–3 seconds after starting.
  • Decreasing Table Values (Slower Fuel Decay):
  • Effect: Holds extra after-start enrichment longer.
  • Real-World Feel: Essential for smooth cold-start drive-away in winter weather. Keeps the engine revving cleanly during initial startup flare.
