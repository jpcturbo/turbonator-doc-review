1. Overview & Purpose

O2TLMH_HighLimitForDelayedSwitchToClosedLoop defines the upper voltage threshold required to qualify O2 sensor readiness before the ECU officially activates closed-loop feedback operation.

When an oxygen sensor is cold and inactive, its internal electrochemical cell cannot pass current and rests at an indeterminate floating voltage (typically 0.40V to 0.50V). To prevent entering closed-loop on a dead or cold sensor, the ECU requires the sensor to provide positive physical proof of life by swinging above O2TLMH (rich) or below O2TLML (lean) before closed-loop control is allowed to engage.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Warmup Qualification Window:
  • Engine coolant temperature has surpassed CLTEMP.
  • The startup delay timer O2TIME has elapsed.
  • Threshold Evaluation:
  • If O2SensorVolts >= O2TLMH: The sensor has actively pulled rich. Sensor is confirmed operational! Closed loop activates (BitFlags_81 bit 4 set).
  • If O2SensorVolts <= O2TLML: The sensor has actively pulled lean. Confirmed operational! Closed loop activates.
  • If O2TLML < O2SensorVolts < O2TLMH: Sensor is still resting in the unheated deadband. Closed loop remains blocked, and the ECU stays in open loop warm-up mode.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x1F (31 counts ≈ 0.605V).
  • Sensor Health Diagnostic: If an engine has reached full operating temperature (180Â°F+) but steadfastly refuses to enter closed-loop (scantool shows open-loop status indefinitely), the O2 sensor may have lost its heater or deteriorated to where it cannot swing above 0x1F (0.605V).

  • When using a wideband controller's simulated narrow-band output (e.g. Innovate LC-2, AEM X-Series), ensure the emulated analog curve crosses cleanly above O2TLMH (0.605V) and below O2TLML (0.293V).

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • O2TLMH represents a much wider safety margin (0.605V) than normal switching thresholds (O2HSWP at 0.488V). This ensures the sensor is genuinely hot and active, rather than hovering near stoichiometric rest.
