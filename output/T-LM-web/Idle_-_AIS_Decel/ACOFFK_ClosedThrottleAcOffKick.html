1. Overview & Purpose

ACOFFK (ClosedThrottleAcOffKick) is a calibration parameter used during the secondary stage of A/C compressor disengagement at closed throttle.

After the initial negative kick (IAISOP) arrests engine flare, ACOFFK establishes the transitional intermediate airflow ceiling while Timer_AisOrO2Sensor counts down, ensuring the stepper motor does not plunge too deep and cause a rebound stall.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at line 5082 and line 5308.
  • Active during closed-throttle A/C disengagement transitions.
  • Evaluated alongside Timer_AisOrO2Sensor countdown timer.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Rebound Stall After A/C Cycling: If the engine revives from an A/C disengagement flare only to dip and stall 1-2 seconds later, increase ACOFFK to provide a firmer transitional floor.
