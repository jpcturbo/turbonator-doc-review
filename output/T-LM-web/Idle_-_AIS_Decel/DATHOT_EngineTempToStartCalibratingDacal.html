1. Overview & Purpose

DATHOT (EngineTempToStartCalibratingDacal) defines the minimum engine coolant temperature required to activate DACAL adaptive learning.

DACAL (Deceleration Adaptive Calibration) is Chrysler's self-learning idle trim mechanism that learns the mechanical air leakage characteristics of the throttle plate and engine over time. Because cold engines exhibit excessive internal friction, high oil drag, and changing throttle body thermal expansion, learning decel trims on a cold or lukewarm engine would permanently corrupt the baseline airflow memory. DATHOT ensures DACAL learning only runs when the engine has reached full steady-state operating temperature (stock calibration = 0xCD / ~195°F).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside CalculateAisPosition_MM at line 3599.
  • Compares CoolantTemp against DATHOT.
  • If CoolantTemp <= DATHOT, the routine branches to LDA74 and disables DACAL learning for that loop cycle.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • 180°F / 160°F Thermostats: This is a major calibration trap! Factory stock DATHOT is set to 0xCD (~195°F). If a tuner installs a 180°F or 160°F thermostat, engine coolant temperature will hover around 175°F - 185°F, meaning CoolantTemp NEVER exceeds DATHOT! As a result, the ECU will NEVER run DACAL adaptive idle learning, causing decel hanging or dipping over time.
  • Recommended Setting for Cooler Thermostats: Lower DATHOT to 0xB5 (~175°F) for 180°F thermostats, or 0xA0 (~155°F) for 160°F thermostats.
