1. Overview & Purpose

DAISCL (DecelAisOpenStepsFromPos_FromDeltaMap) operates as the companion table to DAISOP.

After the AIS motor has stepped open to catch a falling engine on trailing-throttle decel, manifold pressure begins to stabilize and rise back toward normal idle levels (+Δ MAP). DAISCL looks up negative (closing) step corrections based on this positive pressure rise, smoothly stepping the bypass valve back down to prevent the engine from settling into a high, hanging idle.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside CalculateAisPosition_MM at line 3667.
  • Selected when the delta between the decel MAP target and live MapValue is negative (requiring negation to positive index).
  • Active during post-decel stabilization.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hanging Idle After Decel: If the engine catches decel successfully but then sits at 1,300 - 1,500 RPM for several seconds before slowly coming down, DAISCL is not closing the valve quickly enough. Increase the closing step values in DAISCL.
  • Secondary Decel Dip: If the engine catches, revives, and then immediately dips and stalls on the rebound, DAISCL is closing the valve too aggressively.
