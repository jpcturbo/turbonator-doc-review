1. Overview & Purpose

DANOM (Base_nominalCal) provides the foundational, non-adaptive baseline offset added to the scaled DACAL countdown timer (Timer_DacalCountdown / 4) during deceleration airflow processing.

The Chrysler decel airflow subsystem combines an adaptive learned term (derived from Timer_DacalCountdown) with a fixed calibrated floor. DANOM sets this non-volatile minimum floor (calibrated at 16 steps / 0x10). Even if the adaptive decel timer clears or zeroes out, DANOM guarantees that the deceleration routine always starts with a solid mechanical airflow cushion.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Active during all trailing-throttle decel conditions.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Base Decel Airflow Cushion: Raising DANOM increases the baseline air cushion on every deceleration event, regardless of adaptive memory state. Useful for lightweight flywheels or engines with aggressive cams that need a guaranteed minimum air floor.
  • Excessive Coasting Forward Push: If the car feels like it is pushing forward or coasting too easily without engine braking, decrease DANOM to lower the baseline airflow.
