1. Overview & Purpose

DAISOP (DecelAisOpenStepsFromNeg_FromDeltaMap) is a dynamic anti-stall table that responds directly to the rate of manifold vacuum increase (negative Delta MAP).

When the driver suddenly snaps the throttle shut at high RPM, manifold pressure plunges downward at an extreme rate (-Δ MAP). If the ECU waits for the standard closed-loop idle speed routine to detect an RPM drop, the engine will already have stalled before the stepper motor can travel. DAISOP detects this sharp negative pressure drop and instantly commands the AIS motor to step open by a calibrated count, arresting the pressure drop and catching the falling engine speed smoothly.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside CalculateAisPosition_MM at line 3663.
  • Triggered when manifold pressure is below the controlled MAP floor (suba MapValue; bcc LDAA7).
  • Active during decel transient events when Timer_AisChanges >= DCLRFQ.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Sudden Lift Stalling (Blow-Off / Vent-to-Atmosphere Valves): Turbo cars that stall immediately when shifting gears or snapping the throttle shut under boost often suffer from sudden vacuum collapse. Increasing DAISOP commands a quicker, larger air cushion to catch the RPM drop.
  • Lightweight Aluminum Flywheels: Ultra-light rotating assemblies drop RPM much faster than stock heavy cast flywheels. Increase DAISOP by 3 to 6 steps at high delta MAP inputs to catch the rapid engine deceleration.
