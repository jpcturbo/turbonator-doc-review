1. Overview & Purpose

DCLRFQ (TimeBetweenAisPulsesForDecelAis) establishes the pulse frequency / damping interval between consecutive stepper motor movements while in deceleration control mode.

During deceleration, the ECU continually calculates required step corrections via DAISOP and DAISCL. However, physical stepper motors require finite settling time to move their pintles, and manifold pressure requires time to equilibrate. If the ECU pulsed the motor on every program pass, the pintle would rapidly buzz, skip steps, and cause erratic pressure fluctuations. DCLRFQ enforces a calibrated delay timer (Timer_AisChanges) before the next step pulse can be dispatched.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside CalculateAisPosition_MM at line 3655.
  • Compares Timer_AisChanges against DCLRFQ.
  • If Timer_AisChanges < DCLRFQ, the routine branches to LDAB4 and exits without moving the motor.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x3F (63 timer ticks / ~0.7 seconds).
  • Faster Anti-Stall Response: Lowering DCLRFQ (e.g. to 0x20 or 0x18) allows the ECU to step the AIS motor open more rapidly during rapid deceleration, which is beneficial for lightweight flywheels or quick-revving road race cars.
  • Motor Buzzing / Hunting: Setting DCLRFQ too low can cause the stepper motor to chatter or overshoot its intended step target.
