1. Overview & Purpose

AC2KCK (AcClutchKickOpenAfterAcEnabled) delivers an immediate, pre-emptive opening kick (stock calibration = 7 steps) to the AIS stepper motor the instant the ECU detects an A/C request or commands the clutch relay to energize.

Before refrigerant compressor displacement builds head pressure against the piston domes, AC2KCK cracks the idle air bypass open. This ensures that the engine is already producing extra torque the exact millisecond the mechanical clutch plates mate, completely eliminating the initial RPM sag or shudder associated with A/C engagement.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at line 5102 and line 5311.
  • Active during the A/C clutch engagement sequence when Timer_AisOrO2Sensor reaches 1.
  • Added to desired AIS position: adda AC2KCK_AcClutchKickOpenAfterAcEnabled.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • A/C Engagement Shudder / Stumble: If turning on the A/C causes an immediate 100-200 RPM dip or vibration before the engine recovers, increase AC2KCK (e.g. from 7 steps to 12-16 steps).
  • A/C Turn-On Flare: If turning on the A/C causes the engine to flare up to 1,300 RPM before settling, decrease AC2KCK.
