1. Overview & Purpose

TIMEDC (AISDecelBaseAirflowCurveOffset) provides a timed deceleration airflow decay curve for the idle air bypass system.

When the vehicle is cruising at speed and the driver suddenly lifts off the throttle, the engine transitions into trailing-throttle overrun. Snapping the throttle shut instantaneously produces extreme intake manifold depression (vacuum spike), which pulls oil past the valve stem seals, scavenges unburned fuel from port walls, and can induce lean misfire or stalling. TIMEDC injects an initial airflow offset that smoothly ramps down over several seconds, allowing manifold vacuum to stabilize smoothly as the vehicle coasts.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Decel Popping / Exhaust Burble: High manifold vacuum during trailing throttle often causes exhaust popping or backfiring with open 3" downpipes. Increasing TIMEDC provides more decel bypass airflow, smoothing manifold vacuum and reducing harsh decel exhaust pops.
  • Engine Braking Response: If you want more aggressive engine braking when lifting off the throttle on road courses or autocross, reduce the initial values in TIMEDC to allow the throttle to close down quicker.
