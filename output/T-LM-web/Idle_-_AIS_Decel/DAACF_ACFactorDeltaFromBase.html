1. Overview & Purpose

DAACF (ACFactorDeltaFromBase) is a calibrated step adder that is combined with the deceleration airflow base calculation whenever the A/C clutch is engaged.

When the air conditioning compressor is pumping, it exerts substantial mechanical torque drag on the engine crankshaft. If the driver releases the accelerator pedal while the A/C is active, the engine's rotating assembly decelerates much more rapidly than when the A/C is off. Without compensation, the engine would violently undershoot target idle and stall. DAACF adds a calibrated step cushion (stock calibration = 9 steps) to keep the deceleration airflow floor higher while the compressor is loading the engine.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine CalculateAisPosition_MM at line 3543.
  • Active during closed-throttle deceleration when bit 2 of Z83_PortCdata is cleared (indicating active A/C clutch engagement).
  • Added directly to the decel airflow accumulator: adda DAACF_ACFactorDeltaFromBase.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Decel Stall with A/C On: If the engine idles fine with A/C on, but stalls every time you push the clutch in or brake to a stop with the A/C running, increase DAACF (e.g. from 9 steps to 14-18 steps).
  • Hanging RPM with A/C On: If deceleration RPM hangs up around 1,500 RPM when the A/C is running and won't drop, reduce DAACF.
