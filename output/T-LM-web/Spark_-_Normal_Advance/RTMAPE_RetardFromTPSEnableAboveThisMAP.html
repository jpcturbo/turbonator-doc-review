1. Overview & Purpose

RTMAPE_RetardFromTPSEnableAboveThisMAP is the manifold pressure enable threshold for the Tip-In / Rapid Throttle Opening Retard routine (CheckForTPSRetard).

When an engine is already operating under positive manifold pressure (boost) and the driver suddenly snaps the throttle open further, the immediate surge of air into the intake manifold can outpace fuel film delivery and transient enrichment, causing an instantaneous lean spike. Under boost, a momentary lean spike paired with steady ignition advance can trigger immediate tip-in knock. To counter this, the ECU monitors the rate of throttle opening (\DeltaTPS) and pulls timing instantaneously via RETTPS, but only if manifold pressure is above the boost threshold defined by RTMAPE.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

RTMAPE is the first gatekeeper in the CheckForTPSRetard logic: 1. MAP Threshold: Manifold Absolute Pressure (MapValue) must be strictly greater than RTMAPE. - In stock calibrations, 0x93 represents roughly ~1.5 psi of positive manifold boost. 2. Additional Gates: - RPM must be above RTRPME (~2500 RPM). - Average throttle position must be above RTTPSE (~1.57 V). - Instantaneous TPS voltage must be higher than rolling average TPS voltage (\DeltaTPS > 0).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing the Value (Higher MAP Threshold):
  • What it does: Requires higher boost pressure before rapid-throttle ignition retard is allowed to activate.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Prevents tip-in retard during low-boost throttle adjustments, providing sharper throttle response. However, setting it too high leaves the engine unprotected against transient tip-in knock under moderate boost.
  • Decreasing the Value (Lower MAP Threshold):
  • What it does: Enables transient throttle retard at lower boost levels or even into deep vacuum.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Enhances knock protection during aggressive throttle snaps, but setting it too low can cause a noticeable "bog" or hesitation when blipping the throttle.
  • Disabling TPS Retard:
  • Setting RTMAPE to 0xFF effectively disables the entire TPS transient retard mechanism.
