1. Overview & Purpose

HOTMAP_AdvanceFromMAPWarmPart is the primary warm part-throttle load advance table. In Chrysler's 2D speed-density architecture, ignition timing is decomposed into an engine speed component (GOVNER) and an engine load / manifold pressure component. HOTMAP provides the load component across vacuum and part-throttle boost when the engine is fully warmed up.

During part-throttle cruising under light engine load (high manifold vacuum), the cylinder charge is lean and less dense, burning significantly slower than a dense WOT mixture. To achieve maximum thermal efficiency and fuel economy, HOTMAP introduces high advance figures (up to +12° or more of additional MAP advance) in high-vacuum regions, progressively decreasing the timing as manifold pressure approaches atmospheric and transitions into boost.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

HOTMAP is looked up when all of the following conditions exist: 1. Engine is Warm: Coolant temperature is at or above TMPSWP (typically ≥ 166°F / 74°C). 2. Part-Throttle Operation: The throttle is open (off-idle), but has not reached Wide-Open Throttle (WOT). 3. PTE & Boost Blending Active: When accelerating at part throttle into boost, the raw output of HOTMAP is dynamically reduced by the ARADVR retard factor toward the WOTMAP curve.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

Total part-throttle ignition timing is the sum of RPM advance (GOVNER), the effective part-throttle MAP advance derived from HOTMAP, and barometric/temperature trims:

Total Timing ≈ GOVNER(RPM) + \max(WOTMAP, HOTMAP - Retard) ± Trims

  • Increasing Table Values (Adding Timing in HOTMAP):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Increases part-throttle advance across the corresponding MAP region.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Delivers sharper, crisper throttle response under cruising and light tip-in, improves fuel economy, and reduces exhaust gas temperatures (EGTs) during highway driving.
  • Cautions: Excessive advance in the mid-vacuum / light-boost region can induce part-throttle ping or knock, especially in taller gears under light acceleration.
  • Decreasing Table Values (Pulling Timing in HOTMAP):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Decreases part-throttle advance.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Softens throttle response and makes part-throttle acceleration feel sluggish. Excessive reduction increases EGTs and reduces fuel efficiency.
  • Relation to WOTMAP:
  • In the ECU assembly code, WOTMAP serves as a hard lower floor. HOTMAP values under boost should blend logically into WOTMAP to ensure smooth transition behavior.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • MPTune 3D Display (PTTIME):
    In MPTune, the 3D surface labeled PartThrottleTiming (PTTIME) is a calculated display table synthesized by adding GOVNER and HOTMAP. It does not exist as an independent 3D table in the ROM; editing the 3D surface in MPTune updates either GOVNER or HOTMAP.
  • Coupling with ARADVR: When modifying HOTMAP in positive manifold pressure regions, always verify how ARADVR blends timing down toward WOTMAP.
