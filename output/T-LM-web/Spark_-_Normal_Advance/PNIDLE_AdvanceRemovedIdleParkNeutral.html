1. Overview & Purpose

PNIDLE_AdvanceRemovedIdleParkNeutral provides a coolant temperature-dependent spark advance trim applied specifically when an automatic transmission vehicle is in Park or Neutral at idle speeds.

In automatic transmission vehicles, placing the shifter into Drive or Reverse engages the torque converter and applies continuous parasitic drag load to the crankshaft. Conversely, when shifted into Park or Neutral, this parasitic load is removed, causing engine manifold vacuum to deepen and idle speed to flare unless timing and airflow are trimmed. PNIDLE modifies ignition timing as a function of coolant temperature to smooth out gear engagement transitions (Park-to-Drive / Drive-to-Park).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

PNIDLE is looked up only when all of the following conditions are met: 1. Engine Speed is Low: EngineRpm_HB ≤ PNRPM (typically cranking/idle speeds). 2. Transmission in Park or Neutral: Evaluated via hardware input port C (Z83_PortCdata bit 3 is 0 when the Park/Neutral switch is grounded). 3. Engine Cold or Warm: Executed via the closed-throttle path.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Table Values (Adding Timing in Park/Neutral):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Advances idle timing when in Park or Neutral.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Raises idle RPM and increases vacuum in Park/Neutral. If set too high, will cause a noticeable idle "thump" or flare when shifting into Park or Neutral from Drive.
  • Decreasing Table Values (Pulling Timing in Park/Neutral):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Retards idle timing in Park/Neutral.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Calms idle flare when coming out of gear, making the transition from In-Gear to Park/Neutral feel seamless and controlled.
  • Manual Transmission (MTX) Calibrations:
  • In manual transmission calibrations (such as the A171 stock MTX calibration), the Park/Neutral switch circuit is typically tied to clutch/ground or ignored; however, the table remains defined in the template for architecture parity across ATX/MTX calibrations.

        cmpa PNRPM_EngineSpeedBelowWhich_PNIDLE_Enabled ; Compare against PNRPM
