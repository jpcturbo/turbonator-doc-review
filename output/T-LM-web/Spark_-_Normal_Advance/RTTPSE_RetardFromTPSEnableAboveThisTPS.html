1. Overview & Purpose

RTTPSE_RetardFromTPSEnableAboveThisTPS sets the minimum baseline throttle position voltage required before rapid throttle opening retard can be engaged.

Small throttle adjustments around idle or light cruise (such as minor highway speed corrections) do not create significant air inrush or risk tip-in detonation. RTTPSE prevents the transient retard logic from triggering on minor pedal movements, requiring the throttle to already be open to a moderate baseline position (~31% throttle opening / 1.57V) before rapid stabs can pull ignition timing.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

RTTPSE is evaluated against the filtered rolling average throttle voltage: 1. TPS Threshold: AverageTPSVolts must be strictly greater than RTTPSE (stock 0x50 = ~1.57V). 2. Coupled Conditions: - MAP must be above RTMAPE (~1.5 psi boost). - RPM must be above RTRPME (~2,496 RPM). - \DeltaTPS = TpsVolts - AverageTPSVolts > 0.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing the Value (Higher Baseline TPS):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Restricts TPS transient retard to transitions occurring higher in the throttle travel range (e.g., from half-throttle to full-throttle).
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Prevents hesitation during mid-pedal tip-ins.
  • Decreasing the Value (Lower Baseline TPS):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Allows rapid throttle snaps starting from lighter pedal positions to trigger transient timing retard.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Increases knock protection when stomping the throttle from light cruise, but can feel laggy if set too low.
