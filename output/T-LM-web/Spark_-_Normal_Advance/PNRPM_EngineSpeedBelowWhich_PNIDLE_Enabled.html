1. Overview & Purpose

PNRPM_EngineSpeedBelowWhich_PNIDLE_Enabled establishes the maximum engine speed threshold below which the Park/Neutral idle spark modifier (PNIDLE) is permitted to operate.

Park/Neutral spark compensation is designed strictly for low-RPM idle stabilization. If an automatic transmission vehicle is shifted into Neutral while coasting at speed or while free-revving the engine, applying idle-specific Park/Neutral timing trims at elevated RPM could disrupt engine braking or cause erratic timing shifts. PNRPM acts as an RPM ceiling to ensure that Park/Neutral trims are applied only within the expected idle window.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

PNRPM is evaluated directly against EngineRpm_HB: * If EngineRpm_HB > PNRPM: The ECU branches immediately to LEFCF, bypassing PNIDLE. * If EngineRpm_HB <= PNRPM: The ECU proceeds to check the Park/Neutral switch on Port C.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing the Value (Higher RPM Threshold):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Extends Park/Neutral idle spark trims to higher engine speeds.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Useful on engines with very high target idle speeds (such as race engines with large cams idling at 1,200–1,500 RPM) to ensure the ECU still recognizes the engine as being within the idle timing zone.
  • Decreasing the Value (Lower RPM Threshold):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Disables Park/Neutral trims at lower speeds.
  • Stock Setting Note: In many factory calibrations, PNRPM is set to 0x01 or small values to tightly constrain or disable this modifier in specific configurations.
