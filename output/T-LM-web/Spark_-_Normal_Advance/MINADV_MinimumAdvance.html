1. Overview & Purpose

MINADV_MinimumAdvance is an absolute minimum safety floor applied to the calculated barometric advance limit.

When the ECU calculates its altitude timing ceiling using ABCBAS, ABCFBR, and ABCFRP, high altitude multipliers could theoretically subtract so much timing that the resulting ceiling becomes zero or negative (excessive retard). Overly retarded ignition timing under load can cause combustion to continue after exhaust valve opening, generating extreme exhaust gas temperatures (EGTs) that can warp exhaust manifolds, burn exhaust valves, and melt turbocharger turbines. MINADV guarantees that the baro-calculated timing ceiling will never fall below a defined minimum floor (+3.0° in stock calibrations).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

MINADV is checked immediately after the baro compensation calculation: * If the calculated baro ceiling ($A$) is less than MINADV (or negative): The ECU clamps $A$ up to MINADV.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing the Value (Higher Minimum Floor):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Ensures the engine never runs less than this floor from the baro limiter.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Protects against excessive EGT buildup and sluggish performance at extreme altitudes (such as Pike's Peak or mountain passes over 10,000 ft).
  • Decreasing the Value (Lower Minimum Floor):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Allows the baro calculation to pull timing further downward.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Provides maximum knock protection at extreme altitudes, but risks high exhaust temperatures under extended wide-open throttle climbs.
  • Stock Setting:
  • In the stock A171 calibration, MINADV is set to 0x06 (6 / 2 = +3.0° advance), which represents an ideal failsafe baseline.
