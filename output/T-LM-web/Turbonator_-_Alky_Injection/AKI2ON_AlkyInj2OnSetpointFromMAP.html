1. Overview & Purpose

AKI2ON (AlkyInj2OnSetpointFromMAP) establishes the trigger threshold for the high-flow Stage 2 water/methanol injection nozzle solenoid.

Under maximum boost conditions (e.g. 15-25+ psi), engine thermal loading and octane demand escalate drastically. AKI2ON activates a secondary high-volume methanol nozzle, delivering supplemental fuel volume, charge cooling, and high octane equivalent to support elevated boost without detonation.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside AlkyInjector_Control_MM at line 7437.
  • Active when cfg_AlkyInj is enabled.
  • When MapValue >= AKI2ON, Stage 2 injection driver is triggered.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • High Boost Staging: Set AKI2ON 4-8 psi higher than AKI1ON (e.g. Stage 1 at 8 psi, Stage 2 at 16 psi). This two-stage delivery provides linear octane supplementation without drowning the motor at mid-range boost.
