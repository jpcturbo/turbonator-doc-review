1. Overview & Purpose

FACALC_AdaptiveRetardEnable is a mathematical mode-switch flag that dictates the arithmetic operation (subtraction versus addition) applied to the part-throttle boost blend timing calculation.

When an engine transitions from vacuum to boost under part throttle, the ECU calculates a proportional timing delta (Temp1) based on ARADVR, boost pressure, and Part-Throttle Enrichment. FACALC determines whether this delta is subtracted from HOTMAP (reducing timing toward WOT levels) or added to [HOTMAP].

In factory calibrations, FACALC is set to 0xFF (signed negative), configuring the system to retard timing under part-throttle boost.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

FACALC is evaluated on every part-throttle spark advance calculation: 1. Engine Warm: Coolant temperature ≥ TMPSWP. 2. Part-Throttle Boost Transition: Throttle is off-idle, but not wide-open throttle (WOT). 3. PTE / Boost Active: Part-throttle enrichment is active or MAP is approaching/exceeding OBCONST_OverboostConstant1.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

The CPU evaluates only the most significant bit (bit 7) of FACALC via the branch-if-minus (bmi) opcode:

  • Stock Setting (0xFF / Negative / Bit 7 = 1):
  • Function: Subtractive Mode (Timing Retard).
  • Effect on Total Timing: Subtracts the ARADVR calculation (Temp1) from HOTMAP. Total ignition timing drops progressively as boost builds at part throttle until clamped by WOTMAP.

  • Tuning Recommendation: Standard for all turbocharged gasoline engines. Keeps the engine safe from part-throttle detonation during boost onset.

  • Non-Stock Setting (0x00–0x7F / Positive / Bit 7 = 0):

  • Function: Additive Mode (Timing Advance).
  • Effect on Total Timing: Adds Temp1 to HOTMAP, increasing part-throttle advance under load, and clamping from above against WOTMAP.
  • Tuning Recommendation: DANGEROUS on turbocharged gasoline engines. Adding advance under rising manifold boost can trigger instant severe detonation. Do not change this value to a positive number unless testing specialized naturally-aspirated or alternative-fuel strategies.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

[!NOTE] Not an Adaptive Retard Knock Toggle:
Like ARADVR, the label FACALC_AdaptiveRetardEnable is a legacy misnomer from early 1990s disassemblers. It does not enable or disable knock-sensor adaptive retard. Knock detection and retard logic operate independently in routines KnockDetect_MM and HandleKnockRetard.
FACALC is strictly an arithmetic sign control for the part-throttle boost blend timing routine.
