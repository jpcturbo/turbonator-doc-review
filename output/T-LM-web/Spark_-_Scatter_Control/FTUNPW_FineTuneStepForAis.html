1. Overview & Purpose

FTUNPW_FineTuneStepForAis is the enable switch and step-size multiplier for spark scatter idle stabilization.

During closed-throttle idle, when engine RPM deviates outside the neutral deadband (FWDARP and REVARP), the ECU applies high-speed ignition timing dithering to stabilize engine speed. FTUNPW dictates the incremental step size applied by the spark scatter algorithm on each correction pass.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

FTUNPW is evaluated in the idle timing loop: * Operating State: Engine at closed-throttle idle above SSIPRD. * Enable Check: If FTUNPW == 0x00, the ECU branches to DontUseSparkScatter, completely disabling all spark scatter idle corrections. * Active Scaling: If non-zero, FTUNPW scales the size of the timing trim applied per event.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Setting to 0x00 (Disable Spark Scatter):
  • Disables spark scatter completely.
  • Real-world feel: The ECU will rely 100% on the mechanical AIS stepper motor for idle speed control. Highly recommended by tuners when running aggressive high-overlap camshafts or lightweight flywheels, where spark scatter dithering can cause the engine to "hunt" or rhythmically surge.
  • Stock Setting (0x01):
  • Enables standard 1-step spark scatter. Provides rapid, imperceptible idle corrections on stock or mild street camshafts.
  • Increasing Value (> 0x01):
  • Multiplies the timing correction step size. Can induce severe idle surging if the engine responds quickly to timing shifts.
