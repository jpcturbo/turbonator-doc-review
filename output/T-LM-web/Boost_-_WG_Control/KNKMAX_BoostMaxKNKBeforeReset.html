1. Overview & Purpose

KNKMAX_BoostMaxKNKBeforeReset defines the maximum knock count ceiling allowed under boost before wastegate duty cycle dumping is triggered and the counter resets.

When the boost knock counter reaches KNKMAX (16 events), the ECU confirms persistent, dangerous detonation and commands immediate wastegate duty cycle reduction (KDCINC) to pull boost down.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Persistent Knock Protection: Checked in CalcWGDutyCycle_MM.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x10 (16 counts).
  • Tuning Guidance: Low values dump boost aggressively on minor detonation; high values tolerate brief knock ticks. Leave near stock.
