1. Overview & Purpose

INCLIM_UpperLimitOnWastegateDutyCycleCorrection establishes the primary Stage 1 upper clamp limit on adaptive wastegate duty cycle correction.

Caps the maximum correction allowed during the initial spool-up adaptive phase to 49.0% (0x62), preventing excessive duty cycle accumulation before high RPM is reached.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Stage 1 Adaptive Clamp: Evaluated in ControlWastegate.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x62 (49.0% DC).
  • Tuning Guidance: If upgrading to a large turbo that requires high duty cycle to spool, raise INCLIM to 65–75% (0x82–0x96).
