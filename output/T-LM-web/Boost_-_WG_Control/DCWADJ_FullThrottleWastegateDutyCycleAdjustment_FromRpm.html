1. Overview & Purpose

DCWADJ_FullThrottleWastegateDutyCycleAdjustment_FromRpm provides an RPM-dependent duty cycle correction adder applied to the base wastegate duty cycle (DCWOT).

Turbocharger volumetric efficiency and turbine backpressure change dramatically across the rev range. At low RPM, exhaust energy is weak and extra duty cycle is required to spool the turbo. At high RPM, rising exhaust manifold backpressure pushes the internal wastegate flapper open, often requiring progressive duty cycle addition to prevent boost from tapering off towards redline. DCWADJ curves the duty cycle across RPM.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Preventing High-RPM Boost Drop:
  • Small stock turbos (Garrett T03 / Mitsubishi TE04H) frequently drop boost by 2–4 psi above 5000 RPM because exhaust backpressure overcomes the weak wastegate canister spring.
  • Adding positive duty cycle in DCWADJ at 4800–6000 RPM compensates for this backpressure bleed and holds flat boost to redline.
