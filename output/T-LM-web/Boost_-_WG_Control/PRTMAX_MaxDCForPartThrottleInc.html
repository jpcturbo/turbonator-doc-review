1. Overview & Purpose

PRTMAX_MaxDCForPartThrottleInc establishes the maximum permissible wastegate duty cycle ceiling allowed during part-throttle boost control.

Prevents the closed-loop duty cycle accumulator from winding up beyond 100% duty cycle (0xC8) when modulating boost at part throttle.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Part Throttle Clamping: Caps wastegate duty cycle in CalcWGDutyCycle_MM.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0xC8 (100%).
  • Taming Sensitive Throttles: Lowering PRTMAX (e.g. to 70% / 0x8C) caps part-throttle boost aggressiveness if the car feels too twitchy in traffic.
