1. Overview & Purpose

BSOLCO_BoostSolenoidCutout specifies the knock spark retard threshold above which the wastegate solenoid is completely de-energized (cut out).

When severe knock occurs, the ECU initially retards ignition timing. If timing retard reaches BSOLCO (3.5° of retard), the ECU determines that timing retard alone cannot control the detonation. It immediately cuts power to the wastegate solenoid (0% duty cycle), dropping the turbocharger down to mechanical actuator spring pressure (approx. 5–7 psi).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Solenoid Failsafe Cutout:
  • In ControlWastegate:
  • Total knock retard (TotalKnockRetard) is compared against BSOLCO.
  • If TotalKnockRetard >= BSOLCO: The wastegate solenoid output is forced to 0% duty cycle (staa BaseWastegateDutyCycle).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x07 (pprox 3.5° spark retard).
  • Engine Protection: Immediate, physical boost cut to protect the head gasket and pistons if bad gasoline or extreme heat causes persistent detonation.
  • Tuning Guidance: Do not raise excessively; cutting boost when knocking is the safest behavior possible on a turbocharged engine.
