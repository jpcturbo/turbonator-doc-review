1. Overview & Purpose

DCDECK_WastegateDutyCycleDecrementWhenSparkStillDerated specifies the continuous decrement rate pulled from wastegate duty cycle while ignition timing remains derated from a knock event.

If knock has occurred and spark timing has not yet fully recovered to baseline advance, the ECU steadily bleeds off wastegate duty cycle by DCDECK counts every clock interval (DECTIM), ensuring boost ramps down until ignition advance can safely recover.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Post-Knock Boost Bleed: Subtracted in ControlWastegate while timing retard > 0.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x02 (1.0% DC per interval).
  • Tuning Guidance: Provides smooth, gradual boost relaxation during sustained knock.
