1. Overview & Purpose

MAPOFS_BoostMapOffsetForDCWOT establishes the manifold pressure threshold below target boost where wastegate solenoid closed-loop modulation begins.

While manifold pressure is far below the boost target (e.g. during initial spool-up), the wastegate should remain clamped 100% shut to achieve the fastest possible spool. Once manifold pressure climbs within MAPOFS of target boost, the ECU transitions into modulating wastegate duty cycle to prevent overshooting.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Spool-Up Modulation Gate: Evaluated in CalcWGDutyCycle_MM during boost rise.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x17 (pprox 2.65 psi).
  • Faster Spool: Keeping MAPOFS around 2–3 psi ensures the wastegate stays clamped shut right up until the turbo is within striking distance of the boost target.
