1. Overview & Purpose

FMODDV1_StandardFuelModifierDivisor_1 serves as the internal arithmetic scaling divisor used when aggregating composite fuel modifiers into the final running fuel multiplier.

In CalculateSumOfAllFuelModifiers_MM, various fractional fuel compensations (warmup decay, charge temp, transient enrichment) are summed. FMODDV1 scales the intermediate sum to fit cleanly within 16-bit accumulator registers without mathematical clipping.
