1. Overview & Purpose

DMLMT_DeltaMAPLeanoutLimit establishes the maximum authority clamp for deceleration fuel leanout.

Caps the maximum fuel reduction that the deceleration algorithm is permitted to enforce, ensuring the mixture does not become so lean as to cause backfiring or exhaust popping.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Decel Clamping:
  • Caps the accumulated decel modifier to DMLMT (0x08).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Setting: 0x08.
  • Tuning Guidance: Increasing DMLMT produces more aggressive decel fuel cutting; lowering DMLMT reduces decel exhaust gurgle/pops.
