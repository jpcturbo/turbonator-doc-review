1. Overview & Purpose

MINCTS_MinCoolantTempForPTE sets the minimum coolant temperature required to allow Part Throttle Enrichment (PTE) flag activation.

On cold engines (below 138Â°F), cold warmup enrichment curves (CURVEA/CURVEB) already deliver substantial supplemental fuel. Stacking PTE on top of full cold enrichment would cause plug-fouling richness. MINCTS blocks PTE until the engine has warmed up past the heavy cold-enrichment phase.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • PTE Gate: Checked in SetFlagForPTE_MM. If CoolantTemp < MINCTS, PTE is blocked.
