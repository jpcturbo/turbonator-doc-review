1. Overview & Purpose

AESLOP_DeltaMAPEnrichment_FromTemp2 defines the temperature-dependent gain curve for MAP-based acceleration enrichment pump shots.

Cold intake manifolds require substantially larger acceleration fuel shots than warm manifolds because cold fuel does not atomize readily and coats the intake runner walls. AESLOP provides high fuel multipliers when coolant temperature is cold, decaying down to lower values as the engine reaches operating temperature.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Transient MAP Enrichment:
  • Active whenever \DeltaMAP > MAPTRG.
  • Injected fuel pulse width is:

PW_MAP Pump = \DeltaMAP_effective × AESLOP(CoolantTemp)

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing AESLOP Values:
  • Injects more fuel on quick throttle movements.
  • Cures lean tip-in stumbles, backfiring through the intake manifold, or hesitations upon sudden acceleration.
  • Decreasing AESLOP Values:
  • Necessary when installing larger injectors. If not scaled down, large injectors will cause rich bogging and black puff smoke upon every throttle punch.
