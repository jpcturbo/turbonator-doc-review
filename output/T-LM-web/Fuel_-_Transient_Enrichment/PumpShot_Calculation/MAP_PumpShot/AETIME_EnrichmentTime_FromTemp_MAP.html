1. Overview & Purpose

AETIME_EnrichmentTime_FromTemp establishes the decay time constant for MAP-based transient enrichment.

Once a MAP acceleration enrichment shot is triggered, the extra fuel pulse should not vanish instantly on the very next cycle, nor should it linger forever. AETIME defines the exponential decay rate at which the MAP transient fuel addition bleeds down back to zero.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Transient Filter Decay:
  • Updated in CalculatePTEFactors_MM when manifold pressure is rising (MAP > AVMAP).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Setting: 0x14 (20 counts).
  • Increasing AETIME:
  • Stretches the duration of the MAP pump shot across more engine revolutions. Helps engines with heavy turbo lag or large intake plenums maintain smooth AFR while boost builds.
  • Decreasing AETIME:
  • Quenches the pump shot quicker.
