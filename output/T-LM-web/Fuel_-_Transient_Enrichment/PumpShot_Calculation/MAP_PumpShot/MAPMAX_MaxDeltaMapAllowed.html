1. Overview & Purpose

MAPMAX_MaxDeltaMapAllowed defines the maximum allowable \DeltaMAP value permitted when calculating the size of a MAP acceleration enrichment pump shot.

If the driver violently stomps the gas pedal from deep vacuum (25 inHg vac) to full atmospheric pressure or boost, the calculated delta MAP could theoretically produce an excessively large pump shot that bogs or drowns the engine. MAPMAX acts as the safety ceiling, clamping the calculated \DeltaMAP before it is multiplied by the temperature enrichment curve (AESLOP).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Pump Shot Magnitude Clamping:
  • Evaluated immediately after subtracting MAPTRG.
  • If \DeltaMAP_effective > MAPMAX, it is clamped to MAPMAX.
