1. Overview & Purpose

HMPMUL_ColdLoadFromMAP defines the manifold load weighting curve for cold-engine high-load enrichment.

Works in partnership with HIGHMP: while HIGHMP dictates how cold the engine is, HMPMUL dictates how heavily the throttle is loaded. At high vacuum (idle/coast), HMPMUL is near zero (no cold load fuel added). As manifold pressure climbs toward atmospheric pressure and boost, HMPMUL ramps up to deliver full cold-load enrichment.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Load-Based Cold Fueling: Looked up vs. MapValue and multiplied with HIGHMP.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Load Progression: Ensures cold enrichment is applied only when the engine is under load, keeping cold idle smooth and unflooded.
