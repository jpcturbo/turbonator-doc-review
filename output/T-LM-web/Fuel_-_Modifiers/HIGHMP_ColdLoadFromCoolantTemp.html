1. Overview & Purpose

HIGHMP_ColdLoadFromCoolantTemp provides the temperature-dependent gain for Cold Engine High-Load Enrichment.

Accelerating under heavy engine load (or climbing a hill) while an engine is still cold requires substantially more supplemental fuel than cold idling does, because the high manifold air mass will condense fuel against cold intake walls. HIGHMP scales this heavy-load warmup enrichment based on coolant temperature, tapering to zero once the engine reaches 196Â°F.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Driveability:
  • Prevents cold-engine hesitation or backfiring when accelerating onto the highway immediately after starting.
  • Tuning Guidance: If an engine bogs heavily under throttle when cold, slightly decrease HIGHMP. If it stumbles lean, increase it.
