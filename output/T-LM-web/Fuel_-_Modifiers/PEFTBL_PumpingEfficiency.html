1. Overview & Purpose

PEFTBL_PumpingEfficiency is the core Volumetric Efficiency (VE) curve vs. Engine RPM in the Chrysler speed-density fuel algorithm.

In speed-density engine management, fuel mass required is proportional to:

Air Mass ∝ (MAP × Displacement / Gas Constant × Temperature) × Volumetric Efficiency

While base manifold pressure fuel tables (MAPTBL / WOTTBL) scale with load, PEFTBL models the engine's mechanical breathing curve across the rev range reflecting valve timing, intake runner harmonics, cylinder head port flow, and exhaust backpressure.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Universal Running Fuel Modifier:
  • Looked up continuously during CalculateSumOfAllFuelModifiers_MM using EngineRpm.
  • Multiplied into running fuel delivery across all throttle states (part-throttle, cruise, and WOT).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • The Primary VE Shaping Tool:
  • When installing an aftermarket performance camshaft (e.g. Taft, FWD-P, or custom grind), cylinder filling shifts toward higher RPMs.
  • Low RPM Cam Drop: Big camshafts breathe worse at low RPM (1500 - 2500 RPM) due to overlap reversion. Lowering PEFTBL here cleans up rich low-RPM operation.
  • High RPM Cam Peak: At 4500 - 6000 RPM, the big camshaft flows dramatically more air. Raising PEFTBL here adds the necessary fuel to support higher peak horsepower without running lean at the top end.
  • Ported Cylinder Heads / Big Valves:
  • Upgrading cylinder head flow increases VE across the board, requiring corresponding increases in PEFTBL.
