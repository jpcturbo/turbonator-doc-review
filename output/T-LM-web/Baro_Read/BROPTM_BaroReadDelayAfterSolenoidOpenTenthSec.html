1. Overview & Purpose

BROPTM (BaroReadDelayAfterSolenoidOpenTenthSec) specifies the precise pneumatic stabilization delay (stock calibration = 5 tenths of a second / 0.50 sec) between energizing the baro solenoid and sampling the MAP sensor analog-to-digital converter.

When the baro solenoid opens, atmospheric air rushes through the vacuum hoses into the MAP sensor chamber, equalizing the pressure from intake manifold vacuum up to ambient atmospheric pressure. Due to pneumatic hose volume, restriction orifices, and sensor diaphragm inertia, pressure equalization takes several hundred milliseconds. If the ECU sampled the ADC immediately, it would record an artificially low barometric reading (simulating high altitude). BROPTM guarantees the sensor cavity has fully equalized to ambient atmosphere before the reading is captured into BaroPressure.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside HandleBaroReadRequirement_MM at line 1727.
  • Compares Counter_BaroReadTime against BROPTM.
  • If elapsed time is less than BROPTM (bcs LCDAE), the ECU waits before capturing the ADC conversion.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Long Vacuum Lines / Relocated Sensors: If the MAP sensor or baro solenoid has been relocated with longer vacuum lines, increase BROPTM to 0x08 (0.8 seconds) to ensure full pressure equalization.
  • Stock Hardware: Leave at 0x05 (0.50 seconds).
