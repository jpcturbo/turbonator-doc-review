1. Overview & Purpose

BRCLTM (BaroReadDelayAfterSolenoidClosedTenthSec) defines the required pneumatic recovery delay (stock calibration = 5 tenths of a second / 0.50 sec) following the de-energization (closure) of the barometric read solenoid.

During an in-flight baro reading, the solenoid vents the MAP sensor chamber to atmospheric air. Once the reading is captured into RAM, the solenoid de-energizes, reconnecting the sensor port to the intake manifold vacuum runner. Because the sensor chamber was filled with atmospheric air, several hundred milliseconds are required for manifold vacuum to evacuate the vacuum line and pull the sensor diaphragm back down to live manifold depression. BRCLTM enforces a 0.5-second settling window before manifold pressure tracking resumes, preventing false transient acceleration enrichments or boost error flags.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine HandleBaroReadRequirement_MM at line 1745.
  • Triggered immediately after the baro read sequence concludes and the solenoid is turned off.
  • Compares Counter_BaroReadTime against BRCLTM before re-enabling normal MAP A/D updates.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x05 (0.50 seconds).
  • Extended Vacuum Routing: If the MAP sensor is installed far from the intake plenum with long rubber/silicone hoses, increasing BRCLTM to 0x07 or 0x08 gives the intake vacuum additional time to evacuate the line before fuel and spark calculations read the sensor.
