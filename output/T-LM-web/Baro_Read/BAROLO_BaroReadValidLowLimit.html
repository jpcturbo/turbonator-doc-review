1. Overview & Purpose

BAROLO (BaroReadValidLowLimit) establishes the lower physical and electrical sanity limit for atmospheric barometric pressure.

Even at extreme drivable elevations (such as the summit of Pikes Peak at 14,115 feet), ambient atmospheric pressure hovers around 17.8 - 18.0 inHg (~60 kPa). If an in-flight baro reading returns an ADC value below BAROLO (stock calibration = 0x45 / ~16.5 inHg / ~15,000 feet), the reading is physically impossible for a ground-driven vehicle. The ECU flags the sensor as open-circuit, disconnected, or leaking intake vacuum into the vent line, discards the reading, and substitutes BARDFT.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside BaroReadIfRequired at line 4262 and CheckBaroPressure at line 4959.
  • Compares barometric reading against BAROLO.
  • If BaroPressure < BAROLO (bcs LDEDD), branches to substitute BARDFT.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Pikes Peak / Extreme High Altitude Racing: Stock BAROLO permits operation up to ~15,000 feet. If operating specialized vehicles at extreme altitudes, verify that raw baro readings do not dip below BAROLO.
  • Vacuum Leaks in Baro Solenoid: If the baro solenoid internal vent valve sticks partially closed, engine vacuum will bleed into the reading, dropping the reading below BAROLO and triggering fallback to BARDFT.
