1. Overview & Purpose

FMODDV2 (StandardFuelModifierDivisor_2) is an internal mathematical scaling divisor (stock calibration = 0x1A / 26 decimal) utilized inside subroutine CalculateSumOfAllFuelModifiers_MM.

The ECU calculates multiple environmental fuel enrichment and leanout multipliers (coolant warmup enrichment, air charge temperature trim, barometric trim, and closed-loop trims). To prevent arithmetic overflow and maintain fractional precision across 8-bit integer multiplications, intermediate products are scaled and divided by FMODDV2 before being combined into the master fuel pulsewidth equation.
