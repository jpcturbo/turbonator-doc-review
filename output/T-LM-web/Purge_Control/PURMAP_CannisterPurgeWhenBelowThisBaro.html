1. Overview & Purpose

PURMAP (CannisterPurgeWhenBelowThisBaro) establishes the intake manifold vacuum threshold (stock calibration = 0x05 / ~1.0 inHg vacuum below baro) required to energize the evaporative emission canister purge solenoid.

Evaporative purge pulls fuel vapors from the charcoal canister directly into the intake plenum. Under boost or near wide-open throttle, manifold pressure is positive (boost), which would blow boost pressure backwards through the purge lines into the gas tank! PURMAP requires manifold pressure to be strictly below atmospheric pressure (extMapValue < extBaro - extPURMAP) before the purge solenoid is allowed to open.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine ControlPurgeSolenoid_MM at line 1647.
  • Subtracts PURMAP from BaroPressure.
  • Canister purge is only energized when extMapValue < ( extBaroPressure - extPURMAP - extPURHYS).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Boost Check Valve Integrity: Turbo cars have one-way check valves on the canister purge line, but PURMAP provides software protection by shutting the solenoid off before manifold pressure reaches atmospheric zero.
  • Shift Light Mod: When bit 4 of CONFIG is enabled, the Purge Solenoid output is repurposed to drive an RPM Shift Light!
