1. Overview & Purpose

SLRPM (ShiftLightSetpoint) sets the precise engine RPM at which the ECU illuminates the instrument cluster upshift indicator or an external shift light.

Turbonator code repurposes the purge/EGR driver output or the factory cluster upshift arrow to function as a programmable high-RPM shift light. When engine speed climbs to SLRPM, the ECU grounds the driver circuit, illuminating the shift light to tell the driver to shift before hitting the rev limiter. Once RPM drops below SLRPM during gear change, the light turns off immediately.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine ShiftLight_Run at line 7499.
  • Active when cfg_ShiftLight is enabled in configuration flags.
  • Turns on shift light output when EngineRpm_HB >= SLRPM.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Power Peak Shift Point: Set SLRPM roughly 200-400 RPM past peak horsepower on your dyno sheet, but 300-500 RPM below your hard rev limiter (REVLMT). This gives the driver human reaction time to initiate the shift without bouncing off the limiter.
  • Stock Valvetrain Safety: On stock hydraulic lifter 8-valve engines, set SLRPM to 5,600 - 5,800 RPM to protect valvetrain components from valve float.
