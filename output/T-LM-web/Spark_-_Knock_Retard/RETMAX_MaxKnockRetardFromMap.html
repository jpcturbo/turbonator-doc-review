1. Overview & Purpose

RETMAX_MaxKnockRetardFromMap defines the maximum allowable knock retard ceiling as a function of intake manifold pressure.

Under heavy turbocharger boost, persistent detonation is destructive and can quickly lead to engine failure. To protect the engine under severe knock, the ECU must be authorized to pull significant timing (up to 10.5° or more in stock calibrations). Conversely, under high vacuum or light load, allowing massive knock retard would be counterproductive, potentially causing combustion to flame out into the exhaust. RETMAX scales the maximum permissible knock retard proportionally with manifold pressure.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

RETMAX is looked up during every pass of KnockDetect_MM once engine speed exceeds KLMRPM: * Lookup Input: Current MapValue. * Action: The resulting retard ceiling is stored in MaxRetardFromMAP. Whenever knock retard accumulates in HandleKnockRetard, total per-cylinder retard is clamped so it never exceeds MaxRetardFromMAP.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Table Values (Higher Retard Ceiling):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Gives the knock control system authority to pull more total timing under heavy boost if detonation persists.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Crucial safety protection when pushing high boost levels (15–25+ psi) on pump gasoline.
  • Decreasing Table Values (Lower Retard Ceiling):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Restricts the maximum amount of timing that knock retard can remove.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Prevents excessive loss of power if false knock occurs, but leaves the engine vulnerable to melted pistons if real detonation is present.
