1. Overview & Purpose

RTRDRT_KnockRetardRateNoBoost defines the step-size of knock retard applied when knock occurs out of boost (under vacuum or light load below KNKOFS).

When detonation occurs at light load or part-throttle vacuum, cylinder filling is low and pulling a substantial step of timing (3.0° in stock calibrations) quickly snuffs out the knock event without significantly impacting drivability or torque.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

RTRDRT is selected when: 1. Active knock is detected (BitFlags_7f bit 7 = 1). 2. Manifold pressure is at or below the boost threshold: MapValue <= BaroPressure + KNKOFS.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing the Value (Larger Retard Step):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Pulls more timing (e.g., 4.0° or 5.0°) on the very first knock event.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Extremely fast knock suppression, but large timing drops under light load can cause a brief momentary surge or hiccup in vehicle feel.
  • Decreasing the Value (Smaller Retard Step):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Pulls smaller timing increments (e.g., 1.5° or 2.0°).
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Smoother recovery from light-load knock, but if fuel octane is poor, multiple consecutive knock events may occur before timing is retarded sufficiently.
