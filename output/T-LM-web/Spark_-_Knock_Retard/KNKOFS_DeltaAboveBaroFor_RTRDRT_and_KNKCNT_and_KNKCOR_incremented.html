1. Overview & Purpose

KNKOFS_DeltaAboveBaroFor_RTRDRT_and_KNKCNT_and_KNKCOR_incremented is the manifold pressure boost threshold dividing knock retard response into Vacuum / Low Boost Mode versus High Boost Mode.

Combustion knock occurring at light load or moderate boost requires a different corrective approach than severe detonation occurring under high turbocharger boost. When knock occurs below KNKOFS (less than ~5.4 psi of boost), the ECU applies larger retard steps (RTRDRT) to rapidly snuff out the knock. When manifold pressure exceeds barometric pressure plus KNKOFS (higher boost), the ECU transitions to RTRDRT2 and increments the boost knock counter (Counter_Over5psiBoost).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

KNKOFS is evaluated whenever active detonation is detected (BitFlags_7f bit 7 = 1): * Calculated Threshold: Effective Threshold = BaroPressure + KNKOFS. * Evaluation: - If MapValue <= BaroPressure + KNKOFS: The ECU uses RTRDRT_KnockRetardRateNoBoost (stock 3.0° per step). - If MapValue > BaroPressure + KNKOFS: The ECU uses RTRDRT2_KnockRetardRateInBoost (stock 1.5° per step) and increments Counter_Over5psiBoost.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing the Value (Higher Boost Threshold):
  • What it does: Extends the low-boost retard rate (RTRDRT) to higher boost levels.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Pulls larger chunks of timing if knock occurs in the 5–8 psi transition zone.
  • Decreasing the Value (Lower Boost Threshold):
  • What it does: Engages the boost knock retard rate (RTRDRT2) earlier.
  • Stock Setting: 0x2F corresponds to ~ 5.4 psi above ambient barometric pressure on a 2-bar MAP sensor.
