1. Overview & Purpose

KNKRPM_MinRpmforKnockRetard sets the minimum engine speed required for the ECU to activate knock retard.

At idle and very low engine speeds (below ~1,000 RPM), mechanical valvetrain chatter, piston slap in cold forged pistons, and accessory vibration can generate acoustic noise resembling detonation. Furthermore, true combustion detonation under light load at low RPM is virtually non-existent on properly tuned engines. KNKRPM disables knock sensor-driven ignition retard below this engine speed to prevent false knock triggers from destabilizing engine idle.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

KNKRPM is evaluated on every execution pass of KnockDetect_MM: * Evaluation: EngineRpm_HB is compared against KNKRPM. * Action: If EngineRpm_HB < KNKRPM (speed < ~1,024 RPM), the ECU branches to LD48C, setting MaxRetardFromMAP = 0 and suppressing all knock retard.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing the Value (Higher RPM Threshold):
  • What it does: Knock retard will remain disabled up to a higher RPM (e.g., 0x14 = 1,280 RPM).
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Prevents false knock retard caused by noisy mechanical valve gear, aggressive solid lifters, or lightweight flywheel rattle at low speeds.
  • Decreasing the Value (Lower RPM Threshold):
  • What it does: Allows knock retard to activate closer to idle speeds.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Risky on older engines; mechanical noise can trick the ECU into pulling timing at idle, leading to idle hunting, stalling, or erratic off-idle stumble.
  • Stock Value: 0x10 equals 16 × 64 = 1,024 RPM, perfectly clearing normal idle speeds (800–950 RPM).
