1. Overview & Purpose

RTRDRT2_KnockRetardRateInBoost defines the step-size of knock retard applied when knock occurs under boost (above KNKOFS).

Under positive manifold pressure (boost), pulling huge chunks of timing abruptly (such as 3° or 4° in a single cylinder firing) can cause a violent power drop, severe torque oscillation through the drivetrain, and skyrocket exhaust gas temperatures (EGTs). To manage boost detonation smoothly while preserving power delivery, RTRDRT2 uses a finer step size (1.5° in stock calibrations) applied sequentially to each knocking cylinder.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

RTRDRT2 is selected when: 1. Active knock is detected (BitFlags_7f bit 7 = 1). 2. Manifold pressure exceeds the boost threshold: MapValue > BaroPressure + KNKOFS.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing the Value (Larger Retard Step in Boost):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Pulls more timing (e.g., 2.0° or 2.5°) on each knock event under boost.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Highly protective for high-boost setups (18+ psi) where detonation can crack piston ring lands in just a few cycles, but may feel slightly harsher when knock occurs.
  • Decreasing the Value (Finer Retard Step in Boost):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Pulls smaller increments (e.g., 1.0°).
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Extremely smooth power delivery without sudden torque drops, but requires the calibration to be very close to optimal so small retard steps are sufficient to quell knock.
