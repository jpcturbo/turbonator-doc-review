1. Overview & Purpose

MPSHFT_OverboostFuelShutoffEnablePointFromMap_Timer establishes the timed overboost fuel shutoff threshold.

To prevent catastrophic engine destruction from an overboosting turbocharger (e.g. disconnected wastegate actuator hose or frozen wastegate flapper), the Chrysler ECU cuts fuel to all four injectors if boost pressure exceeds factory safety margins. MPSHFT defines the initial threshold (13.6 psi boost): if boost exceeds this level, the ECU starts the overboost timer (TRNTIM). If boost does not fall below MPSHFT before the timer expires, the ECU cuts fuel completely.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Overboost Timer Evaluation:
  • In ControlEngineCutout_MM:
  • Compares actual manifold pressure with (Baro + MPSHFT).
  • If MapValue > (Baro + MPSHFT): The overboost countdown timer (Timer_FuelShutoff) decrements toward zero.
  • If the timer reaches 0: Fuel shutoff flag is set, dropping injector pulse width to zero!
  • Setting MPSHFT = 0xFF disables timed overboost shutoff.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x76 (13.6 psig).
  • The "Hitting a Brick Wall" Sensation:
  • When modifying a Chrysler turbo car with a manual bleed valve or aftermarket boost controller, hitting 14 psi triggers the overboost fuel cut, causing the car to violently buck forward.
  • Raising for More Boost:
  • When tuning for 15+ psi boost, you must raise MPSHFT (e.g. to 18–20 psi, or 0xFF to disable) so higher boost targets can be sustained.
