1. Overview & Purpose

REVLMT_OverRevFuelShutoff is the master engine redline rev limiter (fuel cut enable RPM).

Protects the valvetrain from valve float, piston-to-valve contact, and rod bearing failure. On Chrysler distributor systems, the period timer measures time between ignition pulses (inversely proportional to RPM). When engine speed exceeds REVLMT (approx. 6490 RPM), the ECU immediately cuts fuel delivery to all injectors.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Fuel Cut Engagement:
  • In ControlEngineCutout_MM:
  • When distributor period timer drops below REVLMT (RPM > 6490), fuel shutoff is asserted.
  • Paired with REVLMT2, which provides hysteresis for fuel cut recovery.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Cut Point: ≈ 6490 RPM.
  • Raising Redline:
  • For modified engines with upgraded valve springs and performance camshafts, raise REVLMT to 6800–7200 RPM.
  • Always raise REVLMT2 proportionally to ensure smooth fuel recovery.
