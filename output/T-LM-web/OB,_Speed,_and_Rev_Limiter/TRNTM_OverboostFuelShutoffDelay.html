1. Overview & Purpose

TRNTM_OverboostFuelShutoffDelay specifies the countdown delay duration that boost must exceed MPSHFT before fuel shutoff triggers.

Provides an 0.8-second buffer window so quick gear shifts or momentary spool spikes do not cause irritating fuel cuts if boost quickly settles back down to target.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)
