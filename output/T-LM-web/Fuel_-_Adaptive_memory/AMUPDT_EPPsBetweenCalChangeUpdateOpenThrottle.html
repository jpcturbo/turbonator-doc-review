1. Overview & Purpose

AMUPDT_EPPsBetweenCalChangeUpdateOpenThrottle defines the dwell time in engine events (distributor pulses) that the engine must continuously operate within a specific adaptive cell before an update can occur.

Whenever the driver changes throttle position or engine load transitions to a new adaptive cell, the cell timer (AdaptiveCellUpdateTimer_IncrementedEveryDistRisingEdge) resets to zero. The engine must remain steadily inside that cell for at least AMUPDT consecutive distributor reference pulses before the ECU will permit the cell to learn and update. This prevents transient driving and shifting through gears from corrupting steady-state cells.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Dwell Time Requirement:
  • In UpdateAdaptive_OffIdle:
  • Compares AdaptiveCellUpdateTimer against AMUPDT.
  • If the counter is less than AMUPDT (0x1A), the routine exits without updating the cell.
  • Once the counter reaches AMUPDT, the cell is updated, and the timer is cleared back to zero to begin the next interval.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x1A (26 pulses = 13 crankshaft rotations on a 4-cylinder engine).
  • Increasing AMUPDT:
  • Requires longer steady-state driving in each cell before adaptation occurs.
  • Filters out noise and ensures trim modifications reflect true steady-state conditions.
  • Decreasing AMUPDT:
  • Speeds up adaptive trim learning, but increases vulnerability to throttle flutter and transient fueling noise.
