1. Overview & Purpose

MAPLIM_NoCellUpdateAboveThisMAP sets the maximum manifold absolute pressure threshold above which adaptive fuel learning is completely inhibited.

Closed-loop stoichiometric feedback and long-term adaptive trimming are strictly vacuum/light-load functions. When an engine enters positive boost pressure, the air/fuel ratio must transition immediately to power enrichment (11.5:1 â€“ 12.5:1 AFR) to protect against detonation and piston damage. If the ECU were permitted to adapt fuel trims under boost, it would misinterpret rich boost mixtures as an error and attempt to lean the engine out to 14.7:1! MAPLIM acts as the critical barrier that disables adaptive learning whenever manifold pressure exceeds barometric pressure.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Pressure Gate Check:
  • Evaluated on every adaptive update cycle in UpdateAdaptive_OffIdle.
  • If MapValue > MAPLIM: Adaptive cell updating is aborted immediately (bhi LD93F).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Setting: 0x8A (≈ 1.2 psig / just entering positive boost).
  • Tuning Rule: Never set MAPLIM substantially into boost. It must always stay at or just below atmospheric pressure (typically 0 to 1 psig) to safeguard boosted open-loop fueling.
