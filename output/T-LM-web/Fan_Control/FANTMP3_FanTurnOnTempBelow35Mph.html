1. Overview & Purpose

FANTMP3 (FanTurnOnTempBelow35Mph) is the primary low-speed and curb idle radiator fan turn-on threshold (stock calibration = 0xE3 / ~200°F).

When driving in slow city traffic, waiting at stoplights, or idling in gridlock (< 35 MPH), there is virtually zero passive ram airflow through the front fascia. Engine waste heat rapidly accumulates in the radiator coolant. When coolant temperature reaches FANTMP3, the ECU grounds the cooling fan relay driver, initiating forced airflow through the radiator core and condenser to bring engine temperature under control.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside ControlFan2_MM at line 6908.
  • Selected when vehicle speed is below 35 MPH (Temp0 < 0x46).
  • When CoolantTemp >= FANTMP3, energizes the fan relay driver.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Most Important Fan Setting: FANTMP3 is the parameter tuners adjust most frequently to control hot idle temperatures.
  • 180°F Thermostat Setup: Lower FANTMP3 from stock ~200°F (0xE3) down to 0xCB (~188°F).
  • 160°F Thermostat Setup: Lower FANTMP3 to 0xB5 (~172°F).
  • Summer Traffic Knock Resistance: Keeping city idle coolant temperatures around 185°F - 190°F prevents heat-soak of the intake manifold, sharply reducing tip-in knock when accelerating away from stoplights.
