1. Overview & Purpose

FANMAP (FanTurnsOnWhenMapAboveBaroPlusThis) is a specialized performance feature (stock calibration = 0x1A / ~3.2 psi of boost above baro) that energizes the radiator cooling fan during sustained turbo boost.

In factory turbocharged Chrysler applications (such as Daytona Shelby Z and GLHS models), the radiator and intercooler share the forward front-end airflow pathway. During hard acceleration or boost spool-up, charging the intercooler creates localized heat soak. When manifold boost exceeds barometric pressure by FANMAP (extMapValue ≥ extBaroPressure + extFANMAP), the ECU turns the cooling fan ON immediately to draw extra airflow through the nose of the vehicle, lowering intake charge temperatures.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine ControlFan2_MM at line 6860.
  • Adds FANMAP to BaroPressure.
  • If MapValue >= (BaroPressure + FANMAP), branches to LF1FB and commands the fan ON.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Drag Racing & Heat-Soak Protection: Setting FANMAP to 0x10 (~2 psi) ensures that the moment the turbo spools into boost, the cooling fan spins up immediately, minimizing heat-soak in the engine compartment during a quarter-mile pass.
  • Eliminating Fan Cycling: If you do not want the fan cycling on boost, set FANMAP to 0xFF.
