1. Overview & Purpose

FANTMP4 (FanTurnOffTempBelow35Mph) defines the temperature (stock calibration = 0xDD / ~192°F) where the electric cooling fan shuts off after completing a low-speed cooling cycle.

Once the fan turns on at 200°F (FANTMP3), it stays running until coolant drops below FANTMP4 (192°F), providing an 8°F hysteresis cushion that prevents relay chattering.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside ControlFan2_MM at line 6919.
  • Active when the fan is running at low vehicle speeds (< 35 MPH).
  • When CoolantTemp < FANTMP4, the fan relay de-energizes.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stagger with Thermostat: Ensure FANTMP4 is at least 3°F to 5°F above your thermostat rated opening temperature. For a 180°F thermostat, set FANTMP3 = 190°F and FANTMP4 = 184°F.
