1. Overview & Purpose

VACACC (CruiseControlVacAcceleratedGainCurve_FromDeltaSpeed) defines the high-gain vacuum servo pulse curve activated during large speed errors or when resuming vehicle speed.

When the driver hits Resume from a significantly lower speed, or when encountering a steep incline that causes vehicle speed to drop past ACLRST, the ECU sets the accelerated cruise flag. In this mode, the cruise controller switches from VACREG to VACACC, delivering much larger vacuum solenoid pulse widths to forcefully pull the throttle open and accelerate the car back toward the target set speed rapidly.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine CruiseControlSet at line 2291.
  • Active when the accelerated cruise flag is set (speed deficit exceeds ACLRST).
  • Reverts to VACREG once speed error drops within the normal regulation window.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Resume Sluggishness: If resuming from 45 MPH to 65 MPH feels lethargic, increase the gain values in VACACC.
  • Resume Overshoot: If the car aggressively overshoots the set speed upon resuming (e.g. climbing to 72 MPH before settling back to 65 MPH), reduce VACACC slope.
