1. Overview & Purpose

LOSPCO (CruiseControlLowSpeedCutoutSpeed) establishes the absolute low-speed safety cutout threshold (approx 25 MPH) below which active cruise control will automatically disengage and shut down.

If vehicle speed drops precipitously while cruise is active (for example, coming to a sudden stop in traffic, locking up tires in rain/snow, or spinning the vehicle), LOSPCO immediately disengages the system. Unlike MINSET which prevents arming below 30 MPH, LOSPCO monitors vehicle speed during active cruising; if speed ever drops below LOSPCO (~25 MPH), the cruise logic instantly dumps vacuum and returns control to the driver.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine CruiseIsSwitchedOn at line 1984.
  • Checked continuously on every distance sensor pulse calculation loop.
  • If vehicle speed drops below LOSPCO, subroutine drops out of cruise control immediately.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Safety Mechanism: Never disable or drastically lower this threshold. It is an essential failsafe preventing the cruise actuator from holding the throttle open at very low parking lot or stop-and-go speeds.
