1. Overview & Purpose

SETIME (CruiseControlNoSetupPulseWhenDecelTimerAboveThis) establishes the upper timer limit for inhibiting the initial vacuum setup pulse during vehicle deceleration.

If the driver has been holding down the "COAST / DECEL" cruise switch button and releases it to set a new lower cruise speed, the vehicle is actively decelerating. Applying a standard aggressive setup pulse immediately upon switch release would cause the car to lurch forward abruptly. SETIME checks the deceleration elapsed timer; if the timer is above this limit, the ECU suppresses or modifies the setup pulse to ensure smooth engagement at the new lower speed.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutines DropOutOfCruise (line 2074) and CruiseControlSet (line 2244).
  • Active when transitioning out of Coast/Decel mode into active cruise engagement.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Coast-to-Set Behavior: Retain stock setting (0x37). Ensures smooth deceleration transitions without pedal kickback when tapping coast.
