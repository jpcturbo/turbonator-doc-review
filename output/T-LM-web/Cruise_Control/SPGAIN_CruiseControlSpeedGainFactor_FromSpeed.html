1. Overview & Purpose

SPGAIN (CruiseControlSpeedGainFactor_FromSpeed) adjusts the overall cruise control servo gain as a function of vehicle road speed.

Aerodynamic drag increases with the square of vehicle speed (F_drag ∝ v^2). At 35 MPH, small throttle movements produce rapid speed changes, while at 75 MPH, substantial throttle movements are required to counter wind resistance. SPGAIN provides speed-dependent gain scheduling, scaling vacuum servo responsiveness across the vehicle's speed range to provide uniform control response from 35 MPH to 85 MPH.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine CruiseControlSet at line 2310.
  • Multiplied into the cruise servo command calculations.
  • Active across all cruise regulation cycles.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • High-Speed Sluggishness: If cruise holds speed crisply at 45 MPH but feels soft and unresponsive at 75 MPH, increase SPGAIN at higher road speeds.
  • Low-Speed Twitch: If cruise feels jumpy at 35-40 MPH, ensure SPGAIN gain is calibrated lower in the lower speed range.
