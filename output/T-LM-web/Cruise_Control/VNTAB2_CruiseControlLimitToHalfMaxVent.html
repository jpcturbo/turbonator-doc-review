1. Overview & Purpose

VNTAB2 (CruiseControlLimitToHalfMaxVent) sets a intermediate threshold used to restrict vent solenoid pulses to half-maximum under mild overspeed conditions.

When vehicle speed only moderately exceeds the set target, dumping full vent capacity would vent too much vacuum, causing the vehicle to undershoot set speed and begin an annoying oscillate/hunt cycle. VNTAB2 provides an intermediate throttling clamp, keeping the vent pulse gentle during minor speed corrections.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine CruiseControlSet at line 2280.
  • Active during moderate overspeed regulation cycles.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hunting Around Crests: If the car consistently over-corrects when cresting small rolling highway hills (venting too hard and then surging to catch back up), lower VNTAB2 slightly to smooth out intermediate venting.
