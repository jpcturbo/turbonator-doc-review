1. Overview & Purpose

THGAIN (CruiseControlThrottleGainFactor_FromThrottle) provides a nonlinear gain correction factor applied to cruise servo duty cycle based on the current throttle opening angle.

Mechanical throttle linkages and butterfly throttle bodies are inherently progressive: a 1-degree rotation at 5% throttle allows a massive percentage change in airflow, whereas a 1-degree rotation at 40% throttle produces a much smaller change in airflow. THGAIN compensates for this progressive airflow response by adjusting servo pulse authority as a function of throttle position, linearizing cruise throttle control across the operational range.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine CruiseControlSet at line 2303.
  • Multiplied into the final vacuum and vent pulse calculations during active cruise.
  • Indexed by the current throttle position sensor (TPS) reading.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Large Throttle Bodies: Aftermarket 52mm or 58mm throttle bodies flow significantly more air at small throttle angles than the stock 48mm unit. Flattening or reducing THGAIN at low throttle prevents cruise control twitchiness with oversized throttle bodies.
  • High-Speed Hill Assist: Increasing THGAIN at higher throttle angles gives the cruise controller more authority to hold highway speeds up steep mountain passes.
