1. Overview & Purpose

SCNVLO (CruiseControlForBrakeFlagManualTransmissionClutchDisable) defines the lower analog voltage threshold of the brake/clutch safety cutout window.

In tandem with SCNVHI, SCNVLO forms a noise-immune voltage comparator window. If the brake switch or clutch switch circuit is opened, closed, or shorted to ground, the reading falls outside the safe cruise operating band, commanding immediate cruise disengagement.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine CruiseIsSwitchedOn at line 2015.
  • Evaluated synchronously with SCNVHI on every major background loop.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Circuit Diagnostics: If cruise refuses to engage at all, measure the voltage on the brake/clutch sense pin. It must rest within the window between SCNVLO and SCNVHI when pedals are untouched.
