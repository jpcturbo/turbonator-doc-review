1. Overview & Purpose

SETPW (CruiseControlSetupPulseForSet_FromSpeed) determines the initial "setup pulse" duration applied to the vacuum servo the instant the driver presses the "SET" button.

Before cruise engages, the vacuum servo bellows is completely vented to atmospheric pressure and the throttle is held solely by the driver's foot. When "SET" is pressed, the ECU must instantly pre-charge the bellows with engine vacuum to take over the mechanical throttle load before the driver lifts off the gas pedal. SETPW scales this pre-charge pulse against vehicle speed, ensuring seamless handoff from foot throttle to cruise control without a momentary deceleration dip.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine CruiseControlSet at lines 2116 and 2155.
  • Triggered immediately upon transition from manual driving to cruise SET.
  • Inhibited if deceleration timer conditions (SETIME / SETIM1) are active.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Set-Speed Sag / Dip: If releasing the accelerator pedal right after hitting SET causes the car to momentarily sag or lose 1-2 MPH, increase SETPW at that speed.
  • Set-Speed Surge: If the car surges forward with unexpected acceleration when tapping SET, reduce SETPW.
