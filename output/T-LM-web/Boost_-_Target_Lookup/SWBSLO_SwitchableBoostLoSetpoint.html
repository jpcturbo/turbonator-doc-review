1. Overview & Purpose

SWBSLO_SwitchableBoostLoSetpoint defines the target boost ceiling applied when Low Boost Mode is selected via the dual-stage boost switch.

When the driver flips the boost switch selected by BSTSWS to Low Boost, the ECU clamps the accumulated boost goal to SWBSLO.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x56 ($pprox 9.9 ext{ psig}$).
  • Valet / Economy Mode: Set SWBSLO to spring pressure (e.g. 5–7 psi) for a valet mode, rain/winter mode, or 87-octane emergency mode.
  • High Boost Mode: Set your primary curve (WSGRPM) to 18–25 psi; flipping the switch toggles instantly between this low setting and full high boost.
