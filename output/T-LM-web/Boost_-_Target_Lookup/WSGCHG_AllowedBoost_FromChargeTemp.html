1. Overview & Purpose

WSGCHG_AllowedBoost_FromChargeTemp provides intake air charge temperature boost limiting.

Compressed turbo air heats up dramatically. If the intake charge temperature becomes excessively hot (due to a heat-soaked intercooler, high ambient summer temps, or non-intercooled Turbo I configurations), the risk of catastrophic detonation skyrockets. WSGCHG senses charge temperature and pulls boost down (from 12.1 psi down to 7.6 psi) if intake air temps become dangerously elevated.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Intercooler Health: If your car pulls hard on the first run down the drag strip but falls flat on subsequent runs, check charge temperatures. An undersized intercooler heat-soaks quickly, triggering WSGCHG boost reduction.
  • High Boost Tuning: Remember to raise WSGCHG at normal operating voltages (above 0.3V) when increasing boost targets.
