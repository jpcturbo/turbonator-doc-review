1. Overview & Purpose

WSGCLT_AllowedBoost_FromTemp is the engine thermal protection boost ceiling curve based on coolant temperature.

High boost on a cold engine causes severe piston scuffing, head gasket failure, and high thermal shock. High boost on an overheating engine causes runaway detonation and pre-ignition. WSGCLT restricts boost to low spring pressure (approx. 5.5 psi) when the engine is cold (<100°F) or overheating (>230°F), only allowing full boost when coolant temperature is within safe operating range (120°F–215°F).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Engine Protection: Prevents drivers from beating on a cold engine before the oil and coolant have reached operating temperatures.
  • Overheating Protection: Acts as an automatic failsafe if a radiator hose bursts or the thermostat sticks, dropping the car to mechanical wastegate boost to save the engine.
  • Raising Boost: When raising maximum boost to 15+ psi, increase the plateau of WSGCLT (between 130°F and 200°F) to match your target, while maintaining low boost at cold and overheating extremes.
