1. Overview & Purpose

KNKMIN (FlashCEMinTemp) defines the minimum engine coolant temperature required before the ECU will flash the instrument cluster "Check Engine" / "Power Loss" light upon detecting spark knock.

Turbonator firmware features real-time knock indication: whenever the knock sensor detects detonation and the ECU pulls spark timing on any individual cylinder (Cylinder1-4Retard), the ECU flashes the Check Engine light to alert the driver immediately. However, forged pistons or cold engine blocks naturally exhibit mechanical piston slap and valve train clatter when cold. If knock indication were enabled on a cold motor, the driver would see constant false alarm flashing. KNKMIN blocks knock CEL flashing until the engine warms up to normal operating temperature (~160°F).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine UseCELForKnockIndication_MM at line 7344.
  • Checked continuously while the engine is running.
  • If CoolantTemp < KNKMIN, knock CEL flashing is suppressed.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Forged Piston Engines: Engines built with loose piston-to-wall clearances (forged 2618 pistons) produce noticeable cold slap. Raising KNKMIN to 0xB5 (~175°F) completely silences false knock alerts until the pistons have fully expanded.
  • Instant Detonation Warning: Invaluable tuning tool on the street or dyno: allows the tuner to instantly see real-time detonation without looking away from the road at a laptop screen.
