1. Overview & Purpose

DCYTMH_CurveAHotDecayTime sets the decay rate of post-start enrichment (CURVEA) when the engine is started hot (coolant temperature at or above TMPHLD).

Because a warm engine already has hot intake valves and warm combustion chamber walls, it does not need prolonged post-start enrichment. DCYTMH provides a much faster decay rate (stock 0x09 vs cold 0x04) so the ECU returns to normal closed-loop running fuel quickly.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Condition: Engine started warm/hot (CoolantTemp < TMPHLD in inverse NTC A/D counts, typically ≥ 178°F).
  • Timing: Active after the hot hold timer (HOTHLD) expires.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Decay Formula:

Hot Decay Duration ≈ (89.76 / DCYTMH)

  • Stock Setting (0x09): Yields a rapid decay of roughly ~10 seconds, clearing out excess fuel quickly on warm restarts.
