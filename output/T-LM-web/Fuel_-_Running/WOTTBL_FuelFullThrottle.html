1. Overview & Purpose

WOTTBL_FuelFullThrottle is the core wide-open throttle (WOT) fuel delivery table and the maximum allowable fuel ceiling for part-throttle boost enrichment.

At wide-open throttle under turbocharger boost, the engine operates in open-loop mode where maximum power and combustion safety require rich air/fuel mixtures (typically 11.2:1 to 12.0:1 on pump gasoline). WOTTBL defines the base pulse width delivered under full throttle as boost pressure climbs. Additionally, during heavy part-throttle boost acceleration, the ECU clamps the modified part-throttle fuel delivery so that it never exceeds the pulse width defined in WOTTBL.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

WOTTBL is executed on every speed-density fuel loop pass: 1. Full-Throttle (WOT): When BitFlags_81 bit 0 is set (1), WOTTBL directly sets the baseline pulse width (FuelBaselineResult). 2. Part-Throttle Ceiling: At part throttle under boost, candidate fuel delivery (MAPTBL + PTE) is clamped to WOTTBL.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Table Values (Richer WOT Fuel):
  • Effect: Injects longer fuel pulses under boost and full throttle.
  • Real-World Feel: Lowers wide-open throttle air/fuel ratios (richer), cools combustion chambers, and suppresses detonation under boost.
  • Decreasing Table Values (Leaner WOT Fuel):
  • Effect: Shortens fuel pulses under boost.
  • Real-World Feel: Leans out WOT air/fuel ratio for increased power, but risks high cylinder temperatures and dangerous detonation if leaned beyond ~12.2:1 under boost.
