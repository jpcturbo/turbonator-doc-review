1. Overview & Purpose

MAPIDL_FuelBaselineFromMap is the base fuel delivery curve used exclusively at closed throttle (idling and trailing throttle deceleration overrun).

When the throttle plates are shut, engine airflow dynamics differ drastically from open-throttle cruising. The engine operates in deep manifold vacuum (typically 18 to 22 inHg vacuum), and fuel puddle dynamics on the intake port walls stabilize into an idle film. MAPIDL provides a dedicated base pulse width curve optimized specifically for stable idle air/fuel ratios and smooth deceleration without backfiring or stalling.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

MAPIDL is selected whenever the throttle is closed: * Throttle State: Bit 7 of BitFlags_81 is 0 (Closed Throttle / Idle / Decel). * Branch Action: In T-LMv13.asm at line 4293, if BitFlags_81 is positive (bpl LDF55), the ECU loads X with MAPIDL and jumps directly to lookup pulse width, bypassing part-throttle enrichment.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Table Values (Richer Idle/Decel Fuel):
  • Effect: Enriches closed-throttle fuel delivery.
  • Real-World Feel: Stabilizes idle on engines with aggressive camshafts that produce low or erratic idle vacuum. Eliminates decel popping/backfiring through the exhaust on trailing throttle.
  • Decreasing Table Values (Leaner Idle/Decel Fuel):
  • Effect: Leans out closed-throttle fuel delivery.
  • Real-World Feel: Cleans up rich, smelly idle on engines with oversized fuel injectors. However, leaning excessively will cause idle surging, hunting, or stalling when clutching in during decel.
