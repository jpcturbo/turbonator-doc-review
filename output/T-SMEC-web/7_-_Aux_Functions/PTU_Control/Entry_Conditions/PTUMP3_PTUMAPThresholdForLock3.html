1. Overview & Purpose

PTUMP3 defines the immediate manifold pressure spike threshold for low-speed Part Throttle Unlock lockup engagement.

When vehicle speed is below PTUMSP (under 45 MPH), the engine is capable of generating sudden torque spikes as turbocharger boost builds at lower road speeds. If the driver depresses the throttle and manifold pressure rises rapidly relative to the previous evaluation sample (stored in Temp0), allowing the converter clutch to slip could overheat or glaze the friction lining. In factory calibrations, PTUMP3 is configured to 120 counts, corresponding to approximately 13.8 psi delta on the 0 to 29.4 psi MAP scale. If manifold pressure jumps by more than PTUMP3 at low vehicle speeds, the controller immediately forces the torque converter to lock.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Low-Speed Boost Protection:
  • Forces immediate torque converter lockup when manifold pressure spikes rapidly below PTUMSP.
  • Stall Torque Multiplication:
  • Raise PTUMP3 to 140 to 160 counts to allow higher stall torque multiplication before lockup engages.
  • 3-Bar MAP Conversion:
  • Rescale PTUMP3 to ~80 counts on 3-bar MAP setups to prevent hair-trigger lockup on partial boost.
