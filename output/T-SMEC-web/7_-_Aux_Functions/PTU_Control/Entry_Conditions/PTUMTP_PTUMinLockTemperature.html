1. Overview & Purpose

PTUMTP defines the minimum engine coolant temperature threshold required to permit Part Throttle Unlock (PTU) torque converter clutch modulation.

The Part Throttle Unlock system was adapted from the throttle body injection and V6 engine controllers to control the automatic transmission torque converter lockup clutch during part-throttle cruising. When automatic transmission fluid and engine coolant are cold, fluid viscosity is thick and hydraulic line pressures are high, making converter clutch apply and release harsh. In factory calibrations, PTUMTP is set to 193 counts, corresponding to approximately 147 °F. Until the engine warms above this temperature, the controller holds the torque converter locked and inhibits part-throttle unlock cycling.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: ControlPTU_MM / PTU_CheckThrottleForLock (T-SMEC_v19.asm:10402-10411), instruction line 10408.
  • Entry Prerequisites:
  • Configuration bit cfg_PTU must be set in CONFIG_ConfigurationFlags.
  • Throttle above idle (TpsVolts > MINTHR_LowestSessionTPS).
  • Transmission in gear (b0c_ATXInGear set in BitFlags_AIS).
  • Brake switch released (sw1_Brake clear in StartupSwitchMirror1).
  • Comparison & Branch:
  • Compares CoolantTemp against PTUMTP_PTUMinLockTemperature at line 10408.
  • If CoolantTemp < PTUMTP (bcs PTU_SetPTUBits), branches to force lock state.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Driveline Protection:
  • Raise PTUMTP to 160 °F to 170 °F to prevent harsh lockup cycling on thick transmission fluid.
  • Aftermarket Torque Converters:
  • High-stall lockup converters with aggressive friction linings benefit from higher temperature thresholds to avoid shudder.
  • Warmup Behavior:
  • Below PTUMTP, the transmission remains locked in gear once engaged, bypassing throttle-induced unlock.

  • Entry Gate Interlock: Gated with transmission in-gear switch and brake pedal switch.
