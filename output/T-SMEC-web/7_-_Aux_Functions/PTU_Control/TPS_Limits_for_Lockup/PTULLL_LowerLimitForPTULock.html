1. Overview & Purpose

PTULLL defines the lower throttle position sensor delta threshold curve as a function of vehicle speed when the torque converter is currently in the locked state.

Paired with PTULUL, PTULLL establishes the lower boundary of the locked-state throttle deadband. When the converter is locked and throttle position delta remains between PTULUL and PTULLL, the controller decrements the debounce counter Counter_PartThrottleUnlock. If throttle position drops below PTULLL during coastdown or deceleration, the controller maintains lockup for engine braking. In factory calibrations, PTULLL is configured as a single-point baseline table.
