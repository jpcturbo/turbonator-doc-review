1. Overview & Purpose

EGR9 (ExhGasRecircConstFromTimeEngRunning) establishes the post-start engine running time delay before closed-throttle idle EGR shutoff logic becomes active.

During engine warmup and the initial period following key-on, the engine controller skips closed-throttle EGR shutoff checks while core engine timers and sensor readings stabilize.

At line 5403, the controller compares total engine running time against EGR9. The factory calibration setting of 128 counts corresponds to approximately 128 seconds (roughly 2 minutes) of engine operation. Once the engine has been running for at least 128 seconds, the controller actively monitors the off-idle throttle switch bit. If the throttle closes to idle after this delay, the controller immediately commands the EGR solenoid off, preventing exhaust gas recirculation from causing rough idle or engine stalling.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Enclosing Routine: ExhGasRecirc_MM (lines 5383–5545), specifically at line 5403.
  • Input Variable: KeyOnOrEngineRunningTime (RAM address 0x0041) evaluated in accumulator A.
  • Comparison: cmpa EGR9_ExhGasRecircConstFromTimeEngRunning at line 5403.
  • Warmup Branch: If RunningTime < EGR9 (bcs L1459), skips idle check and branches ahead.
  • Idle Action: If RunningTime >= EGR9 and b4f_OffIdle is clear, lines 5406–5407 set bit 4 in EGRVar2 and jump to EGR_SetEGRtoOff.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Lowering EGR9: Engages closed-throttle EGR shutoff sooner after engine start. Setting to 0 enforces idle EGR shutoff immediately from key-on.
  • Raising EGR9: Extends the post-start window before idle EGR shutoff checks begin. Rarely needed unless diagnosing cold-start emissions cycles.
  • Idle Protection Mechanics: Once RunningTime >= EGR9, any transition to closed throttle (b4f_OffIdle clear) sets bit 4 of EGRVar2 and jumps directly to EGR_SetEGRtoOff.
  • Factory Setting: 128 counts (approx 128 seconds / ~2 minutes) aligns with primary coolant warmup transitions.
