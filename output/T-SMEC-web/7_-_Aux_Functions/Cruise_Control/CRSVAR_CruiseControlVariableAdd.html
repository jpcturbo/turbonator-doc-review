1. Overview & Purpose

CRSVAR defines the variable feedforward addition value applied during tap-up speed adjustment.

When the driver taps the resume switch while cruise control is actively engaged, the engine controller increases the stored target speed by 2 miles per hour (4 counts). To overcome throttle cable compliance and mechanical linkage friction, CRSVAR loads an immediate feedforward bump into the cruise calculation while asserting the accelerated cruise flag, ensuring the vehicle responds promptly to the driver tap-up command.

  • Gating Conditions:
  • Resume switch tapped while cruise is active and engaged.

  • Application:
  • Loads CRSVAR into accumulator A at line 8630.
  • Sets accelerated cruise bit 6 in CruiseStatus at line 8632.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Tap-Up Responsiveness:
