1. Overview & Purpose

SETDLT defines the calibration offset bias added to speed error during evaluation of the throttle cable adaptive delta variable.

In Chrysler SMEC engine management, the cruise controller continuously monitors mechanical throttle linkage compliance and cable stretch by calculating the difference between commanded speed and measured vehicle velocity. SETDLT is added to this delta, allowing calibrators to introduce an intentional bias into the cable compensation routine. In factory calibrations, SETDLT is configured to 0 counts, providing pure symmetrical calculation.
