1. Overview & Purpose

THGAIN provides a progressive throttle position gain correction factor applied to the cruise servo command based on throttle opening angle above idle.

Mechanical butterfly throttle bodies possess non-linear airflow characteristics: cracking the throttle blade open by 2 degrees at near-idle conditions yields a large percentage increase in engine airflow, whereas a 2-degree movement at 30 percent throttle yields a much smaller percentage airflow change. THGAIN compensates for this non-linear airflow curve by adjusting servo pulse authority as a function of throttle position sensor voltage, linearizing cruise control authority across all throttle angles.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Aftermarket Throttle Bodies (52mm / 58mm):
  • Larger throttle bodies flow substantially more air at small opening angles.
  • Lowering THGAIN prevents cruise control twitchiness and surging on oversized throttle setups.
  • High-Load Hill Climbing:
  • Increasing THGAIN boosts servo pull authority as throttle angle increases, helping maintain speed up steep grades.
  • Additive Gain Matrix:
  • The output of THGAIN is stored in scratch RAM Temp4 and added directly to SPGAIN (speed gain factor) to generate combined servo gain Temp3.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Single-Point Factory Table: In stock calibrations, THGAIN is configured as a single-point table (Point 1: 255 counts TPS, 20 scale factor), providing a flat multiplier.
  • Custom Non-Linear Tuning: Tuners can expand THGAIN into a multi-point table in custom calibrations to shape progressive throttle response.
  • Companion Parameter: Combined directly with SPGAIN_CruiseControlSpeedGainFactor_FromSpeed to establish overall servo authority.
