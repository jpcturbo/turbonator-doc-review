1. Overview & Purpose

SPGAIN establishes the vehicle road speed gain correction factor that scales cruise servo responsiveness as a function of road speed.

Aerodynamic drag increases with the square of vehicle velocity: at 35 mph, a minor throttle adjustment produces a noticeable change in vehicle speed, whereas at 75 mph, substantial throttle movement is required to counter high wind resistance. SPGAIN implements speed-dependent gain scheduling, scaling vacuum servo pulse authority upward at higher speeds and downward at lower speeds to deliver uniform, predictable cruise holding behavior from 35 mph to 85 mph.
