1. Overview & Purpose

MAXTHR defines the maximum throttle position ceiling above which the cruise control vacuum solenoid is blocked from applying further vacuum.

Cruise control is engineered solely for maintaining highway cruising speed under moderate load, not for wide-open throttle acceleration. If vehicle speed drops on an extremely steep grade or if the vehicle is towing a heavy trailer, the cruise servo must not be permitted to pull the throttle plate wide open or generate sustained high turbocharger boost. In factory calibrations, MAXTHR is set to 80 counts, corresponding to approximately 31.4 percent throttle opening above idle, capping vacuum authority at a safe partial-throttle boundary.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Mountain Climbing Authority:
  • If cruise control sags and drops speed on steep highway grades, raise MAXTHR from 80 counts to 96 or 112 counts.
  • Gives the servo additional throttle authority to maintain speed without driver foot intervention.
  • Boost & Safety Ceiling:
  • Maintaining MAXTHR below 50 percent throttle opening prevents cruise control from building full turbo boost or pulling wide open.
  • Zeroing Out Output:
  • When throttle exceeds MAXTHR, vacuum command CruiseControlVar3 is zeroed, allowing the return spring to stabilize throttle position.
