1. Overview & Purpose

BRDLAY defines the required duration of steady closed-throttle deceleration before the engine controller is permitted to initiate an in-flight barometric pressure reading.

When the throttle plate abruptly closes from a cruising or boost condition, intake manifold pressure experiences severe pressure pulsations and pneumatic resonance before settling into stable closed-throttle vacuum. In factory calibrations, BRDLAY is configured to 182 counts, representing approximately 2.0 seconds. The throttle must remain continuously closed and engine speed must stay within the BRPMLO to BRPMHI window for 2.0 seconds before the barometric read solenoid is energized.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Mountain Driving Responsiveness:
  • Lowering BRDLAY to 1.2 to 1.5 seconds permits baro sampling during briefer downhill coast periods.
  • Minimum Stabilization Limit:
  • Keep BRDLAY at or above 1.0 second to ensure intake manifold pressure has fully settled.
  • Reset on Throttle Touch:
  • Any touch of the accelerator immediately clears the countdown timer, resetting the stabilization period.
