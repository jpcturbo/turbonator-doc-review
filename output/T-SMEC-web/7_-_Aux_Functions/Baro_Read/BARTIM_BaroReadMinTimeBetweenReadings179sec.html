1. Overview & Purpose

BARTIM defines the minimum time interval required between successive in-flight barometric pressure updates.

Atmospheric weather barometric pressure changes slowly over hours, and vehicular altitude changes occur over minutes. To prevent the barometric read solenoid from repeatedly cycling during frequent stop-and-go driving or repetitive throttle-lift cycles, BARTIM enforces a mandatory rest period between readings. In factory calibrations, BARTIM is set to 64 counts, representing approximately 179 seconds (approximately 3 minutes). Once an in-flight reading is taken, the controller locks out further baro reads until this 3-minute timer elapses.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Mountain Hill-Climb Racing:
  • Lower BARTIM to 60 to 90 seconds to adapt fueling quickly to rapid altitude ascents.
  • Preventing Excessive Solenoid Wear:
  • Do not set BARTIM below 30 seconds to avoid relentless cycling of the pneumatic solenoid.
  • Key-On Reliance:
  • Setting BARTIM to 255 counts effectively locks barometric pressure to the initial engine startup reading.
