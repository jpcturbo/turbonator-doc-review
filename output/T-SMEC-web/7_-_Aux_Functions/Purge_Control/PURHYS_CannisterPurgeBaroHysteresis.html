1. Overview & Purpose

PURHYS defines the vacuum hysteresis deadband for the evaporative canister purge solenoid control routine.

When driving at steady cruising speeds near light throttle, intake manifold vacuum naturally oscillates due to road irregularities and small throttle pedal movements. If the controller used a single fixed vacuum threshold to toggle the purge solenoid, the valve would buzz or chatter rapidly on and off. In factory calibrations, PURHYS is configured to 2 counts, providing approximately 0.23 psi (0.5 inHg) of hysteresis. Once canister purge is energized, PURHYS is subtracted from the barometric headroom, requiring manifold pressure to rise closer to atmospheric pressure before the solenoid is shut off.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: ControlPurgeSolenoid_MM (T-SMEC_v19.asm:1821-1826), instruction line 1823.
  • Active State Gate:
  • Reached only when purge solenoid is currently energized (pia1abuf_PurgeSolenoid set in PIA1_A_Buffer).
  • Math Operation:
  • Subtracts PURHYS_CannisterPurgeBaroHysteresis from BaroPressure before subtracting PURMAP.
  • Effective shutoff threshold becomes MapValue > Baro - PURMAP - PURHYS.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hysteresis Deadband Preservation:
  • Keep PURHYS at factory 2 counts (~0.5 inHg) for quiet, stable purge valve operation.
  • Preventing Solenoid Chatter:
  • Zeroing PURHYS produces rapid on-off clicking on rough pavement or highway undulations.
  • Upper Limit Caution:
  • Do not raise PURHYS beyond 4 counts to avoid delaying purge shutoff as the engine transitions into boost.

4. Technical / Assembly Mechanics (Reference)

Inside T-SMEC_v19.asm: In ControlPurgeSolenoid_MM at line 1822, brset *PIA1_A_Buffer, #pia1abuf_PurgeSolenoid, L1060 tests if the purge solenoid is currently active. If active, it falls through to line 1823, which executes subb PURHYS_CannisterPurgeBaroHysteresis, deducting the hysteresis margin from accumulator B before line 1824 subtracts PURMAP. This widens the pressure margin before cmpb MapValue branches to CanisterPurgeOff.
