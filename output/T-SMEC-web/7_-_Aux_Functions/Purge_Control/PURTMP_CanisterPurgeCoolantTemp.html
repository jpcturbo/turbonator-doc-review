1. Overview & Purpose

PURTMP defines the minimum engine coolant temperature required to enable evaporative canister purge operation.

When an engine is warming up from a cold start, fuel atomization is inefficient, combustion chamber walls are cold, and the engine operates in open-loop fuel enrichment. Introducing unmetered raw hydrocarbon vapors from the carbon canister into a cold intake manifold destabilizes warmup fueling, causing rough idle, spark plug fouling, or engine stalling. In factory calibrations, PURTMP is configured to 130 counts, corresponding to approximately 130 °F. Until engine coolant reaches this temperature, the controller holds the canister purge solenoid closed.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: ControlPurgeSolenoid_MM (T-SMEC_v19.asm:1807-1815), instruction line 1810.
  • Engine Running Check: Engine running flag b54_FuelEngineNotRunning must be clear.
  • Temperature Comparison:
  • Compares CoolantTemp against PURTMP_CanisterPurgeCoolantTemp at line 1810.
  • If CoolantTemp >= PURTMP (bcc L1057), sets flag b50_Purge in BitFlags_50.
  • If CoolantTemp < PURTMP, clears flag b50_Purge and disables purge.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Warmup Idle Stabilization:
  • Raise PURTMP to 160 °F to 170 °F on big-cam or large-injector setups to prevent vapor-induced idle hunting during warmup.
  • Cold Start Protection:
  • Do not lower PURTMP below 120 °F to avoid rich stumbling before closed-loop oxygen sensor feedback is active.
  • Emissions Compliance:
  • Factory 130 °F setting achieves early canister purge without compromising cold drivability.
