1. Overview & Purpose

PURMAP establishes the intake manifold vacuum margin required below atmospheric barometric pressure to enable the evaporative emission canister purge solenoid.

The evaporative emission system draws stored fuel vapors from the charcoal canister into the engine intake manifold using engine vacuum. On turbocharged engines, under boost or wide-open throttle, intake manifold pressure becomes positive relative to atmospheric pressure. If the canister purge solenoid opened during boost, pressurized intake air would blow backwards through the purge lines into the charcoal canister and fuel tank. In factory calibrations, PURMAP is set to 5 counts, corresponding to approximately 0.6 psi (1.2 inHg) of vacuum below barometric pressure. Manifold pressure must stay at least this far below atmospheric pressure before purge is permitted.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • 3-Bar MAP Scaling:
  • Verify and rescale PURMAP on 3-bar sensor conversions to maintain proper vacuum margins on the 44.1 psi scale.
  • Boost Backflow Protection:
  • Software failsafe that guarantees the purge valve closes before intake pressure reaches atmospheric zero or positive boost.
  • Shift Light Repurposing:
  • When option bit cfg_PurgeShiftLight is enabled in Turbonator calibrations, this output drives the driver shift light.
