1. Overview & Purpose

ARTM5P defines the persistence timer threshold required to accumulate in-boost knock before the engine controller increments the adaptive retard index (AdaptiveRetardIndex).

Momentary detonation can occur during rapid gear shifts, throttle tip-in, or turbocharger spool-up without representing chronic combustion instability. To avoid reacting prematurely to brief transient knock, the controller demands that cylinder knock retards persist while operating in boost for ARTM5P consecutive evaluation periods. In factory calibrations, ARTM5P is set to 12 counts (approximately 8.4 seconds of persistent knock under boost), ensuring that only sustained detonation triggers long-term adaptive retard learning.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Transient Knock Filtering:
  • Factory setting of 12 counts requires sustained boost knock across ~8.4 seconds before incrementing adaptive retard, ignoring single-event pinging.
  • Road-Racing Protection:
  • Lowering ARTM5P to 4 to 6 counts provides faster adaptation during prolonged track sessions where sustained knock can cause rapid heat buildup.
  • Pacing Relationship:
  • Total time delay equals ARTM5P multiplied by the ARCMSK evaluation period (~704 ms * 12 ≈ 8.4 seconds).
