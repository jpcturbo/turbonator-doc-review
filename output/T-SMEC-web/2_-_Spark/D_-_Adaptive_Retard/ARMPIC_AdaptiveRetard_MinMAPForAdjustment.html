1. Overview & Purpose

ARMPIC defines the minimum manifold absolute pressure threshold required for adaptive knock retard subtraction to take effect.

Adaptive knock retard is engineered specifically to protect the engine against sustained detonation under high-load boost operation. Under light cruising loads and idle, applying learned retard would be unnecessary and detrimental to fuel economy and drivability. In factory calibrations, ARMPIC is set to 121 counts, corresponding to approximately 14.0 psi absolute (about 0.7 inHg of light vacuum). If manifold pressure is below or equal to ARMPIC, adaptive retard subtraction from both GOVNER and HOTMAP/WOTMAP is completely bypassed. Setting ARMPIC to the maximum value of 255 permanently locks out all adaptive retard.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Disabling Adaptive Knock Retard:
  • Setting ARMPIC to 255 (maximum) permanently disables adaptive knock retard from altering timing under any operating condition.
  • Boost-Only Gate Tuning:
  • Raising ARMPIC to atmospheric pressure (~14.7 psi absolute) ensures adaptive retard only engages when manifold pressure crosses into positive boost.
  • 3-Bar MAP Sensor Rescaling:
  • When converting to a 3-bar MAP sensor, rescale ARMPIC from 121 counts down to approximately 81 counts to maintain the same absolute pressure gate.
