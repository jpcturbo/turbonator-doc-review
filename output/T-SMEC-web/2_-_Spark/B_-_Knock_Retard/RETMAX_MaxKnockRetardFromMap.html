1. Overview & Purpose

RETMAX defines the maximum allowable knock retard ceiling curve indexed by intake manifold pressure.

Under heavy turbocharger boost, persistent detonation is destructive and can quickly lead to engine damage. To safeguard the engine under severe detonation, the controller must be authorized to pull significant timing (up to 14.5 degrees in stock calibrations). Conversely, under high vacuum or light load, allowing massive knock retard would be counterproductive, potentially causing combustion to flame out into the exhaust manifold. RETMAX scales the maximum permissible knock retard proportionally with manifold pressure, starting from 3.0 degrees at deep vacuum and ramping up to 14.5 degrees under positive boost.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • High-Boost Safety Expansion:
  • Increasing RETMAX to 16-18 degrees in positive boost regions ensures the knock control system has enough authority to extinguish severe detonation under high boost.
  • False Knock Protection:
  • Restricting RETMAX in light vacuum regions prevents false mechanical noise from pulling excessive timing and bogging the car.
  • 3-Bar MAP Sensor Rescaling:
  • Recalculate pressure breakpoints when upgrading to a 3-bar MAP sensor to maintain appropriate retard ceilings across the expanded scale.
