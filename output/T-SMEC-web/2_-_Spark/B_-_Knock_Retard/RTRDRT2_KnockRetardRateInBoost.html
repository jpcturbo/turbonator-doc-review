1. Overview & Purpose

RTRDRT2 defines the step-size of knock retard applied to a knocking cylinder when knock is detected under boost (manifold pressure above barometric pressure plus KNKOFS).

Under positive turbocharger boost, pulling massive chunks of timing abruptly (such as 4 or 5 degrees in a single cylinder firing) can induce violent torque drops, driveline shock, and severe spikes in exhaust gas temperature. To manage boost detonation smoothly while preserving engine power delivery and component integrity, RTRDRT2 uses a finer, controlled step size (factory setting 4 counts = 2.0 degrees) applied sequentially to each knocking cylinder.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Boost Detonation Management:
  • Factory setting of 2.0 degrees (4 counts) balances rapid knock quenching with smooth torque delivery under boost.
  • High-Boost Aggressive Protection:
  • On high-boost setups (18+ psi), raising RTRDRT2 to 3.0 degrees (6 counts) pulls timing faster when boost detonation occurs.
  • Smoothness vs. Speed:
  • Lowering RTRDRT2 to 1.0 or 1.5 degrees produces seamless transitions during minor knock, but takes longer to accumulate total retard if severe detonation occurs.
