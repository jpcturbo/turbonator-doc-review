1. Overview & Purpose

ADVDFT establishes the default minimum mechanical baseline spark advance floor (factory setting 20 counts = 10.0 degrees BTDC).

When the engine operates at closed throttle, the calculated mechanical advance from GOVNER (after any adaptive retard subtraction) is compared against ADVDFT; if lower, it is clamped to ADVDFT. This ensures that closed-throttle engine operation maintains a stable minimum 10.0 degrees BTDC timing foundation, preventing excessive timing retard from causing rough idle, stalling, or poor off-idle throttle pickup.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Idle Torque Support for Large Cams:
  • Raising ADVDFT to 12-14 degrees BTDC provides additional base torque to stabilize idle on aggressive camshafts.
  • High Idle Speed Correction:
  • If base idle speed is persistently high due to minor intake air leaks or large throttle bodies, lowering ADVDFT can reduce combustion torque to bring idle RPM down.
  • Closed-Throttle Gate:
  • ADVDFT is active strictly when b4f_OffIdle is clear; off-idle acceleration bypasses this clamp.
