1. Overview & Purpose

PNIDLE defines a coolant temperature-indexed spark advance trim applied specifically when an automatic transmission vehicle is idling in Park or Neutral at low engine speed.

In automatic transmission vehicles, placing the gear selector into Drive or Reverse engages the torque converter, placing continuous parasitic drag on the crankshaft. When shifted back into Park or Neutral, this mechanical drag vanishes, causing engine manifold vacuum to deepen and idle speed to flare unless airflow and ignition timing are adjusted. PNIDLE modifies ignition timing as a function of coolant temperature to smooth out gear engagement transitions between In-Gear and Park/Neutral.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Automatic Shift Flare Taming:
  • If the engine flares aggressively when shifted from Drive to Park or Neutral, lower values in PNIDLE to pull timing in Neutral.
  • Stabilizing Cold Neutral Idle:
  • Adjusting the lower temperature points in PNIDLE helps balance cold idle speed between Drive and Park/Neutral.
  • Manual Transmission (MTX) Note:
  • In manual transmission calibrations, the Park/Neutral switch is grounded or bypassed, rendering PNIDLE inactive.
