1. Overview & Purpose

SPKLMT defines the absolute maximum spark advance ceiling curve across engine speed.

Under light-throttle highway cruising in deep manifold vacuum, the mathematical sum of mechanical advance (GOVNER) and part-throttle vacuum advance (HOTMAP) could easily exceed 50 to 60 degrees BTDC. Running excessive ignition advance—even under very light engine load—can cause misfires, rotor phase misalignment and spark jump inside the distributor cap, and negative mechanical work against the compressing piston. SPKLMT enforces a hard upper limit: if the total combined ignition timing exceeds the value in SPKLMT at that RPM, it is clamped down to SPKLMT. In factory calibrations, SPKLMT is calibrated to approximately 53 degrees BTDC.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Distributor Crossfire Prevention:
  • SPKLMT ensures rotor tip phasing remains aligned with distributor cap terminals, preventing spark jump to adjacent cylinders.
  • Highway Cruising Efficiency:
  • Setting SPKLMT between 42 and 48 degrees BTDC supports optimal fuel economy during light highway cruising.
  • Safety Clamp:
  • Acts as the final clamp on total calculated timing before spark scatter and cylinder-specific knock retard are applied.
