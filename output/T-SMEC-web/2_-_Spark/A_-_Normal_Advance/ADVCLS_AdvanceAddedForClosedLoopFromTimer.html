1. Overview & Purpose

ADVCLS defines an additive spark advance correction table indexed by the open-loop timer fraction during warm part-throttle operation.

When a warm engine transitions from starting into closed-loop operation, combustion conditions stabilize as the oxygen sensor begins closed-loop fuel control. ADVCLS provides a progressive spark advance ramp (up to 1.5 degrees BTDC in stock calibrations) as the open-loop fraction timer increments, helping to smoothly integrate part-throttle torque delivery as the controller enters stable closed-loop running.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: DetermineHotAdvanceFromMap (T-SMEC_v19.asm:7991-8000), called from CalculateSparkAdvance_MM.
  • Gating Conditions:
  • Evaluated only when coolant temperature is at or above TMPSWP and throttle is off-idle (b4f_OffIdle set) but not WOT.
  • Table Lookup Input:
  • Indexed by Timer_OpenLoopFraction (RAM address 0x0084).
  • Addition & Overflow Handling:
  • Output from ADVCLS is added to Temp1 (which holds the HOTMAP advance).
  • If two's complement overflow occurs (bvc not taken), accumulator A is clamped to maximum positive (0x7F) or negative (0x80).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Transition Smoothing:
  • ADVCLS adds small timing increments (up to 1.5 degrees) as the open loop timer increments, smoothing the transition into closed loop.
  • Simplifying Timing Surfaces:
  • Zeroing out ADVCLS eliminates time-based spark shifting, making part-throttle timing strictly dependent on manifold pressure and RPM.
  • Raising for Tip-In Torque:
  • Adding minor advance in ADVCLS can improve light throttle response during the immediate post-crank period.
