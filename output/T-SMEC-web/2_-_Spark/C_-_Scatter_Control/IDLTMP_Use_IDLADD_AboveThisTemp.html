1. Overview & Purpose

IDLTMP defines the minimum engine coolant temperature threshold required to activate IDLADD idle fuel stabilization.

While the engine is cold, fuel puddle dynamics in the intake manifold runners are unstable and cold enrichment fuel multipliers are active. Modulating fuel pulsewidths based on idle speed fluctuations while the engine is cold could destabilize the fuel film and cause erratic fueling. In factory calibrations, IDLTMP is set to 138 counts, corresponding to approximately 176 degF, ensuring that fuel stabilization corrections are only permitted after the engine reaches fully warmed operating temperature.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: LookupInjectorLatency (T-SMEC_v19.asm:5950-5953).
  • Comparison & Branch:
  • ldaa CoolantTemp loads coolant temperature.
  • cmpa IDLTMP_Use_IDLADD_AboveThisTemp at line 5951.
  • If CoolantTemp <= IDLTMP (bls L1511), execution branches to line 5955, bypassing fuel stabilization.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Thermostat Compensation:
  • If running a 160 degF thermostat, lower IDLTMP to approximately 150 degF (125 counts) to allow idle fuel stabilization to engage.
  • Disabling Fuel Stabilization:
  • Setting IDLTMP to 255 disables IDLADD across the board, isolating idle speed control purely to ignition scatter and the idle air motor.
  • Cold Fuel Stability:
  • Keeping IDLTMP above 160 degF prevents fuel modulation while cold warm-up enrichment curves are still active.
