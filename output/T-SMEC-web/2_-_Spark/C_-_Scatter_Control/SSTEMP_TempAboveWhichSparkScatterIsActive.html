1. Overview & Purpose

SSTEMP defines the minimum engine coolant temperature threshold required to activate spark scatter idle speed stabilization.

Spark scatter is a high-speed closed-loop idle control system that rapidly modulates ignition timing advance and retard on each individual distributor pulse to hold engine idle speed precisely on target. During cold warm-up operation, the engine is already running rich warm-up fueling and elevated idle bypass airflow; applying rapid timing modulation on cold cylinder charges could destabilize combustion and cause rough running. In factory calibrations, SSTEMP is set to 88 counts, corresponding to approximately 122 degF, ensuring that spark scatter remains locked out until the combustion chambers reach stable operating temperatures.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Disabling for Track / Road Racing:
  • Setting SSTEMP to 254 permanently disables spark scatter, preventing closed-throttle timing retards between quick manual gear shifts and assisting rev-matching on deceleration.
  • Aftermarket Camshaft Idling:
  • Engines with aggressive camshaft overlap often dislike rapid spark modulation; raising SSTEMP or disabling scatter allows the engine to settle into a natural, steady idle lope.
  • Warmup Stability:
  • Factory setting of 122 degF keeps scatter disabled while cold enrichment curves are active, transitioning smoothly to scatter as closed-loop fueling approaches.
