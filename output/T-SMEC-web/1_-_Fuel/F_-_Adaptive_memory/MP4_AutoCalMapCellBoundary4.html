1. Overview & Purpose

MP4 sets the fourth manifold absolute pressure boundary, separating Cell 6 and 7 from Cell 8 and 9 in the adaptive memory grid.

This boundary represents heavy part-throttle operation where manifold vacuum is dropping toward atmospheric pressure, approaching positive boost. In factory calibrations, MP4 is set to 97 counts, corresponding to approximately 11.2 psia or 7.2 inHg of vacuum.

Operating in this range places high air mass demands on the engine while remaining in closed-loop stoichiometric control, making dedicated long-term fuel trim tracking essential for smooth power delivery.
