1. Overview & Purpose

AMUPDI specifies the number of engine pulse periods (distributor rising edges) required between adaptive fuel memory updates while operating at closed-throttle idle in Cell 12.

At 800 RPM, 26 distributor pulses elapse in approximately one second. In stock calibrations, AMUPDI is set to 26 counts, matching the part-throttle rate.

This establishes a steady, controlled update pace for the dedicated idle fuel trim cell, allowing the oxygen sensor to toggle several times between each long-term trim step.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Idle Fuel Trim Stability:
  • Increasing AMUPDI slows down idle cell updates, preventing aggressive camshafts from destabilizing idle trims.
  • Large Injector Tuning:
  • Larger injectors require a slower idle update rate to prevent hunting around stoichiometric targeting.
  • Units & Timing:
  • Measured in distributor pulse edges (2 per engine revolution). At 800 RPM, 26 counts represents approximately 1.0 second.
