1. Overview & Purpose

MP1 defines the first manifold absolute pressure load boundary in the adaptive fuel memory grid, separating Cell 0 and 1 from Cell 2 and 3.

The Chrysler SMEC organizes long-term fuel trim into 13 distinct memory cells, comprising 12 off-idle cells arranged across 6 manifold pressure load columns and 2 engine speed rows, plus one dedicated closed-throttle idle cell. MP1 represents the transition from extreme high-vacuum conditions, such as trailing-throttle coastdown and steep deceleration overrun, into light-load cruise.

In stock calibrations, MP1 is set to 36 counts, which corresponds to approximately 4.1 psia or 21.6 inHg of vacuum.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Camshaft Upgrades:
  • High-overlap cams produce less engine vacuum at light load; raising MP1 prevents light cruise from being trapped in deceleration fuel cells.
  • 3-Bar MAP Sensor Rescaling:
  • Must be scaled down in counts to match the 44.1 psi absolute range (approximately 24 counts on a 3-bar sensor).
  • Ascending Order Requirement:
  • MP1 through MP5 must strictly maintain ascending numerical order: MP1 < MP2 < MP3 < MP4 < MP5.
  • Lowering the Boundary:
  • Shrinks the decel fuel cell domain, causing Cell 2 and 3 to take effect at higher manifold vacuum.
