1. Overview & Purpose

CLTIME is a two-dimensional lookup table that specifies the elapsed delay time required before the engine controller transitions from open-loop startup enrichment into closed-loop oxygen sensor feedback, indexed against startup coolant temperature.

Freshly cranked engines require rich open-loop fuel delivery to maintain steady combustion until cylinders warm and intake manifold air velocity stabilizes.

In stock calibrations, CLTIME specifies approximately 8.4 seconds at 84 degF and approximately 14 seconds at 132 degF, where each count represents approximately 2.8 seconds. This programmed delay ensures the engine runs smoothly during post-start idle before closed-loop feedback takes authority.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Start Driveability:
  • Increasing table points extends open-loop enrichment after starting, curing lean stumbles and idle surging during cold drive-away.
  • Alternative Fuels (E85):
  • Ethanol requires higher latent heat of vaporization; lengthening CLTIME prevents the narrowband sensor from pulling needed warmup fuel.
  • Warm Restart Optimization:
  • Lowering points at warm temperatures reduces fuel smell and stabilizes hot restart idle more quickly.
  • Minimum Value:
  • Setting to zero allows instantaneous closed-loop entry if CLTEMP is satisfied.
