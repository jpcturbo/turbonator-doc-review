1. Overview & Purpose

O2TLML defines the lower boundary of the delayed closed-loop entry voltage band.

When the engine controller is evaluating whether the oxygen sensor has warmed up sufficiently to begin closed-loop operation, sensor voltage between O2TLML and O2TLMH indicates the sensor is generating partial output. In stock calibrations, O2TLML is set to 15 counts, corresponding to approximately 0.29 volts. Signals falling inside this band initiate the delayed entry countdown rather than being rejected outright.
