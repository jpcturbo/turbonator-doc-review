1. Overview & Purpose

PRIRMP is a specialized 22-word ramp configuration table that governs short-term oxygen sensor feedback fuel corrections across a 3 by 3 grid of engine speed and manifold pressure load cells, plus a dedicated closed-throttle idle cell.

When closed-loop operation is active, the engine controller continuously toggles the air-fuel ratio above and below stoichiometric 14.7:1 by applying rich and lean fuel pulsewidth steps (kicks). PRIRMP defines the RPM and MAP grid boundaries, the individual kick magnitudes for rich and lean corrections, and the accumulation limits for each cell.

This table prevents the short-term fuel trim counter from slamming to extremes while ensuring fast, responsive closed-loop tracking.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Curing Rich Closed-Loop Bias:
  • Factory Mopar Performance calibrations frequently exhibited a rich closed-loop bias due to asymmetrical kick steps. Equalizing rich and lean kick values across cells 0-8 centers feedback precisely at 14.7:1.
  • Relocated O2 Sensor & Header Tuning:
  • Long-tube headers increase exhaust transit delay. Slightly raising ramp steps prevents sluggish response, while adjusting limits maintains oscillation stability.
  • Table Structure (Type 11):
  • 22 words total: 1 word RPM boundaries, 1 word MAP boundaries, 10 words ramp kick steps (9 cruise + 1 idle), 10 words accumulation limits.

4. Technical / Assembly Mechanics (Reference)

Inside T-SMEC_v19.asm: In WorkWithPrimaryRamp, line 3119 loads index register X with #PRIRMP_PrimaryRamp. Line 3120 calls DetermineRampCellFromRPMandMAP, which parses the 2 RPM and 2 MAP boundaries to locate the active cell (cells 0-8 for cruise, or idle cell if closed-throttle). At line 3122, addb 0x00,x adds the cell's ramp step to Counter_PrimaryRamp. Line 3123 subtracts this from the cell limit. 5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Table Structure (Type 11):
  • Word 1: RPM Low / High boundary (factory 0x293F = 1312 / 2016 RPM).
  • Word 2: MAP Low / High boundary (factory 0x335E = 5.9 / 10.8 psia).
  • Words 3-11: Cruise Cells 0-8 Ramp Step (Rich high byte, Lean low byte).
  • Word 12: Idle Cell Ramp Step.
  • Words 13-21: Cruise Cells 0-8 Limits/Rates.
  • Word 22: Idle Cell Limit/Rate.
  • Companion Tables: Paired with SECRMP (secondary ramp) and clamped by O2LIMH/O2LIML.
  • Mathematical Units: Kick values are scaled as (factor * 512) - 512 where 1 bit ≈ 0.2% fuel trim.
