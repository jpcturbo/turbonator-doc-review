1. Overview & Purpose

HMPMU2 defines the part-throttle cold load manifold pressure weighting curve.

When driving off-idle while the engine is warming up, intake manifold vacuum varies widely with throttle position and vehicle acceleration. HMPMU2 multiplies against temperature factor HIGHM2 to scale cold part-throttle enrichment based on intake manifold pressure.

This table ensures that accelerating uphill or passing traffic on a cold engine receives appropriate enrichment, while light cruising at deep vacuum remains clean and efficient.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • 3-Bar MAP Calibration:
  • Re-index pressure break points when migrating to a 3-bar MAP sensor so cold off-idle enrichment activates at the correct vacuum levels.
  • Cold Acceleration Drivability:
  • If acceleration hesitates off-idle before reaching 180 degF, increase HMPMU2 in the mid-vacuum range.
  • Part-Throttle Gating:
  • Evaluated only when b4f_OffIdle is set; closed-throttle idle uses HMPMUL.
  • Multiplication Math:
  • Multiplied by HIGHM2 in Temp4 and divided by 32 in CPU register D.
