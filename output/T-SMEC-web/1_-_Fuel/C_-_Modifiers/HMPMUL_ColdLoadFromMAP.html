1. Overview & Purpose

HMPMUL defines the manifold absolute pressure weighting curve for cold engine load enrichment at closed throttle and idle.

Working in partnership with HIGHMP, HMPMUL ensures that cold load enrichment scales proportionally with intake manifold pressure. When manifold vacuum drops due to mechanical engine loading (such as air conditioning compressor engagement or alternator charging drag), HMPMUL provides an increasing multiplier that enriches fuel delivery.

This table maintains clean, non-fouling idle mixtures at high vacuum while delivering instant fuel enrichment when vacuum drops.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • 3-Bar MAP Rescaling:

  • Accessory Engagement Drivability:
  • If cold idle rpm dips or stumbles when the air conditioner turns on, raise HMPMUL in the 8 to 12 inHg vacuum range.
  • Closed-Throttle Isolation:
  • Operates strictly while b4f_OffIdle is clear; off-idle conditions use HMPMU2.
  • Multiplier Math:
