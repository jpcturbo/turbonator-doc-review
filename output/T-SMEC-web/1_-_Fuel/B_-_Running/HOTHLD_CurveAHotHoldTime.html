1. Overview & Purpose

HOTHLD specifies the duration that after-start enrichment (Curve A) is held at full strength following a hot restart.

When restarting an engine that has heat-soaked above TMPHLD, fuel within the fuel rail and injector bodies may have experienced partial vaporization or vapor bubbles. HOTHLD maintains after-start enrichment for a calibrated dwell period, clearing fuel rail vapor lock and stabilizing cylinder combustion before enrichment decays.

In factory calibrations, HOTHLD is set to 37 counts, representing approximately 26.0 seconds of hold time.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hot Soak Vapor Mitigation:
  • Increasing HOTHLD extends enrichment dwell, purging fuel rail bubbles and stabilizing hot restarts after brief engine stops.
  • Aftermarket Injector Tuning:
  • Large injectors often supply sufficient fuel without prolonged hot enrichment; lowering HOTHLD eliminates dark smoke on warm starts.
  • Hold Duration Scaling:
  • Hold time equals counts multiplied by 0.704 seconds (37 counts = 26.0 seconds).
  • Companion Hold Parameters:
  • Evaluated as the hot-start hold counterpart to cold-start hold constant CLDHLD.

  • Dual-Offset Pairing: Paired with CLDHLD in ROM (TMPHLD + 1 = CLDHLD, TMPHLD + 2 = HOTHLD).
  • Hot Hold Duration: Stock 37 counts produces a 26.0 second hold window, substantially longer than cold hold to account for manifold heat soak.
