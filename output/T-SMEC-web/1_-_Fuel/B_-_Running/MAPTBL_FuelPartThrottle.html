1. Overview & Purpose

MAPTBL defines the primary part-throttle fuel delivery curve indexed by intake manifold pressure.

Under all normal driving conditions between closed idle and wide-open throttle, the engine controller references MAPTBL to establish base injector pulsewidth. This table spans from light intake vacuum during cruising through moderate manifold boost.

MAPTBL directly determines everyday street drivability, cruising air-fuel ratio stability, and steady-state fuel economy.
