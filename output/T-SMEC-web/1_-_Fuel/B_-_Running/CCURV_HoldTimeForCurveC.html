1. Overview & Purpose

CCURV defines the hold duration for intermediate warm-up enrichment (Curve C).

In Chrysler's tripartite cold enrichment strategy, intermediate enrichment supplies supplementary fuel between initial start-to-run decay and full engine warm-up. CCURV specifies the number of seconds that Curve C enrichment remains locked at 100% full intensity before beginning its decay to zero.

In factory calibrations, CCURV is set to 7 counts, corresponding to approximately 4.9 seconds of hold time.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Extending Intermediate Dwell:
  • Increasing CCURV to 10-15 counts (7 to 10 seconds) prevents hesitation when pulling away in freezing weather.
  • Hold Duration Formula:
  • Duration in seconds equals counts multiplied by 0.704 seconds (7 counts = 4.9 seconds).
  • Tripartite Coordination:
  • Works with DCYCC (Curve C decay rate) and CURVEC (intermediate enrichment curve).
  • Timing Parameter Clustering:
  • Positioned alongside DCYTMC and DCYCC to manage intermediate warmup dwell time.
