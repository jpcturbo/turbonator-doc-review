1. Overview & Purpose

DFRPMH defines the engine speed delta above target idle speed at which fuel injection is actively shut off during deceleration.

While DCLRPH arms the decel logic, DFRPMH is the execution threshold tested inside the foreground fuel cutout routine. When the decel fuel cut flag is armed, the difference between actual engine RPM and target idle RPM is compared against DFRPMH.

If delta RPM exceeds DFRPMH, the controller sets Turbonator_MainFuelOff, cutting fuel injection to all cylinders.

In factory calibrations, DFRPMH is set to 30 counts, representing 960 RPM above target idle speed.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: Cutout_UseDecelFuelCut (T-SMEC_v19.asm:5627-5636), instruction line 5633.
  • Gating Conditions:
  • Evaluated inside ControlEngineCutout_MM when main fuel is currently on (Turbonator_MainFuelOff clear).
  • Decel fuel cut flag Turbonator_DecelFuelCut must be set.
  • Comparison & Action:
  • Delta RPM (EngineRpm - TargetIdleSpeed) is compared against DFRPMH.
  • If delta RPM > DFRPMH, sets Turbonator_MainFuelOff at line 5635, shutting off all fuel injection.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Decel Cutoff Onset:
  • Controls the exact RPM delta where injector pulses cease upon throttle lift-off.
  • Smoothness vs Economy:
  • Higher values provide softer deceleration with less driveline lash; lower values maximize fuel savings and engine braking.
  • Exhaust Sound Tuning:
  • If trailing throttle causes harsh exhaust pops or backfiring, raising DFRPMH softens the transition into fuel cut.
  • Hysteresis Relationship:
  • Must exceed DFRPML (fuel turn-on threshold) by at least 8 to 12 counts to prevent injector chatter.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Foreground Execution: Evaluated every pass through ControlEngineCutout_MM in the main foreground loop.
  • Delta RPM Math: 30 counts * 32 RPM = 960 RPM above target idle speed (e.g. cuts at 1760 RPM if idle is 800 RPM).
  • Transition to Hysteresis: Once Turbonator_MainFuelOff is set, fuel stays off until evaluated in Cutout_UseHysteresisToDetermineWhenToTurnFuelBackOn.
