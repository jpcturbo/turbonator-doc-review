1. Overview & Purpose

AESLOP defines the temperature-dependent gain curve for MAP-based acceleration enrichment pump shots.

When manifold pressure rises suddenly, extra fuel must be injected immediately to compensate for manifold wall-wetting and the sudden surge in cylinder air mass. AESLOP scales the magnitude of this pump shot according to engine coolant temperature, providing generous enrichment when the engine is cold and tapering down as the engine reaches operating temperature.

At 88 degF, the factory calibration delivers approximately 1.5 milliseconds of extra pulse width per psi of pressure rise, tapering down to approximately 0.47 milliseconds per psi at 205 degF.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Curing Lean Boost Tip-in: Increasing table values cures lean tip-in stumbles, hesitation, or intake popping that occurs specifically as manifold pressure jumps from vacuum into boost.
  • Large Injector Rescaling: Decreasing table values is required when installing larger injectors to prevent momentary rich bogging and exhaust smoke upon sudden acceleration. Scale down in proportion to injector flow increase.
  • 3-Bar MAP Sensor Calibration: Reduce table values by approximately 33 percent when converting to a 3-bar MAP sensor to compensate for higher pressure per analog voltage step.
  • Cold Engine Sizing: Ensure cold points provide roughly 2 to 3 times the gain of warm points to overcome fuel puddling in cold intake runners.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Units and Scaling: Although legacy template files reference Torr on the raw Y axis, the firmware performs math on native pressure counts. Tuners should calibrate in milliseconds per psi or microseconds per psi.
  • Companion Parameter: Coordinates with MAPTRG, MAPMAX, POSSLP, and AETIME.
  • 3-Bar MAP Interaction: Converting to a 3-bar sensor increases the effective pressure per ADC step, necessitating a proportional reduction in AESLOP table values.
