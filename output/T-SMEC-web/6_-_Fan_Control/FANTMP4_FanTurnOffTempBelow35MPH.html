1. Overview & Purpose

FANTMP4 defines the engine coolant temperature threshold where the cooling fan shuts off after completing a low-speed cooling cycle below 35 MPH.

After the fan engages at low speed or idle (triggered by FANTMP3), it remains energized until coolant temperature is reduced below FANTMP4. In factory calibrations, FANTMP4 is set to 221 counts, corresponding to approximately 194 °F. This creates an approximate 5 to 6 °F hysteresis cushion below the 199 °F turn-on threshold, ensuring the fan runs long enough to achieve meaningful cooling before shutting down.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Thermostat Rating Floor:
  • Set FANTMP4 at least 3 to 5 °F above the thermostat rated opening temperature (e.g. 184 °F for a 180 °F thermostat).
  • Preventing Continuous Running:
  • If FANTMP4 is lower than the thermostat opening point, the fan will never turn off at idle.
  • Hysteresis Stagger:
  • Maintain a 5 to 7 °F gap below FANTMP3 to prevent rapid relay fluttering.
