1. Overview & Purpose

FANTMP2 defines the engine coolant temperature threshold where the radiator cooling fan is turned off after a highway cooling cycle above 45 MPH.

Once the cooling fan is engaged at high vehicle speed, it continues running until coolant temperature is driven down below FANTMP2. In factory calibrations, FANTMP2 is set to 227 counts, corresponding to approximately 199 °F. This establishes a 5 °F hysteresis buffer below the turn-on setpoint (204 °F), preventing rapid cycling and relay flutter while driving on the highway.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Proportional Downward Shift:
  • Lower FANTMP2 alongside FANTMP1 when fitting a cooler thermostat (e.g. 188 °F for a 180 °F thermostat).
  • Thermostat Rating Floor:
  • Ensure FANTMP2 is at least 4 to 6 °F above the thermostat mechanical rating so the fan can shut off.
  • Deadband Width:
  • Retain a 4 to 6 °F gap below FANTMP1 to avoid relay chatter.
