1. Overview & Purpose

MAPADD is the sixteen-bit zero-offset addition constant in the manifold absolute pressure sensor transfer function.

In linear sensor scaling, physical pressure transducers produce a residual electrical offset voltage even under complete vacuum. Added directly to the multiplied voltage product inside the pressure conversion routine, MAPADD mathematically aligns zero internal pressure counts with absolute zero vacuum. In factory calibrations for two-bar sensors, MAPADD is set to eight hundred sixty-five counts. When upgrading to a three-bar sensor, MAPADD is calibrated to seventeen hundred counts to reflect the expanded pressure span.
