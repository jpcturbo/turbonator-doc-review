1. Overview & Purpose

MINAMP defines the absolute lower limit floor for the running average manifold absolute pressure variable.

When transitioning off-idle, the controller fakes a delta pressure surge by subtracting OCTAE from instantaneous manifold pressure. Under severe trailing-throttle overrun deceleration where manifold pressure is already extremely low, this subtraction could cause mathematical underflow. MINAMP prevents this by establishing a hard lower floor. Configured in factory calibrations to six counts, MINAMP represents approximately zero point six nine psi absolute (about twenty-eight point five inches of mercury vacuum).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Mathematical Underflow Prevention:
  • Enforces a non-zero floor for AverageMAPValue during off-idle delta pressure calculations.
  • Three-Bar Sensor Conversions:
  • Can be retained at factory setting of six counts or reduced to four counts on 3-bar MAP conversions.
  • Avoid Excessive Values:
  • Setting MINAMP too high inflates perceived engine load on closed-throttle overrun.
  • Factory Setting:
  • Six counts (~0.69 psi absolute / 28.5 inHg vacuum) in stock calibrations.
