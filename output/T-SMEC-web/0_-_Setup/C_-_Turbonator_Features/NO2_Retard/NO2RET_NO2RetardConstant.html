1. Overview & Purpose

NO2RET defines the amount of ignition timing retard applied when the nitrous oxide activation switch is triggered.

Burning nitrous oxide releases concentrated oxygen into the combustion chamber, accelerating the flame front and causing cylinder pressure to peak much earlier than normal. To protect pistons, connecting rods, and head gaskets from extreme cylinder pressure spikes, ignition timing must be retarded whenever nitrous flows.

NO2RET provides this dedicated safety retard, with the factory setting of 31 counts providing approximately 3.875 degrees of spark retard.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Executed when opt_NO2Retard is enabled in OPTN1 and the switch configured in NO2SWS is active.
  • NO2RET_NO2RetardConstant is loaded into accumulator A at line 10358 and shifted right three times (asra; asra; asra), dividing by 8.
  • Result is stored in RAM variable NO2Retard at line 10364.
  • In CalculateSparkAdvance_MM at line 8006, instruction suba NO2Retard subtracts the retard directly from total spark advance.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Shot Size Scaling: A standard tuning rule of thumb is 1.5 to 2.0 degrees of retard for every 50 horsepower of nitrous shot.
  • Recommended Setpoints: 16 counts (~2.0 degrees) for 50 hp, 32 counts (~4.0 degrees) for 100 hp, and 48 counts (~6.0 degrees) for 150 hp.
  • Combined Boost Caution: When spraying nitrous on top of turbocharger boost, use conservative timing retard and inspect spark plug ground straps for signs of excessive combustion heat.

4. Technical / Assembly Mechanics (Reference)

Inside T-SMEC_v19.asm: When nitrous activation conditions are verified, execution branches to LoadNO2Retard at line 10357. Line 10358 loads NO2RET_NO2RetardConstant into accumulator A. Three consecutive arithmetic shift right operations (asra; asra; asra) at lines 10359 to 10361 divide the calibrated value by 8.
