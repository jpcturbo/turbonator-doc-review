1. Overview & Purpose

AKPMOF establishes the manifold pressure turn-off threshold for the auxiliary alcohol or water injection delivery pump.

Once energized by high boost pressure, the delivery pump must remain running until boost drops cleanly below the operational window. AKPMOF provides the lower boundary of a hysteresis loop, preventing rapid pump relay cycling and pressure fluctuations during transient throttle adjustments.

The factory setting is 255 counts (disabled).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Enclosed in subroutine AlkyPump_CheckHysteresisAndTurnOff at lines 10578-10585 of cals/SMEC/T-SMEC_v19.asm.
  • Evaluated when MapValue falls below AKPMON in MM_AlkyPump_Control.
  • In active firmware, this subroutine is commented out with semicolons in T-SMEC_v19.asm (as documented in smec_anomaly.md), meaning the controller does not execute these instructions unless uncommented in source.
  • Comparison: cmpa AKPMOF_AlkyPumpOffSetpointFromMAP against MapValue (line 10579).
  • If MapValue >= AKPMOF (bcc AlkyPump_Return), the pump remains energized. If MapValue < AKPMOF, line 10581 clears PortA_PurgeEGR to shut off the pump.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hysteresis Band: Calibrate roughly 1 to 2 psi below AKPMON to prevent relay chatter.
  • Threshold Ordering: AKPMOF must always be calibrated lower than AKPMON.
  • Deactivation Baseline: Factory setting of 255 counts keeps the feature inactive.
