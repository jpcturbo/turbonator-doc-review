1. Overview & Purpose

AKI2OF establishes the manifold pressure turn-off threshold for the secondary stage water or alcohol injection solenoid.

When manifold boost drops below this point during deceleration or between gear changes, the engine controller shuts off the secondary injection solenoid. AKI2OF provides the lower boundary of a hysteresis loop, preventing solenoid flutter and preserving steady fueling when engine boost fluctuates around the secondary turn-on threshold.

The factory setting is 255 counts (disabled).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Enclosed in subroutine AlkyInj2_CheckHysteresisAndTurnOff at lines 10629-10636 of cals/SMEC/T-SMEC_v19.asm.
  • Evaluated in AlkyInj_CheckInj2 when manifold pressure drops below AKI2ON.
  • In active firmware, this subroutine is commented out with semicolons in T-SMEC_v19.asm (as documented in smec_anomaly.md), meaning the controller does not execute these instructions unless uncommented in source.
  • Comparison: cmpa AKI2OF_AlkyInj2OffSetpointFromMAP against MapValue (line 10630).
  • If MapValue >= AKI2OF (bcc AlkyInj_Return), secondary injection remains active. If MapValue < AKI2OF, line 10632 clears PortA_CruiseVac to de-energize the solenoid.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hysteresis Gap: Calibrate roughly 1 to 2 psi below AKI2ON to prevent solenoid chattering under boost fluctuations.
  • Threshold Ordering: AKI2OF must always be calibrated lower than AKI2ON.
  • Deactivation Baseline: Factory setting of 255 counts keeps stage two turned off.
