1. Overview & Purpose

AKI1OF establishes the manifold pressure turn-off threshold for the primary stage water or alcohol injection solenoid.

Once triggered under boost, the primary injection solenoid remains open until manifold pressure drops below this threshold. AKI1OF provides the lower boundary of a hysteresis loop, preventing solenoid fluttering and erratic fuel mixture changes when engine load hovers near the activation boost level.

The factory setting is 255 counts (disabled).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Enclosed in subroutine AlkyInj1_CheckHysteresisAndTurnOff at lines 10615-10620 of cals/SMEC/T-SMEC_v19.asm.
  • Evaluated when injector one is active or when MapValue is below AKI1ON.
  • In active firmware, this subroutine is commented out with semicolons in T-SMEC_v19.asm (as documented in smec_anomaly.md), meaning the controller does not execute these instructions unless uncommented in source.
  • Comparison: cmpa AKI1OF_AlkyInj1OffSetpointFromMAP against MapValue (line 10616).
  • If MapValue >= AKI1OF (bcc AlkyInj_Return), injector one stays energized. If MapValue < AKI1OF, line 10618 clears PortA_CruiseVent to shut off injector one.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hysteresis Spread: Calibrate roughly 1 to 2 psi below AKI1ON to prevent solenoid chatter.
  • Threshold Ordering: AKI1OF must always be calibrated lower than AKI1ON.
  • Deactivation Baseline: Factory setting of 255 counts keeps the output disabled.
