1. Overview & Purpose

BAUDMD defines an intermediate serial communication baud rate setting originally intended for custom datalogging programs.

Configured in calibration templates to forty-eight counts, BAUDMD specifies a transfer speed of nine thousand six hundred eight baud (9608 baud). In the T-SMEC v19 firmware codebase, executable assembly instructions bypass BAUDMD and load BAUDHI directly during both high-speed serial transfer initialization and cold startup. Consequently, BAUDMD remains preserved in the calibration ROM image as a legacy constant but is not actively executed by the firmware.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Unreferenced Firmware Constant:
  • In T-SMEC_v19.asm, high-speed serial routines load BAUDHI directly, leaving BAUDMD completely unreferenced.
  • Modify BAUDHI Instead:
  • To change datalogging baud rates in MPTune, modify BAUDHI rather than BAUDMD.
  • Preserve Default Setting:
  • Keep at forty-eight counts (9608 Baud) to maintain calibration checksum integrity.

4. Technical / Assembly Mechanics (Reference)

Inside T-SMEC_v19.asm: BAUDMD_HiSpeedSerialBaudRate is defined in calibration templates at address 0x1E72, directly following BAUDHI. However, in T-SMEC_v19.asm, both SetupHiSpeedDataTransfer (line 3720) and ZeroRamFrom0EtoFE (line 7236) load BAUDHI_DRBBaudRateHi. No executable assembly instructions reference the BAUDMD symbol, making it an inactive calibration constant.
