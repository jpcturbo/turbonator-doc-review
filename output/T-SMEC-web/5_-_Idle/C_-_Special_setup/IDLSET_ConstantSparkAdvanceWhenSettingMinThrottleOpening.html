1. Overview & Purpose

IDLSET defines the fixed spark advance angle commanded when the engine controller enters DRB diagnostic service mode for minimum throttle airflow adjustment.

To properly set the mechanical throttle plate stop screw, engine speed must reflect only physical air passing through the throttle bore, without interference from electronic spark advance hunting or spark scatter idle stabilization.

When the technician selects DRB service mode 14/10, the engine controller disables normal MAP advance lookups and locks ignition timing precisely to IDLSET.

In factory calibrations, IDLSET is configured to 12 degrees BTDC.

Locking ignition advance to 12 degrees BTDC provides a rock-steady spark reference angle, allowing the technician to accurately adjust the mechanical stop screw to achieve the required base idle airflow.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Enclosing Routine: HandleSparkScatter (T-SMEC_v19.asm:8106-8188), specifically at DoUseSparkScatter (line 8177).
  • Gating Conditions:
  • Spark scatter is active or being bypassed; execution reaches DoUseSparkScatter at line 8177.
  • DRB diagnostic mode pointers are tested: DRBPointer1 must equal 0x14 at line 8180, and DRBPointer2 must equal 0x10 at line 8183.
  • If both DRB mode pointers match, the CPU loads IDLSET at line 8185, stores it into Temp5, and clears BitFlags_55 bit b55_UseSparkScatter at line 8187.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Calibration Reference:
  • Keep IDLSET at 12 degrees BTDC to preserve the standard factory reference condition for minimum throttle opening adjustment.
  • Throttle Body Stop Setting:
  • Changing IDLSET alters engine torque during the adjustment procedure, leading to an incorrect mechanical screw position that robs the stepper motor of its dynamic authority.
  • Hunting Prevention:
  • Spark scatter is automatically disabled while IDLSET is active, ensuring RPM changes during service reflect only throttle blade adjustments.
