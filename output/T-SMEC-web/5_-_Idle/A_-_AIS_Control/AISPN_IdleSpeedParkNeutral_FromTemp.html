1. Overview & Purpose

AISPN is the foundational 2D calibration curve defining the target engine idle speed across operating coolant temperatures whenever the drivetrain is unloaded.

This table governs idle speed for all manual transmission vehicles, as well as automatic transmission vehicles when the gear selector is placed in Park or Neutral. A cold engine has poor fuel vaporization and high internal mechanical drag, requiring an elevated idle speed to maintain smooth running and rapid catalytic converter warm-up. As engine coolant temperature warms to normal operating levels (180 °F to 200 °F), the target idle speed gradually steps down to a quiet, fuel-efficient baseline.

In factory calibrations, AISPN commands approximately 1400 RPM during extreme cold weather (-20 °F), tapering smoothly down to a warm steady-state target of 900 to 950 RPM.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Aftermarket Camshaft Idle Stabilization:
  • Elevate warm coolant points (170 °F to 200 °F) to 950 to 1100 RPM to sustain healthy manifold vacuum and prevent stalling on performance camshafts.
  • Manual Transmission Base Idle:
  • On manual transmission vehicles, AISPN is the exclusive idle speed curve; set your desired operating idle speed directly in this table.
  • Low RPM Danger:
  • Do not lower warm idle targets below 850 RPM, as this can lead to insufficient engine oil pressure and alternator under-charging at stoplights.
