1. Overview & Purpose

AISDLY defines the post-startup time delay during which closed-loop automatic idle speed feedback and spark scatter idle stabilization are temporarily inhibited.

Immediately following engine start, combustion chamber conditions undergo rapid thermal changes, and engine speed naturally flares before settling down. If closed-loop stepper motor feedback were active during this initial startup flare, the controller would observe an apparent overspeed error and command the stepper motor to snap shut, causing the engine to dip violently below target idle or stall out completely once the flare subsides.

To prevent this hunting behavior, when transitioning from cranking to run mode, the controller loads AISDLY into a countdown timer.

In factory calibrations, AISDLY is set to 45 counts, representing a feedback lockout period of approximately 2.8 seconds.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Curing Post-Start RPM Dips:
  • Increase AISDLY from 2.8 seconds up to 4.0 to 5.0 seconds if the engine catches with a flare and then immediately dies as the stepper motor prematurely steps shut.
  • Reducing Hang Time:
  • If post-start idle hangs at high RPM for an annoying length of time, trim AISDLY down to 2.0 seconds to bring closed-loop idle regulation online earlier.
  • Stability Margin:
  • Do not set AISDLY below 1.5 seconds, as early closed-loop feedback during the flare transient will trigger severe idle surging.
