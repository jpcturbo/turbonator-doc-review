1. Overview & Purpose

ACPOS defines the fixed auxiliary step opening added to the idle air stepper motor when the air conditioning compressor engages while engine coolant temperature is cold.

A cold air conditioning compressor pump contains thick, viscous refrigeration lubricant that imposes substantial mechanical drag when the magnetic clutch locks up. On a cold engine that is already dealing with high internal mechanical friction, this compressor engagement shock can easily cause a severe RPM plunge or cold stall.

When the air conditioning clutch engages and coolant temperature is below the threshold set in ACTMP, the controller injects ACPOS as a dedicated fixed step adder.

In factory calibrations, ACPOS commands an opening offset of 18 steps.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Enclosing Routine: HandleAcCutout (T-SMEC_v19.asm:2170-2468) within CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM.
  • Gating Conditions:
  • Air conditioning clutch active (sw2_ACclutch clear) and b0c_bit4 clear at line 2447.
  • Engine coolant temperature tested at line 2452 against ACTMP.
  • If CoolantTemp is below ACTMP (bcs L1147), the speed-dependent table ACCTBL is bypassed, and accumulator B retains ACPOS (loaded at line 2448) as the final A/C airflow adder.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold A/C Stall Prevention:
  • Increase ACPOS from 18 to 22 to 26 steps if activating air conditioning or windshield defrost on cold mornings threatens to stall the engine.
  • Taming Cold A/C Flare:
  • If cold A/C engagement causes an annoying flare above 1600 RPM, reduce ACPOS steps.
  • Temperature Threshold Companion:
  • Active exclusively when coolant temperature is below ACTMP (factory 77 °F).
