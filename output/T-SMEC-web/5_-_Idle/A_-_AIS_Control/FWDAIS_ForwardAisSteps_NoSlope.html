1. Overview & Purpose

FWDAIS is the proportional correction lookup table that commands opening steps on the automatic idle speed stepper motor whenever engine speed falls below target idle.

When electrical or mechanical loads suddenly engage, such as the radiator cooling fan turning on, alternator heavy charging, or power steering pump assist, engine speed sags. The SMEC continuously calculates the underspeed error delta by subtracting actual engine speed from target idle speed.

FWDAIS indexes this RPM error to determine how many opening steps to apply to catch the sagging engine speed.

In factory calibrations, FWDAIS incorporates a deadband of approximately 30 RPM where 0 steps are commanded to prevent hunting, then adds 1 step at 50 RPM error, 4 steps at 100 RPM error, and 6 steps at 120 RPM error.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Curing Accessory Load Dips:
  • If turning the steering wheel or activating the headlights causes engine speed to plunge, increase the opening steps in FWDAIS at 50 to 100 RPM error by 1 or 2 steps.
  • Lightweight Flywheel Recovery:
  • Engines with reduced rotating mass decelerate rapidly; slightly larger opening steps help arrest the downward RPM spike before stalling.
  • Surging & Hunting Prevention:
  • If the engine rhythmically surges up and down at idle, reduce FWDAIS opening steps and broaden the zero-step deadband to stabilize the control loop.
