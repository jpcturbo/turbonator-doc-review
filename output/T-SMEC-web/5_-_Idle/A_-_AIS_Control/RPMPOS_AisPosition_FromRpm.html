1. Overview & Purpose

RPMPOS is a 2D calibration curve that establishes a minimum automatic idle speed stepper motor opening based purely on engine RPM.

Even if the throttle is only cracked open slightly, high engine RPM creates massive engine vacuum demand when the throttle plate suddenly snaps shut. If the idle air stepper motor were sitting fully closed during high-RPM deceleration, the sudden throttle closure would create an extreme intake manifold vacuum spike, sucking oil past valve guide seals and causing severe engine braking or sudden stalling.

RPMPOS monitors engine speed and enforces a progressively higher minimum stepper opening floor as engine RPM rises.

In factory calibrations, RPMPOS commands 0 steps below 1000 RPM, transitioning to 3 steps around 1500 RPM, and 6 steps above 2250 RPM.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • High-RPM Decel Stall Catch:
  • Increase RPMPOS step values above 2000 RPM if the engine stalls when abruptly releasing the throttle from high RPM.
  • Lightweight Flywheels:
  • Add 3 to 6 steps to RPMPOS to catch lightweight rotating assemblies that drop revs too quickly on clutch disengagement.
  • Excessive Hang Warning:
  • Setting RPMPOS too high causes the engine to coast forward with unwanted forward push and diminished engine braking.
