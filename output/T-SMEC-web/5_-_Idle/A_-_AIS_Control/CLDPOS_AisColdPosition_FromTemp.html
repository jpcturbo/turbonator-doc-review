1. Overview & Purpose

CLDPOS is a 2D calibration curve that establishes an additional cold off-idle bypass airflow floor based on engine coolant temperature.

When driving a cold engine, releasing the accelerator pedal can cause engine speed to plummet rapidly due to thick engine oil, high cylinder wall friction, and poor fuel atomization. To prevent cold stalling or violent deceleration bucking, CLDPOS injects an auxiliary step opening floor into the idle air motor whenever the engine is operating below normal operating temperature.

In factory calibrations, CLDPOS commands approximately 134 open steps during sub-zero cold driving, decreasing to 70 steps around freezing, and tapering down to 2 steps once the coolant reaches full operating temperature (190 °F).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Decel Stall Prevention:
  • If the engine dies when releasing the throttle while driving during warm-up, increase CLDPOS steps in the 30 °F to 100 °F range.
  • Eliminating Cold Idle Hang:
  • If the car cruises forward on its own or refuses to drop below 1600 RPM during cold warm-up driving, lower CLDPOS steps across the intermediate temperature points.
  • Hot Cell Zeroing:
  • Keep the highest temperature point near zero steps so that warm idle airflow is completely handed off to normal closed-loop regulation.
