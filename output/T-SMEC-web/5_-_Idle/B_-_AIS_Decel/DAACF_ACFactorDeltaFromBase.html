1. Overview & Purpose

DAACF defines the additional manifold pressure offset added to the deceleration control calculation whenever the air conditioning compressor clutch is engaged.

When the air conditioner is running, the mechanical compressor pump imposes a continuous parasitic torque load on the engine crankshaft. If the driver lifts off the throttle abruptly with the A/C clutch engaged, this additional parasitic drag causes engine speed to fall much more rapidly than normal, increasing the likelihood of an undershoot stall.

DAACF elevates the calculated decel control manifold pressure whenever the A/C clutch is active, which commands extra opening steps from DAISOP during throttle lift-off to cushion the compressor load.

In factory calibrations, DAACF is set to 18 counts (approximately 2.1 psi absolute / 4.2 inHg), providing a dedicated decel dashpot buffer for air conditioning operation.

  • Enclosing Routine: CalculateAisPosition_MM (T-SMEC_v19.asm:1859-1996).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Curing A/C Decel Stalls:
  • Increase DAACF from 18 to 22 to 26 counts if the engine stalls on throttle lift-off only when the air conditioning is engaged.
  • Eliminating A/C Decel Hang:
  • If engine speed refuses to come down cleanly when decelerating with the A/C on, reduce DAACF slightly.
  • Parasitic Drag Compensation:
  • Directly counteracts compressor torque absorption on the crankshaft during closed-throttle overrun.
