1. Overview & Purpose

ACTMP defines the engine coolant temperature switch point separating cold fixed A/C compensation from warm speed-dependent A/C airflow compensation.

When the air conditioning compressor engages on a cold engine, compressor oil is thick and the engine requires a fixed, generous step opening defined by ACPOS. Once the engine warms up, compressor friction stabilizes and airflow requirements vary primarily with vehicle road speed rather than engine temperature.

Above ACTMP, the controller switches from the fixed cold step adder (ACPOS) to the dynamic, speed-dependent lookup table ACCTBL.

In factory calibrations, ACTMP is set to 127 counts, corresponding to a temperature transition threshold of 77 °F.

  • Enclosing Routine: HandleAcCutout (T-SMEC_v19.asm:2170-2468) within CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM.
  • Gating Conditions:

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Extending Cold A/C Protection:
  • Raise ACTMP to 100 °F to 120 °F in cold northern climates to ensure generous cold compressor compensation remains active throughout early warm-up.
  • Preventing Warm Flare:
  • Keep ACTMP below 150 °F to ensure the ECU switches over to dynamic speed-based compensation (ACCTBL) once the engine is hot.
  • Factory Baseline:
  • 127 counts equals 77 °F on the Chrysler coolant temperature scale.
