1. Overview & Purpose

PERKCK defines the scaling percentage multiplier applied to the off-idle air conditioning airflow adder ACCTBL.

Depending on whether the air conditioning system is transitioning between active states or operating in steady-state off-idle cruising, the full airflow step offset looked up from ACCTBL may need to be scaled down. PERKCK acts as a global proportioning scalar applied to the raw speed-dependent steps retrieved from ACCTBL.

In factory calibrations, PERKCK is set to 192 counts, representing an airflow scaling factor of exactly 75 percent (192/256).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Global Scaling of ACCTBL:
  • Adjust PERKCK to scale overall off-idle A/C airflow up or down without reshaping the individual speed breakpoints in ACCTBL.
  • Increasing Dashpot Air:
