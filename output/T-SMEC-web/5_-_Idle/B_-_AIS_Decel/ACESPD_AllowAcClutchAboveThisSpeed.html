1. Overview & Purpose

ACESPD defines the vehicle speed threshold above which part-throttle air conditioning compressor cutout is terminated, allowing the compressor to re-engage.

When accelerating from a standstill with part-throttle cutout active, the vehicle quickly accelerates up to normal traffic speeds. Once road speed exceeds ACESPD, the initial low-speed acceleration phase is complete, and the vehicle has sufficient kinetic momentum that compressor drag is no longer noticeable.

If vehicle speed crosses ACESPD while part-throttle cutout is engaged, the controller clears the cutout timer and re-engages the compressor.

In factory calibrations, ACESPD is set to 1280 counts, corresponding to a vehicle speed threshold of approximately 20 mph.
