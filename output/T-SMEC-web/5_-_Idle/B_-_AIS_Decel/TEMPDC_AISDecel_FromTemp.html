1. Overview & Purpose

TEMPDC is a 2D calibration curve that adds a temperature-dependent offset to the deceleration control manifold pressure calculation.

During closed-throttle deceleration on a cold engine, high viscous oil drag and lower cylinder wall temperatures produce rapid deceleration rates that can easily overwhelm the idle air controller. To counteract this cold friction, TEMPDC injects an additional manifold pressure offset into the decel control MAP calculation as a function of coolant temperature.

A higher decel control MAP commands additional opening steps from the stepper motor via DAISOP during throttle lift-off.

In factory calibrations, TEMPDC adds approximately 68 counts of offset at sub-zero temperatures, decreasing to 36 counts near freezing, 16 counts at mild warm-up, and tapering to zero once the coolant reaches 180 °F.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Decel Stumble Prevention:
  • Elevate intermediate temperature points (50 °F to 120 °F) by 5 to 10 counts if the car stumbles during deceleration while warming up.
  • Warm Engine Hand-Off:
  • Maintain zero offset at full operating temperature (180 °F and above) to avoid hanging idle when hot.
  • Viscous Drag Compensation:
  • Particularly beneficial when using heavier-weight engine oils (e.g., 20W-50) that create significant cold rotational drag.
