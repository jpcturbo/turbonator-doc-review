1. Overview & Purpose

PNDLT defines the engine speed error deadband threshold required before the controller triggers the automatic transmission Park/Neutral to Drive kick opening table.

When the driver shifts an automatic transmission from Park or Neutral into Drive or Reverse, the transmission hydraulic clutches engage and the torque converter impeller suddenly loads the crankshaft. To prevent the resulting mechanical drag from causing a severe engine stumble or stall, the ECU monitors the RPM drop.

If the difference between actual engine speed and target idle speed exceeds PNDLT, the controller advances its memory pointer to index the PNDRK opening step table.

In factory calibrations, PNDLT is configured to 400 counts, representing an RPM delta trigger threshold of approximately 200 RPM.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Earlier In-Gear Catch:
  • Lower PNDLT from 200 RPM down to 100 to 140 RPM to trigger the PNDRK kick sooner if the engine dips heavily when shifted into Drive.
  • High-Stall Torque Converters:
  • High-stall converters generate less initial drag; PNDLT can be elevated to prevent unnecessary step kicks on gear selection.
  • Threshold Minimum:
  • Do not lower PNDLT below 50 RPM to avoid false triggering from natural idle lope.
