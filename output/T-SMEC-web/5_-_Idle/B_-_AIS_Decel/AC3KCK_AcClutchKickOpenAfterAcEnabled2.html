1. Overview & Purpose

AC3KCK defines the secondary stepper motor opening kick offset applied after air conditioning clutch engagement under elevated intake manifold pressure conditions.

Once the compressor clutch has successfully engaged, the controller evaluates intake manifold pressure. If manifold absolute pressure is above DCLMN2, indicating that the engine is already drawing adequate air mass, applying the full standard in-gear A/C step addition would over-compensate, causing engine speed to flare above target.

AC3KCK applies a small signed trim offset to fine-tune the post-engagement motor position under elevated manifold pressure.

In factory calibrations, AC3KCK is set to 254 counts, representing a signed trim of -2 steps.
