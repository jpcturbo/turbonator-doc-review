1. Overview & Purpose

AC4KCK defines the secondary stepper motor opening kick offset applied before air conditioning clutch engagement under elevated intake manifold pressure conditions.

If the engine is operating under higher manifold pressure (above the safety vacuum lockout threshold DCLMN2) when the A/C clutch engagement sequence begins, intake manifold density is already elevated. Under these conditions, applying the standard sea-level kick opening would introduce too much air, causing an unwanted idle surge.

AC4KCK supplies a signed corrective offset that fine-tunes the pre-engagement stepper position for elevated manifold pressure conditions.

In factory calibrations, AC4KCK is set to 250 counts, representing a signed negative trim of -6 steps.
