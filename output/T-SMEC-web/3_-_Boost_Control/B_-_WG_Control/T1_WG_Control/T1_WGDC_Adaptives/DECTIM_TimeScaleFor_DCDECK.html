1. Overview & Purpose

DECTIM sets the timing prescaler bitmask that governs how frequently the knock duty cycle recovery decrement (DCDECK) is executed in T1 control.

The engine controller tests this bitmask against its main program loop counter; only when the masked bits equal zero does the controller subtract DCDECK from the knock reduction penalty. By pacing the execution rate, DECTIM prevents boost from recovering too abruptly after a detonation event, ensuring stable and gradual recovery.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Recovery Interval Prescaler:
  • Bitmask applied against Counter_MainLoop to control how often DCDECK is subtracted.
  • Factory setting is 1 (executes every 2nd loop pass, approximately every 22 milliseconds).
  • Slowing Boost Recovery:

  • Pacing Synchronization:
