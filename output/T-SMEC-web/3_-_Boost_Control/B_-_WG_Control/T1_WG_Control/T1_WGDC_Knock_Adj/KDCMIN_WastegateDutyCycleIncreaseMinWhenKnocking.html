1. Overview & Purpose

KDCMIN specifies the minimum baseline wastegate duty cycle reduction applied immediately upon the initial detection of knock under boost in T1 control.

When high-load detonation begins, reducing duty cycle by only a tiny single step might take several engine revolutions to relieve cylinder pressure. KDCMIN forces an immediate substantial drop in solenoid duty cycle on the very first knock event, guaranteeing an immediate physical reduction in boost pressure before subsequent smaller steps take over.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Immediate Initial Boost Drop:
  • Forces an instant duty cycle reduction on the very first knock event under boost.
  • Factory setting is 16 counts (8.0 percent duty cycle reduction).
  • Combating Sudden Severe Knock:
  • Elevating KDCMIN to 20 or 24 counts (10% to 12% duty cycle) delivers rapid cylinder pressure relief when knocking starts.
  • Smoothing Drivability Surges:
  • If minor sensor noise causes intrusive boost drops, lower KDCMIN to 8 to 10 counts for a gentler initial reaction.
  • Zero Floor Option:
  • Setting to zero allows smooth linear stepping from KDCINC without an initial stepped drop.
