1. Overview & Purpose

DCDECK controls the rate at which wastegate duty cycle is restored after a knock event has subsided in T1 control.

Once detonation ceases, the engine controller does not instantly snap boost back to full target, which could immediately re-trigger knock. Instead, the controller gradually bleeds down the knock reduction penalty by DCDECK counts on periodic timer intervals, steadily ramping solenoid duty cycle and manifold pressure back to normal levels as long as combustion remains knock-free.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Knock Recovery Step Rate:
  • Controls how many duty cycle counts are restored per recovery timer interval once knock ceases.
  • Factory setting is 2 counts (1.0 percent duty cycle recovery per step).
  • Fast Recovery vs. Knock Cycling:
  • Raising DCDECK to 4 or 6 counts restores boost quickly on race fuel or drag strip passes.
  • Lowering DCDECK to 1 count prevents re-triggering detonation if fuel octane or intake temperature is marginal.
  • Underflow Clamping:
  • Subtracts from WastegateDutyCycleIncreaseFromKnock; clamps at zero when fully recovered.
