1. Overview & Purpose

T2KDCMIN specifies the minimum baseline duty cycle step applied immediately upon the initial detection of detonation under boost in T2 control.

When knocking starts under high boost, waiting for small incremental steps to accumulate can allow several damaging engine cycles to occur. T2KDCMIN forces an immediate substantial jump in the knock reduction penalty on the very first event, delivering prompt physical relief to knocking cylinders.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Immediate Initial Knock Response:
  • Forces an immediate minimum duty cycle addition on the very first knock event under boost in T2 mode.
  • Factory setting is 16 counts in template (8.0% duty cycle), or 12 counts in ASM (6.0% duty cycle).
  • Aggressive Protection:
  • Raising T2KDCMIN to 20 counts (10.0% duty cycle) provides rapid protection on pump fuel.
  • Transient Smoothing:
  • Lowering T2KDCMIN to 8 counts softens initial power reduction during momentary tip-in knock.
