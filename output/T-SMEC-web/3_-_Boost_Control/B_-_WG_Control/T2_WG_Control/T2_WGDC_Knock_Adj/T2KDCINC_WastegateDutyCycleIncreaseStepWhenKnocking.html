1. Overview & Purpose

T2KDCINC sets the incremental duty cycle step added to the knock reduction penalty on each confirmed detonation event under boost in T2 control.

In T2 pressure-source wastegate plumbing, adding duty cycle increases solenoid flow to vent actuator pressure or route control signals according to T2 hardware topology. When knock is flagged above 5 psi of boost, T2KDCINC counts are added to the knock reduction penalty to adjust wastegate positioning and pull manifold boost down to safe levels.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • T2 Knock Duty Cycle Step:
  • Sets incremental duty cycle addition per confirmed knock event under boost.
  • Factory setting is 8 counts in template (4.0% duty cycle), or 4 counts in ASM (2.0% duty cycle).
  • Fast Knock Suppression:
  • Increasing T2KDCINC to 8 to 12 counts drops boost more rapidly when severe knock occurs.
  • T2 Additive Output Polarity:
  • In T2 control, knock duty cycle is added to final WGDC at line 10032 to open the gate under pressure-source plumbing.
