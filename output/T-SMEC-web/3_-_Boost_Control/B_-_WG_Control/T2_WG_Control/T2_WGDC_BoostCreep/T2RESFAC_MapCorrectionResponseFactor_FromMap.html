1. Overview & Purpose

T2RESFAC supplies a manifold-pressure-dependent response factor gain table used to dynamically scale the rate-of-boost correction in T2 control.

The engine controller multiplies the excess rate of boost change by the response factor looked up from T2RESFAC to calculate a dynamic target correction factor (MPCORR). This gain scaling allows the controller to react with different sensitivity at low boost levels compared to high boost levels, ensuring precise damping against boost creep across the operating map.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Dynamic Response Gain Table:
  • Maps response factor gain vs. manifold pressure to scale rate-of-rise damping.
  • Factory curve sets high gain at low boost (0.7 psi/count) tapering to 0 gain at high boost (approx 12 psi).
  • Damping Spool Spikes:
  • Increasing values in T2RESFAC dampens aggressive boost overshoot on fast-spooling turbochargers.
  • Eliminating Spool Hesitation:
  • If boost hesitates or lags on throttle tip-in, lower T2RESFAC to reduce dynamic damping.
  • Product Clamping:
  • Resulting correction product is clamped by UPRLIM (positive) and LWRLIM (negative).
