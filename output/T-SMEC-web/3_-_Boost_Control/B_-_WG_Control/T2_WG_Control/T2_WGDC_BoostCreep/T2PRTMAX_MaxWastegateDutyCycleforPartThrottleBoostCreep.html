1. Overview & Purpose

T2PRTMAX defines the emergency wastegate duty cycle value forced directly onto the solenoid output whenever the rate of boost rise exceeds the rate limit threshold (T2RATLIM) in T2 control.

In T2 pressure-source plumbing, commanding maximum duty cycle opens the wastegate fully to dump exhaust gas around the turbine wheel and immediately arrest runaway boost creep before dangerous overboost thresholds can be reached.
