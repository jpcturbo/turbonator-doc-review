1. Overview & Purpose

T2DCWADJ supplies an engine-speed-dependent signed duty cycle adjustment to the base wastegate duty cycle in T2 control.

As engine speed climbs, changing exhaust manifold pressure and turbine flow characteristics alter the force acting against the internal wastegate flapper valve. T2DCWADJ allows the calibrator to tailor solenoid duty cycle across the rev range, adding or subtracting duty cycle at specific engine speeds to keep manifold boost flat and stable from initial spool-up through redline.

  • RPM-Dependent Duty Cycle Trim:
  • Signed Type 2 curve mapping engine speed to duty cycle offset in T2 mode (-64.0% to +63.5%).
  • Correcting High-RPM Boost Sag:
  • In T2 pressure-source systems, subtract duty cycle at high RPM to vent actuator pressure and hold the gate closed.
  • Arresting High-RPM Boost Creep:
  • Add duty cycle at high RPM to force actuator open if boost rises above target near redline.
  • Wide-Open Throttle Gate:
  • Bypassed at partial throttle openings (b4f_FullThrottle clear).
