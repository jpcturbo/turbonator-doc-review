1. Overview & Purpose

WSGCHG regulates maximum allowable turbocharger boost pressure according to intake manifold charge air temperature.

Highly compressed intake air heats up significantly, and if intercooler heat soak or elevated ambient summer temperatures drive charge temperatures excessively high, cylinder knock and pre-ignition risks multiply dramatically. The engine controller continuously samples the charge temperature sensor and uses this table to scale back the boost target if intake air temperatures exceed safe thermal thresholds, preventing knock before ignition retard must intervene.
