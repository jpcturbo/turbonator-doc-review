1. Overview & Purpose

SPDLML defines the lower vehicle road speed recovery threshold that restores fuel injection after a top-speed cutout event.

When the vehicle reaches the maximum speed limit set by SPDLMH, fuel injection is interrupted. Without hysteresis, fuel would switch on and off at a single speed point, producing violent vehicle jerking and drivetrain stress. SPDLML establishes the lower speed setpoint (110 counts, or 110 mph in factory calibrations) where fuel injection resumes. This 8 mph deadband between shutoff (118 mph) and recovery (110 mph) ensures stable, predictable vehicle deceleration during speed governor events.

  • Gating Conditions:

  • Comparison & Restoration:
  • Full-range vehicle speed Temp1 is compared against SPDLML (cmpa SPDLML_SpeedLimitFuelShutoffRestore253mph).
  • If Temp1 <= SPDLML (bls Cutout_TurnFuelBackOn), execution branches to restore fuel injection.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Paired Adjustment with SPDLMH:
  • Always adjust SPDLML in tandem with SPDLMH, maintaining a 5 to 10 mph buffer below SPDLMH.
  • Disabling Limiter:
  • Set both SPDLMH and SPDLML to 255 counts to eliminate vehicle speed governing entirely.
  • Hysteresis Integrity:
  • Setting SPDLML equal to or greater than SPDLMH causes erratic fuel pulsing or perpetual fuel lockout once the limit is reached.
