1. Overview & Purpose

SPDLMH establishes the maximum permissible vehicle road speed fuel shutoff threshold.

Automobile manufacturers incorporate vehicle speed limiters to comply with factory tire speed ratings, drivetrain rotational limits, and regional safety mandates. In the factory 2.5L calibration, SPDLMH is set to 118 counts, which corresponds to 118 mph on the 0 to 255 mph vehicle speed scale. Provided engine speed is above the overspeed arming threshold (SPDRPM), when vehicle road speed reaches or exceeds SPDLMH, the controller immediately cuts fuel injection to govern vehicle velocity. Fuel remains deactivated until road speed decelerates below the companion recovery setpoint (SPDLML).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Track & Competition Removal:
  • Set SPDLMH to 255 counts (255 mph) to eliminate the top-speed fuel shutoff completely on race vehicles.
  • Stage IV Benchmark:

  • Fleet & Safety Governing:
  • Lower SPDLMH to 75 to 85 mph for restricted drivers, ensuring SPDLML is lowered proportionally to provide clean hysteresis.
