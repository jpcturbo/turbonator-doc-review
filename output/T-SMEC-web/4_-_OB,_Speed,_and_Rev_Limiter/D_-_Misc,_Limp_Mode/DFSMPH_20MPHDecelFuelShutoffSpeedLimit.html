1. Overview & Purpose

DFSMPH defines the minimum vehicle speed required to enable the secondary deceleration fuel shutoff routine.

To prevent vehicle jerking, driveline clunking, or engine stalling when slowing down in stop-and-go city traffic, the controller verifies that the vehicle is traveling at an adequate road speed before cutting fuel on trailing throttle. In the factory 2.5L automatic calibration, DFSMPH is set to 254 counts, corresponding to approximately 127 mph on the low-range vehicle speed scale. This maximum setting disables the secondary deceleration cut routine for ordinary driving, ensuring that fuel is never abruptly cut at neighborhood speeds through this specific branch.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Enabling Road-Speed Fuel Cut:
  • Set DFSMPH to 40 counts (20.0 mph) if enabling the secondary deceleration cut branch on manual transmission builds.
  • Disabling for Smooth Drivability:
  • Keeping DFSMPH at 254 counts ensures fuel is not abruptly cut during gear shifts or low-speed trailing throttle.
  • Low-Speed Bucking Avoidance:
  • Avoid setting DFSMPH below 15 mph to prevent jerky driveline lash during low-speed crawling.
