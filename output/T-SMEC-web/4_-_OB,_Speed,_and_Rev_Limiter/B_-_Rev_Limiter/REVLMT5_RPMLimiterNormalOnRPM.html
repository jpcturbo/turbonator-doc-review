1. Overview & Purpose

REVLMT5 defines the primary engine speed recovery threshold where fuel injection is restored after reaching the factory engine redline rev limiter under normal, fault-free operating conditions.

To protect engine reciprocating components and valvetrain from valve float and mechanical over-rev damage, Chrysler engine controllers shut off fuel injection when engine speed reaches REVLMT6 (typically 6200 RPM in stock automatic calibrations). Once fuel is cut, engine speed decelerates rapidly. REVLMT5 establishes the lower hysteresis boundary (184 counts, or approximately 5900 RPM in stock automatic calibrations) where fuel injection resumes. This intentional 300 RPM deadband between cutout and restoration ensures smooth engine speed governing without abusive high-frequency chattering.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Synchronized Adjustment with REVLMT6:
  • Always raise REVLMT5 when raising REVLMT6. Maintain a 200 to 300 RPM delta to ensure clean rev limiter cycling.
  • Hysteresis Bandwidth:
  • Narrowing the gap between REVLMT5 and REVLMT6 to 100 RPM produces a faster, tighter rev limiter bounce.
  • Widening the gap to 400+ RPM softens the rev limiter action, useful for endurance or wet weather racing.
  • Inversion Hazard:
  • Setting REVLMT5 >= REVLMT6 causes the engine to shut off fuel indefinitely once redline is touched.
