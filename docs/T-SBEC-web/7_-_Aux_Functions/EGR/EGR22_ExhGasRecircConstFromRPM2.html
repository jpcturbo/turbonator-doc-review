1. Overview & Purpose

EGR22: EGR22 (ExhGasRecircConstFromRPM2) defines calibration boundaries, vacuum switch points, or duty cycle limits for the Exhaust Gas Recirculation (EGR) subsystem. The engine controller modulates the EGR vacuum solenoid to meter inert exhaust gases into the intake manifold during part-throttle cruising, lowering peak combustion flame temperatures to suppress oxides of nitrogen (NOx) emissions.
