1. Overview & Purpose

SCRMP1 (SecondaryRampSubtractor1) establishes the closed-loop oxygen sensor toggle duration modifier used specifically while Phase 2 of the Exhaust Gas Recirculation diagnostic verification routine is active. In normal closed-loop operation, the engine controller looks up secondary ramp timing from a 2D table to pace how rapidly the oxygen sensor feedback counter toggles rich and lean. However, during Phase 2 EGR flow verification, the controller needs heightened sensitivity to detect the rapid exhaust gas composition change when the EGR valve shuts off. At line 3201 in the secondary ramp handler, if Phase 2 is active, the controller subtracts SCRMP1 from the toggle timer instead of the standard table lookup value. The factory setting of 2 counts accelerates the oxygen sensor switching rate, ensuring rapid feedback response during the diagnostic test.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Raising SCRMP1: Accelerates the closed-loop oxygen sensor toggle rate during Phase 2 testing, improving detection speed on older or slower-responding narrowband sensors.
  • Lowering SCRMP1: Slows down the closed-loop integrator adjustment rate during Phase 2. Useful on modified engines with large fuel injectors to avoid overshooting stoichiometric fueling during the test.
  • EGR Diagnostic Cross-Coupling: Directly linked to EGRVar2 bit 7; has no effect during normal driving outside of active Phase 2 flow verification.
  • Factory Calibration: 2 counts provides an optimal balance between fast transient detection and closed-loop fuel stability.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during closed-loop operation, establishing the secondary O2 ramp rate under low-speed conditions.
