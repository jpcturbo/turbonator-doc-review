1. Overview & Purpose

EGR5 (ExhGasRecircConst) establishes the per-trip diagnostic retry limit before the engine controller inhibits further Exhaust Gas Recirculation flow verification attempts for the remainder of the current driving cycle. Following a Phase 2 timeout, the controller increments a retry counter stored in the low nibble of EGRVar2. At line 5532, this counter is compared against EGR5. If the count reaches or exceeds EGR5, the controller sets bit 6 of EGRVar2, which actively latches a test inhibit flag. Once bit 6 is set, the routine bypasses the diagnostic state machine on all subsequent execution loops until the ignition switch is cycled. The factory calibration of 0 counts causes the inhibit flag to latch after the initial sequence, preventing continuous diagnostic cycles during a single trip.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Raising EGR5: Increases the number of diagnostic retry attempts permitted per ignition cycle. Helpful in urban driving where traffic interruptions frequently abort Phase 2 before completion.
  • Factory Setting (0 counts): Sets bit 6 on the first diagnostic cycle, locking out further testing until the next ignition key cycle to maintain smooth cruising.
  • Low-Nibble Counter: Evaluated against the lower 4 bits of EGRVar2, limiting practical calibration values to between 0 and 15.
  • Trip Inhibit Interlock: Governs bit 6 of EGRVar2, which is checked at line 5409 upon every pass of ExhGasRecirc_MM.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during warm, part-throttle cruising, modulating the exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) solenoid duty cycle to control combustion temperatures.
