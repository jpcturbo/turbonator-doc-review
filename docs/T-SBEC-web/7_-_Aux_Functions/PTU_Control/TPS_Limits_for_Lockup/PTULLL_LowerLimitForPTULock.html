1. Overview & Purpose

PTULLL defines the lower throttle position sensor delta threshold curve as a function of vehicle speed when the torque converter is currently in the locked state. Paired with PTULUL, PTULLL establishes the lower boundary of the locked-state throttle deadband. When the converter is locked and throttle position delta remains between PTULUL and PTULLL, the controller decrements the debounce counter Counter_PartThrottleUnlock. If throttle position drops below PTULLL during coastdown or deceleration, the controller maintains lockup for engine braking. In factory calibrations, PTULLL is configured as a single-point baseline table.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Locked Deadband Boundary: * Defines the lower throttle boundary when the torque converter is currently in the locked state.
  • Deceleration Coasting Lock: * Keeps the converter clutch locked during closed-throttle deceleration to support transmission engine braking.
  • Separation Margin: * Maintain adequate margin below PTULUL to avoid rapid toggling of the lockup solenoid.
