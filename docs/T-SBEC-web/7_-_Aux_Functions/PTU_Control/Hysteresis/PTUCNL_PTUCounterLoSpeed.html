1. Overview & Purpose

PTUCNL defines the low-speed debounce counter reload value for Part Throttle Unlock state transitions. When the vehicle is operating below PTUMSP (city and suburban driving), stop-and-go conditions create frequent throttle modulations. When Counter_PartThrottleUnlock expires at low speed, the controller reloads the counter from PTUCNL. In factory calibrations, PTUCNL is configured to 93 counts. This sets the required dwell time before the torque converter clutch can toggle between locked and unlocked states in lower gears.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stop-and-Go Smoothing: * Raise PTUCNL to 120 to 140 counts to reduce annoying converter cycling in heavy urban traffic.
  • Low-Speed Tip-In Response: * Lower PTUCNL to 50 to 60 counts for faster torque multiplication when pulling away from low speeds.
  • Driveline Lash Prevention: * Avoid setting PTUCNL below 30 counts to prevent converter clutch shudder.
