1. Overview & Purpose

CRMSK6 defines the positive clamp ceiling for the adaptive integrator trim. In closed-loop cruise control, integral windup occurs if the controller continues incrementing its learned trim indefinitely when the vehicle cannot maintain speed (such as on an extremely steep mountain pass). Without an upper ceiling, the integrator would build up massive throttle authority that would keep the throttle held wide open long after reaching flat ground. CRMSK6 caps learned positive integrator authority at +30 counts, preventing integrator windup and ensuring safe throttle boundaries.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Integrator Windup Prevention: * Caps positive learned trim BitFlags_27 at +30 counts. * Prevents the controller from holding excessive throttle open after climbing steep mountains.
  • Mountain & Towing Tuning: * Increasing to 40 or 45 counts provides additional steady-state climbing authority for heavy vehicles.
  • Symmetrical Clamping: * Forms the positive boundary paired with negative boundary CRMSK5 (-30 counts).
