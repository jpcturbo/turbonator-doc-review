1. Overview & Purpose

CLOTHR defines the closed-throttle angle threshold below which the cruise vent solenoid is completely inhibited from pulsing. When vehicle speed climbs above the setpoint going down a hill, the cruise controller commands the vent solenoid to bleed vacuum and close the throttle. Once the mechanical throttle lever has returned fully to its resting idle stop, further venting cannot slow the vehicle down any more because the throttle is already fully closed. Pulsing the vent solenoid against a closed throttle causes unnecessary solenoid wear and annoying clicking noises. CLOTHR gates the vent circuit, turning off the solenoid whenever throttle angle above idle is below this limit.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Solenoid Chatter Prevention: * Shuts off the vent solenoid once the throttle returns to idle stop position, eliminating rapid clicking noises.
  • Aftermarket Throttle Bodies & TPS Ground Noise: * If a replacement TPS exhibits electrical ground offset, increase CLOTHR to 2 or 3 counts to ensure clean shutoff.
  • Symmetrical Throttle Gate: * Serves as the lower throttle boundary, while MAXTHR serves as the upper throttle boundary for vacuum pull.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during cruise control downhill coasting, inhibiting vent solenoid pulses once the mechanical throttle linkage is fully closed against its idle stop.
