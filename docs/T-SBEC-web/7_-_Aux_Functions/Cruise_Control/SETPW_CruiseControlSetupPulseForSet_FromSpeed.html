1. Overview & Purpose

SETPW defines the initial vacuum setup pulse duration applied to the cruise servo the instant the driver presses the set switch. Prior to engagement, the cruise servo diaphragm is fully vented to atmospheric pressure, and throttle position is maintained exclusively by the driver foot on the accelerator pedal. When set is pressed, the engine controller must pre-charge the servo bellows with engine vacuum to take over the mechanical throttle load before the driver lifts off the accelerator. SETPW scales this pre-charge pulse duration across vehicle road speed, ensuring a seamless, imperceptible handoff without a sudden deceleration dip or forward jerk.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Set-Speed Sag / Dip: * If releasing the accelerator pedal right after hitting set causes the car to momentarily sag or lose 1 to 2 mph, increase SETPW at that speed. * Provides a longer initial vacuum pulse to build servo holding tension faster.
  • Set-Speed Surge / Pedal Kick: * If the car surges forward with unexpected acceleration when tapping set, reduce SETPW. * Prevents over-charging the vacuum servo diaphragm before driver pedal lift-off.
  • Speed-Scaled Progression: * Factory values scale from 0.49 seconds at 35 mph up to 0.94 seconds at 85 mph, compensating for higher aerodynamic load at highway velocities.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect the instant the driver presses the SET button, delivering an initial vacuum setup pulse to take the slack out of the servo bellows.
