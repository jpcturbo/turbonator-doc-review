1. Overview & Purpose

CRMSK4 defines the step decrement subtracted from the adaptive integrator trim when accumulated servo error is below CRMSK3. When cresting a highway hill, the extra throttle opening learned during the climb is no longer needed. To prevent downhill runaway, the controller sheds learned trim by subtracting CRMSK4 on each evaluation pass. In factory calibrations, CRMSK4 is configured to 2 counts, establishing an asymmetrical strategy where learned throttle trim is shed twice as fast as it is accumulated.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Asymmetrical Recovery Strategy: * Calibrated to 2 counts, shedding trim twice as fast as CRMSK7 adds it (2 counts down vs 1 count up). * Prevents downhill speed creep after cresting highway summits.
  • Downhill Responsiveness: * Increasing CRMSK4 dumps learned throttle trim faster when entering descents.
  • Clamp Boundary: * Bounded at the bottom by negative clamp floor CRMSK5 (-30 counts).

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)
