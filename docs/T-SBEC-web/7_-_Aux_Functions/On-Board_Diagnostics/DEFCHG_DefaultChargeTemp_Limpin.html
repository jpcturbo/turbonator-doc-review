1. Overview & Purpose

DEFCHG specifies the fixed default charge air temperature value substituted into the engine controller's fueling and spark calculations when the charge air temperature sensor is faulted. The charge air temperature sensor provides manifold air temperature readings used by the engine controller to apply air density fuel corrections and charge-temperature spark advance modifiers. If the sensor circuit is disconnected or shorted to ground (Fault Code 23), the engine controller enters limp-home mode and replaces the sensor reading with DEFCHG. In factory calibrations, DEFCHG is set to 173 counts, corresponding to approximately 140 deg F. This represents a typical warm turbocharged intake manifold temperature, providing stable drivability and preventing severe lean fueling or aggressive spark advance.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Intercooled vs. Non-Intercooled Setups: * Factory setting of 140 deg F suits intercooled street setups. * Non-intercooled turbo setups can raise DEFCHG to 160 deg F to ensure adequate spark retard safety margin if the sensor fails.
  • Failsafe Fuel Density Tuning: * Lowering DEFCHG enriches the failsafe fuel mixture by calculating higher air density. * Raising DEFCHG leans out the failsafe fuel mixture.
  • Edge Cases: * Setting DEFCHG to 0 deg F commands heavy cold air enrichment and maximum spark advance, increasing detonation risk under boost. * Setting DEFCHG to maximum causes excessive spark retard and reduced power.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect if the sensor circuit is disconnected or shorted to ground (Fault Code 23), the engine controller enters limp-home mode and replaces the sensor reading with DEFCHG.
