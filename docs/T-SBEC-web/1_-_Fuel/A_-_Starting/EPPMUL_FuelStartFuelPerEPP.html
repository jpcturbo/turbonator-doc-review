1. Overview & Purpose

EPPMUL defines a multiplicative fuel decay factor indexed against crankshaft revolutions during engine starting. When the starter motor begins cranking, maximum fuel pulsewidth is required on the initial rotations to wet dry intake runners and establish a combustible vapor cloud. If the engine does not start immediately, continuous delivery of maximum starting fuel would quickly wet-foul spark plugs. EPPMUL scales down the cranking pulsewidth with each engine position pulse, reducing fueling smoothly over extended cranking sessions to preserve battery life and prevent cylinder wash.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Extended Cranking Tuning: * If the engine fires initially but dies during prolonged cranking, raise the higher-revolution points of EPPMUL.
  • Aftermarket Injector Flooding: * High-flow injectors flood cold cylinders quickly; steepen the decay curve so fuel tapers down rapidly after the first 4 to 6 rotations.
  • Cold Weather Reliability: * Increasing values across the middle of the curve maintains sufficient fuel vapor during sluggish starter rotation in winter.
  • E85 Adaptations: * E85 requires a slower decay rate because ethanol requires more revolutions to generate sufficient vapor heat.
