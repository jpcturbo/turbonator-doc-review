1. Overview & Purpose

STRTFL defines the base cranking fuel delivery curve indexed against engine coolant temperature during starter cranking. When starting a cold engine, fuel droplets condense onto cold intake runners, intake valves, and cylinder walls rather than remaining suspended in the intake airflow. To establish an ignitable air-fuel vapor cloud in the combustion chamber, STRTFL provides heavy initial fuel pulsewidths at low coolant temperatures, tapering down smoothly as the engine warms toward operating temperature. The engine controller uses this base table while the starter motor is actively rotating the crankshaft below running speed.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Injector Size Scaling: * When installing larger injectors, scale the entire STRTFL table downward by the flow ratio (stock flow divided by new flow) to avoid flooding.
  • E85 and Cold Weather Tuning: * Increase values by 30 to 45 percent at low temperatures when running E85 or in sub-freezing climates to ensure reliable ignition.
  • Warm Start Behavior: * Lowering values eliminates black exhaust smoke and flooded spark plugs on hot restarts.
  • Starting Symptoms: * If the engine spins without catching on cold mornings, raise the lower temperature points incrementally.
