1. Overview & Purpose

MP5 defines the fifth and highest manifold absolute pressure boundary in the adaptive memory grid, separating Cell 8 and 9 from Cell 10 and 11. Cell 10 and 11 represent near-atmospheric and initial positive pressure operation before closed-loop lockout occurs. In stock calibrations, MP5 is set to 120 counts, which corresponds to approximately 13.8 psia or 1.8 inHg of vacuum, just below standard sea-level barometric pressure. This final boundary isolates near-boost closed-loop fuel adaptation from standard manifold vacuum operation.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Near-Atmospheric Boundary: * Establishes the ceiling for Cell 8/9 and the floor for Cell 10/11, covering heavy engine load prior to boost.
  • 3-Bar MAP Sensor Rescaling: * Rescale downward from 120 counts to approximately 80 counts on a 3-bar sensor.
  • Ascending Order Constraint: * Must be strictly greater than MP4 and strictly lower than MAPLIM (MP4 < MP5 < MAPLIM).
