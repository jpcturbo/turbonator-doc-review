1. Overview & Purpose

DCYTMC defines the decay rate divisor for after-start cold enrichment (Curve A) following a cold start. Once cold hold duration (CLDHLD) expires, DCYTMC controls how quickly after-start fuel enrichment tapers down to zero. The decay rate is inversely proportional to this constant, producing a smooth reduction in fuel enrichment over time. In factory calibrations, DCYTMC is calibrated to 9 counts, yielding a decay window of approximately 10.0 seconds.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Decay Duration Formula: * Decay time in seconds equals approximately 89.76 divided by calibration counts (9 counts = 10.0 seconds decay).
  • Smoothing the Taper: * Lower DCYTMC to 4-6 counts if the engine hesitates or sags as cold hold ends.
  • Accelerating Warm-Up: * Raising DCYTMC drops cold enrichment faster, reducing exhaust soot and fuel consumption if idle stability permits.
  • Companion Calibration: * Works with CLDHLD; CLDHLD sets the dwell, and DCYTMC sets the ramp-down rate.
