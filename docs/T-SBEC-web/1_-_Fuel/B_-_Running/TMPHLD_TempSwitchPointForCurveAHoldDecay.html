1. Overview & Purpose

TMPHLD defines the coolant temperature switch point that divides cold starts from hot restarts for after-start enrichment hold and decay timing. When an engine starts with coolant temperature below TMPHLD, the controller selects cold hold duration (CLDHLD) and cold decay rate (DCYTMC). When starting above TMPHLD, the controller switches to hot hold duration (HOTHLD) and hot decay rate (DCYTMH). In factory calibrations, TMPHLD is set to 177 counts, corresponding to approximately 176 degF, matching OEM 180 degF thermostat operating temperatures.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Thermostat Matching: * Lower TMPHLD to 155-160 degF when running a 160 degF performance thermostat to prevent warm restarts from triggering cold hold logic.
  • Cold vs Hot Hold Selection: * Determines whether CLDHLD or HOTHLD governs after-start enrichment dwell.
  • Indexed Group Anchor: * Functions as the ROM base pointer for indexed lookups of CLDHLD and HOTHLD.
  • Over-Enrichment Prevention: * Proper setting prevents dark exhaust smoke and flooded plugs when restarting a fully warmed engine.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect when an engine starts with coolant temperature below TMPHLD, the controller selects cold hold duration (CLDHLD) and cold decay rate (DCYTMC).
