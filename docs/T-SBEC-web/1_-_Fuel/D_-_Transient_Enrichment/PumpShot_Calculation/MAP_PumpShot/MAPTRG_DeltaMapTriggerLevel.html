1. Overview & Purpose

MAPTRG defines the minimum rate of intake manifold pressure increase required to trigger a MAP-based acceleration enrichment pump shot. As the throttle opens and manifold pressure rises from vacuum toward atmospheric pressure or positive boost, the air density in the intake plenum increases rapidly. If the instantaneous manifold pressure exceeds the rolling average manifold pressure by more than MAPTRG, the engine controller calculates an immediate transient fuel addition. The factory threshold of 13 counts corresponds to approximately 1.5 psi of sudden pressure rise, effectively screening out harmless vacuum ripples caused by camshaft overlap and normal intake valve cycling while responding promptly to real engine load spikes.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Sensitivity Adjustment: Lowering MAPTRG increases sensitivity to manifold pressure changes, triggering transient enrichment on smaller throttle modulations to eliminate light-throttle tip-in hesitation.
  • Camshaft Pulsation Filtering: Raising MAPTRG prevents false triggers on rough-idling engines or aggressive high-overlap camshafts where vacuum fluctuates naturally.
  • 3-Bar MAP Scaling: When converting to a 3-bar MAP sensor, scale down MAPTRG by roughly 33 percent (e.g. from 13 counts down to 8 or 9 counts) to preserve the stock 1.5 psi sensitivity threshold.
  • Coordination with MAPMAX: Ensure MAPTRG remains well below MAPMAX to provide a functional linear calculation window.
