1. Overview & Purpose

PTENUM defines the hysteresis threshold multiplier required to clear the part-throttle enrichment maximum flag and return ignition advance to standard running schedules after transient enrichment has peaked. When part-throttle enrichment reaches the upper ceiling defined by PTELMT and PTEADJ, the engine controller asserts a status flag that substitutes specialized spark advance curves. Once engine load subsides and transient enrichment decays, the multiplier must drop below the ceiling by a specific margin before the controller clears the flag and restores normal ignition timing. PTENUM establishes this margin by multiplying the negated decrement value from PTENRD. The factory setting of 15 counts requires a drop of 15 counts before normal spark advance resumes.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Spark Hysteresis Window: Increasing PTENUM broadens the hysteresis window, keeping modified ignition timing active longer after peak enrichment is reached.
  • Preventing Timing Chattering: Prevents rapid chattering between standard spark advance and enrichment spark advance when modulating the throttle near the ceiling threshold.
  • Restoring Base Timing Sooner: Decreasing PTENUM narrows the hysteresis window, allowing the controller to restore baseline spark advance sooner as soon as the throttle is relaxed.
  • Zero Setting Warning: Avoid setting PTENUM to zero, which can cause erratic spark oscillation near the limit.
