1. Overview & Purpose

WOTBAT (WOTBatteryVoltsTarget) specifies the target battery regulation voltage commanded during Wide Open Throttle (WOT) acceleration. The Chrysler SBEC directly controls alternator charging field duty cycle via an internal pulse-width modulated voltage regulator. An energized alternator charging at 14.5V under heavy electrical load can consume 3 to 5 engine horsepower. Under WOT acceleration, Turbonator code lowers the target charging voltage to WOTBAT (approx 12.5V - 12.8V). This effectively de-energizes the alternator field, shedding parasitic mechanical drag from the crankshaft and transferring every possible horsepower directly to the drive wheels during wide-open drag strip passes or passing maneuvers.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Free Dyno Horsepower: Frees 3-5 HP at the wheels during full-throttle acceleration pulls.
  • Ignition Coil Considerations: Ensure your ignition system and fuel pump maintain sufficient voltage at ~12.5V. If running an exceptionally high-draw fuel pump without a dedicated booster, keep WOTBAT around 13.0V (0xF0) to prevent fuel pressure drop.
