1. Overview & Purpose

STGSPD defines the vehicle speed cutoff threshold above which launch control is completely disengaged. When launching from the starting line, the staging rev limiter must release the instant the car begins rolling forward so the engine can rev freely through first gear. Once the speed sensor registers a vehicle speed equal to or greater than STGSPD, the controller disables staging spark and fuel cuts, returning full engine power to the drive wheels. The factory setting of 10 mph ensures positive launch disengagement without interfering with stationary staging.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Recommended Cutoff Range: Set between 5 mph and 10 mph for drag racing to ensure the staging limiter drops out immediately upon vehicle movement.
  • Excessive Cutoff Warning: Setting above 20 mph can cause the staging limiter to remain active during wheelspin out of the hole, cutting engine acceleration in first gear.
  • Disabling Feature: Setting STGSPD to 0 mph prevents launch control from engaging under any circumstance.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)
