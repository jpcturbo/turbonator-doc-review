1. Overview & Purpose

SPKFRC selects the active spark cut fraction from the pre-programmed pattern tables for launch control. Rather than cutting fuel or indiscriminately dropping random cylinders, Turbonator launch control uses mathematically balanced rotating patterns to regulate engine speed and build boost on the starting line. SPKFRC acts as a selector pointer (values 0 through 7) that chooses the ratio of suppressed to delivered ignition sparks. The factory default setting is choice 3 (4/5 cut), which fires one cylinder out of every five events, holding launch RPM steady while delivering ample unburnt energy into the turbine housing.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Selection Guidelines: Choice 0 (1/3 cut) fires 66.7% of cylinders; Choice 2 (3/5 cut) fires 40.0% of cylinders; Choice 3 (4/5 cut, factory) fires 20.0% of cylinders; Choice 7 (6/7 cut) fires 14.3% of cylinders.
  • Large Turbochargers: Use 1/3 or 3/5 cut to deliver maximum thermal expansion into the turbine housing for quick boost response.
  • Small Turbochargers & Traction Limited: Use 4/5 or 5/7 cut to cap flywheel torque and prevent wheelspin when staging.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect when launch control or rev limiting is active, establishing the percentage pattern of ignition sparks cut per engine cycle.
