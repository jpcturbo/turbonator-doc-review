1. Overview & Purpose

THRPOS is a 2D calibration curve that establishes a minimum idle air stepper motor position based on throttle position sensor voltage. As the driver depresses the accelerator pedal and drives off-idle, the engine controller tracks throttle plate angle. THRPOS pre-positions the stepper motor further open as throttle opening increases, ensuring that when the driver suddenly lifts off the throttle, the bypass passage is already open to act as a dashpot cushion. In factory calibrations, THRPOS commands 0 steps at minimum throttle opening (below 0.1 Volts delta above idle stop), increasing linearly to 5 steps at approximately 0.3 Volts delta.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Softening Lift-Off Jerk: * Increase THRPOS steps in the 0.2 to 0.5 Volt range to cushion abrupt throttle lift-off during aggressive part-throttle driving.
  • Enhancing Engine Braking: * Lower THRPOS steps to zero or near-zero to allow the idle valve to close down further during off-idle coasting, increasing engine vacuum braking.
  • Zero-Throttle Clearance: * Ensure the lowest voltage cell is set to zero steps so closed-throttle idle feedback is never obstructed.
