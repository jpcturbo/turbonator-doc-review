1. Overview & Purpose

AISHLM establishes the absolute high limit clamp for the maximum number of open steps that can ever be commanded on the automatic idle speed stepper motor. The Chrysler 4-wire stepper motor actuator physically drives a tapered pintle in and out of an auxiliary throttle bypass passage. If the controller were to continuously increment stepper opening steps without an upper limit, the motor mechanism could unscrew the pintle shaft from its internal worm gear drive or jam the actuator against its mechanical end stops, causing lost steps and permanent positioning calibration errors. Furthermore, beyond approximately 160 steps, the annular bypass opening around the pintle is completely unrestricted, and additional opening steps provide zero incremental airflow. In factory calibrations, AISHLM is configured to 160 counts, locking maximum stepper opening at exactly 160 steps across all idle, startup, decel, and air conditioning routines.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Throttle Body Saturation: * Keep AISHLM at 160 steps on factory throttle bodies; the bypass passage is fully open by 150 steps and additional travel yields zero extra air.
  • Extended-Stroke Aftermarket Housings: * For custom high-flow idle air valve housings designed for extended travel, AISHLM can be safely raised to 180 to 200 steps.
  • Mechanical Disassembly Hazard: * Setting AISHLM above 240 steps can cause the stepper motor screw to unthread or bind against physical stops, causing total idle control failure.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect whenever the idle air motor steps open, enforcing an absolute upper mechanical limit on stepper pintle travel to prevent motor jamming.
