1. Overview & Purpose

AC3KCK defines the secondary stepper motor opening kick offset applied after air conditioning clutch engagement under elevated intake manifold pressure conditions. Once the compressor clutch has successfully engaged, the controller evaluates intake manifold pressure. If manifold absolute pressure is above DCLMN2, indicating that the engine is already drawing adequate air mass, applying the full standard in-gear A/C step addition would over-compensate, causing engine speed to flare above target. AC3KCK applies a small signed trim offset to fine-tune the post-engagement motor position under elevated manifold pressure. In factory calibrations, AC3KCK is set to 254 counts, representing a signed trim of -2 steps.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Post-Engagement Flare Suppression: * Adjust AC3KCK to eliminate post-clutch lockup idle flaring under elevated manifold pressure.
  • Signed Two's Complement: * Stored as a signed negative integer (254 counts = -2 steps).
  • Companion Pair: * Works directly with AC4KCK.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect at closed throttle immediately after the A/C clutch engages under elevated intake manifold pressure (low vacuum), trimming the idle air step adder to prevent an idle surge.
