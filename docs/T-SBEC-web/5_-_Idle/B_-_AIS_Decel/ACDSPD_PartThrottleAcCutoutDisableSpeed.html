1. Overview & Purpose

ACDSPD defines the maximum vehicle speed ceiling below which part-throttle air conditioning compressor cutout is permitted to engage. Part-throttle compressor cutout is designed primarily to improve low-speed urban acceleration from a standstill or low speeds. At higher road speeds, the engine is already producing sufficient power to accelerate the vehicle effortlessly, and cutting the compressor would compromise cabin comfort unnecessarily. ACDSPD establishes the vehicle speed boundary: part-throttle A/C cutout can only engage if vehicle speed is below ACDSPD. In factory calibrations, ACDSPD is set to 0 counts (0 mph), keeping part-throttle cutout disabled stock.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • City Take-Off Boost Ceiling: * Set ACDSPD to 35 to 45 mph when enabling part-throttle cutout to limit compressor disengagement to low-speed city driving.
  • Highway Comfort Retention: * Keeping ACDSPD below 50 mph ensures highway part-throttle cruising maintains steady cabin cooling.
  • 16-Bit Word Resolution: * Type 4 constant evaluated against 16-bit register VehicleSpeed_HB.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during part-throttle acceleration with the A/C on, ensuring the compressor is only cut out to assist vehicle acceleration below this road speed ceiling.
