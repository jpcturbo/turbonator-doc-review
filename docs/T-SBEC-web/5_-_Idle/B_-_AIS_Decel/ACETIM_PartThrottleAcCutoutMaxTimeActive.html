1. Overview & Purpose

ACETIM defines the maximum duration that part-throttle air conditioning compressor cutout is permitted to remain continuously active. When part-throttle A/C cutout engages during acceleration, the compressor clutch disengages to shed mechanical drag. However, if the driver continues to drive at part-throttle for an extended period (such as climbing a long mountain grade), holding the compressor off continuously would cause the cabin evaporator to warm up and blow hot, humid air. ACETIM acts as a maximum timeout limiter: once the cutout timer reaches ACETIM, the controller overrides the cutout and forces the compressor clutch to re-engage. In factory calibrations, ACETIM is set to 8 counts, enforcing a maximum cutout window of approximately 0.5 seconds.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Extending Acceleration Boost Window: * Increase ACETIM to 5.0 to 10.0 seconds to allow the compressor to remain disengaged throughout an entire highway passing maneuver.
  • Cabin Comfort Boundary: * Keep ACETIM below 15 seconds to prevent the cabin evaporator from thawing and blowing humid air.
  • Timeout Reset: * When Timer_AcCutout reaches ACETIM, the timer is zeroed at line 2207, re-engaging the compressor.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during part-throttle A/C cutout, capping the maximum continuous duration the compressor clutch can remain disengaged.
