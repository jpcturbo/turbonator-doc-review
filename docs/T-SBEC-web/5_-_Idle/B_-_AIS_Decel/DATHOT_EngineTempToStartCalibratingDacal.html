1. Overview & Purpose

DATHOT defines the minimum engine coolant temperature threshold required before the controller enables adaptive idle airflow calibration. While an engine is warming up, internal friction changes continuously and auxiliary warm-up airflow tables are actively propping the idle stepper open. Attempting to learn long-term baseline idle airflow characteristics while the engine is cold would severely skew the learned trim once the engine reaches full operating temperature. DATHOT locks out adaptive idle airflow learning until the engine reaches full thermal maturity. In factory calibrations, DATHOT is set to 205 counts, corresponding to a coolant temperature threshold of 175 °F.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Thermostat Rating Alignment: * If running a 160 °F or 170 °F thermostat, lower DATHOT to 155 °F to 160 °F so adaptive idle airflow learning can activate during normal cruising.
  • Preventing Cold Trim Distortion: * Keep DATHOT at or above 150 °F to ensure the engine is fully warmed up before the ECU adapts its base idle air calibration.
  • Stock 180 °F Thermostats: * Retain factory setting of 175 °F (205 counts) when using standard 180 °F or 195 °F thermostats.
