1. Overview & Purpose

MPHREF defines the critical vehicle speed threshold that separates moving or rolling deceleration logic from stationary closed-throttle idle control. When a vehicle is moving down the road at highway or suburban speeds, letting off the accelerator pedal requires vehicle deceleration dashpot airflow to cushion engine braking. Conversely, when the vehicle comes to a complete standstill, the engine controller must transition into true stationary idle speed regulation to maintain target idle RPM without causing vehicle creeping or engine surging. MPHREF establishes this vehicle speed boundary across multiple engine control routines, incorporating approximately 1.5 mph of built-in hysteresis to prevent rapid cycling around the switch point. In factory calibrations, MPHREF is set to 8 counts, corresponding to a vehicle speed threshold of 4.0 mph.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Speed Threshold Limits: * Avoid raising MPHREF above 10.0 mph to prevent unwanted idle hunting while coasting down hills.
