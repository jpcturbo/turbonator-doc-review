1. Overview & Purpose

FNSPLO defines the low vehicle speed transition threshold that switches the cooling fan logic back into low-speed mode when the vehicle decelerates. Speedometer pulse period variable VehicleSpeed_HB is compared against FNSPLO after clearing the high-speed check. In factory calibrations, FNSPLO is set to 9 counts, representing approximately 36 MPH. When vehicle speed drops below 36 MPH (pulse period exceeds 9 counts), the controller clears the high-speed status flag and engages the low-speed temperature setpoints FANTMP3 and FANTMP4.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hysteresis Boundary: * Completes the 8 MPH speed deadband with FNSPHI to eliminate mode hunting during suburban cruising.
  • Tuning Direction: * Lower count values raise the transition speed in miles per hour; higher count values lower it.
  • Spread Retention: * Keep FNSPLO 2 to 3 counts lower than FNSPHI to preserve stable speed hysteresis.
