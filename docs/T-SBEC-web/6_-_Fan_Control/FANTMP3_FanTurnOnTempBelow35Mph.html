1. Overview & Purpose

FANTMP3 defines the coolant temperature threshold that energizes the radiator cooling fan during low-speed driving or idle conditions below 35 MPH. When stopped in traffic or driving at low vehicle speeds, no ram airflow enters the front grille to cool the radiator and intercooler cores. Underhood temperatures quickly rise from the turbocharger turbine housing and exhaust manifold. In factory calibrations, FANTMP3 is set to 227 counts, corresponding to approximately 199 °F. When coolant temperature exceeds this threshold, the controller turns on the electric cooling fan to draw airflow through the cooling pack, preventing engine overheating during city operation.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Primary Fan Tuning Target: * Most frequently adjusted cooling parameter; controls stationary idle and slow-traffic engine temperature.
  • Thermostat Calibration: * 180 °F thermostat: calibrate FANTMP3 between 188 °F and 192 °F. * 160 °F thermostat: calibrate FANTMP3 between 172 °F and 176 °F.
  • Detonation Resistance: * Keeping city idle coolant temperatures below 195 °F sharply reduces manifold heat soak and launch knock.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect when driving in slow city traffic or idling below 35 MPH, commanding the radiator cooling fan ON once coolant temperature reaches this threshold.
