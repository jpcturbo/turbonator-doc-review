1. Overview & Purpose

WGDCMN: WGDCMN sets the minimum or maximum allowable wastegate duty cycle clamp limits. Enforcing hard minimum and maximum PWM bounds prevents solenoid coil overheating and ensures the pneumatic actuator flapper valve remains within its controllable modulation window.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Calibration Strategy: Raising the maximum clamp allows higher wastegate duty cycle for stiff external wastegate springs. Raising the minimum clamp prevents wastegate flutter at high exhaust flow.
  • Raising the Value: Increases commanded threshold or extends gating limits in the controller.
  • Lowering the Value: Decreases commanded threshold or restricts operational bounds.
