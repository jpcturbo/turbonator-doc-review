1. Overview & Purpose

GOVNER defines the primary engine speed (RPM) ignition advance curve in the Chrysler engine controller, functioning as the electronic equivalent of centrifugal mechanical advance weights in a conventional distributor. As engine speed increases, each combustion stroke occurs in fewer milliseconds, requiring the spark plug to fire earlier before top dead center so that peak cylinder pressure aligns with optimal mechanical leverage on the connecting rod (typically 14 to 17 degrees after top dead center). GOVNER forms the foundational base advance curve across the entire operating spectrum, scaling from 9 degrees BTDC at 600 RPM up to 30 degrees BTDC at 5000+ RPM. The manifold pressure advance (HOTMAP or WOTMAP) is mathematically added to GOVNER to calculate total timing.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Global Baseline Effect: * Raising GOVNER shifts the entire ignition timing map upward across both cruising and full-throttle boost. * If adding timing to GOVNER for off-boost pep, compensate by pulling equivalent timing from WOTMAP to protect boost regions.
  • High-RPM Torque Tuning: * Optimizing GOVNER between 4000 and 6000 RPM unlocks significant horsepower on modified cylinder heads and camshafts.
  • 3D Surface Synthesis Axis: * In MPTune, GOVNER provides the RPM dimension combined with HOTMAP (to create PTTIME) and WOTMAP (to create FTTIME).
  • Idle Timing Baseline: * At idle speeds (800 to 1000 RPM), GOVNER sets the base mechanical spark advance, which is then fine-tuned by spark scatter.
