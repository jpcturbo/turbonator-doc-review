1. Overview & Purpose

ADVCLS defines an additive spark advance correction table indexed by the open-loop timer fraction during warm part-throttle operation. When a warm engine transitions from starting into closed-loop operation, combustion conditions stabilize as the oxygen sensor begins closed-loop fuel control. ADVCLS provides a progressive spark advance ramp (up to 1.5 degrees BTDC in stock calibrations) as the open-loop fraction timer increments, helping to smoothly integrate part-throttle torque delivery as the controller enters stable closed-loop running.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Transition Smoothing: * ADVCLS adds small timing increments (up to 1.5 degrees) as the open loop timer increments, smoothing the transition into closed loop.
  • Simplifying Timing Surfaces: * Zeroing out ADVCLS eliminates time-based spark shifting, making part-throttle timing strictly dependent on manifold pressure and RPM.
  • Raising for Tip-In Torque: * Adding minor advance in ADVCLS can improve light throttle response during the immediate post-crank period.
