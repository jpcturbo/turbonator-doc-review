1. Overview & Purpose

WOTMAP defines the full-throttle manifold pressure spark advance curve used whenever the engine is operated at wide-open throttle under warm conditions. At wide-open throttle, airflow restriction is minimal, volumetric efficiency reaches its peak, and turbocharger boost pressure forces dense air-fuel mixtures into the cylinders. Because high cylinder pressures and elevated combustion temperatures drastically accelerate flame front speeds, ignition timing must be retarded under boost to keep peak cylinder pressure safely positioned after top dead center and protect the engine from detonation. WOTMAP commands positive advance under high vacuum (up to 15 degrees BTDC) and transitions into substantial retard (-6.5 degrees BTDC at 14.7 psi boost) under boost. It also serves as the absolute minimum timing floor for the warm part-throttle curve (HOTMAP).

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • High-Boost Power Tuning: * Advancing WOTMAP increases wide-open throttle torque and top-end power, but must be monitored closely for knock retard activity.
  • Safety Margins for Pump Gasoline: * Retarding WOTMAP under boost (e.g., pulling 1 to 2 degrees per 2 psi of additional boost) prevents detonation on standard pump fuel.
  • 3D Surface Synthesis (FTTIME): * WOTMAP serves as the load axis combined with GOVNER to generate the complete 3D full-throttle spark table (FTTIME) in MPTune.
  • 3-Bar MAP Sensor Rescaling: * Upgrading from a 2-bar to a 3-bar MAP sensor requires recalculating table pressure points to match the expanded 0 to 44.1 psi scale.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Signed Table Format: Type 2 table storing signed 8-bit values where 1 count = 0.5 degrees (e.g., 0x1E = +15.0°, 0xF3 = -6.5°).
  • Negative Advance Multiplier: When WOTMAP output is negative (retard under boost), MAPMUL multiplies the retard based on RPM.
