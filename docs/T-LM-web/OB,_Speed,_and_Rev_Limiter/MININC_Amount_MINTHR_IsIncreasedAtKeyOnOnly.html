1. Overview & Purpose

MININC_Amount_MINTHR_IsIncreasedAtKeyOnOnly specifies an initial positive voltage buffer added to minimum throttle baseline voltage (MINTHR) during key-on reset initialization.

When the ignition key is first turned on, the throttle return spring might not have seated the throttle blade fully against the stop screw, or electrical ground voltages might experience initial power-up bounce. MININC temporarily pads the initial minimum throttle voltage estimate, ensuring the ECU does not mislearn a false closed-throttle zero position during startup.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Key-On Initialization: Executed during cold startup in ResetStartup.
