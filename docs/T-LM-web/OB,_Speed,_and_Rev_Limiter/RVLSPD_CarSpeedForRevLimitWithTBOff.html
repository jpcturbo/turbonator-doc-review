1. Overview & Purpose

RVLSPD_CarSpeedForRevLimitWithTBOff defines the vehicle speed threshold (20 mph) that transitions between stationary/neutral rev limiters and moving in-gear rev limiters.

Below 20 mph, the ECU evaluates low-speed rev limits; above 20 mph, the vehicle is confirmed driving in motion, and the primary high-speed rev limiter engages.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Vehicle Speed Check: Evaluated in ControlEngineCutout_MM vs VehicleSpeed_HB.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Setting: 0x14 (20 mph).
  • Launch Control Interlock: Used by staging rev limiter routines to determine when the car has launched off the line.
