1. Overview & Purpose

WOTBAT (WOTBatteryVoltsTarget) specifies the target battery regulation voltage commanded during Wide Open Throttle (WOT) acceleration.

The Chrysler logic module directly controls alternator charging field duty cycle via an internal pulse-width modulated voltage regulator. An energized alternator charging at 14.5V under heavy electrical load can consume 3 to 5 engine horsepower. Under WOT acceleration, Turbonator code lowers the target charging voltage to WOTBAT (approx 12.5V - 12.8V). This effectively de-energizes the alternator field, shedding parasitic mechanical drag from the crankshaft and transferring every possible horsepower directly to the drive wheels during wide-open drag strip passes or passing maneuvers.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine DetermineTargetBatteryVoltage_MM at line 1308.
  • Active during Wide Open Throttle (b81_FullThrottle set).
  • Clamps battery voltage target to WOTBAT until throttle is released.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Free Dyno Horsepower: Frees 3-5 HP at the wheels during full-throttle acceleration pulls.
  • Ignition Coil Considerations: Ensure your ignition system and fuel pump maintain sufficient voltage at ~12.5V. If running an exceptionally high-draw fuel pump without a dedicated booster, keep WOTBAT around 13.0V (0xF0) to prevent fuel pressure drop.
