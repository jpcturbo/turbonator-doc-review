1. Overview & Purpose

CLOTHR (CruiseControlDontVentIfThrottleBelowThis) specifies the minimum throttle angle floor below which the cruise vent solenoid is completely inhibited from firing.

When vehicle speed exceeds set speed going down a steep hill, the vent solenoid bleeds vacuum to close the throttle. Once the throttle butterfly has returned completely to its closed idle stop position, further venting cannot slow the car down any more (the mechanical throttle is already at 0%). Pulsing the vent solenoid when the throttle is already fully closed accomplishes nothing except causing rapid wear on the solenoid valve and producing annoying clicking sounds under the hood. CLOTHR gates the vent circuit, shutting off the vent solenoid whenever the throttle is closed below this floor.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine CruiseControlSet at line 2363.
  • Checked during active cruise overspeed regulation.
  • If Throttle_Position < CLOTHR, vent pulses are suppressed.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • TPS Zero Calibration: If you install an aftermarket throttle body and TPS sensor where idle voltage sits slightly higher than stock (e.g. 0.95V vs stock 0.85V), increase CLOTHR slightly to ensure the ECU knows when the throttle has reached its physical mechanical stop.
