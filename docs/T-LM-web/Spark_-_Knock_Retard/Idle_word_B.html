1. Overview & Purpose

Idle_word_B (IDLWB) is the secondary 2-byte parameter structure in the closed-throttle idle spark scatter / stabilization block, used when engine speed drops below target idle (EngineRpm_HB < TargetIdleSpeed_HB).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Active State: Engine at idle speed with throttle closed and EngineRpm_HB < TargetIdleSpeed_HB.
  • Firmware Access: Loaded via ldx #Idle_word_B at line 4828.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Tuning Recommendation: DO NOT MODIFY.
    The high byte (0x0B = 11 counts = 5.5° max advance) and low byte (0x5E = 94/256 slope) govern the proportional under-speed spark advance recovery at idle.
