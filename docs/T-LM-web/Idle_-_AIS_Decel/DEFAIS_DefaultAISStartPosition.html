1. Overview & Purpose

DEFAIS (DefaultAISStartPosition) establishes the default fallback step position for the idle stepper motor when startup conditions or initialization flags dictate a safe baseline bypass opening.

During early power-up before coolant temperature ADC conversion is validated, or if startup flags in BitFlags_80 signal a reset/reinitialization state, the ECU loads DEFAIS (stock calibration = 12 steps / 0x0C) into DesiredNewAisPosition to guarantee adequate bypass air without causing an uncontrollable engine runaway.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at line 5031.
  • Active when bit 7 of BitFlags_80 is set during startup initialization.
  • Stored directly to DesiredNewAisPosition.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x0C (12 steps).
  • Modified Engines: If an engine with a large camshaft or tight converter struggles to catch during early key-on initialization before the temperature table takes over, DEFAIS can be bumped to 18-24 steps.
