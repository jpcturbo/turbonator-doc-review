1. Overview & Purpose

PNDLT (PNDLTN_DeltaRpmChangeToUse_DRPNK_or_PNDRK) is a 16-bit threshold defining the minimum RPM deviation that must occur during a transmission gear selector shift before the ECU will execute the DRPNK or PNDRK step kick tables.

During minor gear lever vibrations or noisy switch contacts, the ECU prevents spurious AIS kicks by verifying that a significant physical RPM perturbation (|Target - Actual| ≥ PNDLT) has occurred as a result of torque converter coupling. In stock calibrations, PNDLT is set to 0x00C8 (200 RPM).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated in CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at line 5156.
  • Subtracts PNDLT from |TargetIdleSpeed - EngineRpm|.
  • If the result causes a borrow/carry (bcs LE565), the shift kick is aborted.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x00C8 (200 RPM).
  • Tight Torque Converters: If an aftermarket torque converter has high stall or very tight lockup that creates a sudden 150 RPM dip that fails to trigger PNDRK, lower PNDLT to 0x0064 (100 RPM) so the kick triggers reliably.
