1. Overview & Purpose

ACTIME (TimeBeforeAcEnabledAndFullDeltaUsed) sets the settling countdown duration (stock calibration = 22 ticks / ~1.5 seconds) loaded into Timer_AisOrO2Sensor whenever the A/C clutch engages.

During this transition window, the pre-emptive kick AC2KCK is maintained, and closed-loop idle trims are held in a transitional state to give the automotive A/C refrigeration circuit time to equalize head pressures before the ECU resumes standard steady-state idle regulation.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at line 5078.
  • Loaded into Timer_AisOrO2Sensor on A/C state change.
  • Decremented on successive program loops until reaching zero.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Setting: 0x16 (~1.5 seconds).
  • Heavy-Duty Compressors: If running an upgraded high-displacement compressor (or high ambient desert temperatures where head pressures take longer to stabilize), increasing ACTIME gives the engine extra cushioned time to absorb the compressor load.
