1. Overview & Purpose

TEMPDC (AISDecel_FromTemp) defines an additive deceleration bypass airflow correction indexed by engine coolant temperature (CoolantTemp).

Cold engines suffer from sluggish fuel vaporization and heightened rotating friction. During closed-throttle deceleration on a cold engine, lifting off the accelerator is far more likely to cause an undershoot stall than on a fully warmed engine. TEMPDC supplements the timed decel curve (TIMEDC) with additional open steps when cold, tapering down to a minimum level as the engine reaches normal operating temperature.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated in CalculateAisPosition_MM at line 3534.
  • Indexed by CoolantTemp.
  • Added directly to the output of TIMEDC (adda Temp0).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold-Engine Decel Stalling: If the car frequently dies when you push the clutch in while decelerating on cold mornings, increase TEMPDC in the 40°F - 120°F coolant temperature range.
  • Cold Fast-Decel Hang: If the engine RPM hangs up around 2,000 RPM on cold lift-off and takes too long to drop, reduce TEMPDC.
