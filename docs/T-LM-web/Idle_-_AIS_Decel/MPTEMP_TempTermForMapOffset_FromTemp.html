1. Overview & Purpose

MPTEMP (TempTermForMapOffset_FromTemp) is a temperature-dependent scaling multiplier applied to the deceleration MAP floor (MAPFLR).

When the engine block and intake manifold are cold, fuel atomization is severely impaired. Fuel clings to port walls in thick liquid puddles, and high manifold vacuum during decel causes fuel to flash-boil off the walls erratically. MPTEMP elevates the deceleration MAP floor when the engine is cold, forcing the AIS motor to supply more bypass airflow and maintain higher manifold pressure during trailing throttle until the engine warms up.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated in CalculateAisPosition_MM at line 3556.
  • Indexed by CoolantTemp.
  • Multiplied directly by MAPFLR via hardware multiplication.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Decel Stumble / Shudder: If releasing the accelerator while the engine is warming up causes vehicle bucking, shudder, or severe jerking, increase the low-temperature values in MPTEMP.
  • Warm Consistency: Above 160°F, MPTEMP plateaus to a constant nominal multiplier to hand steady-state authority over to MAPFLR.
