1. Overview & Purpose

OFFPER2 (AisInstantlyClosesWhenAcDisengages2) specifies the exact fraction of the active A/C step addition (ACCTBL) that is instantly subtracted from DesiredNewAisPosition the moment the A/C compressor clutch disengages while moving.

When the A/C clutch disengages, several horsepower of parasitic load are instantaneously removed from the engine crankshaft. If the AIS motor remained in its open A/C cruising position, the sudden loss of compressor drag would cause the vehicle to lurch forward or the engine to flare noticeably. OFFPER2 multiplies the speed-looked-up ACCTBL value by ~94% (0xF0) and immediately subtracts it from the desired stepper motor position, maintaining constant engine torque.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Engine Flare When A/C Cycles Off: If you feel the car accelerate or hear the engine flare up when the A/C compressor clutch clicks off during cruising, increase OFFPER2 (up to 0xFF = 100% immediate retraction).
  • RPM Sag When A/C Cycles Off: If the car jerks or RPM dips momentarily when the A/C cycles off, reduce OFFPER2 to allow a softer step subtraction.
