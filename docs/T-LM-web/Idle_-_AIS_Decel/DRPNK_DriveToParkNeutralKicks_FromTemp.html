1. Overview & Purpose

DRPNK (DriveToParkNeutralKicks_FromTemp) defines a temporary stepper motor position kick applied when an automatic transmission is shifted out of gear into Park or Neutral.

When shifting out of Drive, torque converter drag instantly vanishes. In stock calibrations, DRPNK is set to 0x00 (zero steps) because the engine naturally tends to flare up rather than stall when load is removed. However, the calibration infrastructure exists to provide positive or negative step offsets across coolant temperatures to tailor shift transition quality.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at line 5145.
  • Triggered when bit 4 of BitFlags_4f is set (detecting a transmission shift transition) and bit 3 is cleared (shifting out of Drive).
  • Requires RPM delta to exceed PNDLTN_DeltaRpmChangeToUse_DRPNK_or_PNDRK.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: Single-point table set to 0x00.
  • Automatic Conversions: On automatic vehicles with high-stall torque converters, shifting into neutral can cause RPM flare; leave at 0x00. On manual transmission cars, this routine is inactive.
