1. Overview & Purpose

ACCTBL (ACOffIdleDelta_FromSpeed) defines the dynamic AIS step addition applied when the Air Conditioning compressor clutch is engaged and the engine is fully warmed up (CoolantTemp >= ACTMP_AisTempSwap).

At cruising speeds, the A/C compressor operates at high RPM, demanding significant horsepower. When cruising on the highway or in city traffic, releasing the throttle while the A/C is pumping requires progressive bypass air to cushion the deceleration. ACCTBL indexes vehicle speed (VehicleSpeed_HB) to provide an increasing step offset as road speed rises, ensuring seamless A/C load handling at all speeds.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated in CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at line 5056 and line 5251.
  • Active when A/C clutch is engaged and CoolantTemp >= ACTMP_AisTempSwap.
  • Indexed by vehicle speed high byte: VehicleSpeed_HB.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • A/C Decel Stumble at Speed: If letting off the gas while driving at 45 MPH with the A/C on causes the car to buck or hesitate, increase the mid-speed points in ACCTBL.
  • A/C Disengagement Flare: When the A/C cycles off at speed, ACCTBL interacts directly with OFFPER2 to instantly subtract the A/C step addition and avoid engine speed flare.
