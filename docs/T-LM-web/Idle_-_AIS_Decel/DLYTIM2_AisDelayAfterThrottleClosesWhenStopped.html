1. Overview & Purpose

DLYTIM2 (AisDelayAfterThrottleClosesWhenStopped) defines the settling time delay loaded into Timer_CountdownFromStartRunForAisFeedback when the throttle is snapped shut while the vehicle is stationary (< MPHREF, < 6 MPH).

When the vehicle is stopped in neutral and the driver revs or blips the throttle, the engine decelerates rapidly under zero driveline load. Engaging closed-loop idle speed control instantly as the throttle hits the stop screw would cause the AIS motor to overreact, resulting in an RPM undershoot and potential stall. DLYTIM2 provides a generous ~3.0-second cushion, allowing the engine to settle softly onto the mechanical stop and dashpot air cushion before closed-loop trims resume.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside CheckThrottle_AtFullThrottle at line 6819.
  • Selected when vehicle speed is strictly below MPHREF_SwitchPointForDecelIdleAisControl and transmission is in Park/Neutral.
  • Accessed in firmware via pointer increment (inx) from DLYTIM.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Rev-Blip Stalling When Stopped: If revving the engine in neutral causes the tachometer needle to drop below 700 RPM or stall when the throttle snaps shut, increase DLYTIM2.
  • Sluggish Free-Rev Settle: If free-revving the engine in the staging lanes or driveway causes the engine to take an annoying 3-4 seconds to return to base idle, reduce DLYTIM2 to 0x1C (~1.8 sec).
