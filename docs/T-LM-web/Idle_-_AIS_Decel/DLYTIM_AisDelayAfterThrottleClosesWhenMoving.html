1. Overview & Purpose

DLYTIM (AisDelayAfterThrottleClosesWhenMoving) defines a settling time delay loaded into Timer_CountdownFromStartRunForAisFeedback whenever the throttle snaps shut while the vehicle is in motion (> MPHREF, approx 6 MPH).

When decelerating while moving, the engine needs time to coast down smoothly under dashpot control. Engaging closed-loop idle speed feedback too early while the car is still rolling in gear would cause the stepper motor to violently close down, inducing driveline jerk and bucking. DLYTIM holds off closed-loop idle regulation for ~1.6 seconds, giving the vehicle time to complete its initial deceleration gracefully.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine CheckThrottle_AtFullThrottle at line 6807.
  • Triggered when throttle transitions from open to closed (tstb; bpl LF192).
  • Selected when vehicle speed is greater than or equal to MPHREF_SwitchPointForDecelIdleAisControl.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Driveline Jerk on Decel: If letting off the gas while cruising causes an immediate jerk or 'thud' as the engine braking kicks in, increasing DLYTIM extends the smooth dashpot transition.
  • RPM Hang While Rolling: If pushing the clutch in while coasting causes the engine RPM to hang above 1,500 RPM for too long before dropping, reduce DLYTIM (e.g. to 0x10 = ~1.0 sec).
