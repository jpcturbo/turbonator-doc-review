1. Overview & Purpose

BARFLR (OffsetCorrectionTo_MAPFLR_FromBaro) provides ambient barometric pressure compensation to the deceleration MAP floor (MAPFLR).

Because ambient atmospheric pressure drops with increasing altitude, the physical pressure differential across the throttle plate changes drastically. A target absolute pressure floor that works perfectly at sea level (101 kPa / 29.9 inHg) represents a vastly different manifold depression at 6,000 feet (80 kPa / 24 inHg). BARFLR scales the target MAP floor so that altitude changes do not disrupt deceleration airflow regulation.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Indexed by BaroPressure.
  • Multiplied directly against the product of MAPFLR and MPTEMP.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Standard Calibrations: Typically calibrated as a flat multiplier (0x80 = 128 / 1.0× normalized) or slight upward taper at low barometric readings.
  • High Altitude Tuning: Only adjust if high-altitude field testing reveals inconsistent trailing-throttle behavior between sea level and mountainous driving.
