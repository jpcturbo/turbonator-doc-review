1. Overview & Purpose

AISHLM (AisHighLimitMaxOpenSteps) enforces an absolute mechanical protection ceiling on the AIS stepper motor position.

The physical Chrysler AIS stepper motor has a finite linear stroke (approximately 230 to 240 steps of total travel). If the ECU software were to command 255 steps or allow step accumulators to roll over, the stepper motor pintle shaft would back out completely, disengage its internal lead screw threads, or jam violently against the motor housing, permanently destroying the actuator. AISHLM places an unyielding hard clamp at 220 steps (0xDC). Any calculated step demand exceeding AISHLM is instantly clamped to AISHLM.

  • Clamps position: if DesiredNewAisPosition > AISHLM, DesiredNewAisPosition = AISHLM.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Safety Critical: NEVER increase AISHLM above 225 steps. Exceeding mechanical travel will jam the stepper motor pintle, requiring throttle body disassembly or motor replacement.
  • Aftermarket Throttle Bodies: Some aftermarket throttle bodies have shallower bypass cavities. If an aftermarket throttle body binds at high step counts, reduce AISHLM to 180 - 200 steps to guarantee the pintle never contacts the rear stop.
