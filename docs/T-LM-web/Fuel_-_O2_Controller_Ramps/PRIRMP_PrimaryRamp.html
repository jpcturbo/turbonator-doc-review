1. Overview & Purpose

PRIRMP_PrimaryRamp is the Proportional Step table of the ECU's Proportional-Integral (PI) closed-loop fuel controller.

In closed-loop feedback, whenever the oxygen sensor switches states (from Lean to Rich, or Rich to Lean), the fuel controller must take immediate, decisive action to reverse the fuel delivery trend before exhaust transport delay allows the engine to overshoot too far into the opposite condition. PRIRMP specifies the instantaneous step jump (proportional jump) applied to the fuel pulse width counter the moment an O2 toggle is detected.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

PRIRMP triggers on O2 state transitions during closed-loop operation: * Rich-to-Lean Toggle: Injects an immediate positive fuel step to quench lean combustion. * Lean-to-Rich Toggle: Pulls an immediate negative fuel step to quench rich combustion. * Cell Selection: * Uses DetermineRampCellFromRPMandMAP to pick one of 9 cruise cells based on current RPM and MAP, or selects the designated Idle Cell if operating at closed throttle. * Cell contents define both rich step and lean step sizes.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Proportional Jump Dynamics:
  • Larger Step Sizes: Creates wider, faster swings across stoichiometry. This helps old catalytic converters light off and perform redox reactions, but if too large, causes noticeable exhaust hunting, audible RPM surging at cruise, and choppy idle.
  • Smaller Step Sizes: Narrows the closed-loop swing limit, smoothing out engine idle and cruise stability. However, if step sizes are too small, the ECU responds too slowly to load changes, causing lean hesitation on gentle tip-in before secondary integration catches up.
  • Tuning for Camshafts and Large Injectors:
  • Upgrading to large injectors (+40% or +52 lb/hr) doubles or triples the fuel mass delivered per step tick! Tuners must scale down PRIRMP values proportionately to restore smooth closed-loop operation.
