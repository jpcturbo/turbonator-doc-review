1. Overview & Purpose

SECRMP_SecondaryRamp represents the Integral Ramp (Slope) table of the ECU's closed-loop PI fueling algorithm.

While PRIRMP provides an immediate step correction when the O2 sensor flips, the exhaust gas may remain on one side of stoichiometry for multiple engine cycles while waiting for physical mixture delivery. SECRMP dictates the rate at which the ECU steadily ramps fuel pulse width richer or leaner over time until the sensor finally crosses the stoichiometric midpoint.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Continuous Closed-Loop Integration:
  • Evaluated on every execution of ModifyO2FeedbackCountVarToMaintainStoich_MM.
  • Executes while the O2 sensor remains on the same side of stoichiometric (between primary toggle events).
  • Uses DetermineRampCellFromRPMandMAP to match current engine RPM and MAP to the appropriate cell slope.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Integral Ramp Dynamics:
  • Steeper Slopes (Higher Values): Causes the fuel pulse width to ramp rapidly towards the opposite limit. Produces higher frequency switching, but risks heavy overshoot and hunting.
  • Flatter Slopes (Lower Values): Causes slow, gradual fueling adjustments. Produces a very smooth AFR curve, but if too flat, the engine will spend excessive time trapped on one side of stoichiometry during sudden load changes.
  • Injector Scaling: Large fuel injectors (+40%, +52 lb/hr) deliver significantly more fuel per tick of pulse width. Scaling SECRMP down is essential to maintain proper integral ramp slopes with larger injectors.
