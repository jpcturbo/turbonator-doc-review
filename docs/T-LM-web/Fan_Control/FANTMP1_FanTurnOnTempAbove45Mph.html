1. Overview & Purpose

FANTMP1 (FanTurnOnTempAbove45Mph) defines the radiator cooling fan turn-on temperature threshold (stock calibration = 0xEE / ~218°F) when the vehicle is cruising at highway speeds (above 45 MPH).

At highway cruising speeds, high-velocity ambient ram-air naturally flows through the front grille, condenser, and radiator core, providing substantial passive cooling without requiring electrical fan assistance. Running the high-amp electric cooling fan at 60 MPH creates aerodynamic drag through the radiator shroud and places unnecessary electrical load on the alternator. FANTMP1 elevates the fan turn-on threshold to 218°F at speed, ensuring the electric fan only engages on the highway if the engine encounters severe heat distress (such as climbing steep mountain grades or towing a trailer).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine ControlFan2_MM at line 6899.
  • Active when vehicle speed is above 45 MPH (Temp0 >= 0x46).
  • When CoolantTemp >= FANTMP1, bit 0 of BitFlags_84 is set and the radiator fan relay is grounded.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • 160°F / 180°F Thermostats: When upgrading to a 180°F thermostat, lower FANTMP1 to 0xD2 (~195°F). For a 160°F thermostat, lower to 0xBC (~175°F).
  • High-Speed Overheating: If the car runs cool in city traffic but temperatures climb on the highway, verify that FANTMP1 is set appropriately to assist ram airflow if the radiator core is partially clogged or heat-soaked.
