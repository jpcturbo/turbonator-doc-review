1. Overview & Purpose

FANBAT (FanTurnsOnWhenBatteryTempAboveThis) defines an underhood temperature threshold (stock calibration = 0x9A / ~120°F) measured by the battery tray thermistor.

Turbocharged engines radiate immense underhood heat soak from the exhaust manifold and turbo turbine housing. If the battery and ECU (which sits inside the Power Module behind the battery) reach extreme temperatures, lead-acid battery plates warp, and alternator field regulators lose efficiency. If battery temperature rises above FANBAT, the ECU turns the cooling fan ON to flush stagnant hot air out of the engine bay.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside ControlFan2_MM at line 6873.
  • Compares AmbientAirTempVolts against FANBAT.
  • When underhood temperature exceeds FANBAT, energizes the fan relay.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Underhood Heat Management: Useful on tight engine bays (like Omni / Charger GLH) where turbo heat buildup is intense.
  • Battery Relocation: If the battery tray thermistor is disconnected or deleted, ensure this check does not trigger false fan activation.
