1. Overview & Purpose

MXSBST_AllowedBoost_FromSpeed implements vehicle speed-dependent boost limiting (gear-based / traction boost limiting).

In front-wheel-drive Chrysler turbo vehicles, hitting full 12+ psi boost in 1st gear causes uncontrollable wheelspin and violent torque steer. Additionally, on automatic transmission vehicles, full-boost low-speed launches cause severe torque converter and differential pin wear. MXSBST limits boost to 7.2 psi below 21 mph, allowing boost to ramp up to full 12.1 psi as vehicle speed crosses 30 mph.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • 1st Gear Launch Traction: Excellent tool for dialing in launch traction on high-horsepower street cars.
  • Manual vs. Automatic: On manual transmission calibrations, if the Park/Neutral switch or speed flag is grounded, this limiter may be bypassed.
  • Drag Racing: If running drag slicks and you want full boost off the line, raise Point 1 to match your target boost so low speed does not hold back launch power.
