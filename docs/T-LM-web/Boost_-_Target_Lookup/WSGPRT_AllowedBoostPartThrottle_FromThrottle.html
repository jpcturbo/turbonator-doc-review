1. Overview & Purpose

WSGPRT_AllowedBoostPartThrottle_FromThrottle establishes the maximum target boost ceiling permitted when the engine is operating at part throttle.

Full boost should only be available when the driver's foot is mashed to Wide-Open Throttle. If the driver is holding part throttle (modulating speed on the highway or in a curve), delivering 15+ psi boost against a partially closed throttle blade creates massive intake throttling losses, high turbo backpressure, and poor drivability. WSGPRT clamps maximum allowable boost to 7.2 psi whenever the throttle is not at WOT.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x3F ($7.2 ext{ psig}$).
  • Part Throttle Drivability: Keeps the car smooth and controllable when passing without needing to downshift or floor the pedal.
  • Tuning Guidance: If you want more responsive passing power at 60–75% throttle without flooring it, raise WSGPRT to 9–10 psi.
