1. Overview & Purpose

WSGKNK_BoostTimeSinceLastKnock defines the knock-triggered boost reduction penalty curve.

When the piezoelectric knock sensor detects engine knock under boost, the ECU instantly retards ignition timing. If knock persists or exceeds knock thresholds, spark retard alone is insufficient. WSGKNK directly subtracts boost from the target boost goal immediately following a knock event, steadily restoring boost back to normal over a 2.8-second decay window as long as no further knock occurs.

extFinal Boost Target = \max(0, extTemp4 - extWSGKNK( extKnockTimer)) + extBaro

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Setting: Subtracts 2.1 psi boost immediately upon knock, decaying to 0 psi over ~2.8 seconds.
  • Safety Failsafe: Protects the engine from repeating high-load detonation cycles.
  • Tuning Note: Do not zero out WSGKNK on street engines running pump gas. If knock occurs, lowering boost is your best defense against broken ring lands and melted pistons.
