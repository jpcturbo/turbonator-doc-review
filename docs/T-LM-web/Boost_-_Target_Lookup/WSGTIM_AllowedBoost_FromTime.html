1. Overview & Purpose

WSGTIM_AllowedBoost_FromTime limits target boost pressure as a function of sustained time in boost.

This table was designed by Chrysler engineers to implement a temporary "overboost burst" feature (delivering a short burst of higher boost during passing maneuvers, tapering down to a lower continuous boost level during sustained high-speed autobahn-style pulls to protect exhaust valves and turbine wheels from thermal saturation).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Setting: Flat 12.1 psi (0x69). In the A171 manual calibration, Chrysler chose not to taper boost over time, keeping the limit flat.
  • Tuning Guidance: If you raise WSGRPM for higher boost (e.g. 18 psi), you must also raise WSGTIM to at least 18 psi. Because the ECU enforces the lowest value between all target tables, leaving WSGTIM at 12.1 psi will cap your boost to 12.1 psi!
