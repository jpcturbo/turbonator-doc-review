1. Overview & Purpose

RATLIM_BoostOverboostPrevention establishes an RPM-dependent rate-of-rise boost buffer used to prevent sudden boost spikes and overboost shutoff triggers.

During rapid turbo spool-up in low gears, boost can spike past the target before the wastegate actuator can physically move. RATLIM models the maximum allowable rate of boost increase vs RPM, preemptively pulling wastegate duty cycle to cushion the spool curve and prevent slamming into the overboost fuel cut ceiling.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration
