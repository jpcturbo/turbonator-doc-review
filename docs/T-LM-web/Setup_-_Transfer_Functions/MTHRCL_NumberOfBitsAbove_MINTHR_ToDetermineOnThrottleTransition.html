1. Overview & Purpose

MTHRCL (NumberOfBitsAbove_MINTHR_ToDetermineOnThrottleTransition) specifies the deadband margin (stock calibration = 3 ADC counts / ~0.059V) above MINTHR_LowestSessionTPS that the throttle voltage must exceed before the ECU declares an ON-throttle transition.

This small deadband filters out electrical noise, alternator ripple, and engine vibration from being misinterpreted as driver throttle inputs, ensuring idle speed regulation is not spuriously kicked out while idling at stoplights.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside CheckThrottle_AtFullThrottle at line 6770.
  • Selected when engine is in off-throttle idle state.
  • Formulates the tripwire for tip-in transient detection.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hypersensitive Tip-In: If light vehicle vibrations or rough road bumps cause the engine to jerk or jump out of closed-loop idle, increasing MTHRCL to 4 or 5 counts increases throttle deadband stability.
  • Snappier Tip-In Response: Lowering MTHRCL to 2 counts creates instant tip-in acceleration enrichment response.
