1. Overview & Purpose

MTHROP (NumberOfBitsAbove_MINTHR_ToDetermineOffThrottleTransition) defines the tight voltage threshold (stock calibration = 1 ADC count / ~0.02V) above MINTHR_LowestSessionTPS for the ECU to declare the engine has returned to the OFF-throttle state.

When the driver releases the accelerator pedal, the throttle plate snaps back toward the mechanical stop. Due to return spring harmonics and cable friction, the final fraction of a millimeter takes a brief moment to settle. MTHROP allows the ECU to recognize closed throttle when the TPS signal is within 1 bit of learned idle, enabling idle closed loop and decel fuel cutoff promptly.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside CheckThrottle_AtFullThrottle at line 6773.
  • Active when transitioning from on-throttle to off-throttle (bpl LF138).
  • Added to MINTHR_LowestSessionTPS to form the comparison threshold.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Sticky Throttle Cables: If a sticky throttle cable or weak return spring causes the throttle to stop 0.05V above the mechanical stop, the ECU may think the driver is still resting a foot on the gas, preventing idle regulation. Increasing MTHROP to 2 or 3 counts widens the off-throttle window.
