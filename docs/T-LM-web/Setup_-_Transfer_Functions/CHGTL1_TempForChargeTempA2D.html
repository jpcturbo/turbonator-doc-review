1. Overview & Purpose

CHGTL1 (TempForChargeTempA2D) is the 4-point 2D transfer curve that converts analog voltage from the Intake Manifold Air Charge Temperature (ACT) sensor into calibrated temperature units (ChargeTemp).

The charge temperature sensor measures the density of air compressed by the turbocharger entering the intake plenum. CHGTL1 maps the non-linear thermistor curve across extreme winter cold (-40°F) to extreme intercooler heat-soak (+250°F). The resulting ChargeTemp value directly scales air density calculations for fuel injection and trims ignition advance (AIRTMP, ADVCHG).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine CheckAndFilterCoolantTempSensor_MM at line 931.
  • Indexed by raw Charge Temperature ADC voltage.
  • Interpolated using Lookup4ByteTable and stored into ChargeTemp.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Aftermarket Fast-Response Thermistors: Standard factory Chrysler ACT sensors are brass-encapsulated and have significant thermal inertia. Many tuners upgrade to open-element GM or Bosch air temp sensors for instant boost temperature tracking. If swapping to a non-Chrysler sensor, CHGTL1 must be revised to match the new sensor's calibration curve.
