1. Overview & Purpose

TMPTL2 (TempForHotScaleA2D) is the companion transfer lookup table to TMPTL1, dedicated to measuring engine coolant temperature during normal warm running and high-temperature conditions (140°F to 260°F+).

Once the engine warms past the crossover threshold, the ECU toggles the hardware bias resistor on Port 1, expanding the hot-temperature ADC span across full 8-bit resolution. TMPTL2 translates these warm ADC voltages into the final runtime variable CoolantTemp used by spark advance, fuel enrichment, and radiator cooling fan control.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside GetCoolantTempQuick at line 946 and ResetRoutine at line 5747.
  • Active when Port 1 bias bit is cleared (BitFlags_Port1 bit 3 = 0).
  • Governs steady-state running temperature readings.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Radiator Fan & Overheat Accuracy: Because normal operating temperature (180°F - 210°F) resides entirely on TMPTL2, accurate calibration of this curve is vital for proper radiator fan cycling (FANTM1, FANTM2) and hot spark retard (HOTSPK).
