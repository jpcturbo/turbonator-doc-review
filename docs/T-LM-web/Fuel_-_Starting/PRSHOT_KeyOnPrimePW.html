1. Overview & Purpose

PRSHOT_KeyOnPrimePW defines the initial "prime shot" fuel injection pulse delivered the instant the ignition key is turned to the ON / RUN position, before the starter motor begins cranking.

When an engine sits overnight, fuel in the intake ports evaporates and the manifold walls dry completely. Delivering a brief, pre-cranking squirt of fuel the moment the key is turned ON wets the intake ports and primes the air charge so that the very first cylinder to reach compression during cranking has vaporized fuel ready to ignite. PRSHOT scales this initial squirt duration based on engine coolant temperature: delivering a large prime shot when ice cold and tapering down to a minimal pulse when the engine is already warm.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

PRSHOT is triggered once per key-on cycle: * Trigger: Ignition switch turned from OFF to ON. * Lookup Input: CoolantTemp read from the sensor immediately upon power-up. * Delivery: Fires all fuel injectors simultaneously for the duration looked up from PRSHOT.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Table Values (Bigger Prime Shot):
  • Delivers more fuel on key turn-on.
  • Real-world feel: Produces instant "touch of the key" firing on cold mornings.
  • Cautions: If set too high on warm restarts (or when running large aftermarket injectors), turning the key on-and-off repeatedly (e.g., listening to the radio or testing electrical circuits) will repeatedly prime the cylinders and flood the engine before cranking even starts!
  • Decreasing Table Values (Smaller Prime Shot):
  • Injects less fuel on key-on.
  • Real-world feel: Prevents hot-start flooding and plug fouling with oversized injectors (+40% or larger).
  • Zeroing Out:
  • Setting PRSHOT to 0x00 eliminates the pre-crank prime shot entirely; fuel delivery will only begin once the starter motor cranks and generates distributor sync pulses.
