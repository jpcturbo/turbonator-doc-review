1. Overview & Purpose

STFBAR_FuelStartReductionFromBaro provides an altitude-dependent reduction factor for cranking fuel delivery.

At high elevations (e.g., Denver, CO or mountain passes), atmospheric pressure is low and ambient air is significantly less dense. Delivering sea-level cranking fuel at high altitudes produces an overly rich air/fuel ratio that can easily wet spark plugs and flood the engine before it catches. STFBAR generates a reduction multiplier that trims base cranking fuel (STRTFL) as ambient barometric pressure decreases.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

STFBAR is evaluated during engine cranking: * Lookup Input: Ambient BaroPressure. * Combination: Multiplied by the coolant modifier (STFTMP), negated, and applied to scale down Temp0 via ScaleXbySignedB_ReturnD.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Table Values (More Altitude Fuel Reduction):
  • Effect: Trims more fuel out during high-altitude starts.
  • Real-World Feel: Clears up black exhaust smoke, rough cold starts, and fouled plugs when starting at high elevation.
  • Decreasing Table Values (Less Altitude Fuel Reduction):
  • Effect: Delivers more cranking fuel at altitude.
  • Sea-Level Calibration:
  • At sea level (~14.7 psi baro / 0x7A), STFBAR is zero (0x00), producing zero reduction.
