1. Overview & Purpose

CLDPOS (AisColdPosition_FromTemp) establishes an extra off-idle bypass airflow opening based on engine coolant temperature.

When a cold engine is driven off-idle and the throttle is opened, the engine requires additional bypass air pre-positioning so that when the throttle suddenly snaps shut, cold intake manifold walls (which condense liquid fuel) do not cause an immediate lean stall. As engine coolant reaches normal operating temperature, CLDPOS decays down towards zero steps, handing full dynamic dashpot authority over to throttle and RPM-based tables.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated in CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at line 5213.
  • Indexed by CoolantTemp.
  • Multiplied by barometric compensation factor BARAIS_AisBaroCompensation before being combined with RPM and throttle off-idle terms.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Stall on Decel / Clutch In: If a modified engine stalls when coming to a stop during the first 3-5 minutes of driving before full warmup, increase the values in CLDPOS in the 70°F - 140°F region.
  • Cold High Idle / Hanging RPM: If cold off-idle cruising causes engine RPM to 'hang' or refuse to drop below 1,500 RPM when you lift off the accelerator, reduce the step values in CLDPOS.
