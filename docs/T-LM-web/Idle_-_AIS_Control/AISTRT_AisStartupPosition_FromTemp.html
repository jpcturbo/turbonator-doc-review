1. Overview & Purpose

AISTRT (AisStartupPosition_FromTemp) is a 2D lookup table that establishes the open-loop baseline step position for the Automatic Idle Speed (AIS) stepper motor actuator during engine cranking and initial startup.

When the ignition key is turned to 'Start' (cranking) or immediately after fire-up before running closed-loop idle speed regulation engages, the ECU positions the idle air bypass pintle to this calibrated step count. Cold engines exhibit high internal boundary friction, viscous oil drag, and poor initial fuel vaporization, requiring significantly more bypass air to guarantee a prompt catch without prolonged cranking. Conversely, a fully warmed engine requires far less bypass airflow to prevent an aggressive startup RPM over-flare. Once the engine catches and exceeds cranking speed threshold, the ECU transitions from this initial starting step count toward running idle closed-loop control.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect exclusively during engine cranking and the immediate transition into key-on run mode: - Active when engine RPM is below the cranking speed exit threshold or during the initial startup initialization phase (CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at address $E451). - Coolant temperature ADC input (CoolantTemp) serves as the X-axis index. - Once startup is validated and the engine enters normal running mode, control branches into running off-idle tracking and closed-loop target idle routines.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Table Values: Forces the AIS motor to open wider during startup. Use this if the engine stumbles, catches sluggishly, or fails to catch cleanly when cold, especially with large camshafts, heavy valve springs, or E85 fuels requiring additional cranking airflow.
  • Decreasing Table Values: Decreases initial bypass opening. Helpful if the engine aggressively flares to high RPM (e.g. > 2,000 RPM) immediately upon starting when hot or warm.
  • Modified Throttle Bodies / Big Cams: Aftermarket throttle bodies (such as 52mm or 58mm units) or throttle plates with enlarged bypass holes flow substantially more air per step. If an aftermarket throttle body causes excessive startup flare, scale this entire curve downward by 15% to 30%.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Hardware Mechanics: The Chrysler AIS unit is a 4-wire bipolar stepper motor. A step count of 0 represents fully seated (minimum bypass airflow), while higher counts retract the pintle to expose the bypass orifice.
  • MPTuning Consideration: Ensure that the warm-temperature end of the curve aligns closely with the baseline warm idle operating steps (CALPOS) to avoid an abrupt RPM sag or jump when transitioning from startup to closed-loop idle.
