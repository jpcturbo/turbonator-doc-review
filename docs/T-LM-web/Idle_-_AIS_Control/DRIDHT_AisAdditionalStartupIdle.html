1. Overview & Purpose

DRIDHT (AdditionalStartupIdle) defines a calibrated idle speed adder that is appended to the base coolant-dependent target idle speed right after the engine starts.

During the first few seconds following combustion initiation, engine friction and alternator drag from initial battery recharging are abnormally elevated. Adding a moderate RPM buffer prevents the engine from stalling or dipping below target as the driver releases the ignition key from the START position to RUN. The duration of this adder is governed by STTIME_AdditionalStartupIdleTime.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine DetermineTargetIdleSpeed_MM at line 5367.
  • Applied when KeyOnOrEngineRunningTime is less than STTIME_AdditionalStartupIdleTime.
  • Added directly to the base target idle speed looked up from AISPN (Park/Neutral) or AISCLD (Drive).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing DRIDHT: Produces a more pronounced initial post-start idle flare, keeping engine speed elevated for positive oil pressure buildup and stall avoidance on cold mornings or with lightweight flywheels.
  • Decreasing DRIDHT: Lowers post-cranking flare RPM, yielding a quieter, softer start. Setting too low can cause the engine to stumble or catch-and-die immediately after cranking.
  • High-Stall / Big Cam Applications: High-duration camshafts with low manifold vacuum at idle benefit from setting DRIDHT slightly higher (e.g., 0x12 to 0x18 = +144 to +192 RPM) to guarantee stable combustion before closed-loop adaptive idle stabilizes.
