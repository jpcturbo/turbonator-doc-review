1. Overview & Purpose

THRPOS (AisPosition_FromThrottle) acts as an electronic dashpot. It maps throttle position above closed-throttle idle (TpsVolts - MINTHR_LowestSessionTPS) directly to a target AIS stepper motor step opening.

When the driver depresses the accelerator pedal, the ECU pre-emptively retracts the AIS pintle to an open position corresponding to throttle angle. If the driver suddenly snaps the throttle shut at high RPM, the AIS valve is already open, providing a cushioned air bypass buffer that prevents catastrophic manifold vacuum spikes, oil pull past piston rings, and decel engine stalling. As engine speed and throttle return to idle, the ECU ramps the motor back down.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Throttle Snap Stalling: If dropping the throttle quickly causes the engine to dip below idle speed and die, increase THRPOS values, particularly in the lower throttle angle range (0% to 15% throttle).
  • Hanging Throttle / Slow Decel: If the vehicle feels like cruise control is stuck on when lifting off the gas, or if the RPM takes several seconds to return to idle, THRPOS is set too high or the physical throttle body has excess mechanical leakage.
