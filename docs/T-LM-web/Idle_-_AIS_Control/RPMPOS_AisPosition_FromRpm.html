1. Overview & Purpose

RPMPOS (AisPosition_FromRpm) establishes a minimum AIS stepper motor opening based purely on engine RPM (EngineRpm_HB).

Even if the throttle is only cracked slightly, high engine RPM creates immense cylinder intake demand. If the throttle closes at 4,500 RPM in neutral or with the clutch depressed, manifold absolute pressure collapses instantly to near zero (extreme vacuum). RPMPOS ensures that at higher engine speeds, the idle air bypass is pre-emptively opened to a designated step count, preventing vacuum collapse and providing a progressive RPM coastdown curve.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated in CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM at line 5227.
  • Indexed by high byte of engine speed (EngineRpm_HB).
  • Peak-selected against the cold baro-compensated idle term (CLDPOS * BARAIS).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Decel Dip / Return-to-Idle Catching: If pressing the clutch at 4,000 RPM causes the tachometer needle to plummet like a rock and dip below target idle (or stall), increase the upper RPM steps in RPMPOS.
  • Engine Braking: Excessive values in RPMPOS diminish engine braking effect during off-throttle deceleration. Keep values moderate to maintain engine braking while preserving anti-stall protection.
