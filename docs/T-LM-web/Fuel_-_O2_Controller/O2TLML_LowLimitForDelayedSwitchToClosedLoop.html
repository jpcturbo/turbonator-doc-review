1. Overview & Purpose

O2TLML_LowLimitForDelayedSwitchToClosedLoop is the lower voltage threshold required to qualify O2 sensor readiness before enabling closed-loop fueling.

Working directly in conjunction with O2TLMH, O2TLML confirms that the O2 sensor is active by verifying that it can pull down strongly into lean voltage territory (< 0.293V). If the sensor has not yet reached sufficient heat to overcome its internal impedance and produce a valid low voltage, closed-loop entry is deferred.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Warmup Qualification Sequence:
  • Evaluated in CheckIfO2SensorDataIsValid after CLTEMP and O2TIME prerequisites have passed.
  • If O2SensorVolts < O2TLML (below 0.293V), the ECU confirms a valid lean reading, validates the sensor, and asserts closed loop (BitFlags_81 bit 4).
  • If O2SensorVolts rests between O2TLML (0x0F) and O2TLMH (0x1F), the ECU considers the sensor unready and exits back to open loop.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x0F (15 decimal ≈ 0.293V).
  • Diagnostic Utility: An O2 sensor with high ground offset (common in older wiring harnesses with corroded engine ground straps) may never register below 0.35V. In such cars, the ECU will fail this test and refuse to enter closed-loop, causing poor gas mileage and continuous open-loop running.
  • Adjustment Guidance: Generally leave at stock unless troubleshooting stubborn closed-loop entry delays caused by slight electrical ground offsets.
