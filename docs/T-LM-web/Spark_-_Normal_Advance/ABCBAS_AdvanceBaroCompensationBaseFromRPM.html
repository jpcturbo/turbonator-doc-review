1. Overview & Purpose

ABCBAS_AdvanceBaroCompensationBaseFromRPM provides the RPM-dependent baseline ceiling for the ECU's barometric pressure spark compensation calculation.

At higher elevations (lower barometric ambient pressure), air is less dense and cylinder filling is reduced at any given throttle position. While naturally aspirated engines can often tolerate additional advance at altitude due to lower charge density, turbocharged engines operating under boost at high elevation face hotter intake charges (since the turbocharger must spin faster and work harder at a higher pressure ratio to achieve target boost) and lower exhaust backpressure ratios. ABCBAS, in conjunction with ABCFBR and ABCFRP, provides an altitude-sensitive ignition timing ceiling.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

ABCBAS is calculated on every execution pass of CalculateSparkAdvance_MM immediately after the base MAP advance calculation: 1. Engine Warm: Coolant temperature is at or above TMPSWP. 2. Evaluated Input: Engine speed (EngineRpm_HB). 3. Clamping Role: The resulting baro compensation value is evaluated against the current base MAP advance (Temp0). If the baro-calculated advance ceiling is lower than Temp0, it pulls Temp0 down to this calculated baro limit (floored at MINADV).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

The barometric calculation determines a maximum allowable MAP advance ceiling ($A$):

A = ABCBAS(RPM) - [(ABCFRP(RPM) × ABCFBR(BaroPressure) / 256)]

If A < Temp0, the ECU clamps base MAP advance to $A$ (provided A ≥ MINADV).

  • Increasing Table Values (Higher Base Factor):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Raises the barometric ceiling, allowing more base MAP advance under altitude conditions.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Prevents premature timing reduction at high elevation, maintaining crisp throttle response.
  • Decreasing Table Values (Lower Base Factor):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Lowers the barometric timing ceiling.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Clamps timing more aggressively at altitude to safeguard against hot turbo air charges.
  • Sea-Level Operation:
  • At sea level (~14.7 psi baro / 0x78), ABCFBR drops to 0, making the subtraction term zero and allowing ABCBAS to act as an absolute RPM-based MAP advance limiter.
