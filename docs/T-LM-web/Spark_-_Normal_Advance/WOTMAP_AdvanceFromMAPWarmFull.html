1. Overview & Purpose

WOTMAP_AdvanceFromMAPWarmFull is the primary full-throttle / wide-open throttle (WOT) load advance table. It establishes the base ignition advance curve as a function of intake manifold pressure whenever the engine is operated at wide-open throttle under warm conditions.

At WOT, volumetric efficiency is maximized, cylinder pressures are at their peak, and combustion chamber temperatures rise rapidly—especially under turbocharger boost. Because high charge density drastically accelerates flame front speeds, ignition timing must be retarded under boost to keep peak cylinder pressure (PCP) safely timed after top dead center (typically ~14–17° ATDC) and prevent destructive detonation.

In addition to serving as the WOT ignition curve, WOTMAP serves as the absolute minimum advance floor for warm part-throttle boost timing via the HOTMAP blending logic.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

WOTMAP is engaged under the following conditions: 1. Engine is Warm: Coolant temperature is at or above TMPSWP (≥ 166°F / 74°C). 2. Throttle is Wide-Open (WOT): Bit 0 of BitFlags_81 is 1 (Full Throttle flag set). - In this mode, the part-throttle blending calculation (HOTMAP and ARADVR) is completely bypassed. 3. Part-Throttle Floor: During part throttle under boost, if HOTMAP minus the ARADVR retard drops below WOTMAP, the ECU clamps timing to WOTMAP.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

Total full-throttle ignition timing is calculated as the base RPM advance (GOVNER) plus the MAP advance from WOTMAP:

Total WOT Advance ≈ GOVNER(RPM) + WOTMAP(MAP) - AntiLagRetard - KnockRetard ± BaroTrims

  • Increasing Table Values (Adding Timing in WOTMAP):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Advances full-throttle ignition timing across the corresponding boost/MAP points.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Increases peak torque, accelerates spool-up, and improves high-RPM horsepower.
  • Cautions: Extremely critical for engine safety! Excessive advance under boost rapidly causes heavy detonation, blown head gaskets, or melted ring lands. Always tune with a knock monitor and appropriate fuel octane.
  • Decreasing Table Values (Pulling Timing in WOTMAP):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Retards full-throttle ignition timing under boost.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Produces a safe, knock-resistant tune (ideal for low-octane fuel or high boost), but excessive retard causes sluggish power delivery and very high exhaust gas temperatures (EGTs), which can overheat turbochargers and exhaust valves.

Table Data (Stock A171 Calibration):

Number of points: 4
Point 1: MAP = 0x66 (14 inHg vac) -> Advance = 0x14 (+20 / 2 = +10.0°)
Point 2: MAP = 0x70 (11 inHg vac) -> Advance = 0x00 (0 / 2   = +0.0°)
Point 3: MAP = 0x92 (1.4 psi boost) -> Advance = 0xEE (-18 / 2 = -9.0°)
Point 4: MAP = 0xB6 (7.1 psi boost) -> Advance = 0xE6 (-26 / 2 = -13.0°)

(Advance values are signed 8-bit integers where 1 bit = 0.5° crankshaft advance).

    In MPTune, FullThrottleTiming (FTTIME) is a calculated display table created by adding GOVNER and WOTMAP. It does not exist as an independent 3D lookup table in the ECU firmware.
  • Anti-Lag Retard (ANTLAG): Notice line 6593: suba AntiLagRetard. In Turbonator-LM calibrations, launch anti-lag retard is subtracted specifically from Temp0 immediately after the WOTMAP lookup.
