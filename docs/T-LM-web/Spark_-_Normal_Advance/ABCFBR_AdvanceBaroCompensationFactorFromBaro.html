1. Overview & Purpose

ABCFBR_AdvanceBaroCompensationFactorFromBaro provides the barometric pressure weighting factor for the ECU's altitude ignition timing limiter.

As ambient atmospheric pressure drops with increasing elevation, the turbocharger must work at higher pressure ratios to achieve target boost, resulting in significantly higher compressor outlet temperatures. To prevent detonation under these conditions, ABCFBR generates a scaling multiplier that grows as ambient barometric pressure decreases. This multiplier scales the RPM factor (ABCFRP), and the product is subtracted from the baseline ceiling (ABCBAS) to reduce allowable MAP spark advance at high altitudes.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  1. Ambient Pressure Dependent: Evaluates the BaroPressure RAM variable (latched during key-on and updated periodically during driving).
  2. Altitude Operation: In the factory calibration: - At sea level (~14.7 psi / 0x78 Baro): ABCFBR outputs 0x00 (zero compensation). - At high altitude (e.g., Denver, CO / ~12.2 psi / 0x6D Baro): ABCFBR outputs a positive multiplier (0x04), activating the altitude timing reduction.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Table Values (Higher Baro Factor):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Retards total ignition timing more aggressively as elevation increases by pulling more timing away from ABCBAS.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Provides an extra safety margin against detonation when driving high in the mountains where intercoolers and radiators are less effective.
  • Decreasing Table Values (Lower Baro Factor):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Maintains higher ignition timing at altitude.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Restores mid-range punch and responsiveness at high elevation, but requires adequate fuel octane or effective intercooling to prevent high-altitude knock.
  • Setting to Zero (0x00 across table):
  • Disables barometric timing reduction completely; the ECU will enforce only the raw ABCBAS curve regardless of altitude.
