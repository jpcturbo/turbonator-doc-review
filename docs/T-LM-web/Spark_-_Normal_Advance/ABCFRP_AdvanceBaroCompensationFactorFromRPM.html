1. Overview & Purpose

ABCFRP_AdvanceBaroCompensationFactorFromRPM is the engine speed modifier for the barometric spark compensation system. While ABCFBR senses how high the vehicle is operating above sea level, ABCFRP determines at which engine speeds altitude-related ignition retard should be applied.

Turbocharged engines operating at altitude typically experience their highest thermal stress and knock vulnerability across the peak torque / mid-RPM band (typically 2,500 to 4,500 RPM) where cylinder pressures are highest. ABCFRP concentrates the barometric retard into this RPM range while tapering it off at high RPM.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  1. Active Under Elevation: ABCFRP is looked up continuously, but only produces an effect when the ambient barometric pressure factor (ABCFBR) is non-zero (i.e., operating above sea level).
  2. Evaluated Input: Engine speed high byte (EngineRpm_HB), shifted left by 1 for table lookup indexing.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Table Values (Higher RPM Factor):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Pulls more timing at the specified RPMs when operating at altitude.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Calms mid-range detonation tendencies when driving under load in mountain passes.
  • Decreasing Table Values (Lower RPM Factor):
  • Effect on Total Timing: Reduces altitude-induced timing retard across the specified RPM range.
  • Real-World Driving Feel: Restores mid-range torque and responsiveness at high elevation.
  • Tuning RPM Distribution:
  • By altering the RPM points in ABCFRP, tuners can selectively protect the engine in the spool-up/peak-torque window without compromising top-end power.
