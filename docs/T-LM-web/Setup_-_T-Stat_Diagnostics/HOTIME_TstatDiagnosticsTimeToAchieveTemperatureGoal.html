1. Overview & Purpose

HOTIME (TstatDiagnosticsTimeToAchieveTemperatureGoal) sets the maximum elapsed driving time window (stock calibration = 174 ticks / ~8 to 10 minutes) during which the engine must reach the thermostat diagnostic target temperature (TDAGTP).

If the vehicle is driven at road speed (> VehicleSpeed ≥ 7) and the coolant temperature remains below TDAGTP after HOTIME has elapsed, the ECU determines that coolant is freely bypassing a missing or stuck-open thermostat. The ECU immediately sets OBD Trouble Code 17 ('Engine operating temperature not reached'), alerting the technician to a cooling system efficiency defect.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine VerifyOperationOfThermostat_MM at line 849.
  • Compares the warmup timer Timer_ThermostatDiagnostics against HOTIME.
  • If elapsed time exceeds HOTIME without reaching TDAGTP, sets error flag.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Winter Warmup / High Flow Radiators: In cold winter climates or with massive aluminum racing radiators, warmup can take slightly longer. Increasing HOTIME gives the engine extra time to warm up before setting a trouble code.
  • Cooler Thermostat Compatibility: Pair with TDAGTP to prevent nuisance Check Engine lights on modified cooling setups.
