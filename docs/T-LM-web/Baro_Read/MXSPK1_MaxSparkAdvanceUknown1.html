1. Overview & Purpose

MXSPK1 (MaxSparkAdvanceUknown1) provides a hard physical ceiling on total ignition advance (stock calibration = 0x6E = 110 decimal / 110 × 0.5° = 55.0° BTDC) evaluated inside StoreDwell.

In distributor-equipped Chrysler 2.2L/2.5L engines, the distributor rotor sweeps across a physical brass cap terminal. If total spark advance exceeds ~55° to 60° BTDC, the rotor tip becomes physically closer to the adjacent cylinder's cap terminal than the target cylinder terminal. Firing the ignition coil beyond this mechanical limit causes cross-fire (spark jumping to the wrong cylinder), which can break ring lands or bend connecting rods. MXSPK1 clamps ignition timing to safe mechanical limits.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine StoreDwell at line 4804.
  • Active when vehicle speed is below MPHREF (low speed / decel conditions).
  • Loaded into Temp5 to establish the maximum dwell timing limit.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Mechanical Rotor Phase Limit: NEVER increase MXSPK1 above 56° BTDC on factory distributor systems. Doing so will cause distributor cap cross-firing, erratic misfires, and backfires under light cruising conditions.
  • Coil-on-Plug / Distributorless (DIS) Conversions: Only on custom distributorless ignition systems (which eliminate the physical rotor and cap) can spark ceilings be safely raised.
