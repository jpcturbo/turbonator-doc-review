1. Overview & Purpose

BRPML2 (BaroReadLowRPM18002) defines the minimum engine speed (stock calibration = 1,800 RPM / 0x1C20) required to execute an in-flight barometric pressure read.

Chrysler turbo vehicles utilize a single Manifold Absolute Pressure (MAP) sensor to measure both intake manifold boost/vacuum and ambient atmospheric barometric pressure. To measure barometric pressure while driving, the ECU momentarily energizes the Baro Solenoid, venting the MAP sensor port to ambient atmosphere. To ensure this pressure pulse does not disrupt engine fueling or cause a stumble, the ECU requires the engine to be running within a stable, moderate cruising RPM window between BRPML2 (1,800 RPM) and BRPMH (6,650 RPM).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine HandleBaroReadRequirement_MM at line 1704.
  • Compares 16-bit EngineRpm_HB register against BRPML2.
  • If EngineRpm < BRPML2 (bcs LCDAE), the baro reading cycle is aborted.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Setting: 0x1C20 (1,800 RPM).
  • Preventing Low-Speed Baro Cycling: Solenoid clicks during slow city driving can occasionally cause tiny manifold pressure dips. Keeping BRPML2 at or above 1,800 RPM ensures the baro solenoid only cycles during highway cruising where intake airflow momentum absorbs the momentary vent pulse seamlessly.
