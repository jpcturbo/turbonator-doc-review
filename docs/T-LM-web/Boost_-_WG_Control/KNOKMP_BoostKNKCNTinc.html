1. Overview & Purpose

KNOKMP_BoostKNKCNTinc sets the boost error threshold above barometric pressure required to increment the boost knock counter (KNKCNT).

Knock occurring under deep vacuum or cruise is handled strictly with spark retard. Knock occurring under significant boost (> KNOKMP) increments KNKCNT, signaling the boost control subsystem that boost pressure itself must be curtailed to protect the engine.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Boost Knock Integration: Evaluated in CalcWGDutyCycle_MM when knock is active.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Tuning Guidance: Preserves knock-triggered boost dump protection under load.
