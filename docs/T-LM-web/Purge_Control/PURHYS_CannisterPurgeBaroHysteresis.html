1. Overview & Purpose

PURHYS (CannisterPurgeBaroHysteresis) provides a 2-count (~0.4 inHg) hysteresis deadband for canister purge control.

When driving at light throttle near atmospheric pressure, manifold vacuum naturally fluctuates due to road bumps and subtle pedal adjustments. PURHYS prevents the evaporative purge solenoid from rapidly buzzing or cycling on and off as manifold pressure hovers around the purge threshold.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside ControlPurgeSolenoid_MM at line 1646.
  • Subtracted from BaroPressure when the purge solenoid is currently active.
  • Prevents solenoid rapid cycling.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x02.
  • Tuning Usage: Retain stock value. Ensures quiet, reliable canister purge valve operation.
