1. Overview & Purpose

CLDHLD_CurveAColdHoldTime specifies the duration that post-start enrichment (CURVEA) is held at 100% full strength before decay begins, following a cold engine start.

On freezing mornings, starting the decay of post-start fuel immediately after startup causes the engine to lean out and stumble as soon as the driver puts the car in gear. CLDHLD locks Curve A at full enrichment for a solid ~90 seconds to allow combustion stability to establish.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Condition: Engine started cold (CoolantTemp >= TMPHLD in inverse NTC A/D counts, i.e., colder than ~178°F).
  • Duration Formula:

Hold Time (Seconds) = CLDHLD × 0.704 seconds

128 × 0.704 ≈ 90.1 seconds.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing the Value: Holds full cold after-start enrichment longer (e.g., 2+ minutes). Helpful for extreme sub-zero winter climates or E85 conversions.
  • Decreasing the Value: Begins decaying cold enrichment sooner.
