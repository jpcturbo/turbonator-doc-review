1. Overview & Purpose

HOTHLD_CurveAHotHoldTime sets the duration that post-start enrichment (CURVEA) is held at full strength when restarting a warm/hot engine.

On a warm engine, holding post-start fuel for 90 seconds (like cold starts) would cause severe rich loading and exhaust odor. HOTHLD restricts the warm hold time to just ~15 seconds—just long enough to clear vapor bubbles from the fuel rail after hot soak.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Condition: Engine started hot (CoolantTemp < TMPHLD in inverse NTC A/D counts, i.e., warmer than ~178°F).
  • Duration Formula:

Hot Hold Time = HOTHLD × 0.704 seconds = 21 × 0.704 ≈ 14.8 seconds.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Value: Extends the hold window on warm starts, helping combat hot-soak vapor lock or lean surges on quick gas station restarts.
  • Decreasing Value: Reduces warm start hold time.
