1. Overview & Purpose

CURVEA_FuelColdEnrichmentCurveA is the initial post-start enrichment curve (often called "After-Start Enrichment" or ASE).

Immediately after an engine starts, the intake ports are wet with raw fuel but the combustion chamber surfaces and valve faces are still cold. The engine requires a temporary burst of heavy enrichment during the first 15 to 60 seconds of running to ensure smooth idle and clean initial throttle tip-in. CURVEA establishes the magnitude of this initial post-start enrichment as a function of coolant temperature, which is held for a set duration (CLDHLD / HOTHLD) before decaying away via DCYTMC or DCYTMH.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Trigger: Engaged immediately upon completion of the start-to-run transition.
  • Duration: Maintained during the hold time, then decays incrementally until its multiplier reaches zero.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Table Values (Richer Post-Start):
  • Delivers heavier enrichment during the first 30–60 seconds after startup.
  • Real-world feel: Fixes post-start idle stumble or stalling that occurs 5–10 seconds after a cold morning start.
  • Decreasing Table Values (Leaner Post-Start):
  • Leans out initial post-start fuel.
  • Real-world feel: Eliminates strong raw gasoline odor and black exhaust smoke immediately after warm restarts.
