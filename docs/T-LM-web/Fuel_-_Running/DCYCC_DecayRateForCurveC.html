1. Overview & Purpose

DCYCC_DecayRateForCurveC dictates the decay rate of intermediate warm-up enrichment (CURVEC).

After intermediate enrichment is held for the duration specified by CCURV, DCYCC controls the speed at which Curve C's enrichment multiplier ramps down to zero.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Condition: Active once KeyOnOrEngineRunningTime * 4 >= CCURV during phase 1 of DealWithColdLoadEnrichment_MM.
  • Decay Duration Formula:

Decay Duration (Seconds) ≈ (89.76 / DCYCC)

(89.76 / 13) ≈ 6.9 seconds.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing the Value: Causes intermediate warm-up enrichment to decay faster (shorter duration).
  • Decreasing the Value: Causes intermediate warm-up enrichment to decay slower (longer duration).
