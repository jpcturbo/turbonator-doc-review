1. Overview & Purpose

CURVEC_FuelColdEnrichmentCurveC is the intermediate warm-up enrichment curve in Chrysler's tripartite cold enrichment architecture.

While CURVEA provides high initial enrichment that fades quickly and CURVEB provides long-duration enrichment that tracks engine metal temperature, CURVEC fills the intermediate window. It provides supplementary fueling during the critical 1 to 3 minute driving window where Curve A has decayed but the engine oil, intake manifold, and combustion chambers have not yet absorbed enough heat to sustain pure Curve B operation.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Trigger: Engaged after start-to-run transfer while bit 1 of BitFlags_84 is clear.
  • Timing: Held for the duration specified by CCURV, then decayed at the rate set by DCYCC.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Table Values (Richer Intermediate Warmup):
  • Delivers extra fuel during the intermediate warm-up phase (1–3 minutes into driving).
  • Real-world feel: Fixes lean hesitations or stumbling that occur just as the vehicle drives out of the driveway on cold mornings.
  • Decreasing Table Values (Leaner Intermediate Warmup):
  • Leans out intermediate warmup fuel delivery.
