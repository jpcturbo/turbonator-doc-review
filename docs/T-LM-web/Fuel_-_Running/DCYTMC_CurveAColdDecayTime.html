1. Overview & Purpose

DCYTMC_CurveAColdDecayTime sets the decay rate of post-start enrichment (CURVEA) when the engine is started cold (coolant temperature below TMPHLD).

After the cold hold time (CLDHLD) expires, the ECU gradually steps down Curve A's enrichment factor to zero. DCYTMC dictates how quickly this ramp-down occurs.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Condition: Engine started cold (CoolantTemp >= TMPHLD in inverse NTC A/D counts, typically < 178°F).
  • Timing: Becomes active immediately after the cold hold timer (CLDHLD) reaches zero.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Decay Formula: Decay time in seconds is calculated as:

Decay Duration ≈ (89.76 / DCYTMC)

  • Increasing Value: Results in a faster decay rate (Curve A enrichment fades out more quickly).
  • Decreasing Value: Results in a slower decay rate (Curve A enrichment lingers longer).
