1. Overview & Purpose

CCURV_HoldTimeForCurveC specifies the duration that intermediate cold enrichment (CURVEC) is held at full strength before decay begins.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Condition: Evaluated inside DealWithColdLoadEnrichment_MM during phase 1 (while Curve C is active, prior to Curve A decay phase).
  • Hold Duration Formula:

Curve C Hold Time = CCURV × 0.704 seconds = 10 × 0.704 ≈ 7.04 seconds.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Tuning Tip: Increasing CCURV extends the initial dwell of intermediate enrichment, helping smooth cold-start transitions on vehicles with large aftermarket intake manifolds or cold aluminum cylinder heads.
