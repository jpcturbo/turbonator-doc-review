1. Overview & Purpose

DEFTMP (DefaultEngineTemp) defines the safe, middle-ground fallback coolant temperature (stock calibration = 0x95 / ~150°F / 65°C) used by the ECU whenever the coolant temperature sensor is unplugged or shorted (Code 22).

If a coolant sensor fails, the ECU cannot know if the engine is freezing cold or boiling hot. Assuming -40°F would flood the cylinders with extreme choke enrichment, while assuming 220°F would cause lean stalling on a cold morning. DEFTMP chooses a moderately warm ~150°F baseline that allows the vehicle to start, run, and be driven safely to a repair facility without catalytic converter damage or engine stalling.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Substituted inside CheckAndFilterCoolantTempSensor_MM at line 923.
  • Overwrites CoolantTemp whenever Code 22 is active.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x95 (~150°F).
  • Limp-Home Behavior: When DEFTMP is active, warm-up enrichment curves and closed-loop O2 timers operate under the assumption of a lukewarm engine.
