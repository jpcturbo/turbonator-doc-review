1. Overview & Purpose

DEFCHG (DefaultChargeTemp_Limpin) defines the fail-safe default air charge temperature (stock calibration = 0xAD / ~100°F / 38°C) substituted into ChargeTemp whenever the intake manifold charge temperature sensor circuit fails (Fault Code 23).

The ECU uses charge temperature to adjust air density calculations and spark timing advance (AIRTMP, ADVCHG). If the ACT sensor fails, defaulting to extreme cold (-40°F) would command dangerous timing advance under boost, while defaulting to boiling heat (200°F) would retard spark and hurt power needlessly. DEFCHG establishes a moderate ~100°F air temperature baseline that preserves safe combustion and normal drivability under boost.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Substituted inside subroutine CheckAndFilterChargeTempSensor_MM at line 885.
  • Active whenever Charge Temp ADC is outside [CHGLOW, CHGHIG].
  • Overwrites live ChargeTemp variable.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0xAD (~100°F).
  • Non-Intercooled vs Intercooled: On non-intercooled Turbo I cars with higher baseline manifold temperatures, DEFCHG is calibrated slightly warmer (0xAD / ~100°F). On front-mount intercooled builds, DEFCHG can remain stock.
