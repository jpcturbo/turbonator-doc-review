1. Overview & Purpose

MAPTRG_DeltaMapTriggerLevel establishes the minimum rate of manifold pressure increase (\DeltaMAP) required to fire a MAP Acceleration Enrichment Pump Shot.

When the throttle is snapped open, manifold absolute pressure spikes upward almost instantly. This sudden pressure jump causes fuel droplets in the intake runner to fall out of suspension, leading to a momentary lean stumble ("tip-in leanout") unless compensated immediately. If the instantaneous increase in manifold pressure exceeding the rolling average exceeds MAPTRG, the ECU fires an asynchronous extra fuel pulse (analogous to the accelerator pump on a carburetor).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Transient Detection:
  • In CalculateTransientFuelAddition_MM:
  • Instantaneous MapValue is compared with average manifold pressure (AVMAP).
  • If (MapValue - AVMAP) > MAPTRG: A MAP pump shot is triggered.
  • The trigger threshold MAPTRG is subtracted from the delta to compute the effective pump shot magnitude:

\DeltaMAP_effective = (MapValue - AVMAP) - MAPTRG

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x05 (≈ 0.58 psi jump).
  • Lowering MAPTRG (e.g., to 2 or 3 counts):
  • Makes the ECU highly sensitive to throttle movements, firing acceleration enrichment on smaller throttle nudges.
  • Eliminates tip-in hesitation, but if set too low, minor manifold pressure pulsations from aggressive camshafts will cause false pump shots and wasted fuel.
  • Raising MAPTRG:
  • Requires a more aggressive throttle stab before enrichment triggers.
