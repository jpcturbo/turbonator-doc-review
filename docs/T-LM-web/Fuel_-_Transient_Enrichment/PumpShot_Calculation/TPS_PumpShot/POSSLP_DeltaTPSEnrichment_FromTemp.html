1. Overview & Purpose

POSSLP_DeltaTPSEnrichment_FromTemp defines the temperature-dependent gain curve for TPS-based acceleration enrichment pump shots.

Whenever the throttle blade is opened rapidly (> POSTRG), the engine requires an instant squirt of fuel before the manifold pressure change can be measured. POSSLP scales the magnitude of this pump shot according to engine coolant temperature, delivering heavy enrichment on cold engines and tapering down to moderate enrichment at operating temperature.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Transient TPS Enrichment:
  • Active whenever \DeltaTPS > POSTRG.
  • Injected fuel pulse width is:

PW_TPS Pump = \DeltaTPS_effective × POSSLP(CoolantTemp)

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Increasing Table Values:
  • Cures instant off-idle hesitation or stumble when stabbing the throttle pedal.
  • Decreasing Table Values:
  • Crucial when upsizing fuel injectors (+40%, +52 lb/hr) to avoid flooding the engine on sudden throttle tip-in.
