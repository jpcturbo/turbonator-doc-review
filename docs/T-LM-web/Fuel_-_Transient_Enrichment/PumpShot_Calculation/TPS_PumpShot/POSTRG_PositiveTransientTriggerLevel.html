1. Overview & Purpose

POSTRG_PositiveTransientTriggerLevel sets the throttle position sensor delta trigger threshold (\DeltaTPS) required to fire a TPS Acceleration Enrichment Pump Shot.

While manifold pressure (MAPTRG) detects incoming air density changes, the throttle position sensor (TPS) detects the driver's physical foot movement before the air has even passed the throttle blade. POSTRG acts as the immediate, early-warning trigger: if the throttle blade moves open faster than POSTRG (0.08V), a TPS acceleration pump shot is fired instantly.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • TPS Transient Detection:
  • In CalculateTransientFuelAddition_MM:
  • Throttle voltage is compared with rolling average throttle voltage (AVTHR).
  • If (ThrottleVolts - AVTHR) > POSTRG: A TPS pump shot is initiated.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x04 (0.08 V throttle opening).
  • Lowering POSTRG:
  • Increases throttle hair-trigger response. If set too sensitive (e.g. 1 count / 0.02V), foot vibration over bumps will trigger unintended pump shots.
  • Raising POSTRG:
  • Dulls throttle sensitivity, requiring a deliberate press before TPS pump shot activates.
