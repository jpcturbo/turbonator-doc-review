1. Overview & Purpose

THRMAX_MaxDeltaTHR defines the maximum allowable \DeltaTPS clamp applied when calculating the volume of a TPS acceleration pump shot.

If the throttle is smashed wide open from idle (a 3.5V+ delta), an uncapped TPS calculation would inject an overwhelming flood of fuel. THRMAX clamps the effective throttle opening delta to 0x26 (0.76V), preventing throttle-stomp engine drowning.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • TPS Shot Clamping:
  • Clamps effective \DeltaTPS prior to multiplying with POSSLP.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Setting: 0x26 (0.76 V).
  • Scaling for Large Injectors:
  • When upgrading to large injectors (+40%, +52 lb/hr), reducing THRMAX prevents the car from bogging or stumbling rich when the throttle is kicked open aggressively from a dead stop.
