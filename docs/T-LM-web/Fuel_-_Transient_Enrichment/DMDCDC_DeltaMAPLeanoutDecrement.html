1. Overview & Purpose

DMDCDC_DeltaMAPLeanoutDecrement specifies the step rate at which deceleration leanout decays back to normal running fueling when throttle is re-applied.

When exiting deceleration, normal fueling must be restored faster than it entered to eliminate throttle re-application lag. DMDCDC (3 counts) is configured higher than DMDCIC (2 counts) to ensure rapid fueling recovery.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Decel Recovery:
  • Subtracted from the decel multiplier when the driver presses the gas pedal.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Setting: 0x03.
  • Tuning Guidance: Faster exit than entry ensures responsive tip-in coming off decel.
