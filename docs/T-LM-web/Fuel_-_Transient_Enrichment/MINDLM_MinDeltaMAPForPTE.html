1. Overview & Purpose

MINDLM_MinDeltaMAPForPTE specifies the minimum manifold pressure rate of change (\DeltaMAP) required to set the Part Throttle Enrichment (PTE) operating flag.

PTE is a continuous transient enrichment multiplier applied to injector pulse width during prolonged acceleration. To prevent PTE from engaging on minor throttle flutter or road vibration, MINDLM enforces a positive pressure delta threshold before the PTE logic can qualify for activation.
