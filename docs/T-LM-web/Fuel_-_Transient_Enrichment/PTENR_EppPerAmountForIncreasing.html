1. Overview & Purpose

PTENR_EppPerAmountForIncreasing sets the engine position pulse countdown timer governing the rate at which part-throttle transient enrichment ramps back down toward baseline fuel delivery.

Following a throttle tip-in event that triggers part-throttle transient enrichment (PTE), extra fuel is delivered to overcome manifold wall-wetting. PTENR determines how many distributor reference pulses must elapse between incremental decays of this transient enrichment multiplier.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Transient Enrichment Decay:
  • Evaluated during ADC and sensor scanning in A2DScan_MM.
  • Manages the decay timing counter for open-loop transient enrichment.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Setting: 0x64 (100 pulses).
  • Increasing PTENR:
  • Keeps transient enrichment active for more engine cycles, providing a richer, longer fuel tail following acceleration tip-in.
  • Helps prevent lean hesitation on heavy vehicles or setups with large intake plenum volumes.
  • Decreasing PTENR:
  • Causes transient fuel to decay away rapidly, improving fuel economy and crispness on light cars.
