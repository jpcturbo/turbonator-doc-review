1. Overview & Purpose

MNDCRP_MinRPMforDecelLeanout defines the absolute minimum engine speed threshold below which Deceleration Fuel Leanout is aborted.

When the driver lifts off the throttle to decelerate, the ECU leans out the fuel mixture to conserve fuel and prevent catalytic converter overheating. However, as engine speed falls near idle, deceleration leanout must disengage immediately to prevent engine stalling. MNDCRP serves as this anti-stall floor.
