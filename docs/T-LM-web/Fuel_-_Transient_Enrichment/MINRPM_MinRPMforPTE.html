1. Overview & Purpose

MINRPM_MinRPMforPTE sets the minimum engine speed required to activate Part Throttle Enrichment.

At low idle speeds (under 1120 RPM), tip-in fuel delivery is managed entirely by the discrete MAP and TPS pump shots (AESLOP / POSSLP). Engaging the sustained PTE multiplier at idle speeds would cause engine loading down and rough idling. MINRPM locks PTE out below 1120 RPM.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • PTE Engine Speed Gate:
  • In SetFlagForPTE_MM: If EngineRpm < MINRPM, the PTE flag cannot be set.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Value: 0x23 (1120 RPM).
  • Tuning Guidance: If your car has a high-stall torque converter or aggressive camshaft that idles around 1100 RPM, raise MINRPM slightly (e.g., to 1250 RPM) so off-idle blips don't trip PTE prematurely.
