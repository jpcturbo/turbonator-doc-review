1. Overview & Purpose

REVLMTX (StagingModFuelShutoff) defines the primary engine speed setpoint for the Turbonator 2-step staging launch limiter.

When sitting stationary at the starting tree with the staging switch activated (e.g. clutch pedal depressed or launch button held), the ECU compares distributor period against REVLMTX. When engine RPM reaches this threshold, the ECU cuts injector fuel pulses. This holds engine speed perfectly steady at the calibrated launch RPM (e.g. 3,300 - 3,800 RPM), allowing the exhaust pulses and ignition anti-lag retard to build several pounds of launch boost on the starting line before the clutch is released.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine ControlEngineCutout_MM at line 6469.
  • Active when cfg_StageLim is set, vehicle speed is below RVLSPS, and the selected staging switch is closed.
  • Cuts fuel injector pulses when engine period drops below REVLMTX.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Launch Boost vs. Traction: Calibrate REVLMTX based on tire compound and track prep. Street radial tires typically launch best around 3,200 - 3,500 RPM to avoid excessive wheel hop. Drag slicks on a prepped track can launch at 4,000 - 4,500 RPM for maximum boost off the line.
  • Anti-Lag Retard Synergy: When the staging limiter is active, Turbonator code triggers the anti-lag timing retard curve to spool the turbocharger rapidly on the 2-step.
