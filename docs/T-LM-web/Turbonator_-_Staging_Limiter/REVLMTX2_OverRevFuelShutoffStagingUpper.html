1. Overview & Purpose

REVLMTX2 (StagingModFuelShutoff) establishes the secondary hysteresis recovery threshold for the Turbonator 2-step staging limiter.

To prevent the engine from violently misfiring or jerking at a single knife-edge RPM, the fuel shutoff routine implements a dual-threshold hysteresis window. REVLMTX defines where fuel cut initiates, and REVLMTX2 (located at offset +2 from REVLMTX) defines the recovery period where fuel resumes. This hysteresis deadband smooths out the 2-step limiter sound and maintains stable cylinder temperatures on the starting line.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine ControlEngineCutout_MM at line 6478.
  • Accessed as indexed offset 0x02,x from REVLMTX pointer.
  • Controls the fuel-resume threshold of the staging rev limiter.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Limiter Cadence: Spacing REVLMTX and REVLMTX2 by roughly 150-200 RPM creates a rapid, machine-gun stutter on the 2-step, ideal for building consistent manifold boost.
  • Synchronized Tuning: Always adjust REVLMTX2 proportionally whenever REVLMTX is moved to a new launch RPM.
