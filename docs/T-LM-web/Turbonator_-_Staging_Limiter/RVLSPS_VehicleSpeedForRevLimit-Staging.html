1. Overview & Purpose

RVLSPS (Staging) defines the vehicle speed cutoff threshold for the Turbonator 2-step staging launch rev limiter.

When staging at the drag strip starting line, the driver wants an aggressive low-RPM rev limit (e.g. 3,300 RPM) to spool the turbocharger against the brakes without over-revving. However, the moment the vehicle launches and rolls off the starting line, normal high-RPM power is required immediately. RVLSPS sets the road speed threshold (stock = 4 MPH): while vehicle speed is below RVLSPS, the staging limiter remains active; once the car exceeds this speed, the ECU automatically hands over to the primary high-RPM engine rev limiter (REVLMT).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside subroutine ControlEngineCutout_MM at line 6455.
  • Active when the 2-step staging feature flag (cfg_StageLim) is enabled in configuration flags.
  • Works in conjunction with staging switch selector RVLSWS.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Drag Launch Optimization: Stock value of 4 MPH (0x04) works exceptionally well for both slicks and street tires, ensuring the limiter does not cut fuel down-track if wheelspin occurs.
  • Speed Sensor Prerequisite: Requires a functioning vehicle distance/speed sensor (VSS). If the speed sensor is disconnected or broken, the ECU will remain locked in the staging limiter!
