1. Overview & Purpose

AIRTMP_FuelEnrichmentFromChargeTemp provides the intake air / charge temperature air density fuel compensation curve.

Air density is inversely proportional to temperature (Charles's Law): colder intake air is denser and contains more oxygen molecules per unit volume, requiring proportionally more fuel mass to maintain the targeted air/fuel ratio. Conversely, hot intake air (common after hard turbo boost pulls or during hot summer idling) is less dense and requires less fuel. AIRTMP dynamically scales fuel injector pulse width based on the thermistor reading in the intake manifold.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Continuous Fuel Calculation:
  • Active on every pass of CalculateSumOfAllFuelModifiers_MM.
  • Uses raw charge temperature sensor voltage (ChargeTempVolts).
  • Inverted NTC thermistor scaling: high raw voltage represents freezing cold intake air (high enrichment), while low raw voltage represents hot intake air (lower fuel factor).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Weather Protection:
  • In sub-freezing winter weather, AIRTMP adds up to +25% fuel to prevent dangerously lean mixtures under boost.
  • Hot Heat-Soak Protection:
  • Prevents over-fueling and bogging when intake air temperatures rise after sitting in traffic.
