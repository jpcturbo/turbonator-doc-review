1. Overview & Purpose

BAROCP_BaroCompensationOfWarmupFactor_FromBaro scales cold warmup fuel enrichment based on ambient atmospheric pressure (altitude).

At high elevations, reduced ambient barometric pressure decreases overall air density. In cold weather at high altitudes, applying full sea-level cold enrichment would over-fuel the engine and cause rich cold-start stalling. BAROCP reduces the cold warmup enrichment multiplier at lower barometric pressures.
