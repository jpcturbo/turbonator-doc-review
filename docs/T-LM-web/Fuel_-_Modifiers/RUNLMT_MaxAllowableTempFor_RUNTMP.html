1. Overview & Purpose

RUNLMT_MaxAllowableTempFor_RUNTMP defines the maximum baseline coolant temperature below which heat soak addition (RUNTMP) is permitted.

Heat soak modeling is only relevant when an engine stalls while warming up. If the engine is already at operating temperature, heat soak calculations are bypassed to prevent artificial temperature inflation.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Heat Soak Gate:
  • If CoolantTemp > RUNLMT, heat soak stall modification is bypassed.
