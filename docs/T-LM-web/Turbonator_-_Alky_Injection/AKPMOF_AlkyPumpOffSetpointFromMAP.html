1. Overview & Purpose

AKPMOF (AlkyPumpOffSetpointFromMAP) establishes the shut-off MAP threshold for the water/methanol injection pump.

Once the water/methanol pump has been energized at AKPMON, AKPMOF creates a pressure hysteresis cushion. As the driver lifts off the throttle and boost falls below AKPMOF, the pump driver de-energizes. This hysteresis deadband prevents the pump relay from buzzing or cycling rapidly if manifold pressure fluctuates near the trigger point.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside AlkyPump_Control_MM at line 7394.
  • Checked when current MAP is lower than AKPMON.
  • De-energizes pump output driver when MAP drops below AKPMOF.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hysteresis Calibration: Set AKPMOF approximately 1.5 - 2.0 psi below AKPMON. For example, if the pump turns on at 5 psi boost, set it to turn off at 3 psi boost.
  • Smooth Cutoff: Prevents sudden pump shutdown on momentary boost fluctuations during gear shifts.
