1. Overview & Purpose

AKI1OF (AlkyInj1OffSetpointFromMAP) specifies the MAP threshold where the Stage 1 water/methanol solenoid shuts off as manifold pressure drops.

Provides hysteresis cutoff protection for Stage 1 water/methanol injection, ensuring the nozzle snaps closed cleanly when throttle lifts occur.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside AlkyInjector_Control_MM at line 7430.
  • Active when Stage 1 injection is currently firing.
  • De-energizes Stage 1 solenoid when MAP drops below AKI1OF.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Staggering: Calibrate AKI1OF approximately 1.5 - 2.0 psi below AKI1ON. Ensures clean cutoff without premature shutoff during boost ramping.
