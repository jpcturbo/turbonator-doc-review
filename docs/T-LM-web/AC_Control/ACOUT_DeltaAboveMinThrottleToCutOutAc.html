1. Overview & Purpose

ACOUT (DeltaAboveMinThrottleToCutOutAc) is the Wide Open Throttle (WOT) A/C cutout threshold (stock calibration = 133 counts / ~2.60V above learned idle MINTHR).

When the driver pins the throttle to accelerate, pass a vehicle, or launch at the drag strip, the ECU temporarily disengages the A/C compressor clutch. This unloads 5 to 10 parasitic horsepower from the crankshaft, delivering 100% of engine power to the front wheels. Once the accelerator pedal is released back below ACOUT, the A/C clutch re-engages smoothly.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside ControlAirConditioning_MM at line 1778.
  • Subtracts MINTHR_LowestSessionTPS from TpsVolts.
  • If DeltaTPS >= ACOUT (bcc LCDEE), the A/C clutch relay is commanded OFF.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Passing Power: Stock value 0x85 cuts the A/C at ~75% throttle. Lowering ACOUT to 0x60 cuts the A/C sooner (at ~50% throttle), giving a sportier, more responsive feel during mid-throttle passing maneuvers.
