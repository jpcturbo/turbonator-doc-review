1. Overview & Purpose

ACCRPM (DoNotAllowAcToEngageAboveThisRPM_4000) provides critical mechanical over-speed protection for the automotive air conditioning compressor (stock calibration = 0x3E80 = 4,000 RPM).

Automotive reciprocating piston and rotary vane A/C compressors are designed for maximum operating speeds of ~4,000 to 5,000 engine RPM. Running an A/C compressor at 6,000 RPM causes extreme piston velocities, reed valve flutter, and catastrophic clutch bearing destruction. If engine speed exceeds ACCRPM, the ECU disengages the A/C compressor clutch immediately, re-engaging it only when engine speed falls back below 4,000 RPM.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Evaluated inside ControlAirConditioning_MM at line 1781.
  • Compares 16-bit EngineRpm_HB register against ACCRPM.
  • If EngineRpm > ACCRPM (bhi LCDF2), de-energizes the A/C clutch relay.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Compressor Longevity: Never raise ACCRPM above 4,500 RPM. Spinning a factory Sanden or Nippondenso compressor beyond 4,500 RPM frequently leads to thrown compressor belts, seized bearings, or blown shaft seals.
