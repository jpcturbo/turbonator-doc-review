1. Overview & Purpose

SBNRRP_BankScalingFromRPM provides the engine speed weighting curve for Selective Bank Fuel Enrichment.

Intake runner resonance and distribution biases change with engine RPM. SBNRRP modulates the selective bank trim across the RPM band, peaking where runner acoustic imbalances are greatest.
