1. Overview & Purpose

SBNRBS_BankSelect defines which physical injector driver bank receives the selective bank fuel enrichment pulse.

In the 6803 hardware architecture, fuel injection is driven by two physical output driver transistors: * 0x80: Injector Bank 1 (Cylinders 1 & 2) * 0x40: Injector Bank 2 (Cylinders 3 & 4)

In the factory calibration, SBNRBS is set to 0x40, directing the supplemental enrichment to Cylinders 3 & 4 to compensate for lean flow into the rear of the stock intake manifold.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Injector Firing Routine:
  • During injector interrupt scheduling in IncludeSelectiveBankEnrichment:
  • The ECU tests which bank is currently scheduled to fire.
  • If the active bank matches SBNRBS, the supplemental bank enrichment pulse width is added to the firing timer.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Factory Setting: 0x40 (Bank 3-4).
  • Valid Settings:
  • 0x40: Enriches Bank 2 (Cylinders 3 & 4).
  • 0x80: Enriches Bank 1 (Cylinders 1 & 2).
  • 0x00: Disables selective bank enrichment completely.
  • Equal Length Manifolds: If running an aftermarket equal-length intake manifold, setting SBNRBS = 0x00 completely disables bank differential fueling, ensuring identical injector pulse widths on all 4 cylinders.
