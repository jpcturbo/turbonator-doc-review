1. Overview & Purpose

SBNRMP_BankScalingFromMAP provides the manifold pressure scaling curve for Chrysler's Selective Bank Fuel Enrichment feature.

The factory Chrysler 2.2L / 2.5L 8-valve single-plenum intake manifold has asymmetrical runner lengths and uneven plenum distribution: Cylinders 1 & 2 breathe differently than Cylinders 3 & 4 at specific airflow rates. Because the ECU fires injectors in two paired banks (Bank 1: Cylinders 1 & 2; Bank 2: Cylinders 3 & 4), the firmware includes selective bank enrichment to deliver extra fuel to the leaner bank. SBNRMP scales this trim based on manifold pressure.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Bank Fuel Delivery Adjustment:
  • In CalculateSelectiveBankEnrichment:
  • Looked up vs. MapValue and multiplied by the RPM bank curve (SBNRRP).
  • Applied exclusively to the injector bank selected by SBNRBS.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stock Calibration: Compensates for factory log and two-piece intake manifold distribution biases.
  • Custom Aftermarket Manifolds:
  • If installing an aftermarket custom intake manifold with equal-length runners or an upgraded center-feed plenum, the factory distribution asymmetry is eliminated!
  • Critical Tuning Action: When running an aftermarket equal-length intake manifold, zero out SBNRMP (or set SBNRBS to neutral) to ensure both injector banks deliver perfectly matched fuel delivery.
