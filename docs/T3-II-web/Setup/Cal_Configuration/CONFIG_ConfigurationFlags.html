1. Overview & Purpose

CONFIG serves as the master factory vehicle hardware configuration register for the engine controller. Configured in factory calibrations to one hundred sixty-two counts, CONFIG uses individual binary bits to declare the presence or absence of core vehicle hardware systems. These include whether the vehicle is equipped with air conditioning, exhaust gas recirculation, an automatic or manual transmission, an intake manifold air charge temperature sensor, a part throttle unlock solenoid, and throttle-position-based transient fueling. By reading CONFIG during initialization and main control loops, the controller enables or disables entire branches of control logic and diagnostic error tracking to match the physical powertrain hardware.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Automatic to Manual Drivetrain Swaps: * Clearing the automatic transmission switch (Bit 5) transitions the controller from automatic to manual idle dashpot decay rates, park/neutral switch logic, and target idle speed curves.
  • Air Conditioning & EGR Deletion: * Clearing the air conditioning switch (Bit 7) or exhaust gas recirculation switch (Bit 6) disables diagnostic circuit testing and suppresses fault codes 32 and 33 when emissions or comfort equipment is removed.
  • Charge Temperature Sensor Bypass: * Clearing the charge temperature sensor switch (Bit 4) directs the firmware to substitute engine coolant temperature directly into intake charge fueling equations if no intake air thermistor is installed.
  • Transient Fuel TPS Enable: * Bit 0 toggles throttle-based transient fuel enrichment modifiers to tailor tip-in response.
