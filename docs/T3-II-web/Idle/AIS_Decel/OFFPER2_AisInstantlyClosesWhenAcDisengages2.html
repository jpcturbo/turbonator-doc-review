1. Overview & Purpose

OFFPER2 defines the secondary percentage proportion of the off-idle air conditioning airflow adder ACCTBL that is retracted during alternate clutch disengagement transitions. When the air conditioning system cycles off in the secondary branch, a different residual airflow cushion may be required than in the primary branch. OFFPER2 establishes this alternate percentage multiplier, scaling the amount of ACCTBL air that is subtracted from the idle stepper motor position. In factory calibrations, OFFPER2 is set to 64 counts, commanding a modest retraction of exactly 25 percent (64/256) of the ACCTBL airflow adder, leaving 75 percent of the air cushion intact to decay away smoothly.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Secondary Branch Air Retraction: * Adjust OFFPER2 to fine-tune deceleration air retraction in the secondary A/C disengagement sequence.
  • Preserving Gentle Decay: * Factory setting of 64 counts (25%) leaves 75% of the A/C air cushion intact, creating a very gradual ramp down.
  • Percentage Scalar: * 255 counts represents 100%; factory setting of 64 counts represents 25%.
