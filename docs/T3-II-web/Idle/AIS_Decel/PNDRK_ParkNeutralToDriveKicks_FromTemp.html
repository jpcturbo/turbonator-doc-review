1. Overview & Purpose

PNDRK is a 2D calibration curve that establishes the pre-emptive opening steps added to the idle air stepper motor when shifting an automatic transmission from Park or Neutral into Drive. When the transmission shifts into gear, the hydraulic torque converter immediately places a heavy mechanical drag on the crankshaft. If the engine controller relied purely on closed-loop feedback to catch this load, engine speed would sag significantly before the stepper motor could respond. PNDRK injects a rapid, pre-emptive kick opening into the stepper motor as a function of coolant temperature. In factory calibrations, PNDRK commands a large opening kick during cold warm-up (approximately 20 to 25 steps) that tapers down to 4 to 6 steps once the engine and transmission fluid are fully warmed up.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Curing In-Gear Shudder & Stalls: * Increase PNDRK step values by 4 to 8 steps if shifting from Park/Neutral into Drive causes RPM to plunge or stumble.
  • Preventing In-Gear Vehicle Surge: * If the car surges forward or pulls hard against the brakes upon gear engagement, reduce PNDRK step points.
  • High-Stall Aftermarket Converters: * Looser converters exhibit lower fluid coupling drag at idle, allowing smaller PNDRK opening kicks.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect at idle when shifting an automatic transmission from Park/Neutral into Drive or Reverse, pre-emptively opening the idle air motor to catch driveline drag.
