1. Overview & Purpose

ACERPM defines the engine speed threshold above which part-throttle air conditioning compressor cutout is terminated, allowing the compressor clutch to re-engage. Part-throttle cutout is meant to assist low-RPM engine torque during the initial phase of acceleration. Once the engine has revved past its mid-range torque peak, engine horsepower output is plentiful and turbocharger boost is fully spooled. At higher engine speeds, compressor parasitic load represents a negligible fraction of total engine output. If engine speed rises above ACERPM while part-throttle cutout is active, the controller immediately clears the cutout timer and re-engages the compressor. In factory calibrations, ACERPM is set to 68 counts, corresponding to an engine speed threshold of 2176 RPM (nominally 2200 RPM).

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Extending Mid-Range Boost: * Raise ACERPM to 3000 to 3500 RPM to sustain compressor cutout through the entire mid-range acceleration pull.
  • Belt Shock Prevention: * Keep ACERPM below 4500 RPM to avoid harsh high-speed clutch engagement that can glaze or squeal the accessory drive belt.
  • Factory Baseline: * 68 counts corresponds to 2176 RPM (32 RPM per count on the 8-bit scale).

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during part-throttle driving with the A/C on, allowing compressor re-engagement once engine RPM climbs above this threshold.
