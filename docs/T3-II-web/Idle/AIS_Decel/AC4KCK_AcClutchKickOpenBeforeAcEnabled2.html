1. Overview & Purpose

AC4KCK defines the secondary stepper motor opening kick offset applied before air conditioning clutch engagement under elevated intake manifold pressure conditions. If the engine is operating under higher manifold pressure (above the safety vacuum lockout threshold DCLMN2) when the A/C clutch engagement sequence begins, intake manifold density is already elevated. Under these conditions, applying the standard sea-level kick opening would introduce too much air, causing an unwanted idle surge. AC4KCK supplies a signed corrective offset that fine-tunes the pre-engagement stepper position for elevated manifold pressure conditions. In factory calibrations, AC4KCK is set to 250 counts, representing a signed negative trim of -6 steps.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Elevated MAP Surge Suppression: * Adjust AC4KCK to suppress idle surging when the A/C compressor cycles on while manifold pressure is elevated.
  • Signed Two's Complement: * Stored as a signed byte (250 counts = -6 steps).
  • Gated by DCLMN2: * Only applies when manifold absolute pressure is above DCLMN2.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect at closed throttle just before the A/C clutch engages under elevated intake manifold pressure (low vacuum), fine-tuning the pre-engagement idle air kick.
