1. Overview & Purpose

CRSCN2 defines the speed error deadband threshold for the cable adaptive delta variable. Road surfaces are never perfectly flat; tire expansion, pavement seams, and minor wind gusts cause momentary road speed variations of 1 to 2 miles per hour. If the cruise controller reacted to every minor speed fluctuation, the throttle cable would constantly twitch and the vacuum solenoids would experience excessive wear. CRSCN2 establishes a deadband of 6 counts (representing approximately 3.0 miles per hour of speed error), suppressing adaptive servo updates when speed remains within this acceptable margin.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Error Deadband Filtering: * Filters out road surface noise, tire expansion, and wind gusts below 3.0 mph. * Prevents rapid solenoid cycling and throttle cable flutter during steady highway driving.
  • Precision vs. Drivability: * Lowering to 3 or 4 counts tightens speed holding on flat interstates. * Raising to 8 counts provides a more relaxed, fuel-efficient cruise profile.
  • Factory Setting: * Set to 6 counts (≈ 3.0 mph) in production calibrations.
