1. Overview & Purpose

MINSET defines the minimum vehicle road speed threshold required for cruise control engagement. Cruise control is engineered strictly for open highway and rural driving. Engaging cruise control in congested city traffic, residential neighborhoods, or school zones represents a significant safety hazard. In factory calibrations, MINSET is configured to 70 counts (representing 35 miles per hour in stock 2.5L calibrations, or 60 counts / 30 miles per hour in Stage IV calibrations). If the driver attempts to engage cruise control below this threshold, the engine controller locks out engagement and clears the saved setpoint.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Secondary Road Engagement: * Lowering MINSET from 70 counts (35 mph) to 60 counts (30 mph) permits cruise operation on slower country roads.
  • City Driving Safety Lockout: * Keeps cruise control locked out in low-speed urban environments to ensure driver remains in manual control.
  • Zeroing on Violation: * If setpoint is below MINSET, register B is cleared to zero at line 8644 and stored to CruiseSpeedSetpoint, locking out engagement.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect when the driver presses the cruise control SET button, enforcing the minimum low-speed cutoff threshold for cruise engagement.
