1. Overview & Purpose

SPGAIN establishes the vehicle road speed gain correction factor that scales cruise servo responsiveness as a function of road speed. Aerodynamic drag increases with the square of vehicle velocity: at 35 mph, a minor throttle adjustment produces a noticeable change in vehicle speed, whereas at 75 mph, substantial throttle movement is required to counter high wind resistance. SPGAIN implements speed-dependent gain scheduling, scaling vacuum servo pulse authority upward at higher speeds and downward at lower speeds to deliver uniform, predictable cruise holding behavior from 35 mph to 85 mph.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • High-Speed Holding Authority: * Aerodynamic resistance increases exponentially with speed; increase SPGAIN above 65 mph if highway cruising feels weak or lags on gentle inclines.
  • Low-Speed Surge Prevention: * If cruise pulses or surges rhythmically at 35 to 40 mph, reduce the lower speed points of SPGAIN to soften low-speed servo authority.
  • Combined Gain Output: * The looked-up SPGAIN value is added to Temp4 (from THGAIN) to establish final servo scaling multiplier Temp3.
