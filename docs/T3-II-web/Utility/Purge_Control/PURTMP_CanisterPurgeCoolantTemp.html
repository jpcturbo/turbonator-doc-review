1. Overview & Purpose

PURTMP defines the minimum engine coolant temperature required to enable evaporative canister purge operation. When an engine is warming up from a cold start, fuel atomization is inefficient, combustion chamber walls are cold, and the engine operates in open-loop fuel enrichment. Introducing unmetered raw hydrocarbon vapors from the carbon canister into a cold intake manifold destabilizes warmup fueling, causing rough idle, spark plug fouling, or engine stalling. In factory calibrations, PURTMP is configured to 130 counts, corresponding to approximately 130 °F. Until engine coolant reaches this temperature, the controller holds the canister purge solenoid closed.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Warmup Idle Stabilization: * Raise PURTMP to 160 °F to 170 °F on big-cam or large-injector setups to prevent vapor-induced idle hunting during warmup.
  • Cold Start Protection: * Do not lower PURTMP below 120 °F to avoid rich stumbling before closed-loop oxygen sensor feedback is active.
  • Emissions Compliance: * Factory 130 °F setting achieves early canister purge without compromising cold drivability.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect when the engine is warm under moderate intake manifold vacuum, energizing the evaporative canister purge solenoid to draw fuel vapors into the intake.
