1. Overview & Purpose

HIGHMP defines the temperature-dependent gain curve for cold engine high-load enrichment at closed throttle and idle. When an engine is cold, intake manifold and cylinder head metal surfaces cause injected fuel to puddle rather than vaporizing into the airflow. If an engine load is applied while cold (such as engaging drive on an automatic transmission or steering against power steering lock at idle), extra fuel is required to prevent an engine stall. HIGHMP scales cold enrichment as a function of coolant temperature, multiplying against manifold pressure factor HMPMUL to provide load-proportional enrichment.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Idle Load Compensation: * If an automatic vehicle bogs or stalls when shifting into Drive or Reverse while cold, increase HIGHMP at that coolant temperature.
  • Aftermarket Injector Scaling: * Scale down proportionally with injector flow increases to prevent spark plug fouling during cold in-gear idling.
  • Closed-Throttle Gating: * Active only at closed throttle; when the throttle is opened off-idle, the ECU automatically switches to HIGHM2.
  • Mathematical Pairing: * Multiplied directly with HMPMUL and divided by 32 in foreground fuel modifier math.
