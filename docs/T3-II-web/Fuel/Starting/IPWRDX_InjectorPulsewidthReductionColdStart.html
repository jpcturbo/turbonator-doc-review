1. Overview & Purpose

IPWRDX: IPWRDX (InjectorPulsewidthReductionColdStart) calibrates post-crank cold start fuel enrichment decay timers, loop counters, and percentage multipliers. Immediately after the engine fires and transitions from cranking to running, extra fuel must be delivered for a brief duration to wet cold intake runners and establish stable combustion before standard coolant warmup tables take over.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Calibration Strategy: Raising the values increases the duration or volume of post-start fuel enrichment, eliminating cold-start lean stumble. Lowering the values prevents spark plug fouling on large fuel injectors during cold winter starts.
  • Raising the Value: Increases commanded threshold or extends gating limits in the controller.
  • Lowering the Value: Decreases commanded threshold or restricts operational bounds.
