1. Overview & Purpose

HYTRPM provides extra engine speed hysteresis added to the idle cell qualification threshold once the engine is already operating inside Cell 12. If the engine is currently in Cell 12, the controller adds HYTRPM on top of IDLOFS before comparing actual engine speed against the ceiling. In factory calibrations, HYTRPM is set to 2 counts, providing an additional 64 RPM of headroom. This prevents electrical loads (such as radiator cooling fan engagement or air conditioning compressor cycling) from momentarily kicking the engine out of Cell 12 during minor idle flares.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Idle Cell Retention: * Adds extra RPM tolerance while already operating in Cell 12 to absorb accessory load surges.
  • Preventing Hunting: * Higher values (3-4 counts / 96-128 RPM) prevent lopey performance cams from repeatedly toggling between Cell 12 and Cell 0.
  • Lowering Hysteresis: * Makes the exit from Cell 12 sharper upon throttle tip-in, but setting too low causes idle oscillation.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)
