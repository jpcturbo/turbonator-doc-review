1. Overview & Purpose

NEGSL2: NEGSL2 (DeltaMAPLeanout_FromTemp) calibrates decel fuel lean-out factors, delta-MAP decay slopes, and time constants applied when the throttle plate snaps shut. During sudden deceleration, excess fuel pooling on intake runner walls evaporates rapidly due to rising manifold vacuum; this table reduces fuel pulsewidths to prevent rich stumble and emissions spikes.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Calibration Strategy: Raising values leans out fuel more aggressively on throttle lift, maximizing engine braking and fuel economy. Lowering values keeps fueling slightly richer on lift-off, smoothing driveline snatch and enabling subtle exhaust burble.
  • Raising the Value: Increases commanded threshold or extends gating limits in the controller.
  • Lowering the Value: Decreases commanded threshold or restricts operational bounds.
