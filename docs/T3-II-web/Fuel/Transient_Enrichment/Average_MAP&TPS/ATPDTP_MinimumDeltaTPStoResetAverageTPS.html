1. Overview & Purpose

ATPDTP: ATPDTP (MinimumDeltaTPStoResetAverageTPS) defines filter thresholds, delta-MAP/TPS triggers, and enrichment durations for the speed-density acceleration enrichment (pump shot) subsystem. When manifold pressure rises rapidly before manifold airflow reaches equilibrium, this parameter triggers an instantaneous fuel injector pulse to maintain stoichiometric or rich combustion.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Calibration Strategy: Raising trigger thresholds makes acceleration enrichment less sensitive to minor throttle flutter; lowering thresholds makes the pump shot fire on smaller throttle movements. Tune carefully to eliminate tip-in flat spots.
  • Raising the Value: Increases commanded threshold or extends gating limits in the controller.
  • Lowering the Value: Decreases commanded threshold or restricts operational bounds.
