1. Overview & Purpose

DCLRPH defines the high engine speed delta above target idle speed required to arm the decel fuel cut flag (Turbonator_DecelFuelCut). When the driver lifts off the throttle and all entry criteria are satisfied, the difference between actual engine RPM and target idle RPM must exceed DCLRPH before the controller will arm fuel shutoff. This threshold prevents the engine controller from cutting fuel during low-speed crawling in traffic or parking lot maneuvering. In factory calibrations, DCLRPH is set to 17 counts, representing 544 RPM above target idle speed.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stop-and-Go Drivability: * Raise DCLRPH if the engine cuts fuel annoyingly when creeping along in first or second gear in traffic.
  • RPM Delta Scaling: * Each count represents 32 RPM above target idle (17 counts = 544 RPM above idle; on an 800 RPM idle, arms at 1344 RPM).
  • Hysteresis Rules: * Ensure DCLRPH remains at least 2 to 4 counts higher than DCLRPL to prevent hunting.
  • Arming vs Fuel Cut: * DCLRPH arms the Turbonator_DecelFuelCut flag; actual injector shutoff is governed downstream by DFRPMH.
