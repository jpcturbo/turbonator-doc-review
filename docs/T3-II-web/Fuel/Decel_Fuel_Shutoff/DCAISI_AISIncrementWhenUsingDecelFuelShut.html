1. Overview & Purpose

DCAISI defines the Automatic Idle Speed (AIS) stepper motor position increment added when disarming decel fuel shutoff. When engine RPM falls below DCLRPL and fuel injection is about to be restored, the sudden transition from fuel cut to combustion can produce an engine speed undershoot or stall if intake airflow is too low. DCAISI commands the idle air stepper motor to step open by a calibrated increment, providing a dashpot air cushion that catches falling engine speed and stabilizes idle recovery. In factory calibrations, DCAISI is configured to 0 steps.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Lightweight Flywheel Stall Fix: * Increase DCAISI to 2 to 6 steps if a lightweight flywheel causes rapid RPM drop and stalling upon clutch-in.
  • Dashpot Air Cushioning: * Acts as a predictive electronic dashpot, supplying extra air bypass before closed-loop idle governing takes over.
  • High Setting Idle Hang: * If engine speed hangs above 1200 RPM when stopping, reduce DCAISI toward zero.
  • Disarming Trigger: * Injected at the exact moment engine speed drops below DCLRPL.
