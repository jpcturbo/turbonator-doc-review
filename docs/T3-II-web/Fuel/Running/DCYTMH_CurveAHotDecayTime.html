1. Overview & Purpose

DCYTMH defines the decay rate divisor for after-start enrichment (Curve A) following a hot restart. After hot hold duration (HOTHLD) expires, DCYTMH governs how quickly the remaining hot restart fuel enrichment tapers to zero. In factory calibrations, DCYTMH is calibrated to 1 count, producing a very gradual, smooth taper. This gentle decay ensures that hot engine restarts remain smooth and stable without abrupt lean transitions as the fuel rail stabilizes.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hot Decay Rate Formula: * Decay duration in seconds equals approximately 89.76 divided by calibration counts (1 count = ~89.8 seconds maximum taper).
  • Large Injector Tuning: * Increase DCYTMH to 4-9 counts on large injector setups to eliminate prolonged rich hot idling.
  • Lean Surge Prevention: * Keep values low if hot restarts exhibit lean bucking or hesitation when shifting into gear.
  • Companion Decay Rate: * Evaluated as the hot-restart counterpart to cold decay rate constant DCYTMC.
