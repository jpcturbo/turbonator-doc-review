1. Overview & Purpose

IDLSUB: IDLSUB defines the fuel pulsewidth deduction curve applied during idle speed flares on the Lotus 16-valve Turbo III. When the clutch is disengaged or neutral is selected and RPM momentarily overshoots target idle, this table briefly subtracts fuel to dampen the flare and bring engine speed smoothly to idle.
