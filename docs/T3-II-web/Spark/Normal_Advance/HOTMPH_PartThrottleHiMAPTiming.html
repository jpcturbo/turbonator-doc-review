1. Overview & Purpose

HOTMPH: HOTMPH defines the 3D ignition spark advance surface for heavy part-throttle acceleration approaching boost on the Lotus 16-valve DOHC Turbo III engine. Unlike Chrysler 8-valve engines which utilize a 2D curve, the Lotus Turbo III DDIS platform interpolates ignition timing across both engine speed (RPM) and manifold pressure (MAP) simultaneously using the 3D table lookup routine. Because the Lotus 16V pent-roof combustion chamber features a central spark plug and highly efficient intake tumble ports, flame front propagation is drastically faster than in a 2-valve head, requiring lower total spark advance to reach peak cylinder pressure safely after top dead center.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Calibration Strategy: Raising values in HOTMPH advances ignition timing for heavy part-throttle acceleration approaching boost, increasing torque output and throttle responsiveness. However, because the Lotus 16-valve cylinder head has exceptionally high combustion efficiency, advancing timing too aggressively—particularly under boost—can trigger severe detonation. Always tune with high-octane fuel and monitor knock retard telemetry. Lowering values retards timing, creating a wider safety margin against knock on hot days or low-octane pump fuel at the expense of slight power loss and higher EGTs.
  • Raising the Value: Increases commanded threshold or extends gating limits in the controller.
  • Lowering the Value: Decreases commanded threshold or restricts operational bounds.
