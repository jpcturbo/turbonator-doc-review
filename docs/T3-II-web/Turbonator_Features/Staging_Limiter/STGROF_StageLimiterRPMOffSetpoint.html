1. Overview & Purpose

STGROF establishes the upper engine speed limit where launch control cuts ignition and/or fuel injection while staging. When staging at wide-open throttle, the engine revs rapidly toward this setpoint. The instant engine speed reaches STGROF, the engine controller triggers rotating spark cuts and/or fuel cuts depending on configured options, arresting engine acceleration and locking RPM within the staging window. The factory setting of 125 counts corresponds to 4000 RPM, providing a solid rev limit ceiling for turbo boost generation on the starting line.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Target Staging RPM: Calibrate to desired launch ceiling (3200-3600 RPM for street tires, 4200-4800 RPM for drag slicks or high-stall converters).
  • Pairing With Resume Point: Always maintain STGROF strictly higher than STGRON by roughly 150 to 250 RPM to establish proper hysteresis.
  • Rev Limit Strategy: Enable opt_SprkRevLim in OPTN2 for boost building via rotating spark cut. Fuel cut (opt_FuelRevLim) halts turbine drive energy and slows boost spool.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect when staging at wide-open throttle, the engine revs rapidly toward this setpoint.
