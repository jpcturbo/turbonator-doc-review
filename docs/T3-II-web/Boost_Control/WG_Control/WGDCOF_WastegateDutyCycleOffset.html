1. Overview & Purpose

WGDCOF: WGDCOF establishes the baseline open-loop wastegate solenoid duty cycle offset commanded during boost regulation. This baseline duty cycle positions the wastegate flapper valve close to its required boost equilibrium before closed-loop PID error trim fine-tunes the pressure to exact target boost.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Calibration Strategy: Raising the value increases initial wastegate duty cycle, speeding up transient boost spool-up but risking initial boost overshoot spikes. Lowering the value prevents boost spikes on large turbos but softens spool.
  • Raising the Value: Increases commanded threshold or extends gating limits in the controller.
  • Lowering the Value: Decreases commanded threshold or restricts operational bounds.
