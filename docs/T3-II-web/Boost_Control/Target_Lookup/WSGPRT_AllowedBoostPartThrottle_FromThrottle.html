1. Overview & Purpose

WSGPRT controls the maximum allowable turbocharger boost target under part-throttle operating conditions based on throttle position sensor voltage. When cruising at partial throttle openings, allowing the turbocharger to build full manifold boost against a partially closed throttle plate can create unpleasant compressor surge, jerky driveability, and excessive intake air heating. If the throttle is not wide open, the controller references this table to ensure boost scales proportionally with accelerator pedal angle, reserving full target boost exclusively for heavy or wide-open throttle demands.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Part-Throttle Boost Scaling: * Matches boost ceiling to accelerator pedal position, preventing full boost against a partially closed throttle blade. * Eliminates compressor surge and violent bucking during partial-throttle acceleration.
  • Pedal Responsiveness Tuning: * Elevating mid-pedal points (2.5 to 3.5 Volts) gives a strong, torquey passing response without needing to floor the accelerator. * Keeping low-pedal points (below 2.0 Volts) at base vacuum/zero boost preserves highway fuel economy.
  • Wide-Open Throttle Bypass: * At WOT (b4f_FullThrottle set), the controller completely skips this table, allowing full boost from WSGRPM.
  • Throttle Voltage Baseline: * Indexed by TPS voltage minus closed-throttle minimum (TpsVolts - MINTHR_LowestSessionTPS).

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during part-throttle boost operation, scaling target boost based on throttle position to provide progressive boost modulation.
