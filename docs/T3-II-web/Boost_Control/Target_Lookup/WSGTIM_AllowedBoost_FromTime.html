1. Overview & Purpose

WSGTIM governs the rate at which turbocharger boost is permitted to ramp upward over time once wide-open throttle is applied. The engine controller monitors an internal boost timer that increments while the engine is producing power above minimum tracking RPM. By looking up allowed boost as a function of elapsed timer counts, the controller prevents instantaneous boost spikes and delivers a progressive, predictable torque onset. Factory calibrations use this table to smooth powertrain shock loads on front-wheel-drive transaxles and prevent sudden tire spin when the throttle snaps open.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Spool-Up Ramp Tuning: * Controls how quickly full boost target is made available after entering wide-open throttle. * Setting high boost targets at 0.0 seconds allows instant maximum boost onset for drag racing.
  • Drivetrain Protection & Traction: * Ramping boost progressively over 0.4 to 0.7 seconds reduces front-wheel-drive torque steer and preserves manual transaxle gearsets.
  • Minimum-Wins Limitation: * Evaluated as the second table in GetBoostTargetFromTables; lower values will restrict the boost target calculated from WSGRPM.
  • Timer Incrementing Dependency: * Timer_BoostTimer increments only while engine speed exceeds RPMBST (T1) or T2RPMBST (T2).
