1. Overview & Purpose

DLYOF1 defines the main program loop counter bitmask that paces the rate at which the automatic idle speed stepper motor is permitted to step closed during off-idle driving. When the driver operates the throttle pedal off-idle, the stepper motor is dynamically pre-positioned open based on engine speed and throttle angle. When vehicle operating conditions change and require the motor to close down to a lower step position, snapping the stepper motor shut too quickly would cause sudden manifold vacuum drops and abrupt driveline shock. DLYOF1 acts as an off-idle rate limiter, restricting stepper motor closing movement to one step decrement only when the masked loop counter reaches zero. In factory calibrations, DLYOF1 is set to 7 counts, representing a binary bitmask allowing the motor to close by one step every 8 main loop cycles, or approximately every 88 milliseconds.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Smoothing Off-Idle Jerk: * Increase DLYOF1 from 7 to 15 counts to slow off-idle stepper closure, smoothing part-throttle transitions and gentle deceleration.
  • Sharpening Throttle Response: * Lower DLYOF1 to 3 counts if you desire faster stepper retraction and more direct engine braking when modulating the throttle.
  • Mask Formatting: * Must be configured as a power-of-two minus one value (3, 7, 15, 31) to ensure proper modulo masking with the main loop counter.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect when the driver operates the throttle pedal off-idle, the stepper motor is dynamically pre-positioned open based on engine speed and throttle angle.
