1. Overview & Purpose

KNKOFS defines the positive manifold boost pressure offset above barometric pressure that divides knock retard into non-boost versus in-boost operational modes. Combustion knock occurring under light engine load requires a different correction strategy than detonation occurring under heavy turbocharger boost. In factory calibrations, KNKOFS is set to 35 counts, corresponding to approximately 5.0 psi of positive boost above atmospheric pressure. When knock is detected below this threshold, the controller applies the non-boost retard step size (RTRDRT). When manifold pressure exceeds barometric pressure plus KNKOFS, the controller switches to the in-boost retard step size (RTRDRT2) and sets the boost flag to accumulate boost knock history.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Transition Pressure Tuning: * Factory setting of 5.0 psi boost represents the boundary where cylinder pressures begin to climb steeply.
  • 3-Bar MAP Scaling: * On a 3-bar MAP sensor, rescale KNKOFS from 35 counts down to approximately 23 counts to maintain the same 5.0 psi boost switch point.
  • Boost History Flag: * Crossing above BaroPressure + KNKOFS sets the boost flag that drives adaptive retard learning and wastegate boost reduction.
