1. Overview & Purpose

KNKLIM defines a fixed maximum knock retard limit applied during low-speed transient throttle transitions. When accelerating from low engine speeds with significant throttle input, sudden mechanical torque loading, engine mount flex, and exhaust shield vibrations can produce mechanical noise spikes. Under these low-RPM, high-throttle conditions (below KLMRPM with throttle opening above THRKNK), KNKLIM restricts maximum allowable knock retard to a modest limit (factory setting 5 counts = 2.5 degrees) to prevent false vibration noise from triggering excessive, bog-inducing timing retard.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Low-RPM Tip-In Hesitation Prevention: * Factory setting of 2.5 degrees (5 counts) prevents false knock spikes during sudden throttle stomps from bogging the engine.
  • Stiff Engine Mounts & Clutches: * Polyurethane engine mounts or aggressive clutch discs transmit heavy vibration; lowering KNKLIM prevents false retard bogs.
  • Raising for Lugging Protection: * If an engine experiences genuine low-RPM detonation when accelerating in high gears at 1200 RPM, raise KNKLIM to 4.0 or 5.0 degrees.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during rapid throttle tip-in at lower engine speeds, capping the maximum allowable transient knock retard the ECU can apply.
