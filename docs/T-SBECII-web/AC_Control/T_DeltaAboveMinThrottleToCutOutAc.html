1. Overview & Purpose

ACOUT defines the throttle position sensor voltage delta above learned closed throttle (MINTHR) that disengages the air conditioning compressor clutch during heavy acceleration. When the driver depresses the accelerator pedal for passing, merging, or wide-open throttle acceleration, disengaging the mechanical A/C compressor eliminates 5 to 10 horsepower of parasitic crankshaft load, delivering maximum engine power to the drive wheels. In factory calibrations, ACOUT is configured to 133 counts, corresponding to approximately 2.60 Volts above closed throttle (approximately 70% to 75% throttle opening). Once throttle opening exceeds this threshold, the controller cuts off the A/C clutch, smoothly re-engaging it when the throttle is released.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Mid-Throttle Passing Response: * Lower ACOUT to 90 to 100 counts (~50% throttle) to drop compressor drag during moderate passing maneuvers.
  • Sustained Cabin Cooling: * Raise ACOUT to 160 to 180 counts to maintain air conditioning during moderately aggressive mountain driving.
  • Disabling Wide-Open Cutout: * Set ACOUT to 255 counts to keep the A/C clutch engaged across all throttle angles.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during heavy or wide-open throttle acceleration, immediately disengaging the A/C compressor clutch to eliminate accessory drag and maximize engine power.
