1. Overview & Purpose

ACTDEF: ACTDEF establishes the time delay required after A/C engagement or start-to-run transfer before the A/C compressor clutch is permitted to engage. Delaying compressor clutch engagement allows the idle air stepper motor to preemptively kick open, preventing engine RPM droop when the heavy mechanical compressor load hits the accessory drive belt.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Calibration Strategy: Raising the delay gives the idle stepper motor more time to stabilize idle before the A/C clutch pulls engine torque down. Lowering the delay makes A/C cooling engage more rapidly.
  • Raising the Value: Increases commanded threshold or extends gating limits in the controller.
  • Lowering the Value: Decreases commanded threshold or restricts operational bounds.
