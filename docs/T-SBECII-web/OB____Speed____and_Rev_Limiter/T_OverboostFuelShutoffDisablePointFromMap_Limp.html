1. Overview & Purpose

LMPSHF establishes the overboost fuel cutout threshold used whenever the engine controller detects an electrical fault in the wastegate solenoid circuit. If the wastegate solenoid wiring is damaged, disconnected, or shorted, the controller cannot actively modulate boost pressure and loses electronic wastegate bleed control. Under these compromised conditions, the normal two-stage timed overboost routine (MPSHFT and MPSHFA) is completely bypassed. Instead, the controller compares manifold boost directly against LMPSHF. In factory calibrations, LMPSHF is set to 115 counts, representing approximately 13.3 psi of boost above atmospheric pressure. If boost exceeds LMPSHF while a wastegate solenoid fault is flagged, fuel injection is cut instantly on the current engine rotation without any timer delay.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Failsafe Preservation: * Retain LMPSHF as an emergency protection ceiling in case the boost control solenoid wiring is compromised or unplugged.
  • Proportional Adjustment for High Boost: * When raising target boost and MPSHFT, elevate LMPSHF to match the mechanical spring pressure of the upgraded wastegate actuator.
  • 3-Bar MAP Scaling: * Rescale LMPSHF when migrating to a 3-bar sensor to maintain appropriate emergency pressure limits on the 44.1 psi scale.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect only when the ECU enters Limp-In mode due to an active sensor fault, enforcing a reduced manifold pressure ceiling for overboost fuel cutoff.
