1. Overview & Purpose

THRTHR defines the throttle position offset above closed throttle that delineates light-throttle driving from loaded acceleration during MAP sensor limp-in mode. When the engine controller flags a defective manifold absolute pressure sensor, it must apply special rev limiting to prevent the engine from operating under high load without accurate fuel metering. If the driver is merely feathering the throttle to maneuver the vehicle at low speed, a lower or less intrusive limit may apply. However, if the throttle position exceeds MINTHR by more than THRTHR, the controller confirms the driver is attempting loaded acceleration and enforces the strict MAP limp-in rev limiter (REVLMT2 and REVLMT). In factory calibrations, THRTHR is set to 33 counts, representing approximately 0.65 volts above base closed throttle, or roughly 25 percent of throttle travel.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Throttle Body Sizing: * Large aftermarket throttle bodies flow more air at lower opening angles. Lowering THRTHR maintains protective gating on modified inductions.
  • High-Stall Converters: * Raising THRTHR slightly allows additional pedal travel to overcome tight or high-stall converters without triggering the limp limiter.
  • Relative Voltage Operation: * Because THRTHR is added to MINTHR, it dynamically tracks closed-throttle voltage adjustments automatically.
