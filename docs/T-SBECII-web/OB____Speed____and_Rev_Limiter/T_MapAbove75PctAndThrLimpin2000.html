1. Overview & Purpose

REVLMT4 establishes the upper engine speed fuel cutout threshold for the plausibility safety check that protects against throttle mechanical sticking and low-speed throttle sensor faults. When the engine controller observes manifold absolute pressure exceeding 75 percent of barometric pressure (indicating near-wide-open throttle manifold conditions) while the throttle position sensor indicates idle or closed throttle, the engine may be experiencing a broken throttle return spring, mechanical linkage hang-up, or massive intake air leak. Additionally, if the throttle position sensor signal is lost while the vehicle is moving below 10 mph, this safety mode engages. In factory calibrations, REVLMT4 is set to 94 counts, corresponding to approximately 3000 RPM. When engine speed climbs to REVLMT4 under these fault conditions, the controller cuts fuel injection to halt unwanted acceleration.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Camshaft Compatibility: * If a high-overlap performance camshaft causes idle vacuum to drop below 7.5 inHg (manifold pressure > 75% of barometric pressure), raise REVLMT4 to 4500+ RPM to eliminate false rev limiter cuts.
  • Hysteresis Gap: * Keep REVLMT4 approximately 500 to 1000 RPM above REVLMT3 to provide a wide, stable hysteresis band.
  • Safety Preservation: * Retaining REVLMT4 at 3000 RPM ensures that a severed throttle spring cannot cause uncontrolled acceleration.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect at elevated engine RPM, establishing a secondary over-rev fuel shutoff threshold.
