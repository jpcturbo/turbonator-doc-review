1. Overview & Purpose

REVLMT3 defines the lower engine speed recovery setpoint for the plausibility rev limiter that activates when manifold pressure indicates significant engine load while the throttle position sensor or idle switch reports a closed throttle. If a vehicle suffers a severe vacuum leak, a jammed or broken throttle cable, or an open throttle plate while the idle contact is made, the controller detects an impossible operating condition (manifold absolute pressure exceeding 75 percent of barometric pressure at closed throttle). To protect against unintended vehicle acceleration and runaway engine speeds, the controller cuts fuel injection above REVLMT4. REVLMT3 serves as the lower restore threshold (62 counts, or approximately 2000 RPM), allowing fuel injection to re-engage once engine speed has fallen back into a safe operating range.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Camshaft & Low-Vacuum Tuning: * Radical camshafts with poor idle vacuum can cause manifold pressure to exceed 75 percent of barometric pressure at idle, mistakenly triggering this limiter. Raise REVLMT3 and REVLMT4 in tandem if surging occurs.
  • Hysteresis Matching: * Maintain a 500 to 1000 RPM gap between REVLMT3 and REVLMT4 for smooth recovery.
  • Stock Safety Retention: * Preserving stock settings provides essential runaway protection if a throttle cable sticks or a large vacuum hose detaches.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect as a runaway engine safety cutout if manifold pressure exceeds 75% of baro while the throttle idle contact is closed, limiting engine speed to 2,000 RPM.
