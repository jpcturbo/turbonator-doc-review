1. Overview & Purpose

SPDLML defines the lower vehicle road speed recovery threshold that restores fuel injection after a top-speed cutout event. When the vehicle reaches the maximum speed limit set by SPDLMH, fuel injection is interrupted. Without hysteresis, fuel would switch on and off at a single speed point, producing violent vehicle jerking and drivetrain stress. SPDLML establishes the lower speed setpoint (110 counts, or 110 mph in factory calibrations) where fuel injection resumes. This 8 mph deadband between shutoff (118 mph) and recovery (110 mph) ensures stable, predictable vehicle deceleration during speed governor events.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Paired Adjustment with SPDLMH: * Always adjust SPDLML in tandem with SPDLMH, maintaining a 5 to 10 mph buffer below SPDLMH.
  • Disabling Limiter: * Set both SPDLMH and SPDLML to 255 counts to eliminate vehicle speed governing entirely.
  • Hysteresis Integrity: * Setting SPDLML equal to or greater than SPDLMH causes erratic fuel pulsing or perpetual fuel lockout once the limit is reached.
