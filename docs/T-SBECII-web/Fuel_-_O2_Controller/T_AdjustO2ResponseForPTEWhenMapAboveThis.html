1. Overview & Purpose

BOOSTP defines the manifold absolute pressure threshold above which closed-loop oxygen sensor feedback is inhibited and the engine controller is forced into open-loop fueling. Under positive turbocharger boost pressure, the engine requires a rich air-fuel mixture to manage in-cylinder combustion temperatures and suppress knock. Standard closed-loop feedback attempts to oscillate fueling continuously around stoichiometric 14.7:1 air-fuel ratio, which is dangerously lean under positive boost. In factory stock calibrations, BOOSTP is configured to 255 counts, corresponding to approximately 29.4 psi absolute pressure or 14.7 psi gauge boost. Because 255 counts sits at the very top of the factory 2-bar sensor scale, this threshold acts as a secondary failsafe while throttle angle thresholds and power enrichment flags manage standard open-loop transitions.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Lowering the Threshold: * Lowers the boost level where closed-loop feedback is terminated. Setting this value near barometric pressure (approximately 128 counts or 0 psi gauge) ensures immediate open-loop fueling as the turbocharger builds positive pressure during part-throttle operation.
  • 3-Bar MAP Sensor Rescaling: * Must be recalibrated when changing MAP sensor scaling. On a 3-bar sensor, 14.7 psi absolute sits at approximately 85 counts; failure to rescale leaves closed loop enabled deep into positive boost.
  • Raising the Threshold: * Leaves closed loop active at higher manifold pressures. If set too high, the engine controller may attempt to command stoichiometric 14.7:1 fueling under mild boost conditions, causing high exhaust gas temperatures.
  • Open-Loop Verification: * Works in concert with part-throttle enrichment flags and full-throttle detection to ensure positive cylinder enrichment under load.
