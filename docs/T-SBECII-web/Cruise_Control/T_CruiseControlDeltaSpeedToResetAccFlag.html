1. Overview & Purpose

ACLRST defines the vehicle speed deficit threshold below which the accelerated cruise mode flag is cleared, returning the cruise servo loop from high-gain VACACC to standard VACREG operation. When the vehicle is accelerating back toward its setpoint during resume, the engine controller commands aggressive vacuum duty cycles via VACACC. As road speed approaches the target setting, high acceleration must be tapered off before reaching the setpoint to avoid overshooting and surging. ACLRST establishes this handoff threshold, calibrated in stock calibrations to 9 counts, corresponding to approximately 4.5 miles per hour below target speed.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Overshoot Suppression: * Increasing ACLRST drops out of accelerated cruise earlier, tapering throttle before the vehicle reaches target speed. * Recommended if the vehicle consistently pulls 2 to 4 mph past the set speed when resuming.
  • Aggressive Resume Pull: * Lowering ACLRST sustains high vacuum pull closer to the target setpoint, sharp for heavy vehicles.
  • Factory Setting: * Set to 9 counts (≈ 4.5 mph) in production calibrations, offering balanced handoff without perceptible throttle hunting.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during cruise control acceleration or resume, disengaging accelerated pull-up mode and returning to regular cruise gain once speed error drops below this threshold.
