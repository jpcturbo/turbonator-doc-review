1. Overview & Purpose

SCNVHI defines the upper gear ratio threshold for manual transmission clutch pedal disengagement and clutch slip detection. On manual transmission vehicles, depressing the clutch pedal or suffering clutch slippage disconnects engine speed from wheel speed, allowing the engine to free-rev if the cruise servo continues pulling the throttle open. The engine controller monitors the ratio of vehicle road speed to distributor pulse period. When the clutch is engaged in high gear, this product remains within a defined mechanical gear ratio window. If the clutch pedal is pressed or the clutch slips, engine RPM flares, causing the calculated product to spike past SCNVHI and instantly disengaging cruise control.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Clutch Slip & Shift Protection: * Automatically drops out of cruise control when driver presses the clutch pedal or if clutch disc slips under boost. * Prevents engine free-revving flare when clutch is disengaged without brake pedal touch.
  • Gear Ratio / Tire Changes: * After changing transmission final drive or tire diameter, verify top gear ratio product remains below SCNVHI.
  • Stage IV Calibration Setting: * Factory 2.5L calibration uses 241 counts; Stage IV calibrations set SCNVHI to 255 counts for maximum ratio tolerance.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect continuously while cruise control is armed, forming the upper threshold of the analog voltage safety window that instantly disengages cruise when the brake or clutch pedal is tapped.
