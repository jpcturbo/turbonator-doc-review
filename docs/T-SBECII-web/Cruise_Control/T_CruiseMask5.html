1. Overview & Purpose

CRMSK5 defines the negative clamp floor for the adaptive integrator trim. Just as positive integrator windup must be prevented during climbs, negative integrator windup must be bounded during extended descents. If a vehicle descends a long mountain pass with the cruise engaged, continuous overspeeding could drive the integrator to extreme negative values, which would cause the throttle to refuse to open when the road levels out. CRMSK5 clamps negative integrator authority at -30 counts (stored as 226 counts in two's complement), establishing symmetrical bounds with CRMSK6.
