1. Overview & Purpose

THGAIN provides a progressive throttle position gain correction factor applied to the cruise servo command based on throttle opening angle above idle. Mechanical butterfly throttle bodies possess non-linear airflow characteristics: cracking the throttle blade open by 2 degrees at near-idle conditions yields a large percentage increase in engine airflow, whereas a 2-degree movement at 30 percent throttle yields a much smaller percentage airflow change. THGAIN compensates for this non-linear airflow curve by adjusting servo pulse authority as a function of throttle position sensor voltage, linearizing cruise control authority across all throttle angles.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Aftermarket Throttle Bodies (52mm / 58mm): * Larger throttle bodies flow substantially more air at small opening angles. * Lowering THGAIN prevents cruise control twitchiness and surging on oversized throttle setups.
  • High-Load Hill Climbing: * Increasing THGAIN boosts servo pull authority as throttle angle increases, helping maintain speed up steep grades.
  • Additive Gain Matrix: * The output of THGAIN is stored in scratch RAM Temp4 and added directly to SPGAIN (speed gain factor) to generate combined servo gain Temp3.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during cruise control operation, applying a progressive servo gain correction based on throttle opening angle.
