1. Overview & Purpose

PTUMP1 defines the primary manifold pressure delta threshold for Part Throttle Unlock lockup enforcement under moderate-to-heavy throttle modulation. When the driver applies more throttle than PTUDTP, the controller selects PTUMP1 to evaluate engine load changes. If manifold pressure rise (stored in Temp0) meets or exceeds PTUMP1, the controller commands the torque converter clutch to lock, preventing converter slippage under engine load. In factory calibrations, PTUMP1 is set to 120 counts, corresponding to approximately 13.8 psi on the 0 to 29.4 psi MAP scale. If manifold pressure increases by more than this threshold, the torque converter is locked to protect transmission friction plates.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • 3-Bar MAP Rescaling: * Rescale PTUMP1 to ~80 counts on 3-bar sensor conversions to preserve 13.8 psi delta threshold.
  • Torque Converter Multiplication: * Raise PTUMP1 to permit longer unlocked operation during moderate acceleration for better torque multiplication.
  • Clutch Slippage Prevention: * Lower PTUMP1 on high-torque turbocharged engines to lock the converter before high cylinder pressure can slip the clutch.
