1. Overview & Purpose

PTUMSP defines the vehicle speed boundary that splits the Part Throttle Unlock control logic between low-speed city operation and high-speed highway cruising. Automatic transmission torque converter lockup behavior must balance low-speed drivability against highway fuel efficiency. In factory calibrations, PTUMSP is set to 90 counts, corresponding to approximately 45 MPH on the 0 to 127.5 MPH scale. Below 45 MPH, the controller enforces manifold pressure delta checks (PTUMP3 and PTUMP1/PTUMP2) to prevent torque converter slippage under load, and reloads low-speed counter PTUCNL. Above 45 MPH, manifold pressure checks are bypassed and high-speed counter PTUCNH governs unlock debounce timing.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Suburban Drivability Tuning: * Lower PTUMSP to 35 to 40 MPH to allow earlier converter unlocking for passing maneuvers without reaching highway speeds.
  • Highway Fuel Economy: * Raise PTUMSP to 50 MPH to hold torque converter lockup tighter on rolling hills.
  • Counter Selection Interlock: * Selects whether PTUCNH or PTUCNL reloads into Counter_PartThrottleUnlock when timer expires.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect while cruising in gear, establishing the minimum vehicle road speed required before torque converter clutch lockup is permitted.
