1. Overview & Purpose

PURHYS defines the vacuum hysteresis deadband for the evaporative canister purge solenoid control routine. When driving at steady cruising speeds near light throttle, intake manifold vacuum naturally oscillates due to road irregularities and small throttle pedal movements. If the controller used a single fixed vacuum threshold to toggle the purge solenoid, the valve would buzz or chatter rapidly on and off. In factory calibrations, PURHYS is configured to 2 counts, providing approximately 0.23 psi (0.5 inHg) of hysteresis. Once canister purge is energized, PURHYS is subtracted from the barometric headroom, requiring manifold pressure to rise closer to atmospheric pressure before the solenoid is shut off.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hysteresis Deadband Preservation: * Keep PURHYS at factory 2 counts (~0.5 inHg) for quiet, stable purge valve operation.
  • Preventing Solenoid Chatter: * Zeroing PURHYS produces rapid on-off clicking on rough pavement or highway undulations.
  • Upper Limit Caution: * Do not raise PURHYS beyond 4 counts to avoid delaying purge shutoff as the engine transitions into boost.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during evaporative canister purge control, providing manifold pressure hysteresis to prevent purge solenoid chattering.
