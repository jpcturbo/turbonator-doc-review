1. Overview & Purpose

WSGRP2: WSGRP2 defines the allowable target boost derating curve across engine speed (RPM) when knock retard is detected. When the knock sensor detects detonation and ignition timing retard reaches critical thresholds, the ECU lowers commanded boost target to relieve cylinder pressure and safeguard pistons and head gaskets.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration
