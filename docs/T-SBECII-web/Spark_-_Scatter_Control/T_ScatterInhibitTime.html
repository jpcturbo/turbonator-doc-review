1. Overview & Purpose

SSITIM defines the start-up inhibit duration that delays the activation of spark scatter idle stabilization after engine cranking. When an engine first fires and catches, the rotational speed naturally flares upward before settling down to target idle speed. During this initial post-start flare, applying rapid ignition timing advance and retard would interfere with natural engine settling and disrupt automatic idle speed motor initialization. SSITIM enforces an elapsed-time grace period (factory setting 136 counts at 11 milliseconds per count, approximately 1.5 seconds) during which spark scatter remains inhibited.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Post-Start Flare Taming: * Raising SSITIM to 2.5 or 3.0 seconds (220 to 270 counts) allows modified engines with aggressive cams to settle smoothly before spark modulation begins.
  • Startup Stability: * Factory setting of 1.5 seconds allows starter disengagement and primary flare stabilization before closed-loop timing takes over.
  • Word Constant Scaling: * 16-bit timer constant scaled at 11 milliseconds per count.
