1. Overview & Purpose

LOTRG2 defines the second, more aggressive trigger threshold for throttle-based deceleration lean-out. While small throttle closures between the primary trigger and LOTRG2 cause the engine controller to increment lean-out gradually, rapid throttle closures that exceed LOTRG2 immediately engage full deceleration lean-out from the temperature table. In the stock calibration, this parameter is set to 255 counts, preventing maximum throttle decel lean-out from ever being triggered.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Two-Stage Deceleration Tuning: When enabling throttle deceleration lean-out, set LOTRG2 approximately 5 to 10 counts higher than LOTTRG (e.g. LOTTRG = 5 counts, LOTRG2 = 10 counts).
  • Immediate Full Lean-out: Abrupt pedal lift-offs that exceed LOTRG2 bypass the gradual ramping logic and jump directly to full table lean-out.
  • Incremental-Only Configuration: Leaving LOTRG2 at 255 counts while lowering LOTTRG limits deceleration lean-out to gradual, incremental mode only.
  • Threshold Alignment: Must always be set strictly greater than LOTTRG.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during aggressive throttle drop-off, activating a secondary, stronger deceleration leanout rate.
