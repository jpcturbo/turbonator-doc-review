1. Overview & Purpose

MPDAGL defines the lower manifold pressure boundary for the steady-state manifold absolute pressure sensor rationality validation window. While the engine is operating between the calibrated low and high engine speed boundaries, the engine controller checks whether manifold pressure falls within a realistic intake vacuum band. During high-RPM overrun or deep trailing-throttle deceleration, intake manifold vacuum reaches extreme levels that lie outside normal idle operating conditions. In factory calibrations, MPDAGL is set to 16 counts, representing approximately 1.8 psi absolute pressure (equivalent to -12.9 psi gauge pressure or roughly 26.2 inHg of manifold vacuum). If manifold pressure drops below MPDAGL, the controller suspends the steady-state rationality validation, preventing deceleration vacuum spikes from failing the sensor health check.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • 3-Bar MAP Sensor Rescaling: * Rescale MPDAGL by multiplying by 0.667 when upgrading to a 3-bar MAP sensor (from 16 counts down to 11 counts) to preserve the 1.8 psi absolute lower limit.
  • Deceleration Overrun Margin: * Factory setting of 1.8 psi absolute (26.2 inHg vacuum) keeps trailing-throttle overrun vacuum from interfering with steady-state sensor validation.
  • Edge Cases: * Setting MPDAGL higher than warm idle manifold pressure (e.g., above 35 counts / 18 inHg vacuum) prevents b47_MapOK from ever setting. * Zeroing MPDAGL allows sensor validation down to absolute zero pressure.
