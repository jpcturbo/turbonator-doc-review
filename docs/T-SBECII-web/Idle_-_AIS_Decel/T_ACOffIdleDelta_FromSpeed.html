1. Overview & Purpose

ACCTBL is a 2D calibration curve that establishes the off-idle stepper motor opening offset as a function of vehicle road speed whenever the air conditioning compressor is operating. At highway cruising speeds, high condenser ram airflow and elevated compressor displacement impose substantial mechanical drag when the driver lifts off the throttle. To prevent abrupt deceleration jerking and ensure smooth coasting while the air conditioner is active, ACCTBL injects progressively larger opening step offsets as vehicle speed increases. ACCTBL is evaluated off-idle when coolant temperature is above ACTMP. In factory calibrations, ACCTBL commands approximately 25 steps at 25 mph, scaling up to 42 steps at 40 mph, and 60 steps at 80 mph.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Highway Lift-Off Jerk Fix: * Elevate step points above 40 mph in ACCTBL if highway deceleration feels harsh or jerky with the air conditioner running.
  • Preventing Forward Push on Decel: * Lower ACCTBL steps across the 25 to 50 mph range if the vehicle coasts too freely and lacks adequate engine braking with the A/C active.
  • Scaling Multiplier Interaction: * Output is multiplied by PERKCK (factory 75%), which scales the effective step adder applied to the motor.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during off-idle driving while the A/C compressor is running, adding extra bypass airflow through the idle air motor based on vehicle road speed.
