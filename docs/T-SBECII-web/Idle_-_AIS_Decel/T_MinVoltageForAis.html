1. Overview & Purpose

BTVAIS defines the minimum electrical system battery voltage required before the controller allows actuation of the automatic idle speed stepper motor. The 4-wire stepper motor actuator relies on electromagnetic coils to overcome internal friction, spring pressure, and intake manifold vacuum force to move the pintle. If battery voltage sags significantly, coil current is insufficient to overcome these mechanical forces, leading to weak torque, skipped steps, and permanent loss of motor position calibration. BTVAIS establishes a protective voltage floor: whenever battery voltage is below BTVAIS, stepper motor stepping pulses are completely inhibited. In factory calibrations, BTVAIS is set to 113 counts, corresponding to a battery voltage floor of approximately 11.3 Volts.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Lightweight Battery Dip Accommodation: * Lower BTVAIS to 10.5 to 10.8 Volts if high-draw electric water pumps or fuel pumps pull system voltage below 11.3 Volts at hot idle.
  • Skipped Step Hazard: * Do not lower BTVAIS below 9.5 Volts, as weak coil magnetism at low voltage causes the stepper motor to miscount steps and lose physical calibration.
  • Factory Safety Margin: * Factory setting of 113 counts (11.3 Volts) ensures robust coil stepping torque under all factory operating conditions.
