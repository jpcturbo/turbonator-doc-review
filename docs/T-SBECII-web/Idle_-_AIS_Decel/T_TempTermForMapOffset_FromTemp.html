1. Overview & Purpose

MPTEMP is a 2D calibration curve that provides a coolant-temperature-dependent scaling multiplier applied to the RPM-based deceleration control MAP term. During cold engine operation, separate warm-up tables (such as TEMPDC) already provide substantial auxiliary bypass airflow. If the full RPM-dependent decel MAP adder from MAPFLR were applied on top of these cold offsets, total decel airflow would be excessive, causing the engine to flare or race during cold deceleration. MPTEMP acts as a progressive blend multiplier: it attenuates the RPM-based decel MAP term when the engine is cold and gradually blends it in to full strength as the engine warms up. In factory calibrations, MPTEMP scales from 0.0 (zero contribution) at 36 °F up to 1.0 (100% full contribution) at 170 °F and above.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Warm-Up High Idle Hang: * If engine speed hangs on decel while the car is warming up, reduce the intermediate multiplier points in MPTEMP.
  • Cold High-RPM Stall Catch: * Elevate cold points in MPTEMP if high-RPM clutch disengagements during warm-up cause the engine to plunge past idle and die.
  • Full Operating Range: * Always maintain a full 1.0 multiplier (255 counts) above 170 °F.
