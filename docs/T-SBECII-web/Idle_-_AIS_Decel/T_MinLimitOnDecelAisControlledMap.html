1. Overview & Purpose

DCLMIN establishes a minimum lower clamp on the calculated deceleration control manifold absolute pressure value. During closed-throttle deceleration, intake manifold vacuum reaches extreme levels. If the synthetic decel control MAP calculation were allowed to drop to near-absolute zero pressure, the difference between actual MAP and control MAP would become artificially small, preventing the controller from recognizing deep deceleration vacuum and failing to open the dashpot. DCLMIN enforces a hard lower floor on the calculated decel control MAP. In factory calibrations, DCLMIN is set to 10 counts, enforcing an absolute pressure floor of approximately 1.1 psi absolute (2.3 inHg / 27.6 inHg vacuum).

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Deep Overrun Anti-Stall: * Increase DCLMIN to 14 to 18 counts if the engine pulls extreme overrun vacuum and stalls when the clutch is pressed.
  • Factory Minimum Baseline: * Factory setting of 10 counts (~1.1 psi absolute) guarantees a safe floor on stock induction systems.
  • Part-Throttle Boundary: * Keep DCLMIN below 25 counts to prevent false decel triggering during light part-throttle cruising.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during closed-throttle deceleration, intake manifold vacuum reaches extreme levels.
