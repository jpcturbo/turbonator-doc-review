1. Overview & Purpose

DLYTIM2 defines the time delay that the engine controller holds the idle air stepper motor in open dashpot position after the throttle snaps shut while the vehicle is stopped or moving at low speed with engine RPM below DLYRPM. When the driver revs or blips the throttle at a standstill, or when coming to a halt at a stoplight, the throttle plate snaps closed. Holding the idle air valve open for a calibrated period cushions the engine's return to idle and prevents the descending crankshaft speed from plunging into an undershoot stall. Whenever closed throttle is detected at speeds below MPHREF and engine speed is below DLYRPM, the controller loads DLYTIM2 into the feedback lockout countdown timer. In factory calibrations, DLYTIM2 is set to 18 counts, providing approximately 1.1 seconds of dashpot hold time.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stationary Blip Stall Fix: * Increase DLYTIM2 from 1.1 to 1.8 to 2.2 seconds to eliminate stalling when blipping the throttle in neutral or rolling to a stop.
  • Lightweight Flywheel Inertia: * Engines with lightened rotating mass lose speed rapidly; extending DLYTIM2 provides a softer landing into idle.
  • Reducing Neutral Hang: * If idle lingers above target for several seconds after free-revving in neutral, lower DLYTIM2 to 12 to 14 counts (~0.8 seconds).

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect when the throttle snaps shut, holding the idle air motor in a dashpot position for a calibrated delay before closed-loop idle speed control resumes.
