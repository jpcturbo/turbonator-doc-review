1. Overview & Purpose

ACDTHR defines the throttle position sensor voltage delta threshold required to activate part-throttle air conditioning compressor cutout. When accelerating moderately to merge onto a highway or pass traffic, disengaging the air conditioning compressor eliminates parasitic pumping loss and delivers maximum engine torque to the drive wheels. ACDTHR establishes the throttle opening threshold (measured relative to the base closed-throttle stop) above which the controller initiates a temporary compressor cutout. In factory calibrations, ACDTHR is set to 255 counts (5.0 Volts delta above idle stop). This maximum setting disables part-throttle cutout on factory calibrations, ensuring that the air conditioning remains active until true wide-open throttle is reached.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Brisk Passing Boost: * Lower ACDTHR to 2.0 to 2.5 Volts to automatically cut the A/C compressor during half-throttle acceleration, improving passing power.
  • Uninterrupted Cabin Cooling: * Retain factory 255 counts (5.0 Volts) to prevent the air conditioner from cutting out during part-throttle driving.
  • Delta TPS Input: * Evaluated relative to MINTHR, meaning physical TPS mounting variations are automatically zeroed out.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during part-throttle acceleration with the A/C on, requiring throttle position increase to exceed this threshold before disengaging the compressor clutch.
