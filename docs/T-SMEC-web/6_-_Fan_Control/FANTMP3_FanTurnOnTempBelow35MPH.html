1. Overview & Purpose

FANTMP3 defines the coolant temperature threshold that energizes the radiator cooling fan during low-speed driving or idle conditions below 35 MPH.

When stopped in traffic or driving at low vehicle speeds, no ram airflow enters the front grille to cool the radiator and intercooler cores. Underhood temperatures quickly rise from the turbocharger turbine housing and exhaust manifold. In factory calibrations, FANTMP3 is set to 227 counts, corresponding to approximately 199 °F. When coolant temperature exceeds this threshold, the controller turns on the electric cooling fan to draw airflow through the cooling pack, preventing engine overheating during city operation.
