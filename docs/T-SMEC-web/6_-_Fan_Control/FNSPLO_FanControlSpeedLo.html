1. Overview & Purpose

FNSPLO defines the low vehicle speed transition threshold that switches the cooling fan logic back into low-speed mode when the vehicle decelerates.

Speedometer pulse period variable VehicleSpeed_HB is compared against FNSPLO after clearing the high-speed check. In factory calibrations, FNSPLO is set to 9 counts, representing approximately 36 MPH. When vehicle speed drops below 36 MPH (pulse period exceeds 9 counts), the controller clears the high-speed status flag and engages the low-speed temperature setpoints FANTMP3 and FANTMP4.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: ControlFan_MM / PreventRadSteaming (T-SMEC_v19.asm:1729-1744), instruction line 1733.
  • Evaluation Chain:
  • Evaluated only when VehicleSpeed_HB < FNSPHI_FanControlSpeedHi.
  • Comparison & Branch:
  • Compares VehicleSpeed_HB against FNSPLO_FanControlSpeedLo at line 1733.
  • If VehicleSpeed_HB < FNSPLO (bcs Above36MPH), road speed exceeds ~36 MPH (accelerating into highway range).
  • Clears b51_SpeedUnder40 and advances index pointer by 2.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hysteresis Boundary:
  • Completes the 8 MPH speed deadband with FNSPHI to eliminate mode hunting during suburban cruising.
  • Tuning Direction:
  • Lower count values raise the transition speed in miles per hour; higher count values lower it.
  • Spread Retention:
  • Keep FNSPLO 2 to 3 counts lower than FNSPHI to preserve stable speed hysteresis.
