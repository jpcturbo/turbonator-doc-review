1. Overview & Purpose

FANBAT defines the underhood temperature threshold measured at the battery tray thermistor that triggers radiator cooling fan activation.

In turbocharged Chrysler vehicles, high underhood radiant heat from the turbocharger turbine housing and exhaust manifold can accumulate in the engine compartment during low-speed driving or after hard boost runs. Because the battery and the engine controller power module share the engine compartment behind the left headlight, extreme heat degrades lead-acid battery life and reduces alternator voltage regulation efficiency. In factory calibrations, FANBAT is set to 27 counts, corresponding to approximately 120 °F. If battery temperature rises above this threshold, the controller energizes the cooling fan to evacuate hot air from the engine bay.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: ControlFan_MM / PreventRadSteaming (T-SMEC_v19.asm:1766-1775), instruction line 1773.
  • Gating Conditions:
  • Evaluated after coolant temperature and boost checks have passed.
  • Sensor voltage confirmed above AMBLOW_BattTempLowWhenHot (sensor plausibility check).
  • Hysteresis & Comparison:
  • If fan is already running, decrements accumulator A by 2 counts.
  • Compares sensor reading against FANBAT_FanTurnsOnWhenBatteryTempAboveThis (cmpa FANBAT at line 1773).
  • If sensor voltage is less than FANBAT (hotter than threshold), execution falls through to TurnFanOn.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Tight Engine Bay Heat Management:
  • Lower FANBAT to 110 °F to 115 °F on cramped L-body and G-body turbo cars to purge radiant exhaust heat.
  • Battery Relocation Safeguard:
  • If the battery is relocated to the trunk and the thermistor is deleted, set FANBAT high to prevent false fan triggers.
  • Sensor Inversion Note:
  • The battery thermistor uses negative temperature coefficient logic; lower sensor voltage counts represent higher physical temperature.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • NTC Sensor Polarity: Lower A-to-D voltage counts correspond to higher ambient battery temperatures.
  • Built-In Hysteresis: 2 counts of voltage hysteresis are applied when the fan relay is already active.
  • Sensor Plausibility Gate: Protected by AMBLOW_BattTempLowWhenHot to ignore shorted sensor wiring.
