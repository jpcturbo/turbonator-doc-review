1. Overview & Purpose

THRTCN defines the digital filter time constant governing the duration of throttle-based acceleration enrichment pump shots across engine coolant temperatures.

Following a rapid throttle opening, the controller injects transient fuel and then decays it back toward steady-state delivery as the rolling average throttle voltage catches up with actual throttle voltage. THRTCN sets this tracking rate, providing a longer enrichment tail when the engine is cold to compensate for poor fuel vaporization, and a shorter, snappier duration once the engine reaches normal operating temperature.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Sharpening Throttle Response: Increasing table values accelerates the rolling average filter, causing the TPS transient fuel pulse to decay away more rapidly, producing a sharper, crisper pedal feel.
  • Curing Post-Tip-in Lean Sag: Decreasing table values slows down the decay rate, extending the duration of the throttle pump shot to support intake manifold filling.
  • Intake Manifold Runner Length: Engines with large intake plenums or long runners benefit from lower values that sustain transient fueling longer.
  • Companion Parameter Interaction: Works closely with POSSLP and LOTIME.
