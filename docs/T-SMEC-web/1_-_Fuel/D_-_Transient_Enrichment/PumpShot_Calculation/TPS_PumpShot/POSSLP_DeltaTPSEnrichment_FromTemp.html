1. Overview & Purpose

POSSLP establishes the temperature-dependent gain curve for throttle-based acceleration enrichment pump shots.

When the throttle blade opens rapidly, incoming air rushes in faster than liquid fuel can atomize and travel through intake runners, causing fuel to condense on cold manifold walls. To maintain stoichiometric combustion during this transition, the engine controller calculates a transient fuel pulse by multiplying the effective throttle delta voltage by the slope from POSSLP.

The table provides high gain when the engine coolant is cold, tapering down to lower values at normal operating temperatures. The factory calibration delivers approximately 13.8 milliseconds per volt at 116 degF, decreasing to 7.3 milliseconds per volt at 205 degF.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Curing Tip-in Hesitation: Raising table values increases the size of the throttle pump shot, curing lean tip-in stumbles, intake manifold pops, or hesitations upon sudden acceleration.
  • Injector Upsizing Rescaling: Lowering table values reduces transient fuel volume, which is essential when installing larger fuel injectors. Scale down POSSLP across all temperature points by the ratio of stock injector flow to new injector flow.
  • Cold Engine Compensation: Keep cold-temperature points significantly higher than operating temperature points to compensate for poor fuel atomization in cold intake runners.
  • Coordination with AESLOP: Balanced tip-in performance requires harmonizing POSSLP (immediate pedal movement) with AESLOP (manifold filling).

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Primary Accelerator Pump Curve: This is the primary parameter governing throttle tip-in fueling.
  • Injector Flow Rate Dependency: In stock form, calibrated for 27 lb/hr or 33 lb/hr injectors. Upgrading to +40% (46 lb/hr) or +52 lb/hr injectors requires reducing table values by 30 to 45 percent.
  • Companion Parameter: Works in direct conjunction with AESLOP, POSTRG, and THRTCN.
