1. Overview & Purpose

AETIME establishes the digital filter time constant that governs the decay rate of MAP-based acceleration enrichment.

When manifold absolute pressure rises above the rolling average, the engine controller calculates an acceleration pump shot based on the pressure delta. To prevent the enrichment from vanishing on the very next engine cycle, AETIME sets the weighting factor used to update the rolling average manifold pressure.

The factory setting of 20 counts allows the rolling average to track upward smoothly, sustaining transient enrichment across several engine cycles while the intake plenum fills.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Shortening Pulse Duration: Increasing AETIME causes the rolling average to catch up faster, shortening the duration of the MAP pump shot for crisper throttle response on light cars.
  • Supporting Large Plenums: Decreasing AETIME slows the filter update rate, allowing the rolling average to lag longer and extending the duration of transient enrichment.
  • Turbocharger Spool Support: Extending transient enrichment helps turbocharged engines with large intercoolers or significant turbo lag maintain stable air-fuel ratios while boost pressure builds.
  • Filter Stability: Avoid setting AETIME to zero, which freezes the rolling average and locks transient fuel permanently active.
