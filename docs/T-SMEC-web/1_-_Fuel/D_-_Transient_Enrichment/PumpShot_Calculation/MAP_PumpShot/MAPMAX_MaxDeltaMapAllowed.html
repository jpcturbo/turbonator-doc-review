1. Overview & Purpose

MAPMAX sets the maximum allowable manifold pressure delta used when calculating a MAP-based acceleration enrichment pump shot.

When the driver floors the accelerator from deep engine overrun, manifold pressure can transition from 20 inHg of vacuum to full atmospheric pressure or positive turbo boost in tens of milliseconds. Without a ceiling, multiplying this huge pressure swing by the enrichment slope would result in an excessive transient fuel pulse that floods the combustion chambers. MAPMAX limits the effective manifold pressure delta after the trigger threshold is subtracted.

The factory setting of 51 counts caps the calculation at approximately 5.9 psi above the trigger level.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Preventing Rich Tip-in Bog: Lowering MAPMAX caps the maximum transient fuel delivered during rapid manifold pressure spikes, preventing rich bogging when snapping the throttle open at low RPM.
  • Injector Upsizing Protection: Crucial when tuning engines with oversized injectors where un-clamped manifold deltas deliver far too much raw fuel.
  • 3-Bar MAP Recalibration: Scale MAPMAX downward by approximately 33 percent when converting to a 3-bar MAP sensor so the clamp represents the same real-world pressure delta.
  • Large Intake Plenums: Engines with massive intercoolers and intake piping may require raising MAPMAX to support large air mass influx.
