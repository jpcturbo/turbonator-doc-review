1. Overview & Purpose

POSTIG sets the minimum idle air stepper motor step increase required to trigger automatic idle air acceleration enrichment.

When the engine is idling with the throttle closed, rapid electrical or accessory load increases such as the air conditioning compressor engaging or turning the steering wheel cause the idle speed motor to open quickly to maintain target idle speed. This sudden rush of bypass air leans out the intake plenum before the throttle position sensor can react. If the commanded stepper motor opening delta meets or exceeds POSTIG, the engine controller triggers an auxiliary fuel pulse defined by POSFUL.

The factory setting of 13 steps prevents minor idle trim adjustments from triggering extra fuel while responding instantly to major accessory loads.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Preventing Idle Sag: Lowering POSTIG makes the idle transient enrichment activate on smaller idle air movements, preventing RPM dips or stumbles when shifting an automatic transmission into gear or when the electric radiator fan turns on.
  • Preventing Idle Hunting: Setting POSTIG too low can cause the engine to inject extra fuel during normal idle speed stabilization, leading to an erratic or hunting idle.
  • Aggressive Camshafts: Raising POSTIG prevents false enrichment on engines with aggressive camshafts that cause rapid stepper hunting.
  • Companion Parameter: Must be calibrated in direct conjunction with POSFUL.
