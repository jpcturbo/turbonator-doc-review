1. Overview & Purpose

PTEADJ provides signed manifold pressure-dependent scaling to the part-throttle enrichment ceiling established by PTELMT.

Because engine air density and knock vulnerability vary dramatically across vacuum and positive boost, a static RPM ceiling is insufficient. PTEADJ modifies the maximum allowable enrichment multiplier based on instantaneous manifold absolute pressure.

In calibration software, zero counts represents a neutral 1.00 multiplier, leaving the RPM ceiling unchanged. Negative and positive values in PTEADJ scale the enrichment ceiling down or up, providing extra fuel headroom at high boost while capping enrichment at light loads.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Boost-Dependent Fuel Headroom: Increasing table values in the positive boost region raises the maximum enrichment ceiling, ensuring that heavily boosted engines receive sufficient fuel under sustained part-throttle acceleration.
  • Trimming Low-Load Fueling: Lowering values in moderate vacuum or low boost ranges prevents excessive fuel buildup during mild acceleration.
  • Signed Table Behavior: Remember that zero represents a neutral 1.00 multiplier in firmware math. Positive and negative offsets scale the RPM limit proportionally.
  • 3-Bar MAP Alignment: When converting to a 3-bar MAP sensor, verify that table pressure axis breakpoints are updated so that boost-dependent ceiling scaling takes effect at the intended real-world manifold pressures.
