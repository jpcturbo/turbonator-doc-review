1. Overview & Purpose

PTENRD specifies the signed step decrement added to the open-loop fraction timer on each decay interval when manifold pressure falls below the RPMMAP boundary curve.

Stored as an 8-bit two's complement negative integer, the factory setting of 255 counts represents minus one count, subtracting one fractional increment from the enrichment multiplier every PTENR2 pulse interval.

Furthermore, PTENRD is used in the hysteresis math that clears the maximum part-throttle enrichment status flag, ensuring that spark timing returns smoothly to normal advance schedules after boost enrichment ends.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Enclosed in routine Bit7_IsSet at lines 5865-5925 of cals/SMEC/T-SMEC_v19.asm.
  • Applied during decay loop via addb 0x01,x at line 5877 when index register X points to PTENR2.
  • Hysteresis evaluation at line 5904: ldaa PTENRD_AmountForDecreasingMultiplier_Negative.
  • Line 5906 executes nega to invert the negative count into a positive magnitude before multiplying by PTENUM.
  • If the decay delta from maximum enrichment exceeds PTENRD * PTENUM, bclr *BitFlags_4f, #b4f_PTEisMaxed clears the flag at line 5914.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Decay Step Magnitude: Setting PTENRD to more negative values such as minus two or minus three counts accelerates enrichment decay, rapidly leaning out the mixture to save fuel.
  • Factory Smoothness: The factory setting of minus one provides the smoothest and most imperceptible transition back to baseline fueling.
  • Spark Hysteresis Interaction: Because PTENRD is also multiplied by PTENUM in the spark advance hysteresis check, changing this value alters the decay window required to clear the maximum enrichment spark status flag.
  • Decay Verification: Verify that wideband air-fuel ratio traces taper cleanly without abrupt lean steps when lifting out of boost.
