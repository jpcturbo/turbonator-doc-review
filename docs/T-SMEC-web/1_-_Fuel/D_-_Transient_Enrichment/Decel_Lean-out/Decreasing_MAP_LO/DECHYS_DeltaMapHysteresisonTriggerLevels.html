1. Overview & Purpose

DECHYS establishes hysteresis for MAP deceleration lean-out trigger levels to stabilize fuel control during unsteady deceleration.

Once MAP deceleration lean-out is triggered, the engine controller adds DECHYS to the calculated pressure drop before comparing against DECTRG. This prevents the controller from rapidly dropping out of decel lean-out if manifold pressure oscillates slightly as the engine slows down.

The factory setting of 2 counts provides approximately 0.23 psi of hysteresis, ensuring smooth, continuous fuel tapering during deceleration.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hysteresis Window Setting: Keep DECHYS calibrated between 1 and 3 counts (0.115 to 0.345 psi) to maintain stable deceleration fueling.
  • Preventing Tip-in Hesitation: Setting DECHYS too high delays the exit from decel lean-out when the driver begins lightly re-applying the throttle, resulting in a lean stumble or hesitation upon tip-in.
  • Preventing Driveline Bucking: Setting DECHYS to zero causes the controller to cycle rapidly between decel lean-out and normal fueling if the vehicle experiences driveline lash or minor vacuum surges, creating noticeable bucking.
  • Companion Parameters: Operates in direct coordination with DECTRG and DTRG2.
