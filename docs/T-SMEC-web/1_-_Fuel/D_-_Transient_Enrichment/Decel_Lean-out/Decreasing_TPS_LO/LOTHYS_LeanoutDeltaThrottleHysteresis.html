1. Overview & Purpose

LOTHYS provides hysteresis for throttle deceleration lean-out triggers to prevent rapid toggling between normal fueling and decel lean-out.

Once deceleration lean-out becomes active, the engine controller adds LOTHYS to the closing throttle delta before comparing it to the trigger thresholds. This artificially expands the active window, requiring the driver to make a more deliberate pedal movement before decel lean-out is cancelled.

The factory calibration sets this value to zero because throttle deceleration lean-out is disabled.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hysteresis Sizing: When configuring throttle deceleration lean-out, set LOTHYS to approximately 2 to 4 counts (0.04 to 0.08 volts).
  • Eliminating Throttle Chatter: Eliminates chattering and jerky fuel cuts when the driver holds the throttle near the edge of the deceleration threshold.
  • Preventing Tip-in Flat Spots: Setting this value too high keeps deceleration lean-out active even as the driver attempts to roll back into the throttle, causing a momentary flat spot or hesitation.
  • Zero Setting Behavior: Setting to zero leaves the trigger boundaries identical on both entry and exit.
