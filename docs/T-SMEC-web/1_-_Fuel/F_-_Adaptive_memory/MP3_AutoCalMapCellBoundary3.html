1. Overview & Purpose

MP3 defines the third manifold absolute pressure boundary in the adaptive fuel memory structure, separating Cell 4 and 5 from Cell 6 and 7.

This boundary corresponds to moderate part-throttle engine loads, such as pulling a slight incline or accelerating into traffic. In factory calibrations, MP3 is configured to 75 counts, which equals approximately 8.6 psia or 12.4 inHg of vacuum.

By isolating moderate load fueling into dedicated adaptive cells, the engine controller compensates for intake manifold resonance and injector non-linearities without affecting light-load cruising trims.

  • Mid-Range Load Isolation:
  • Separates moderate acceleration load (Cell 4/5) from heavy part-throttle cruise (Cell 6/7).
  • 3-Bar MAP Sensor Rescaling:
