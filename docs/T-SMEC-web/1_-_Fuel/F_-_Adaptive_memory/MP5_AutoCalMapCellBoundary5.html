1. Overview & Purpose

MP5 defines the fifth and highest manifold absolute pressure boundary in the adaptive memory grid, separating Cell 8 and 9 from Cell 10 and 11.

Cell 10 and 11 represent near-atmospheric and initial positive pressure operation before closed-loop lockout occurs. In stock calibrations, MP5 is set to 120 counts, which corresponds to approximately 13.8 psia or 1.8 inHg of vacuum, just below standard sea-level barometric pressure.

This final boundary isolates near-boost closed-loop fuel adaptation from standard manifold vacuum operation.
