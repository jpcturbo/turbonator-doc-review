1. Overview & Purpose

O2HSWP establishes the rich switching threshold for closed-loop oxygen sensor feedback.

During closed-loop operation, the engine controller monitors sensor voltage against this threshold: when sensor voltage equals or exceeds O2HSWP, the mixture is recognized as rich, triggering the primary ramp logic to begin subtracting fuel.

In stock calibrations, O2HSWP is set to 25 counts, corresponding to approximately 0.49 volts, positioned directly at the steep stoichiometric curve of a narrowband zirconium sensor.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Rich Bias Tuning:
  • Raising O2HSWP to 26-28 counts biases the closed-loop stoichiometric oscillation slightly richer (approx 14.5:1), stabilizing idle on performance cams.
  • Lean Bias Tuning:
  • Lowering O2HSWP brings the switching point closer to 0.45V, centering fuel delivery strictly at 14.7:1.
  • Deadband Maintenance:
  • Must be strictly greater than O2LSWP to maintain switching hysteresis.
