1. Overview & Purpose

PEFTBL defines the engine pumping efficiency (volumetric efficiency) curve versus engine speed (RPM) in Chrysler speed-density calibrations.

Speed-density fuel calculation estimates mass airflow based on intake manifold pressure, displacement, charge air temperature, and the mechanical breathing ability of the engine across the RPM range. PEFTBL scales base fuel pulsewidth across the entire operating range from idle to 8160 RPM to match cylinder head port airflow, camshaft lift and duration, and intake manifold runner resonance.

In Turbonator SMEC v19, the firmware utilizes the proven stock 0 to 99.6 percent volumetric efficiency scaling routine.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Performance Camshaft Profiling:
  • Reshape PEFTBL to match the engine's new volumetric efficiency curve when installing aftermarket camshafts (e.g. Taft, FWD-P, or custom grinds).
  • RPM-Specific Fuel Shaping:
  • Raise values where cylinder filling improves (peak torque and upper mid-range); lower values at low RPM if a large cam causes low-speed airflow reversion.
  • Universal Fueling Effect:
  • Modifies part-throttle, wide-open throttle, and idle fueling equally at each RPM point because volumetric efficiency is an engine airflow physical property.
  • SMEC v19 Scaling Architecture:
  • Turbonator SMEC v19 re-adopted the stock 0 to 99.6 percent volumetric efficiency routine (experimental 150% scaling code is commented out in assembly).
