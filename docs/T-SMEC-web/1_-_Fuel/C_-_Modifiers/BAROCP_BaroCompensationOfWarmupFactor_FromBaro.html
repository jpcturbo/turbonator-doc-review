1. Overview & Purpose

BAROCP defines the barometric pressure compensation curve for warm-up fuel enrichment.

At higher elevations with reduced atmospheric pressure and thinner air, the engine requires less supplementary fuel during warm-up because air mass per cylinder stroke is naturally lower. BAROCP scales down the total accumulated warm-up enrichment factor as barometric pressure decreases.

This table prevents rich stumbling, carbon foul on spark plugs, and excessive exhaust emissions during cold engine warm-up in high-altitude environments.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • High Altitude Warm-Up Tuning:
  • If cold driveaway in the mountains produces black smoke and rich stumbling, increase the reduction factor at low barometric pressures.
  • Sea Level Retention:
  • Keep BAROCP at zero reduction near 14.7 psi barometric pressure so sea-level warm-up enrichment remains unaffected.
  • 3-Bar MAP Sensor Calibration:
  • Barometric pressure is sampled from the MAP sensor at key-on; verify sensor transfer curves match the sensor hardware.
  • Multiplicative Scale:
  • Multiplies the entire accumulated cold enrichment sum (Temp0) before charge temperature compensation.
