1. Overview & Purpose

STFBAR defines the barometric pressure compensation curve for cranking fuel delivery.

At high altitudes or during low ambient barometric pressure conditions, the air is less dense and contains fewer oxygen molecules per cylinder fill. If sea-level cranking fuel were injected at mountain elevations, the air-fuel mixture would be excessively rich, causing spark plug fouling and extended cranking.

STFBAR generates an altitude-based reduction factor that scales down cranking fuel as atmospheric pressure decreases.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • High Altitude Starting:
  • Increasing STFBAR values removes cranking fuel at low barometric pressures, preventing flooded plugs in mountain passes.
  • Sea Level Preservation:
  • Keep STFBAR at zero near sea-level pressure (14.7 psi absolute) so full cold start enrichment is delivered.
  • 3-Bar MAP Sensor Calibration:
  • Barometric pressure is sampled from the MAP sensor at key-on; ensure sensor transfer curves match the sensor hardware.
  • Lean Stumble at Altitude:
  • If high-altitude starts pop lean, decrease STFBAR reduction factors to restore cranking pulsewidth.
