1. Overview & Purpose

PRSHOT defines the single key-on injector priming pulsewidth delivered immediately upon turning the ignition key from off to run.

Before the starter engages or the crankshaft begins rotating, the intake runners and intake valve backs may be completely dry. PRSHOT fires all fuel injectors simultaneously for a brief duration based on coolant temperature, pre-wetting the intake tracts with liquid fuel so that a combustible charge is available on the very first compression stroke.

Colder coolant temperatures receive a larger priming charge, while hot restarts receive minimal or zero priming.

  • Gating Conditions:

  • Hardware Firing:
  • Looked up from CoolantTemp, multiplied by 250 (#0xFA), and loaded into injector hardware timer registers via ACCOUNTFORBATVOLTAGE_LOAD_INJECTOR_DRIVERS_ROUTINE.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Injector Size Scaling:
  • Scale PRSHOT down proportionally when installing larger injectors (e.g. +40% injectors require reducing PRSHOT by roughly 30%) to prevent wet-fouling.
  • Cold Start Cranking Speed:
  • Increasing PRSHOT at cold temperatures improves instant engine catch without extended starter churning.
  • Hot Restart Flooding:
  • If hot restarts smell of raw fuel or require throttle propping to start, verify PRSHOT is set to zero at warm operating temperatures.
  • Race & Multi-Spark Setups:
  • Zeroing PRSHOT across the entire temperature range eliminates key-on injector clicking and prevents intake runner puddling on carbureted or throttle-body conversions.
