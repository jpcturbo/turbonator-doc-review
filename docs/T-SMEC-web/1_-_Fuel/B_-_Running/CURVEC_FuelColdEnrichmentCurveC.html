1. Overview & Purpose

CURVEC defines the intermediate warm-up enrichment curve (Curve C) indexed against engine coolant temperature.

In Chrysler's tripartite cold enrichment architecture, CURVEC serves as a bridge between the intense, short-lived after-start enrichment (Curve A) and the gradual warm-up enrichment (Curve B). CURVEC is held at full strength for a duration determined by CCURV and then decayed via DCYCC.

This intermediate enrichment supplies additional fuel during the first one to three minutes of operation, preventing lean hesitation as the vehicle transitions from idle to driving load.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Intermediate Drivability Flat Spots:
  • Increase CURVEC if acceleration stumbles 1 to 2 minutes after startup while leaving a parking lot or driveway.
  • Rich Idle Hunting Reduction:
  • If cold idle hunts up and down during intermediate warm-up, reduce CURVEC values to prevent over-fueling.
  • Tripartite Balancing:
  • Balance CURVEC against CURVEA and CURVEB; CURVEC provides the middle-stage fuel ramp.
  • Hold & Decay Timing:
  • Controlled by companion parameters CCURV (hold duration) and DCYCC (stepwise decay rate).
