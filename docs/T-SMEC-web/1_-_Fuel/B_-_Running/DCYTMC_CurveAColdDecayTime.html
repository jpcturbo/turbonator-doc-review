1. Overview & Purpose

DCYTMC defines the decay rate divisor for after-start cold enrichment (Curve A) following a cold start.

Once cold hold duration (CLDHLD) expires, DCYTMC controls how quickly after-start fuel enrichment tapers down to zero. The decay rate is inversely proportional to this constant, producing a smooth reduction in fuel enrichment over time.

In factory calibrations, DCYTMC is calibrated to 9 counts, yielding a decay window of approximately 10.0 seconds.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: CalculateSumOfAllFuelModifiers_MM (T-SMEC_v19.asm:6022-6036), instruction lines 6022, 6033.
  • Gating Conditions:
  • Active when b50_FuelCurveACToggle is set (decay active) and cold start conditions apply.
  • Indexed Subtraction:
  • When cold, pointer X points to TMPHLD. In L1516, subb 0x03,x reads TMPHLD + 3 = DCYTMC.
  • Subtracted from Factor_ACVDCY every 32 loop passes.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Decay Duration Formula:
  • Decay time in seconds equals approximately 89.76 divided by calibration counts (9 counts = 10.0 seconds decay).
  • Smoothing the Taper:
  • Lower DCYTMC to 4-6 counts if the engine hesitates or sags as cold hold ends.
  • Accelerating Warm-Up:
  • Raising DCYTMC drops cold enrichment faster, reducing exhaust soot and fuel consumption if idle stability permits.
  • Companion Calibration:
  • Works with CLDHLD; CLDHLD sets the dwell, and DCYTMC sets the ramp-down rate.
