1. Overview & Purpose

CLDHLD specifies the duration that after-start cold enrichment (Curve A) is held at 100% full strength before decay begins following a cold start.

When the engine starts with coolant temperature below TMPHLD, fuel droplets condense heavily on cold intake runner walls. CLDHLD locks after-start enrichment at maximum intensity for several seconds, ensuring that intake manifold wall wetting stabilizes and idle speed settles before fuel enrichment starts tapering away.

In factory calibrations, CLDHLD is set to 18 counts, representing approximately 12.7 seconds of hold time.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: CalculateSumOfAllFuelModifiers_MM (T-SMEC_v19.asm:6015-6021), instruction line 6020.
  • Gating Conditions:
  • Reached when CoolantTemp < TMPHLD (cold start).
  • Evaluated against KeyOnOrEngineRunningTime shifted left twice.
  • Comparison & Branch:
  • Read via indexed offset 0x01,x from TMPHLD.
  • When running time reaches CLDHLD (bcc L1514), full hold ends and decay begins.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Start Stabilization:
  • Increasing CLDHLD extends the full-strength enrichment duration, smoothing cold idle on performance camshafts or in sub-freezing weather.
  • E85 Cold Tuning:
  • Ethanol requires extended wall-wetting time; increase CLDHLD to 25-35 counts (18 to 25 seconds) on E85 calibrations.
  • Fuel Economy & Emissions:
  • Lowering values reduces rich idle dwell, speeding up transition to closed-loop oxygen sensor control.
  • Hold Time Formula:
  • Dwell duration in seconds equals calibration counts multiplied by 0.704 seconds (18 counts = 12.7 seconds).
