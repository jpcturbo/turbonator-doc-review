1. Overview & Purpose

DCLMCT defines the minimum coolant temperature required before decel fuel shutoff is allowed to arm.

Cutting fuel injection during deceleration on a cold engine can cause severe bucking, backfiring, or engine stalling because cold combustion chambers have inadequate heat to sustain instant reignition when fuel turns back on. DCLMCT inhibits decel fuel shutoff until the engine warms to operating temperature.

In factory calibrations, DCLMCT is configured to 210 counts, representing approximately 178 degF.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Thermostat Matching:
  • Lower DCLMCT to 155-160 degF when running a 160 degF performance thermostat so decel fuel cut functions normally.
  • Cold Decel Stall Prevention:
  • If lifting off the throttle during warm-up causes the engine to chug or die, raise DCLMCT to lock out decel fuel cut until the engine is hotter.
  • Emission & Fuel Economy:
  • Proper temperature qualification maximizes coast-down fuel economy without sacrificing cold-engine drivability.
  • Closed Throttle Gating:
