1. Overview & Purpose

DFRPML defines the engine speed delta above target idle speed at which fuel injection is restored during deceleration.

While fuel is shut off during deceleration, engine speed steadily drops toward idle. DFRPML is evaluated inside the hysteresis recovery routine to determine when to re-enable the injectors.

When engine speed drops to within DFRPML of target idle speed, the controller clears Turbonator_MainFuelOff, restoring injector pulses before the engine reaches idle speed.

In factory calibrations, DFRPML is set to 17 counts, representing 544 RPM above target idle speed.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Smooth Fuel Re-engagement:
  • If fuel restoration produces a harsh jerk or stumble, raise DFRPML by 2 to 4 counts to turn fuel on earlier.
  • Idle Undershoot Prevention:
  • Restoring fuel at least 450 to 550 RPM above target idle gives combustion time to stabilize before reaching idle speed.
  • Highway Fuel Economy:
  • Lowering DFRPML prolongs zero-fuel coast-down duration, saving fuel during gradual deceleration.
  • Hysteresis Protection:
  • Governs fuel turn-on; paired with DFRPMH (fuel turn-off) to form the decel fuel cut operating envelope.
