1. Overview & Purpose

DCLRPL defines the low engine speed delta above target idle speed at which the decel fuel cut flag is disarmed.

Once decel fuel shutoff has been armed, engine speed delta must remain above DCLRPL.

As the vehicle slows down and engine speed drops toward idle, approaching within DCLRPL of target idle disarms the decel fuel cut flag and commands the idle air stepper motor to step open.

In factory calibrations, DCLRPL is set to 15 counts, representing 480 RPM above target idle speed.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: CalculateTargetIdleSpeedAndAisPosition_MM (T-SMEC_v19.asm:2047-2056), instruction line 2052.
  • Gating Conditions:
  • Evaluated when Turbonator_DecelFuelCut flag is currently set.
  • Comparison & Branch:
  • Compares delta RPM against DCLRPL.
  • If delta RPM > DCLRPL (bhi SetDFCFlag), the flag remains armed.
  • If delta RPM <= DCLRPL, loads DCAISI, calls ClearAisTimer, and falls into ClearDFCFlag.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Coast-Down Stall Prevention:
  • If depressing the clutch during deceleration causes RPM to plummet below idle, increase DCLRPL by 2 to 4 counts.
  • Extended Fuel Shutoff:
  • Lowering DCLRPL extends fuel shutoff duration as the engine slows down, improving fuel economy on highway decel.
  • AIS Dashpot Preparation:
  • Crossing below DCLRPL triggers DCAISI, adding bypass air steps to catch falling RPM.
  • Hysteresis Pair:
  • Works with DCLRPH; delta between DCLRPH and DCLRPL prevents flag hunting.
