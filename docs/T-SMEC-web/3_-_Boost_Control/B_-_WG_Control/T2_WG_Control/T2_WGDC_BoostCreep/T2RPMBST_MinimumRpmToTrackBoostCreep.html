1. Overview & Purpose

T2RPMBST defines the minimum engine speed threshold required to advance the boost ramp countdown timer (Timer_BoostTimer) in T2 control.

The boost ramp timer indexes the time-based allowed boost curve (WSGTIM), controlling how quickly target boost ramps up to full pressure. T2RPMBST prevents the timer from advancing when the engine is operating at low RPM under heavy load where the turbocharger cannot physically build boost, ensuring the ramp timer remains synchronized with real engine airflow.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • T2 Spool Tracking RPM Threshold:
  • Minimum engine speed required to advance Timer_BoostTimer in T2 mode.
  • Factory setting is 76 counts (approximately 2432 RPM).
  • Synchronizing with Turbocharger Size:
  • Set to match the engine speed where the turbocharger begins producing positive boost.
  • Raise to 3200 to 3800 RPM for large aftermarket turbochargers (Garrett GT30R, Holset HX35).
  • T1 vs. T2 Separation:
  • Dedicated T2 companion to T1 constant RPMBST.
