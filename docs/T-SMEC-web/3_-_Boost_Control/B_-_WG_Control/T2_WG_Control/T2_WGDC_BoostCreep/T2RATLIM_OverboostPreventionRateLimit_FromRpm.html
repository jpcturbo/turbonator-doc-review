1. Overview & Purpose

T2RATLIM establishes the maximum allowable rate of turbocharger boost pressure rise as a function of engine speed in T2 control.

If wastegate port flow is restrictive or exhaust volume surges quickly, manifold boost can rise faster than closed-loop feedback can control, causing dangerous boost creep and overboost spikes. The controller continuously calculates the delta of boost change between successive loops (BoostDelta_Signed); if the positive rate of boost rise exceeds T2RATLIM, the controller immediately bypasses normal duty cycle calculations and clamps duty cycle to T2PRTMAX to halt the creep instantly.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Boost Creep Rate Limiter Table:
  • Maximum permitted rate of boost rise per main program loop pass vs. engine speed.
  • Factory curve sets approx 12 counts (1.4 psi/pass) at low RPM tapering to 6 counts (0.7 psi/pass) at high RPM.
  • Preventing Spool Hesitation & Dips:
  • Fast-spooling ball-bearing turbochargers can trip this table on rapid spool-up; raise entries by 20% to 50% to prevent unwanted T2PRTMAX clamping.
  • Arresting High-RPM Creep:
  • If boost creeps uncontrollably near redline due to small wastegate ports, lower high-RPM points to force early wastegate venting.
  • Emergency Intervention Mechanism:
  • When exceeded, the routine immediately stores T2PRTMAX (100% duty cycle) and exits CalcWGDutyCycleT2.
