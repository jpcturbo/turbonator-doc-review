1. Overview & Purpose

T2WGKNRD defines the individual cylinder spark retard threshold required to trigger wastegate duty cycle reduction and decay pacing in T2 control.

The engine controller monitors knock spark retard across individual cylinders (Cylinder 2 through Cylinder 4). If any cylinder's accumulated knock retard exceeds T2WGKNRD, the controller recognizes that spark retard alone is struggling to extinguish detonation, prompting the wastegate routine to maintain or decay knock-induced boost reduction until combustion stabilizes.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Knock Retard Trigger Threshold:
  • Minimum individual cylinder knock retard required to trigger wastegate duty cycle decay pacing.
  • Factory setting is 7 counts (approximately 0.875 degrees of spark retard, scaled at 0.125 degrees per count).
  • Prioritizing Spark Retard First:
  • Raising T2WGKNRD to 16 to 24 counts (2.0 to 3.0 degrees) allows ignition timing retard to resolve minor knock without dumping boost.
  • Aggressive Engine Safety:
  • Lowering T2WGKNRD to 4 counts triggers wastegate boost reduction immediately upon detection of light knock.
