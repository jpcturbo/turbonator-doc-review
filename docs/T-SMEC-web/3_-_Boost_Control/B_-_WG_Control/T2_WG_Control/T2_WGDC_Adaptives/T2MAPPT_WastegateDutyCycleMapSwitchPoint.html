1. Overview & Purpose

T2MAPPT sets the manifold gauge boost pressure threshold that divides low-load adaptive wastegate learning from high-load RPM-split adaptive learning in T2 control.

In T2 wastegate control, Cell 1 regulates boost below T2MAPPT, while higher boost targets switch into the RPM-partitioned cells (Cells 2 through 5). In factory Stage II calibrations, T2MAPPT is configured to 83 counts (approximately 9.5 psi of boost), ensuring that lower boost levels and spool-up are governed by Cell 1 while sustained full-boost pulls are managed across the RPM cells.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Adaptive Boundary Positioning:
  • Set 1 to 2 psi above mechanical wastegate spring pressure so initial spool-up stays isolated in Cell 1.
  • Factory setting is 83 counts (approximately 9.5 psi boost above atmospheric pressure in template, 105 counts in ASM).
  • 3-Bar MAP Sensor Scaling:
  • Converting to a 3-bar sensor requires rescaling T2MAPPT to half its count value to preserve the same physical psi switch point.
  • Transition to RPM Cells:
  • When target boost exceeds BaroPressure + T2MAPPT, the controller advances to evaluate DCRPM1, DCRPM2, and DCRPM3.
