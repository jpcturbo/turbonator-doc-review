1. Overview & Purpose

LWRLIM specifies the lower authority clamp applied to the dynamic manifold pressure response correction (MPCORR) in T2 boost creep control when the rate of boost change is negative.

If boost is falling or dipping, the T2 algorithm applies a negative correction to restore duty cycle. Chrysler configured LWRLIM to 32 counts (16.0 percent duty cycle offset), exactly twice the magnitude of UPRLIM, giving the controller stronger authority to recover duty cycle and prevent boost sagging during transient shifts.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Negative Boost Creep Authority Clamp:
  • Caps maximum dynamic correction applied when boost rate is falling or dipping.
  • Factory setting is 32 counts (approximately 16.0 percent duty cycle offset authority).
  • Eliminating Shift Lag & Boost Dip:
  • Increasing LWRLIM provides stronger authority to prop up wastegate duty cycle after an upshift.
  • Asymmetric Authority Architecture:
  • Configured twice as large as UPRLIM to ensure rapid recovery from boost drops without causing runaway overboost.
