1. Overview & Purpose

UPRLIM specifies the upper authority clamp applied to the dynamic manifold pressure response correction (MPCORR) in T2 boost creep control.

When boost is rising rapidly, the T2 algorithm computes a correction factor by multiplying the rate of boost rise by the response factor table (T2RESFAC). UPRLIM limits the maximum positive correction authority that this calculation can apply to the base boost target, preventing excessive control windup and sudden over-correction.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Positive Boost Creep Authority Clamp:
  • Caps the maximum dynamic correction applied when boost is rising rapidly.
  • Factory setting is 16 counts (approximately 8.0 percent duty cycle offset authority).
  • Damping Aggressive Spool Spikes:
  • Increasing UPRLIM to 24 to 32 counts gives the ECU more authority to suppress severe boost overshoot on fast-spooling turbos.
  • Preventing Sluggish Spool:
  • If the car hesitates or dips boost during initial spool-up, lower UPRLIM to 8 to 12 counts to prevent excessive dampening.
