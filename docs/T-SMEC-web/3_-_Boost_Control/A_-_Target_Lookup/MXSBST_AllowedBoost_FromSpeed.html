1. Overview & Purpose

MXSBST defines the upper boundary for allowable turbocharger boost pressure as a function of road vehicle speed.

Because turbocharged front-wheel-drive vehicles easily break traction and induce severe wheel spin or torque steer in first and second gears, Chrysler engineers incorporated speed-dependent boost limits into the controller. At low vehicle speeds (such as 0 to 25 mph), the table can limit boost to moderate levels, smoothly elevating the ceiling to full boost as vehicle speed climbs and available tire grip increases at higher speeds.
