1. Overview & Purpose

WSGTIM governs the rate at which turbocharger boost is permitted to ramp upward over time once wide-open throttle is applied.

The engine controller monitors an internal boost timer that increments while the engine is producing power above minimum tracking RPM. By looking up allowed boost as a function of elapsed timer counts, the controller prevents instantaneous boost spikes and delivers a progressive, predictable torque onset. Factory calibrations use this table to smooth powertrain shock loads on front-wheel-drive transaxles and prevent sudden tire spin when the throttle snaps open.
