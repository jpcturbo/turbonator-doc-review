1. Overview & Purpose

WSGCLT establishes the maximum allowable turbocharger boost pressure as a function of engine coolant temperature.

Cold engine operating conditions place severe thermal and mechanical stresses on cast iron cylinder blocks, aluminum pistons, and head gaskets, while thick engine oil limits hydrodynamic bearing protection at high turbocharger shaft speeds. To safeguard mechanical components, the controller looks up the coolant temperature and restricts boost to wastegate spring pressure or low levels until the engine warms up to normal operating temperatures above 140 to 160 degF.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Engine Protection:
  • Restricts boost targets when coolant temperature is low to protect cold pistons, rings, and turbo bearings.
  • Clamping entries below 140 degF to base wastegate spring boost prevents cold-engine hammering.
  • Warm-Up Transition:
  • Blend boost progressively from 140 degF to 180 degF to deliver full power only once oil and coolant are thoroughly warmed.
  • Overheat Safety Limiting:
  • Lowering entries above 220 degF provides built-in thermal protection, de-rating boost if the engine begins running hot.
  • Minimum-Wins Integration:
