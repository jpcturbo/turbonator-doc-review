1. Overview & Purpose

STGBST defines the maximum allowable turbocharger boost pressure permitted while launch control staging mode is actively engaged.

When the staging logic is triggered by clutch or brake engagement at a standstill, the engine controller applies specialized spark cut and fuel rev limiting while restricting manifold pressure to this threshold. In factory Turbonator calibrations, STGBST is calibrated to 41 counts, corresponding to approximately 4.7 psi of boost above atmospheric pressure. Holding boost to this calibrated ceiling prevents excessive wheel spin on launch while spooling the turbocharger to build immediate torque at the starting line.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Launch Boost Management:
  • Sets the stationary boost limit enforced while on the two-step launch limiter.
  • Balances immediate turbo spool against starting line tire spin and driveline shock.
  • Tire & Surface Optimization:
  • For prepped drag surfaces with drag slicks, raise STGBST to 8 to 12 psi for explosive 60-foot times.
  • For street tires or unglued tracks, lower STGBST to 3 to 5 psi (or base spring pressure) to avoid instant tire smoke on release.
  • Staging Flag Dependency:
  • Active only while Turbonator_Staging flag is set in Turbonator_Flags; bypassed during normal rolling driving.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Two-Step Coordination: Works directly with staging rev limiters STGRPM and ignition retard STGSPK.
  • Pneumatic Limitation: Cannot force boost lower than the mechanical wastegate actuator spring pressure.
  • Instantaneous Release: As soon as the staging switch (clutch or handbrake) opens, Turbonator_Staging clears, releasing full boost target.
