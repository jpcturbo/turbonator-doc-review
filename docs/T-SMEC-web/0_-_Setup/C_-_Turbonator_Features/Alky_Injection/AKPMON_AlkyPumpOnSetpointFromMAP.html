1. Overview & Purpose

AKPMON establishes the manifold absolute pressure threshold where the auxiliary water or methanol injection pump relay is energized.

In high-boost turbocharged applications, water and alcohol injection suppresses intake charge temperatures and dramatically elevates detonation resistance. To prevent delivery pressure lag when injection nozzles open, the supply pump must be energized early to pressurize lines and fuel rails. In Turbonator firmware, unused output drivers (such as the purge or EGR solenoid driver) can be repurposed to control the pump relay.

The factory setting is 255 counts (disabled).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Enclosed in subroutine MM_AlkyPump_Control at lines 10562-10577 of cals/SMEC/T-SMEC_v19.asm.
  • Requires configuration flag cfg_AlkyInj set and cfg_SpeedSenseAero clear in CONFIG_ConfigurationFlags (lines 10565-10568).
  • In active firmware, this subroutine is commented out with semicolons in T-SMEC_v19.asm (as documented in smec_anomaly.md), meaning the controller does not execute these instructions unless uncommented in source.
  • Comparison: cmpa AKPMON_AlkyPumpOnSetpointFromMAP against MapValue (line 10572).
  • When MapValue >= AKPMON, bit PortA_PurgeEGR in Z83_PortA_copy is asserted to turn on the pump relay. If below setpoint, execution branches to AlkyPump_CheckHysteresisAndTurnOff.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Priming Strategy: Set AKPMON roughly 2 to 3 psi of boost below the stage one injector setpoint (AKI1ON) to build line pressure before fluid is sprayed.
  • Typical Setpoints: For an 8 psi primary injection stage, calibrate AKPMON between 5 and 6 psi of boost.
  • Disabling Setpoint: Setting to 255 counts (factory setting) permanently disables pump activation.
