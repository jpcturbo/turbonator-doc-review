1. Overview & Purpose

AKI1ON establishes the manifold pressure turn-on threshold for the primary stage water or alcohol injection solenoid.

Two-stage auxiliary injection provides progressive fluid delivery: stage one introduces a modest spray volume at moderate boost to suppress early detonation, while stage two adds higher flow under maximum boost. In Turbonator firmware, an auxiliary output driver (such as Cruise Vent) can be assigned to trigger the stage one injection solenoid when boost reaches this setpoint.

The factory setting is 255 counts (disabled).

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Enclosed in subroutine MM_AlkyInjector_Control at lines 10592-10614 of cals/SMEC/T-SMEC_v19.asm.
  • Requires cfg_AlkyInj set and cfg_SpeedSenseAero clear in CONFIG_ConfigurationFlags (lines 10596-10599).
  • In active firmware, this subroutine is commented out with semicolons in T-SMEC_v19.asm (as documented in smec_anomaly.md), meaning the controller does not execute these instructions unless uncommented in source.
  • Comparison: cmpa AKI1ON_AlkyInj1OnSetpointFromMAP against MapValue (line 10609).
  • When MapValue >= AKI1ON, bit PortA_CruiseVent in Z83_PortA_copy is asserted to open injection solenoid one. If below setpoint, branches to AlkyInj1_CheckHysteresisAndTurnOff.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Activation Boost: Typically set between 7 and 10 psi of boost to initiate primary charge cooling as boost ramps up.
  • Coordination With Pump: Ensure AKI1ON is set at least 2 psi above AKPMON so the delivery pump is fully pressurized before spraying.
  • Disabling Setpoint: Factory setting of 255 counts keeps stage one disabled.
