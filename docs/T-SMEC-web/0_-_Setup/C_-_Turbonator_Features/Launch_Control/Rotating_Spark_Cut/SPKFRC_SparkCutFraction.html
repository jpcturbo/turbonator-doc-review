1. Overview & Purpose

SPKFRC selects the active spark cut fraction from the pre-programmed pattern tables for launch control.

Rather than cutting fuel or indiscriminately dropping random cylinders, Turbonator launch control uses mathematically balanced rotating patterns to regulate engine speed and build boost on the starting line. SPKFRC acts as a selector pointer (values 0 through 7) that chooses the ratio of suppressed to delivered ignition sparks.

The factory default setting is choice 3 (4/5 cut), which fires one cylinder out of every five events, holding launch RPM steady while delivering ample unburnt energy into the turbine housing.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Enclosed in subroutine SetBaseandPattern at lines 10279-10298 of cals/SMEC/T-SMEC_v19.asm.
  • Active when opt_StageLim and opt_SprkRevLim are enabled in OPTN2 and launch control staging conditions are met.
  • Serves as an offset value loaded into accumulator B at lines 10280 and 10292 to index LNCBDT and LNCPDT.
  • Valid enumerated choices are integers 0 through 7.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Selection Guidelines: Choice 0 (1/3 cut) fires 66.7% of cylinders; Choice 2 (3/5 cut) fires 40.0% of cylinders; Choice 3 (4/5 cut, factory) fires 20.0% of cylinders; Choice 7 (6/7 cut) fires 14.3% of cylinders.
  • Large Turbochargers: Use 1/3 or 3/5 cut to deliver maximum thermal expansion into the turbine housing for quick boost response.
  • Small Turbochargers & Traction Limited: Use 4/5 or 5/7 cut to cap flywheel torque and prevent wheelspin when staging.

4. Technical / Assembly Mechanics (Reference)

Inside T-SMEC_v19.asm: At line 10280 within SetBaseandPattern, accumulator B loads SPKFRC_SparkCutFraction. The CPU executes aby with index register Y pointing to #LNCBDT_BaseDataTable, reading the counter base value for that cut pattern. At line 10292 within StoreSparkCutCounter, accumulator B again loads SPKFRC_SparkCutFraction and executes aby against #LNCPDT_PatternDataTable, retrieving the 8-bit rotation bitmask.
