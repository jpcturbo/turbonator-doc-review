1. Overview & Purpose

ANTLAGX sets the absolute ignition timing commanded by the engine controller when anti-lag launch control is active on the staging line.

In turbocharged racing applications, anti-lag burns fuel inside the exhaust manifold and turbine housing rather than pushing down on the piston, spinning the turbocharger turbine wheel to generate boost while the vehicle is stationary. When the engine speed exceeds the anti-lag activation threshold, the controller overrides the normal spark advance schedule and substitutes this absolute ignition angle.

The factory setting provides 8 degrees of retard after top dead center, sending burning charge directly past the opening exhaust valve to spool the turbo rapidly.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Ignition Angle Selection: Positive numbers advance timing toward top dead center, creating gentler anti-lag action with lower exhaust temperatures and slower boost rise. Negative numbers retard timing past top dead center into the exhaust stroke, creating violent exhaust drive pressure that spools large turbochargers instantly.
  • Recommended Launch Targets: Setting timing between 4 degrees and 8 degrees after top dead center typically generates 10 to 15 psi of boost on a transbrake or clutch switch.
  • Thermal Management: Avoid holding anti-lag active for more than three consecutive seconds, as prolonged late combustion causes extreme thermal stress in exhaust valves and turbine housings.

4. Technical / Assembly Mechanics (Reference)

Inside T-SMEC_v19.asm: Subroutine CalulateLaunchAdvance executes at lines 10262-10277. The routine first tests OPTN2_CalCustomOptionConfigFlags2 bit opt_AntiLag. If set, it compares ANLGRP_AntiLagRetardRPM against EngineRpm_HB in register B. When engine speed exceeds the threshold, line 10269 loads ANTLAG_AntiLagRetardAbs into accumulator A. Two arithmetic shift right instructions (asra; asra) divide the signed 8-bit value by 4 with sign extension, and adca #0x00 rounds any fractional remainder. This converts the raw byte into an absolute spark timing angle between -16.0 and +15.875 degrees. The resulting value replaces the normal spark advance in accumulator A, gets saved to Temp5 and FinalAdvanceActual at lines 7103-7104, and flag Turbonator_AntiLag is set in Turbonator_Flags.

; --- CalulateLaunchAdvance (T-SMEC_v19.asm:10262-10277) ---
CalulateLaunchAdvance:
        ldx  #OPTN2_CalCustomOptionConfigFlags2
        brclr 0x00, X, #opt_AntiLag, AntiLag_Return

        ldab ANLGRP_AntiLagRetardRPM
        cmpb EngineRpm_HB
        bcc  AntiLag_Return ; branch if
        ldaa ANTLAG_AntiLagRetardAbs
        asra
        asra ; divide by 4 - min/max = +/-16 deg
        adca #0x00 ; round up any remainder
        ldx  #Turbonator_Flags
        bset 0x00, x, #Turbonator_AntiLag ;just to indicate that the anti-lag is active, for logging purposes

AntiLag_Return:
        rts

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Absolute Timing Override: Unlike knock retard or nitrous retard which subtract a delta angle from base advance, ANTLAGX completely overwrites total ignition advance with an absolute target angle.
  • Option Flag Requirement: Must enable opt_AntiLag in OPTN2 and opt_StageLim in OPTN2.
  • Companion Parameter: Operates in direct coordination with ANLGRP, which defines the engine speed window where anti-lag timing takes over.
