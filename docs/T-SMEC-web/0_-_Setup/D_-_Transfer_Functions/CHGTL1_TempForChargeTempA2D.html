1. Overview & Purpose

CHGTL1 defines the four-point non-linear transfer table converting raw voltage counts from the intake manifold air charge temperature sensor into engineering temperature units.

Measuring intake charge temperature is essential for calculating intake air density, setting charge-air fuel density trims, and applying spark advance knock retards when intake manifold air becomes excessively hot. Additionally, if the primary coolant temperature sensor fails, the controller evaluates CHGTL1 to substitute intake air temperature as a temporary fallback to maintain engine operation.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Open-Element Sensor Conversions:
  • When replacing factory brass-encapsulated sensors with open-element thermistors, rescale CHGTL1 to match the new sensor's voltage-temperature profile.
  • Air Density Compensation:
  • Proper calibration prevents summer-to-winter air-fuel ratio drift by maintaining accurate intake air mass calculations.
  • Coolant Temp Fallback Substitution:
  • If the coolant temperature sensor fails and cfg_CTS is enabled in CONFIG, CHGTL1 supplies fallback operating temperatures.
  • Single-Range Circuit:
  • Uses a single fixed pull-up resistor, unlike the dual-range coolant temperature sensor circuit.
