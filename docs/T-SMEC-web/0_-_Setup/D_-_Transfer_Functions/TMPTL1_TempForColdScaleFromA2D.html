1. Overview & Purpose

TMPTL1 is the primary non-linear transfer table converting raw voltage counts from the engine coolant temperature sensor into engineering temperature units under cold operating conditions.

The Chrysler SMEC hardware incorporates a dual-range thermistor input circuit with a switched pull-up resistor. When the engine is cold (ranging from minus forty degrees Fahrenheit up to approximately one hundred forty degrees Fahrenheit), the controller switches the circuit to high sensitivity and looks up coolant temperature through this seven-point curve. By expanding sensor resolution across low temperatures, TMPTL1 provides precise cold-start fueling enrichment, cranking fuel scheduling, and cold idle target control.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Factory Thermistor Match:
  • Matches the low-temperature resistance curve of the factory Chrysler two-pin coolant temperature sensor.
  • Aftermarket Sensor Swaps:
  • If swapping to a non-stock sensor (such as a GM or Bosch thermistor), TMPTL1 must be re-curved to maintain cold-start enrichment accuracy.
  • Cold-Range Resolution:
  • Dedicated to engine temperatures below one hundred forty degrees Fahrenheit, where fuel vaporization is minimal.
  • Companion Curve:
  • Seamlessly hands off to TMPTL2 as the engine reaches normal operating temperature.
