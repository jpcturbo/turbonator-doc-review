1. Overview & Purpose

VENTRG defines the proportional vent solenoid gain curve utilized when vehicle speed exceeds the target cruise setting.

When descending highway grades or following an acceleration event, gravity or residual engine power causes road speed to climb above the setpoint. In response, the engine controller calculates the negative speed error and queries VENTRG to determine the required vent solenoid pulse duration. Energizing the vent solenoid bleeds trapped vacuum from the actuator diaphragm to atmospheric pressure, allowing the throttle return spring to pull the throttle blade closed smoothly and re-establish the desired road speed without harsh deceleration jerks.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Downhill Speed Creep Control:
  • Increasing VENTRG values commands wider vent solenoid openings when vehicle speed rises above target.
  • Prevents the car from gaining 5 to 10 mph on moderate interstate downhill descents.
  • Driveline Lash & Bucking:
  • If the car exhibits abrupt throttle snap-shut behavior or driveline shudder when cresting hills, lower the lower points of VENTRG.
  • Particularly important for manual transmission vehicles with solid engine mounts or lightweight flywheels.
  • Closed Throttle Inhibit:
  • Venting is automatically inhibited below CLOTHR (1 count above closed idle), protecting the solenoid from rapid chatter when the throttle is already fully resting on its idle stop.
