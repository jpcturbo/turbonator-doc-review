1. Overview & Purpose

CRMASK defines the startup delay count and reset mask applied to Counter_Cruise when cruise control is freshly engaged.

Upon engaging cruise control by pressing set or resume, the mechanical throttle cable, vacuum actuator diaphragm, and engine operating conditions require a brief stabilization period before long-term adaptive integration can safely begin. CRMASK enforces a startup delay of 17 counts while initialization bit 0 of CruiseStatus is active, preventing premature integrator adjustments before the vehicle stabilizes at its target speed.
