1. Overview & Purpose

MAXSET establishes the maximum vehicle road speed setpoint permitted for cruise control engagement.

Operating cruise control at excessive vehicle velocities presents serious safety risks and exceeds federal motor vehicle safety standards. In Chrysler engine controllers, vehicle speed setpoints are stored in internal counts where Value equals miles per hour multiplied by two. Stock calibrations configure MAXSET to 170 counts, establishing an exact maximum cruise engagement speed limit of 85 miles per hour. If the driver attempts to set or tap-up cruise speed above this ceiling, the controller clamps the setpoint to 85 miles per hour.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Extended Highway Cruising:
  • For open western highway travel, raise MAXSET to 180 counts (90 mph) or 190 counts (95 mph).
  • Fleet & Speed Governing:
  • Lowering MAXSET to 130 to 140 counts (65 to 70 mph) restricts maximum cruising speed for fleet or teen vehicles.
  • Setpoint Clamping:
  • Evaluated in CruiseBumpUpSpeedBy2Mph; any setpoint exceeding MAXSET is clamped directly to MAXSET.
