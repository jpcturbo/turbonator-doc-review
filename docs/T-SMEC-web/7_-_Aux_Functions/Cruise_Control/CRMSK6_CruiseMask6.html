1. Overview & Purpose

CRMSK6 defines the positive clamp ceiling for the adaptive integrator trim.

In closed-loop cruise control, integral windup occurs if the controller continues incrementing its learned trim indefinitely when the vehicle cannot maintain speed (such as on an extremely steep mountain pass). Without an upper ceiling, the integrator would build up massive throttle authority that would keep the throttle held wide open long after reaching flat ground. CRMSK6 caps learned positive integrator authority at +30 counts, preventing integrator windup and ensuring safe throttle boundaries.
