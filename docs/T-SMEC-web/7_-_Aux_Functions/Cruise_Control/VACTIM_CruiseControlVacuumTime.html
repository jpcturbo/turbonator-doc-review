1. Overview & Purpose

VACTIM defines the minimum vacuum solenoid pulse duration mask applied whenever the cruise controller commands throttle opening.

Electromagnetic solenoid valves have mechanical inertia and spring preload that require a minimum electrical energization duration before the valve disc cracks off its seat and passes vacuum. If commanded pulse widths are too short, the solenoid buzzes without physically moving the throttle. VACTIM enforces a minimum electrical on-time of 1 count, ensuring every commanded vacuum pulse produces clean, reliable mechanical actuator movement.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Solenoid Stiction Compensation:
  • Enforces minimum electrical on-time so the vacuum solenoid valve physically cracks open.
  • Raising to 2 counts helps older or stiffer solenoids respond cleanly.
  • Surge Prevention:
  • Setting VACTIM too high delivers excessive vacuum on tiny corrections, inducing low-speed surging.
