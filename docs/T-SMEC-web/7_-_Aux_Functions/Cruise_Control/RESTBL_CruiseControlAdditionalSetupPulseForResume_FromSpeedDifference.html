1. Overview & Purpose

RESTBL defines the manifold absolute pressure compensation curve applied to the cruise setup pulse during set and resume engagement.

The rate at which the cruise servo bellows fills with vacuum depends heavily on available engine intake manifold vacuum. At high vacuum (light throttle cruising), the servo draws vacuum rapidly; under low vacuum or positive boost conditions, the servo fills much more slowly. RESTBL evaluates manifold gauge pressure relative to barometric pressure to scale the initial setup pulse, guaranteeing consistent throttle pickup regardless of prevailing engine load or altitude.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Compensating for Camshaft Vacuum Loss:
  • Performance camshafts produce lower manifold vacuum at cruising engine speeds.
  • Increasing RESTBL values compensates for reduced vacuum draw, ensuring the servo bellows pre-charges firmly.
  • Altitude Adaptation:
  • The routine subtracts barometric pressure from manifold pressure, ensuring true gauge vacuum is evaluated across varying altitudes.
  • Integration with Adaptive Trim:
  • The product of SETPW and RESTBL is added to BitFlags_27 (the learned adaptive mechanical cable trim), tailoring engagement to linkage slack.
