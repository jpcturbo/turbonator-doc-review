1. Overview & Purpose

PTUULL defines the lower throttle position sensor delta threshold curve as a function of vehicle speed when the torque converter is currently in the unlocked state.

In conjunction with PTUUUL, PTUULL forms the lower boundary of the throttle position deadband for the unlocked torque converter state. When the converter is unlocked and throttle delta is between the upper and lower limits, the controller decrements the debounce counter Counter_PartThrottleUnlock. If throttle position drops below PTUULL, the controller branches to force converter lockup. In factory calibrations, PTUULL is configured as a single-point baseline table.
