1. Overview & Purpose

PTUCNH defines the high-speed debounce counter reload value for Part Throttle Unlock state transitions.

When the vehicle is traveling at or above PTUMSP (highway cruising speed), rapid cycling of the torque converter clutch must be prevented to avoid driveline shudder and premature clutch wear. When the PTU counter expires, the controller reloads Counter_PartThrottleUnlock from PTUCNH. In factory calibrations, PTUCNH is configured to 93 counts. Because the PTU routine executes every 8 main loop passes, this sets the delay duration that throttle conditions must remain within the hysteresis window before changing lockup state.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Highway Shift Stability:
  • Raise PTUCNH to 110 to 130 counts to hold converter lockup firmly against highway throttle flutter.
  • Accelerated Passing Kickdown:
  • Lower PTUCNH toward 50 to 60 counts for faster converter unlock when tipping into throttle on the freeway.
  • Debounce Filter Integrity:
  • Avoid setting PTUCNH below 30 counts to maintain adequate lockup deadband damping.
