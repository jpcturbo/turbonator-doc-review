1. Overview & Purpose

PTUMSP defines the vehicle speed boundary that splits the Part Throttle Unlock control logic between low-speed city operation and high-speed highway cruising.

Automatic transmission torque converter lockup behavior must balance low-speed drivability against highway fuel efficiency. In factory calibrations, PTUMSP is set to 90 counts, corresponding to approximately 45 MPH on the 0 to 127.5 MPH scale. Below 45 MPH, the controller enforces manifold pressure delta checks (PTUMP3 and PTUMP1/PTUMP2) to prevent torque converter slippage under load, and reloads low-speed counter PTUCNL. Above 45 MPH, manifold pressure checks are bypassed and high-speed counter PTUCNH governs unlock debounce timing.

  • Speed Calculation: VehicleSpeed_HB shifted left 3 times (asld; asld; asld).

  • Line 10484: If speed >= PTUMSP (bcc LD67B), loads PTUCNH; otherwise loads PTUCNL.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Suburban Drivability Tuning:
  • Lower PTUMSP to 35 to 40 MPH to allow earlier converter unlocking for passing maneuvers without reaching highway speeds.
  • Highway Fuel Economy:
  • Raise PTUMSP to 50 MPH to hold torque converter lockup tighter on rolling hills.
  • Counter Selection Interlock:
  • Selects whether PTUCNH or PTUCNL reloads into Counter_PartThrottleUnlock when timer expires.
