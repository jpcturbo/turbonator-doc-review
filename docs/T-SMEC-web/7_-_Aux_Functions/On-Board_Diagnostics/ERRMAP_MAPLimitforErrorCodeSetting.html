1. Overview & Purpose

ERRMAP specifies the manifold vacuum threshold required before the engine controller latches major system fault codes and illuminates the check engine lamp.

Major error confirmation requires that the vehicle be operated under moderate, steady cruising conditions rather than during deep trailing-throttle overrun. During deceleration with the throttle closed, manifold vacuum is extremely high, and airflow through the engine is minimal, which can distort sensor readings.

In factory calibrations, ERRMAP is configured to 43 counts, corresponding to approximately 4.9 psi or roughly 10.1 inHg of manifold vacuum below atmospheric pressure. The engine controller requires measured intake manifold vacuum to be less than ERRMAP (meaning the engine must be under sufficient load) to confirm and latch major trouble codes.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • 3-Bar MAP Sensor Rescaling:
  • Multiply ERRMAP by 0.667 (from 43 counts down to 29 counts) when converting to a 3-bar MAP sensor.
  • Preserves the 10.1 inHg manifold vacuum qualification window.
  • Overrun Suppression:
  • Ensures that deep trailing-throttle deceleration vacuum (18 to 22 inHg vacuum) does not confirm major trouble codes.
  • Edge Cases:
  • Zeroing ERRMAP completely blocks major error code escalation.
  • Setting ERRMAP to maximum allows codes to latch during closed-throttle coastdown.
