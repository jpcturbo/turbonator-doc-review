1. Overview & Purpose

MPDELT establishes the minimum pressure delta between successive sensor readings required to verify that the manifold absolute pressure sensor is dynamically responding to engine airflow changes.

A functioning pressure sensor on an operating engine exhibits minor continuous pressure pulsations caused by intake valve opening events. If a sensor output becomes frozen due to an internal electronic failure or disconnected vacuum line, its reading remains completely static.

In factory calibrations, MPDELT is configured to 1 count, corresponding to approximately 0.12 psi of pressure change. When the measured change between consecutive samples equals or exceeds MPDELT, the engine controller sets an internal flag indicating dynamic pressure activity, which is required both to clear previous fault codes and to validate sensor health.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Sensor Activity Verification:
  • 1 count (0.12 psi) reliably detects the normal pressure ripple of an idling multi-cylinder engine.
  • 3-Bar Sensor Compatibility:
  • 1 count on a 3-bar sensor represents 0.17 psi, which is still easily satisfied by normal intake stroke pressure ripples.
  • Edge Cases:
  • Setting MPDELT to zero marks the sensor as dynamically active even when disconnected or frozen.
  • Setting MPDELT too high prevents the engine controller from clearing Fault Code 13 after a repaired vacuum line.
