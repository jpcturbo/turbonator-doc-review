1. Overview & Purpose

THROFF introduces a calibrated voltage bias to the measured throttle position signal prior to looking up simulated manifold absolute pressure during MAP sensor limp-home mode.

When the primary manifold pressure sensor fails, the engine controller must deduce engine load from throttle position. Because small throttle openings produce nonlinear airflow changes around the closed throttle plate, a small positive offset is needed so the table lookup responds immediately to driver tip-in.

In factory calibrations, THROFF is set to 11 counts, corresponding to approximately 0.22 volts or 4.3 percent throttle angle. This bias ensures prompt fueling enrichment and prevents off-idle hesitation when the vehicle is driven under emergency limp-home conditions.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Throttle Body Upgrades:
  • When upgrading from the stock 46mm throttle body to a 52mm or 58mm aftermarket unit, lowering THROFF from 11 counts down to 6 to 8 counts prevents over-enrichment during light-throttle tip-in.
  • Tip-In Hesitation Tuning:
  • If the vehicle exhibits a flat spot or hesitation off idle when testing limp-in mode, raising THROFF provides earlier load recognition.
  • Lowering & Edge Cases:
  • Zeroing THROFF delays synthetic load enrichment on throttle tip-in.
  • Setting THROFF to maximum causes continuous high-load enrichment even at closed throttle.
