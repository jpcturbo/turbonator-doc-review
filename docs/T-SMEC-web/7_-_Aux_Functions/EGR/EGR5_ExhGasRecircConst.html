1. Overview & Purpose

EGR5 (ExhGasRecircConst) establishes the per-trip diagnostic retry limit before the engine controller inhibits further Exhaust Gas Recirculation flow verification attempts for the remainder of the current driving cycle.

Following a Phase 2 timeout, the controller increments a retry counter stored in the low nibble of EGRVar2. At line 5532, this counter is compared against EGR5. If the count reaches or exceeds EGR5, the controller sets bit 6 of EGRVar2, which actively latches a test inhibit flag.

Once bit 6 is set, the routine bypasses the diagnostic state machine on all subsequent execution loops until the ignition switch is cycled. The factory calibration of 0 counts causes the inhibit flag to latch after the initial sequence, preventing continuous diagnostic cycles during a single trip.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Enclosing Routine: ExhGasRecirc_MM (lines 5383–5545), specifically at line 5532.
  • Trigger Site: L1471 (line 5525), executed after failure counter check.
  • State Variable: Low nibble of EGRVar2 (RAM address 0x009E), masked with anda #bit_bothalf.
  • Comparison: cmpa EGR5_ExhGasRecircConst at line 5532.
  • Inhibit Latch: If EGRVar2_low >= EGR5, executes orab #bit_bit6 at line 5534, latching the drive-cycle test inhibit flag in EGRVar2.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Raising EGR5: Increases the number of diagnostic retry attempts permitted per ignition cycle. Helpful in urban driving where traffic interruptions frequently abort Phase 2 before completion.
  • Factory Setting (0 counts): Sets bit 6 on the first diagnostic cycle, locking out further testing until the next ignition key cycle to maintain smooth cruising.
  • Low-Nibble Counter: Evaluated against the lower 4 bits of EGRVar2, limiting practical calibration values to between 0 and 15.
  • Trip Inhibit Interlock: Governs bit 6 of EGRVar2, which is checked at line 5409 upon every pass of ExhGasRecirc_MM.

4. Technical / Assembly Mechanics (Reference)

Inside T-SMEC_v19.asm: At L1471 (line 5525), accumulator B loads EGRVar2 and clears bit 7 (andb #~bit_bit7) to exit Phase 2. Accumulator A receives B, increments via inca, and isolates the lower 4 bits via anda #bit_bothalf (0x0F) at line 5529. If zero, it branches to L1472. Otherwise, it increments B (incb) and executes cmpa EGR5_ExhGasRecircConst at line 5532. If accumulator A is less than EGR5 (factory 0x00), bcs L1473 branches around latching. If A meets or exceeds EGR5, line 5534 executes orab #bit_bit6, setting the trip inhibit flag in EGRVar2. Line 5535 stores B back to EGRVar2 (stab EGRVar2). On subsequent passes, line 5409 tests bit 6 and branches to EGR_ResetEGRVars.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Single-Byte Type 3 Constant: Located at calibration address 0x1AA3 (calculated ROM address 0x86E6 / template org 0x882E).
  • Drive Cycle Persistence: Bit 6 of EGRVar2 remains set until engine shutoff clears unbacked zero-page RAM.
  • Inhibit Mechanism: When bit 6 is active, line 5409 branches directly to EGR_ResetEGRVars, keeping the EGR solenoid on while bypassing all diagnostic checks.
