1. Overview & Purpose

EGR14 (ExhGasRecircConstFromTPSVoltsLoLimit) establishes the lower throttle position threshold for the Phase 1 Exhaust Gas Recirculation diagnostic baseline sampling window.

The engine controller must ensure that the vehicle is operating under genuine part-throttle cruising conditions before recording baseline oxygen sensor behavior. At line 5431, the controller subtracts the minimum learned closed-throttle voltage from current throttle voltage, and tests the delta against EGR14.

The factory setting of 5 counts corresponds to approximately 0.10 volts (roughly 2 percent throttle opening). If throttle position drops below this threshold (such as when the driver lifts off the accelerator into idle or deceleration), Phase 1 immediately aborts and resets, preventing deceleration fueling effects from corrupting the EGR baseline.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Enclosing Routine: ExhGasRecirc_MM (lines 5383–5545), specifically at line 5434.
  • Subroutine Called: EGR_CheckHiLoLimits (line 5462).
  • Throttle Calculation: TpsVolts - MINTHR_LowestSessionTPS evaluated in accumulator A.
  • Early Inhibit: Line 5433 tests bls EGR_ResetEGRVars; if throttle delta is zero or negative, aborts immediately.
  • Boundary Check: If throttle delta < EGR14 (bcs L1464), subroutine pulls stack and aborts via EGR_ResetEGRVars.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Raising EGR14: Demands more throttle pedal opening before Phase 1 baseline recording begins. Recommended for large throttle body conversions where 2 percent throttle represents a larger fraction of total engine airflow.
  • Lowering EGR14: Allows baseline recording at very light throttle cruise. Do not lower to zero, as closed-throttle idle transitions will destabilize the baseline calculation.
  • EGR Elimination (255 counts): Setting EGR14 to 255 creates an impossible lower throttle gate that prevents Phase 1 from ever completing, permanently suppressing Fault Code 32 without setting error flags.
  • Factory Calibration: 5 counts (approx 0.10 volts / 2 percent throttle) reliably distinguishes cruise from closed-throttle deceleration.
