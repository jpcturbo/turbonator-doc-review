1. Overview & Purpose

ACCRPM defines the maximum engine speed lockout threshold for air conditioning compressor clutch engagement.

Automotive reciprocating piston and rotary vane air conditioning compressors are engineered with strict rotational speed limits. Spinning an automotive A/C compressor beyond 4500 to 5000 engine RPM generates severe internal friction, reed valve flutter, excessive head pressure, and clutch bearing failure. In factory calibrations, ACCRPM is configured to 4000 RPM (32000 counts in 16-bit word format). If engine speed exceeds 4000 RPM, the controller prevents the A/C clutch from engaging, protecting the mechanical compressor and accessory drive belt during high-RPM driving.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Compressor Protection Ceiling:
  • Never calibrate ACCRPM above 4500 RPM to protect internal reed valves and clutch bearings.

  • Keep ACCRPM at least 500 RPM above normal 70 MPH highway cruising engine speed to maintain cabin cooling.
