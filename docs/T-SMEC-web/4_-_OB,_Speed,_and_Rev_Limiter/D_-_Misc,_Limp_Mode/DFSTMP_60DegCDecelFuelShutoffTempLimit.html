1. Overview & Purpose

DFSTMP establishes the minimum engine coolant temperature required to permit secondary deceleration fuel shutoff.

When an engine is cold or still warming up, cutting fuel injection completely on trailing throttle can cause intake runner fuel puddling to evaporate, leading to severe lean misfires, cylinder wall washing on fuel restoration, or engine stalling when the clutch is disengaged. In factory calibrations, DFSTMP is set to 192 counts, which corresponds to approximately 140 °F (60 degrees Celsius). Until engine coolant reaches this stable operating threshold, the controller inhibits deceleration fuel shutoff through this routine, ensuring smooth warmup drivability.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Drivability Protection:
  • Inhibit decel fuel cut during warmup to prevent tip-in stumbling and stalling on large aftermarket injectors.
  • Elevating Warmup Threshold:
  • Elevating DFSTMP to 200 counts (approx 160 °F) ensures the combustion chamber is thoroughly heat-soaked before fuel cuts engage.
  • Disabling Option:
  • Setting DFSTMP to 255 counts locks out this deceleration branch across all temperatures.
