1. Overview & Purpose

SPDRPM2 establishes the lower engine speed recovery threshold that disarms the overspeed fuel shutoff condition.

If the vehicle speed limiter triggers fuel cutout at high speed, both vehicle speed and engine RPM begin to drop. While SPDLML provides the road speed recovery setpoint, SPDRPM2 provides an alternate, engine-speed-based recovery path. If engine speed drops below SPDRPM2 (119 counts, or approximately 3800 RPM in factory calibrations), the controller immediately restores fuel injection, even if road speed has not yet decelerated all the way down to SPDLML. This ensures that an upshift or sudden engine deceleration immediately restores power to maintain vehicle control.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: CHECK_IFOVER_1850_2000_6167RPM (T-SMEC_v19.asm:5802-5812), line 5806.
  • Gating Conditions:
  • Evaluated when Turbonator_MainFuelOff is set and EngineRpm_HB has dropped below the active rev limit restore threshold.
  • Comparison & Restoration:
  • EngineRpm_HB is compared against SPDRPM2 (cmpa SPDRPM2_EngineSpeedLowLimitToDisableOverspeed).
  • If EngineRpm_HB <= SPDRPM2 (bls Cutout_TurnFuelBackOn), fuel injection is restored immediately without waiting for road speed to fall below SPDLML.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Overspeed Recovery Gap:
  • Maintain a 150 to 200 RPM margin below SPDRPM to prevent hunting on high-speed deceleration.
  • Proportional Raising:
  • If raising SPDRPM, elevate SPDRPM2 equally to ensure the engine speed disarm point tracks properly.
  • Inversion Prevention:
  • Never set SPDRPM2 greater than or equal to SPDRPM.
