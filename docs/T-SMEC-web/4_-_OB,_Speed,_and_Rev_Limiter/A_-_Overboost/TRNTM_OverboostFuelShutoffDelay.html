1. Overview & Purpose

TRNTIM defines the duration of the countdown timer that delays overboost fuel shutoff when manifold pressure exceeds the primary timed overboost threshold (MPSHFT).

Turbochargers frequently produce transient pressure overshoots when the throttle snaps open or during quick gear shifts before the wastegate actuator can physically react and stabilize boost. In the factory calibration, TRNTIM is set to 45 counts, corresponding to approximately 0.49 seconds on a 0 to 2.8 second scale. As long as manifold boost drops back below MPSHFT within this half-second window, normal fueling continues uninterrupted. If boost persists above MPSHFT after the countdown expires, the controller cuts fuel injection to protect the engine.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: Cutout_ResetOverboostTimer (T-SMEC_v19.asm:5651-5655), called from ControlEngineCutout_MM (T-SMEC_v19.asm:5641, 5658).
  • Reload Conditions:
  • Reloaded into Timer_OverboostCountdown whenever gauge boost is negative (MapValue < BaroPressure) or boost is below MPSHFT.
  • Also reloaded in Cutout_ResetOverboostTimerAgain (T-SMEC_v19.asm:5758-5761) during hysteresis recovery.
  • Countdown Operation:
  • Decremented once per main loop pass in Cutout_CheckNormalOB (T-SMEC_v19.asm:5661-5664) while boost exceeds MPSHFT but remains below MPSHFA.
  • When Timer_OverboostCountdown reaches zero (beq Cutout_SetOBCodeAndShutdown), fault code 0x0708 is recorded and fuel is shut off.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Extending for Transient Spikes:
  • Increasing TRNTIM to 1.0 to 1.5 seconds (90 to 140 counts) allows brief boost overshoots during fast gear shifts or aggressive spool-up without triggering fuel cut.
  • Disabling or Maxing Out:
  • Setting TRNTIM to 255 counts (approx 2.8 seconds) maximizes the delay window before timed fuel cut engages.
  • Lowering & Hair-Trigger Cutout:
  • Setting TRNTIM to low values (under 20 counts) produces instant fuel cuts on minor boost flutter, degrading drivability.
