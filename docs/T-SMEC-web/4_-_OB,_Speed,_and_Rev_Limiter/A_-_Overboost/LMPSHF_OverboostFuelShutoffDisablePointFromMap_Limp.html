1. Overview & Purpose

LMPSHF establishes the overboost fuel cutout threshold used whenever the engine controller detects an electrical fault in the wastegate solenoid circuit.

If the wastegate solenoid wiring is damaged, disconnected, or shorted, the controller cannot actively modulate boost pressure and loses electronic wastegate bleed control. Under these compromised conditions, the normal two-stage timed overboost routine (MPSHFT and MPSHFA) is completely bypassed. Instead, the controller compares manifold boost directly against LMPSHF. In factory calibrations, LMPSHF is set to 115 counts, representing approximately 13.3 psi of boost above atmospheric pressure. If boost exceeds LMPSHF while a wastegate solenoid fault is flagged, fuel injection is cut instantly on the current engine rotation without any timer delay.

2. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

  • Subroutine & Line Numbers: Cutout_CheckForOBConditions (T-SMEC_v19.asm:5638-5645), called from ControlEngineCutout_MM (T-SMEC_v19.asm:5628).
  • Fault Flag Test:
  • Evaluated when b46_IPL2 bit b46_WGSolFault is set (T-SMEC_v19.asm:5642).
  • If b46_WGSolFault is clear, execution branches to Cutout_CheckNormalOB.
  • Comparison & Branch:
  • Manifold gauge boost (MapValue - BaroPressure) is compared against LMPSHF (cmpa LMPSHF_OverboostFuelShutoffDisablePointFromMap_Limp).
  • If boost is lower or same (bls Cutout_CheckRevLimiters), execution continues to rev limiter checks.
  • If boost exceeds LMPSHF, execution falls through directly to Cutout_ShutOffEngineForThisRotation (T-SMEC_v19.asm:5646-5649).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Failsafe Preservation:
  • Retain LMPSHF as an emergency protection ceiling in case the boost control solenoid wiring is compromised or unplugged.
  • Proportional Adjustment for High Boost:
  • When raising target boost and MPSHFT, elevate LMPSHF to match the mechanical spring pressure of the upgraded wastegate actuator.
  • 3-Bar MAP Scaling:
  • Rescale LMPSHF when migrating to a 3-bar sensor to maintain appropriate emergency pressure limits on the 44.1 psi scale.

  • No Timer Grace Period: Unlike normal overboost, limp-mode overboost cuts fuel immediately on single-rotation detection without waiting for TRNTM.
  • Hysteresis Recovery: In Cutout_UseHysteresisToDetermineWhenToTurnFuelBackOn at line 5754, fuel remains off until gauge boost falls below LMPSHF.
