1. Overview & Purpose

REVLMT6 defines the primary engine speed redline fuel cutout threshold during normal, fault-free vehicle operation.

To prevent valvetrain separation, valve-to-piston contact from valve float, and excessive mechanical stress on pistons and connecting rods, the Chrysler engine controller constantly monitors distributor ignition pulse periods. When engine speed accelerates to REVLMT6 (194 counts, or approximately 6200 RPM in factory automatic calibrations, and 6464 RPM in manual calibrations), the controller instantly terminates fuel injector firing for the current engine revolution. Fuel remains deactivated until the engine speed decelerates below the companion recovery setpoint (REVLMT5).

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Valvetrain-Limited Redline:
  • Stock Chrysler 8-valve engines encounter valve float above 6300 RPM. Raise REVLMT6 only after installing stiffer valve springs and lightweight retainers.
  • Performance Calibration:
  • For built cylinder heads, raise REVLMT6 to 6800 to 7200 RPM (212 to 225 counts) to extend power through the traps.
  • Companion Update:
  • Always raise REVLMT5 when elevating REVLMT6 to maintain a functional hysteresis deadband.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Primary Rev Limiter Pair: REVLMT6 is the upper cutout limit paired with REVLMT5 as the lower restore limit.
  • RPM Scaling: Scaled at 32 RPM per count ($8160 / 255$). 194 counts equals 194 × 32 = 6208 RPM.
  • Stage IV Setting: In Stage IV calibrations, REVLMT6 is set to 219 counts (7008 RPM) with REVLMT5 at 218 counts (6976 RPM).
