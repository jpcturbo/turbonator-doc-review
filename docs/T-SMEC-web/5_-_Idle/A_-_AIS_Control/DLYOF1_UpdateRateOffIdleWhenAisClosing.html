1. Overview & Purpose

DLYOF1 defines the main program loop counter bitmask that paces the rate at which the automatic idle speed stepper motor is permitted to step closed during off-idle driving.

When the driver operates the throttle pedal off-idle, the stepper motor is dynamically pre-positioned open based on engine speed and throttle angle. When vehicle operating conditions change and require the motor to close down to a lower step position, snapping the stepper motor shut too quickly would cause sudden manifold vacuum drops and abrupt driveline shock.

DLYOF1 acts as an off-idle rate limiter, restricting stepper motor closing movement to one step decrement only when the masked loop counter reaches zero.

In factory calibrations, DLYOF1 is set to 7 counts, representing a binary bitmask allowing the motor to close by one step every 8 main loop cycles, or approximately every 88 milliseconds.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Smoothing Off-Idle Jerk:
  • Increase DLYOF1 from 7 to 15 counts to slow off-idle stepper closure, smoothing part-throttle transitions and gentle deceleration.
  • Sharpening Throttle Response:
  • Lower DLYOF1 to 3 counts if you desire faster stepper retraction and more direct engine braking when modulating the throttle.
  • Mask Formatting:
  • Must be configured as a power-of-two minus one value (3, 7, 15, 31) to ensure proper modulo masking with the main loop counter.
