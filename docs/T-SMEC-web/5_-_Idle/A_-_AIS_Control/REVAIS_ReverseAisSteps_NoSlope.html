1. Overview & Purpose

REVAIS is the proportional correction lookup table that commands closing steps on the automatic idle speed stepper motor whenever engine speed exceeds target idle.

During idle operation, sudden reductions in engine load (such as the cooling fan turning off or shifting from Drive into Neutral) can cause engine speed to flare above the desired setpoint. The SMEC continuously calculates overspeed error by subtracting target idle speed from actual engine speed.

REVAIS indexes this overspeed delta to determine how many closing steps (negative values) to apply to bring engine speed back down to target.

In factory calibrations, REVAIS includes a 30 RPM deadband where 0 steps are taken, followed by -1 step at 40 RPM error, -2 steps at 70 RPM error, -3 steps at 100 RPM error, and -4 steps at 160 RPM error.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Curing High Idle Hanging:
  • If engine speed hangs above 1200 RPM after releasing the accelerator, increase the closing step magnitude in REVAIS at 70 to 100 RPM error.
  • Preventing Undershoot Choke:
  • If the engine plunges past target idle and nearly stalls when returning to idle, reduce the closing steps in REVAIS to create a gentler landing.
  • Symmetry with FWDAIS:
  • Maintain balanced proportional authority between FWDAIS (opening) and REVAIS (closing) to prevent cyclic idle hunting.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Two's Complement Scaling: Step values in REVAIS are stored as two's complement negative integers (e.g., 0xFF = -1 step, 0xFE = -2 steps, 0xFD = -3 steps, 0xFC = -4 steps).
  • Companion Parameter: Direct counterpart to FWDAIS.
  • Deadband Width: First entry (0x1E) establishes an identical 30 RPM error deadband before closing steps are triggered.
