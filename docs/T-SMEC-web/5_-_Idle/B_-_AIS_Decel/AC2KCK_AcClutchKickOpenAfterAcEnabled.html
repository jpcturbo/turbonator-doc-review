1. Overview & Purpose

AC2KCK defines the pre-emptive opening step kick added to the idle air stepper motor when the air conditioning compressor clutch is commanded to engage.

When the air conditioning system calls for cooling, the magnetic compressor clutch locks up, suddenly imposing heavy mechanical pumping drag on the engine. If the engine controller waited for engine speed to drop before reacting, closed-loop feedback would be too slow, resulting in a severe idle speed dip or stall.

AC2KCK pre-emptively steps the idle air motor open at the exact instant the compressor clutch is energized.

In factory calibrations, AC2KCK is set to 4 counts, injecting an immediate 4-step opening kick to absorb the compressor engagement shock.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Curing A/C Engagement RPM Sag:
  • Increase AC2KCK from 4 to 7 to 10 steps if engine speed dips noticeably when the compressor clicks on at idle.
  • Lightweight Flywheel Drag Cushion:
  • Lightened rotational mass is vulnerable to compressor shock; higher AC2KCK values maintain smooth idle stability.
  • Preventing Engagement Flare:
  • If the engine revs up abruptly upon A/C engagement, trim AC2KCK down to 3 or 4 steps.
