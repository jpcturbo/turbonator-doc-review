1. Overview & Purpose

DRPNK is a 2D calibration curve that establishes the closing step reduction applied to the idle air stepper motor when shifting an automatic transmission from Drive into Park or Neutral.

While in gear, the idle air motor is held open further to overcome the hydraulic viscous drag of the torque converter. When the driver shifts into Park or Neutral, this converter load vanishes instantly. If the extra in-gear airflow were not removed immediately, the unloaded engine would flare to high RPM.

DRPNK commands an instantaneous reduction in stepper motor opening steps as a function of engine coolant temperature.

In factory calibrations, DRPNK commands a large step reduction during cold warm-up (approximately 15 to 20 steps) that tapers down to 6 to 8 steps when the engine is fully warmed up.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Eliminating Park/Neutral Flare:
  • Increase DRPNK step reduction by 3 to 6 steps if shifting out of gear produces a noticeable flare above 1200 RPM.
  • Preventing Shift Undershoot:
  • If engine speed dives below target idle when shifting into Neutral, reduce DRPNK closing steps.
  • Seamless Gear Transitions:
  • Properly balanced DRPNK and PNDRK curves produce completely flat, seamless gear shifts without RPM flaring or sagging.
