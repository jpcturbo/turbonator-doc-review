1. Overview & Purpose

ACTIME defines the transition delay timer that paces the blending of air conditioning idle airflow compensation during clutch engagement.

When the air conditioning system calls for compressor engagement, applying the entire auxiliary airflow offset instantaneously can cause a momentary engine surge before compressor head pressure builds up. ACTIME establishes a calibrated delay timer loaded into the A/C transition counter upon clutch engagement.

During this delay, auxiliary idle airflow is smoothly ramped into the calculation rather than applied as an abrupt shock.

In factory calibrations, ACTIME is set to 36 counts, providing a smooth transition blending window of approximately 2.25 seconds.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Smoothing A/C Engagement Surge:
  • Lengthen ACTIME to 3.0 seconds if the engine surges upward slightly when the A/C button is pressed before settling.
  • Eliminating Engagement Lag:
  • Shorten ACTIME to 1.5 seconds if the full A/C idle air offset arrives too late to catch compressor drag.
  • Timer Operation:
