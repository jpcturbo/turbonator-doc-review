1. Overview & Purpose

DLYTM3 defines the extended time delay that the engine controller holds the idle air stepper motor in open dashpot position when the throttle snaps shut at a standstill with engine speed above DLYRPM.

When an engine is revved up to high RPM in neutral and the throttle is abruptly released, the rotating assembly generates massive momentum, and intake manifold vacuum plunges violently. To safely catch the engine from high RPM without risking a severe vacuum undershoot stall, the controller enforces an extended dashpot delay before allowing closed-loop idle feedback to take over.

Whenever closed throttle is detected at low vehicle speed (below MPHREF) and engine speed is at or above DLYRPM, the controller loads DLYTM3 into the feedback lockout timer.

In factory calibrations, DLYTM3 is set to 255 counts, providing a prolonged dashpot hold of approximately 16 seconds until engine speed settles safely.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Crisper Free-Rev RPM Return:
  • Lower DLYTM3 from 255 counts (~16 seconds) down to 50 to 80 counts (~3 to 5 seconds) to eliminate excessive idle hang after revving the engine in neutral.
  • High-RPM Neutral Stall Protection:
  • Ensure DLYTM3 remains at or above 40 counts (~2.5 seconds) so aggressive camshafts have adequate time to catch descending revs.
  • Threshold Trigger:
  • Only triggers when engine speed exceeds DLYRPM (factory ~1500 RPM) at standstill.
