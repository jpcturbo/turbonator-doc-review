1. Overview & Purpose

ACAETH defines the lockout delay timer that prevents the air conditioning compressor clutch from engaging immediately following an acceleration enrichment transient.

When the driver steps into the throttle to accelerate, the fuel injection system delivers acceleration enrichment to prevent a lean tip-in stumble. If the air conditioning clutch were permitted to snap shut in the middle of this throttle tip-in transient, the sudden mechanical load shock would absorb engine torque, causing a perceptible hesitation or stumble.

ACAETH enforces a mandatory waiting delay: whenever acceleration enrichment is active, the controller reloads the clutch restriction timer with ACAETH, keeping the compressor disengaged until fueling and combustion stabilize.

In factory calibrations, ACAETH is set to 45 counts, enforcing a tip-in lockout delay of approximately 2.8 seconds.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Curing Tip-In Acceleration Stumble:
  • Increase ACAETH from 2.8 to 3.5 to 4.5 seconds if the vehicle hesitates when stepping on the throttle with the A/C running.
  • Low Setting Drivability Hazard:
  • Setting ACAETH below 1.0 second allows the compressor to engage during turbo spool-up, causing an annoying torque sag.
  • Timer Operation:
  • Loaded into Timer_AcClutchEngageRestrictionTimer, which decrements every loop clock tick.
