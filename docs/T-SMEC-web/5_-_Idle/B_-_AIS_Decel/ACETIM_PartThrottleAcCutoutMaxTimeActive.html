1. Overview & Purpose

ACETIM defines the maximum duration that part-throttle air conditioning compressor cutout is permitted to remain continuously active.

When part-throttle A/C cutout engages during acceleration, the compressor clutch disengages to shed mechanical drag. However, if the driver continues to drive at part-throttle for an extended period (such as climbing a long mountain grade), holding the compressor off continuously would cause the cabin evaporator to warm up and blow hot, humid air.

ACETIM acts as a maximum timeout limiter: once the cutout timer reaches ACETIM, the controller overrides the cutout and forces the compressor clutch to re-engage.

In factory calibrations, ACETIM is set to 8 counts, enforcing a maximum cutout window of approximately 0.5 seconds.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Extending Acceleration Boost Window:
  • Increase ACETIM to 5.0 to 10.0 seconds to allow the compressor to remain disengaged throughout an entire highway passing maneuver.
