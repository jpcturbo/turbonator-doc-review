1. Overview & Purpose

DLYTIM defines the time delay that the engine controller holds the idle air stepper motor in its open dashpot position after the throttle snaps shut while the vehicle is moving.

When the driver releases the accelerator pedal while driving at road speed, holding the idle air valve open momentarily cushions engine deceleration, smooths the transition into closed-throttle overrun, and prevents sudden driveline snatch.

Whenever closed throttle is detected and vehicle speed is above the rolling switch point set in MPHREF, the controller reloads the feedback lockout timer with DLYTIM.

In factory calibrations, DLYTIM is set to 18 counts, enforcing an open dashpot hold delay of approximately 1.1 seconds while moving.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Smoothing Driveline Snatch:
  • Increase DLYTIM to 1.5 to 2.0 seconds to eliminate harsh forward-and-back driveline bucking when lifting off the throttle at highway speeds.
  • Enhancing Engine Braking:
  • Lower DLYTIM to 0.5 to 0.8 seconds if you prefer sharper, immediate engine vacuum braking on throttle lift.
  • Preventing Shift Hang:
  • Setting DLYTIM excessively high makes RPM linger between gear shifts on manual transmission vehicles.
