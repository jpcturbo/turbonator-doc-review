1. Overview & Purpose

ACTIM2 defines the secondary transition delay timer utilized during alternate air conditioning clutch engagement paths.

During certain operational transitions (such as re-engaging the compressor following a part-throttle cutout or recovering from overrun), the idle air motor requires a calibrated blending delay to smoothly integrate the A/C idle air offset.

ACTIM2 reloads the A/C transition timer upon entering the secondary engagement branch, ensuring that airflow transitions remain gradual and do not induce driveline shudder.

In factory calibrations, ACTIM2 is set to 36 counts, providing an engagement blending window of approximately 2.25 seconds.
