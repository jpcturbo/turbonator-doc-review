1. Overview & Purpose

AISLMH defines the upper step limit offset added to baseline idle position to clamp the maximum opening of the idle stepper motor during rolling idle mode.

While operating in rolling idle mode, closed-loop feedback dynamically adjusts stepper motor position to match target rolling idle speed. If an unexpected load or manifold pressure transient occurs, or if the driver coasts downhill with the throttle closed, unconstrained closed-loop feedback could drive the stepper motor open to near-maximum travel, causing the engine to flare loudly upon clutching in.

AISLMH establishes a protective upper boundary: the desired motor position during rolling idle is never permitted to exceed baseline steps plus AISLMH.

In factory calibrations, AISLMH is set to 32 counts, capping rolling idle opening at 32 steps above baseline.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Preventing Rolling Idle Flare:
  • Lower AISLMH from 32 to 16 to 20 steps if engine speed flares upward while coasting in neutral.
  • Heavy Load Headroom:
  • Increase AISLMH to 40 to 45 steps if large dual cooling fans pull down rolling idle speed below target.
  • Pairing with AISLML:
  • Forms the upper boundary of the rolling idle window; balance against AISLML (lower floor).
