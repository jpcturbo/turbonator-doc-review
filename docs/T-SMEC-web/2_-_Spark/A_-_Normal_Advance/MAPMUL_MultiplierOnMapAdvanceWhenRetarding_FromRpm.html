1. Overview & Purpose

MAPMUL defines an RPM-indexed scaling multiplier applied to negative manifold pressure spark advance (boost retard).

In turbocharged Chrysler calibrations, ignition advance from the MAP tables (HOTMAP and WOTMAP) becomes negative under boost, meaning timing is subtracted from the base engine speed curve (GOVNER). Because cylinder volumetric efficiency, charge air heating, and detonation tendencies increase at elevated engine speeds, the engine controller scales the negative MAP retard by MAPMUL(RPM) to apply greater timing reduction at higher RPM. In factory calibrations, MAPMUL scales retard by approximately 1.0x to 1.75x across the engine speed range.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Flat 1.0x Multiplier (T2 Strategy):
  • Setting all MAPMUL points to a 1.0x multiplier factor (40 counts) removes RPM-dependent retard multiplication, allowing the tuner to shape boost timing solely within WOTMAP.
  • High-RPM Knock Suppression:
  • Increasing MAPMUL values above 4500 RPM adds progressive retard at high engine speeds to protect against detonation from elevated intake charge temperatures.
  • Zeroing or Lowering Cautions:
  • Setting MAPMUL too low reduces commanded boost retard, effectively advancing total ignition timing under high boost.
