1. Overview & Purpose

ARMINT defines the minimum coolant temperature threshold required for the engine controller to learn adaptive knock retard.

During cold engine operation and initial warm-up, combustion chamber temperatures are uneven, forged pistons may exhibit piston slap chatter, and fuel atomization is imperfect. Allowing the controller to learn long-term adaptive knock retard while the engine is cold could result in false knock learning that inappropriately pulls timing after the engine reaches full operating temperature. In factory calibrations, ARMINT is set to 166 counts, corresponding to approximately 187 degF, locking out adaptive retard learning until the engine is thoroughly heat-soaked at normal operating temperature.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Thermostat Adaptation:
  • If running a 160 degF thermostat, lower ARMINT from 187 degF down to approximately 165 degF (155 counts) to allow adaptive retard learning to function.
  • Cold False Knock Prevention:
  • Keeping ARMINT above 175 degF prevents mechanical piston slap or cold valvetrain noise from falsely learning permanent retard.
  • Timer Clear on Cold:
  • If coolant temperature is at or below ARMINT, the boost knock timer is instantly cleared to zero.
