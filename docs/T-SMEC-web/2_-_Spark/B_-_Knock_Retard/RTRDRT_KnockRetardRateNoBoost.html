1. Overview & Purpose

RTRDRT defines the step-size of knock retard applied to a knocking cylinder when knock is detected out of boost (under vacuum or light load below KNKOFS).

When combustion knock occurs under light load or partial-throttle cruising, cylinder charge density is relatively low. Under these conditions, pulling a substantial, decisive step of ignition timing quickly extinguishes the knock without significantly disturbing vehicle momentum or causing driveline shudder. In factory calibrations, RTRDRT is set to 6 counts, corresponding to 3.0 degrees of retard applied to the active cylinder's individual retard variable upon knock detection.

  • Sum is clamped to 0x3F (63 counts ≈ 31.5 degrees max) and compared against MaxRetardFromMAP.

3. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Decisive Knock Suppression:
  • Factory setting of 3.0 degrees (6 counts) pulls enough timing on the first detection to snuff out light-load knock immediately.
  • Drivability vs. Response:
  • Raising RTRDRT above 4.0 degrees can cause noticeable hesitation on tip-in ping; lowering below 2.0 degrees risks repeat pinging.
  • Per-Cylinder Application:
  • Applied strictly to the specific cylinder that knocked, preserving full timing on the other three cylinders.
