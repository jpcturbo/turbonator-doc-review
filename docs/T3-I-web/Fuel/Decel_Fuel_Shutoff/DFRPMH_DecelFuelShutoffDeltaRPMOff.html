1. Overview & Purpose

DFRPMH defines the engine speed delta above target idle speed at which fuel injection is actively shut off during deceleration. While DCLRPH arms the decel logic, DFRPMH is the execution threshold tested inside the foreground fuel cutout routine. When the decel fuel cut flag is armed, the difference between actual engine RPM and target idle RPM is compared against DFRPMH. If delta RPM exceeds DFRPMH, the controller sets Turbonator_MainFuelOff, cutting fuel injection to all cylinders. In factory calibrations, DFRPMH is set to 30 counts, representing 960 RPM above target idle speed.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Decel Cutoff Onset: * Controls the exact RPM delta where injector pulses cease upon throttle lift-off.
  • Smoothness vs Economy: * Higher values provide softer deceleration with less driveline lash; lower values maximize fuel savings and engine braking.
  • Exhaust Sound Tuning: * If trailing throttle causes harsh exhaust pops or backfiring, raising DFRPMH softens the transition into fuel cut.
  • Hysteresis Relationship: * Must exceed DFRPML (fuel turn-on threshold) by at least 8 to 12 counts to prevent injector chatter.
