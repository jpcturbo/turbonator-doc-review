1. Overview & Purpose

DCLMER defines the minimum engine run time required after startup before decel fuel shutoff logic is permitted to activate. Immediately following engine start, combustion chamber temperatures are stabilizing, oil pressure is building, and idle speed is settling. DCLMER enforces an initial grace period where fuel injection is never cut on deceleration, ensuring smooth drivability when maneuvering out of a driveway or parking space immediately after starting. In factory calibrations, DCLMER is set to 2 counts, corresponding to approximately 2 seconds of engine run time.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Post-Start Stall Prevention: * Increase DCLMER to 5-10 seconds if the engine hesitates or stalls when closing the throttle shortly after startup.
  • Cold Driveaway Cushioning: * Ensures the engine has established stable manifold vacuum before fuel cutouts engage.
  • Disarming Decel Logic: * If run time has not reached DCLMER, the decel fuel cut flag is forced clear.
  • Companion Qualifications: * Evaluated directly ahead of DCLMCT in the primary decel fuel qualification sequence.
