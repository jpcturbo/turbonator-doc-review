1. Overview & Purpose

PEFTBL: PEFTBL defines the closed-throttle and idle engine pumping efficiency (volumetric efficiency) curve versus engine speed (RPM) for the Lotus 16-valve DOHC Turbo III engine. In factory Turbo III calibrations (T-T3v1 and T-T3_II), PEFTBL is configured as a single-point flat 100% curve (.byte 0x01, .byte 0xFF, 0xFF). On the Lotus 16V engine, the factory Chrysler/Lotus calibration engineers chose not to scale base fuel delivery by an RPM-dependent pumping efficiency multiplier as was standard on Chrysler 8-valve SOHC engines. Instead, fuel shaping is modeled directly within the primary 2D speed-density MAP tables. At closed throttle, the 68HC11 CPU selects PEFTBL when the b66_OffIdle bit in BitFlags66 is clear and executes ScaleYbyA. Because the table data contains 0xFF (representing a 100% unity multiplier), it acts as a neutral pass-through in the stock calibration.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Tuning Strategy: In factory Turbo III calibrations, PEFTBL is set to 100% flat (0xFF) and is not actively used to shape volumetric efficiency. For almost all street and race tuning applications, leave PEFTBL at its stock 100% flat curve. Closed-throttle idle fueling should be adjusted using the idle target AFR, injector offset constants, and low-vacuum MAP cells rather than modifying PEFTBL. If tuning an engine with radical camshaft overlap where low-RPM idle cylinder filling is severely degraded, lowering PEFTBL values at idle speeds leans out closed-throttle fueling to prevent rich idle hunting.
  • Raising the Value: Commands higher volumetric efficiency multiplier, richening fueling across all manifold pressures at that RPM.
  • Lowering the Value: Commands lower volumetric efficiency multiplier, leaning out fueling across all manifold pressures at that RPM.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during closed-throttle idle and decel coastdown on the Lotus 16-valve Turbo III engine, evaluating baseline engine breathing efficiency. In factory calibrations, its flat 100% setting acts as a neutral multiplier.

5. Calibration Notes & System Dependencies

  • Factory 100% Neutral Setting: Factory setting is a 1-point table at 0xFF (100% multiplier), meaning closed-throttle volumetric efficiency is unscaled in stock form.
  • Lotus 16V Architecture: Primary volumetric efficiency modeling is handled by the 2D MAP fuel tables rather than RPM curves.
  • Idle Gating: Evaluated when b66_OffIdle (bit 7 of BitFlags66) is clear; when off-idle, PEFTBL2 is evaluated instead.
