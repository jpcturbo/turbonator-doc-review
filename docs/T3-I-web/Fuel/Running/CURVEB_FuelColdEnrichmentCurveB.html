1. Overview & Purpose

CURVEB defines the steady-state warm-up enrichment curve (Curve B) indexed against engine coolant temperature. Unlike Curve A and Curve C, which operate on countdown timers and decay to zero shortly after starting, CURVEB remains continuously active throughout the entire engine warm-up cycle. It supplies supplementary fuel proportional to coolant temperature from cold start until the engine reaches full operating thermostat temperature (180 degF). This table maintains smooth combustion and drivability while the engine block, cylinder head, and pistons expand to normal operating clearances.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Extended Warm-Up Drivability: * Raise CURVEB if the engine experiences lean hesitation or flat acceleration between 1 and 5 minutes after starting.
  • Adaptive Learning Over-Enrichment: * If adaptive fuel trims learn excessive negative correction while warming up, lower CURVEB values across the 100 to 160 degF range.
  • Zero-Point Taper: * Ensure values taper smoothly to zero at 180 degF so warm running fuel calculations remain undisturbed.
  • WOT Scaling Interaction: * Under wide-open throttle, CURVEB is scaled by FCRCVB to prevent over-rich bogging during high-power acceleration on a cold engine.
