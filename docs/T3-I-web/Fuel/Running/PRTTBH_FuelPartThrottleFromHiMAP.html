1. Overview & Purpose

PRTTBH: PRTTBH defines the primary 3D speed-density fuel pulsewidth lookup surface for heavy part-throttle acceleration on the Lotus 16-valve DOHC Turbo III engine. The engine controller calculates air charge mass by cross-referencing manifold absolute pressure and engine speed in this table, outputting a base fuel injector pulsewidth scaled by charge temperature and barometric density. On the Lotus 16V engine, these 3D fuel surfaces handle all volumetric efficiency modeling directly, bypassing the traditional 8-valve RPM pumping efficiency curve.
