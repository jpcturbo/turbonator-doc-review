1. Overview & Purpose

CURVEA defines the initial post-start enrichment curve (Curve A) indexed against engine coolant temperature. Immediately after the engine catches and enters running mode, fuel condensation on cold metal surfaces remains severe until intake air motion and combustion heat warm the intake tract. CURVEA delivers substantial extra fuel during the first several seconds after startup. This enrichment is held at full strength for a duration determined by CLDHLD or HOTHLD, and then tapers smoothly down to zero via decay constants DCYTMC or DCYTMH.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold Driveaway Stumble: * If the vehicle stumbles or pops in the intake when pulling away within 30 seconds of starting, increase CURVEA in the lower temperature range.
  • Warm Restart Smoking: * Lower CURVEA at temperatures above 140 degF if warm restarts produce dark exhaust smoke or rough, over-rich idling.
  • E85 Conversions: * Ethanol fuels require substantial after-start enrichment due to poor cold vaporization; increase values across cold temperatures.
  • Companion Hold & Decay: * Adjust CURVEA in coordination with CLDHLD (hold time) and DCYTMC (decay rate) to shape the complete after-start enrichment window.
