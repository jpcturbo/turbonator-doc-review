1. Overview & Purpose

LMAPTC establishes the digital filter time constant for the rolling average manifold pressure during deceleration conditions. When manifold pressure drops below the rolling average, the engine controller calculates the rate of vacuum increase by comparing instantaneous pressure against the rolling average. LMAPTC dictates how rapidly the rolling average catches up to the falling manifold pressure. The factory calibration uses a filter weight of 40 counts, providing smooth digital filtering that prevents minor intake pulses from distorting the calculated deceleration rate.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Prolonging Decel Leaning: Lowering table values causes the rolling average to lag further behind the falling manifold pressure, prolonging the calculated pressure delta and extending deceleration lean-out duration.
  • Shortening Decel Window: Raising table values causes the rolling average to adapt more quickly, shortening the deceleration fuel reduction window.
  • Filter Stability: In most applications, keeping this value at its factory setting ensures optimal filter stability.
  • Companion Parameter: Symmetrical counterpart to AETIME (which governs increasing manifold pressure).
