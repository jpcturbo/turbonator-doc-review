1. Overview & Purpose

POSFUL specifies the pulse width of the auxiliary acceleration enrichment pulse injected when the idle air stepper motor opens rapidly. When accessory loads like the air conditioning compressor or power steering pump place a sudden load on the idling engine, the controller commands the idle stepper motor open, admitting extra air into the plenum. POSFUL injects a fixed supplemental fuel pulse directly into the running part-throttle enrichment variable to match this bypass airflow and maintain an even idle speed. The factory setting provides 350 microseconds of supplemental fuel.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Large Injector Scaling: Scale POSFUL inversely when upgrading to larger fuel injectors so that the injected fuel mass remains matched to bypass airflow.
  • Curing A/C Engagement Dip: If the engine dips in RPM, shudders, or threatens to stall when the air conditioning kicks on, increase POSFUL to richen the idle transition.
  • Eliminating Rich Shifter Bog: If the engine puffs black smoke or bogs rich when shifting an automatic transmission into gear, reduce POSFUL.
  • Coordination with POSTIG: Must be calibrated alongside the activation threshold in POSTIG.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect when accessory loads like the air conditioning compressor or power steering pump place a sudden load on the idling engine, the controller commands the idle stepper motor open, admitting extra air into the plenum.
