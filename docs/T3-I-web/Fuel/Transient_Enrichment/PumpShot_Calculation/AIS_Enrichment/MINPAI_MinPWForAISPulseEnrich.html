1. Overview & Purpose

MINPAI: MINPAI (MinPWForAISPulseEnrich) establishes the minimum injector pulsewidth floor or maximum multiplier ceiling enforced during part-throttle acceleration enrichment. It ensures that transient throttle tip-in always injects a meaningful burst of fuel to prevent momentary lean hesitation when the throttle plate snaps open.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Calibration Strategy: Raising the value adds a more aggressive fuel punch on tip-in, curing lean hesitation on engines with big bore throttle bodies. Lowering the value prevents a rich bog or black smoke puff on light throttle movement.
  • Raising the Value: Increases commanded threshold or extends gating limits in the controller.
  • Lowering the Value: Decreases commanded threshold or restricts operational bounds.
