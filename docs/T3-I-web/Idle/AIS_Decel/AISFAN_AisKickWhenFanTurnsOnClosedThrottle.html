1. Overview & Purpose

AISFAN defines the number of proactive stepper motor steps added to the target idle air position whenever the radiator cooling fan is commanded on while the throttle is closed. High-output electric cooling fans draw substantial electrical current upon startup, placing an immediate mechanical torque load on the engine through the alternator. Without anticipatory idle compensation, this sudden rotational drag causes engine idle speed to dip or sag before closed-loop idle feedback can react. In factory calibrations, AISFAN is set to 10 steps. When the cooling fan relay energizes at idle, the controller instantly increases the desired automatic idle speed motor position by this calibrated step amount, admitting extra bypass airflow to support engine idle speed and prevent engine RPM instability.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Aftermarket Electric Fan Upgrades: * High-flow cooling fans drawing substantial amperage impose heavy alternator drag; raise AISFAN to 15 to 20 steps to arrest idle droop.
  • Preventing Idle Flares: * If the engine flares or surges upward when the fan turns on, lower AISFAN toward 4 to 6 steps.
  • Zeroing & Edge Cases: * Setting AISFAN to 0 disables proactive idle feedforward, causing noticeable RPM dips before closed-loop idle recovers.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect at closed throttle when the electric cooling fan turns on, stepping open the idle air motor to compensate for the sudden alternator electrical load.
