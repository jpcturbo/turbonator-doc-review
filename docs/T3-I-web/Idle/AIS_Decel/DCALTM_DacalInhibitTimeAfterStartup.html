1. Overview & Purpose

DCALTM defines the post-start time delay that must elapse before the engine controller is permitted to begin adaptive idle airflow calibration. Immediately following engine start, combustion dynamics, cylinder wall temperature, and manifold pressure undergo rapid transient changes as the engine stabilizes. If the adaptive learning routines were allowed to integrate intake manifold pressure during this settling period, transient post-start vacuum instability would corrupt the long-term learned idle airflow trim. DCALTM enforces a mandatory run-time delay before the DACAL adaptive integrator is unlocked. In factory calibrations, DCALTM is set to 25 counts, enforcing a post-start stabilization period of approximately 1.6 seconds.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Preventing Post-Start Trim Corruption: * Increase DCALTM to 5.0 to 10.0 seconds on modified engines with long post-start stabilization periods to prevent the ECU from learning inaccurate idle trim.
  • Factory Baseline: * Factory setting of 25 counts (~1.6 seconds) is suitable for stock and mild street setups.
  • Low Setting Hazard: * Setting DCALTM too low allows cranking vacuum dips to skew the long-term idle airflow baseline.
