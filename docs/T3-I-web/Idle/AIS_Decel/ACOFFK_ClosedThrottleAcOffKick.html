1. Overview & Purpose

ACOFFK defines the closed-throttle stepper motor kick step reduction applied when the air conditioning compressor clutch disengages. While the air conditioning compressor is pumping, auxiliary bypass airflow is injected to prevent the engine from stalling under compressor load. When the air conditioning thermostat satisfied signal opens and the magnetic clutch disengages, this compressor pumping load drops instantly to zero. If the auxiliary air were left in the intake manifold, the suddenly unloaded engine would flare upward into an annoying high-RPM idle surge. ACOFFK immediately subtracts a calibrated block of steps from the idle stepper motor to neutralize the load drop and prevent idle flaring. In factory calibrations, ACOFFK is set to 8 counts, commanding an immediate 8-step closure upon compressor disengagement.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Eliminating A/C Off Idle Flare: * Increase ACOFFK from 8 to 11 to 14 steps if the engine flares upward when the compressor clutch disengages at a stoplight.
  • Preventing Disengagement Undershoot: * If engine RPM dips below target when the A/C turns off, lower ACOFFK to 5 or 6 steps.
  • Cycling Equilibrium: * Balance ACOFFK against AC2KCK to make compressor cycling completely transparent to vehicle occupants.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect at closed throttle the instant the A/C compressor clutch disengages, closing down the idle air bypass steps to prevent engine flare.
