1. Overview & Purpose

OFFPER defines the percentage proportion of the off-idle air conditioning airflow adder ACCTBL that is instantly retracted when the compressor clutch disengages. While driving off-idle with the air conditioner on, ACCTBL injects auxiliary bypass airflow to counteract compressor drag during coast-down. When the compressor clutch disengages, compressor drag vanishes immediately. If the full off-idle air offset were allowed to decay away slowly, the vehicle would continue to coast forward with diminished engine braking, feeling like the throttle is hanging open. OFFPER dictates what percentage of the ACCTBL airflow adder is instantly subtracted from the stepper motor position at the exact moment the clutch disengages. In factory calibrations, OFFPER is set to 192 counts, commanding an instantaneous retraction of exactly 75 percent (192/256) of the A/C airflow adder.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Eliminating Coasting Forward Push: * Increase OFFPER to 220 to 255 counts (86% to 100%) to instantly eliminate all A/C dashpot air when the clutch opens, restoring firm engine braking.
  • Softening Disengagement Jerk: * Lower OFFPER to 128 counts (50%) if the car jerks backward into abrupt engine braking when the compressor disengages.
  • Percentage Scalar: * 255 counts represents 100%; factory setting of 192 counts represents 75%.
