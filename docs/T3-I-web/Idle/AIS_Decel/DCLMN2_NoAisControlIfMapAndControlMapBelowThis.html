1. Overview & Purpose

DCLMN2 defines the master safety manifold absolute pressure lockout threshold below which all deceleration idle stepper motor control and air conditioning kick routines are inhibited. Under conditions of extreme closed-throttle engine overrun from high RPM (such as downhill engine braking), intake manifold absolute pressure can plummet to near total vacuum. At these extreme vacuum levels, erratic pressure transients can occur, and attempting to actuate the idle stepper motor or engage A/C kicks can cause unexpected engine flare or motor hunting. If both actual manifold pressure and calculated control manifold pressure are below DCLMN2, all stepper motor manipulation is completely suspended. In factory calibrations, DCLMN2 is set to 13 counts, corresponding to an absolute pressure lockout threshold of approximately 1.5 psi absolute (3.0 inHg / 26.9 inHg vacuum).

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Deep Overrun Lockout Safety: * Keep DCLMN2 at 13 counts (~1.5 psi absolute) to preserve the safety envelope during extreme engine braking.
  • Premature Lockout Hazard: * Do not raise DCLMN2 above 20 counts, as this will disable dashpot control during normal deceleration, causing frequent stalls.
  • Multi-Routine Enforcement: * Enforces identical safety lockouts across both the decel dashpot routines and the A/C clutch transition routines.
