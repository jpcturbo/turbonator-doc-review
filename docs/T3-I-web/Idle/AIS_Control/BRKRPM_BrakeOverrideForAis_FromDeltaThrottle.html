1. Overview & Purpose

BRKRPM is a 2D calibration curve that establishes an engine speed brake override threshold for off-idle dashpot airflow. Under normal driving, the idle air stepper motor is held partially open off-idle to soften deceleration and prevent driveline bucking. However, when the driver actively presses the brake pedal to slow or stop the vehicle, auxiliary dashpot air is undesirable because it fights the vehicle brakes and reduces engine braking vacuum assist. When the brake switch contacts close, the Turbo III controller references BRKRPM using throttle position sensor voltage delta. If engine speed is above the looked-up threshold, the controller overrides the off-idle stepper opening floor and closes the bypass passage, restoring maximum engine braking.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Stronger Engine Braking: * Lower BRKRPM thresholds if the vehicle resists deceleration or pushes forward against the service brakes during firm stops.
  • Hard Braking Stall Prevention: * Keep low-throttle points above 1000 RPM to prevent the engine from stalling when stopping abruptly from highway speeds.
  • Switch Activation: * Only evaluates when the hydraulic brake pressure switch or brake pedal switch is actively closed.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect under normal driving, the idle air stepper motor is held partially open off-idle to soften deceleration and prevent driveline bucking.
