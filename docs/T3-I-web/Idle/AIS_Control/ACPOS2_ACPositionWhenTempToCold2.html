1. Overview & Purpose

ACPOS2: ACPOS2 (ACPositionWhenTempToCold2) defines idle air stepper motor kick steps or position offsets applied during accessory loads, gear shifts (Park/Neutral to Drive), or brake pedal depression. Preemptively positioning the stepper motor absorbs torque transients before engine speed can fluctuate.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Calibration Strategy: Raising the values kicks the idle air valve open further during transitions, eliminating idle dip when shifting into gear or turning on accessories. Lowering the values prevents idle speed hung-up or flare.
  • Raising the Value: Increases commanded threshold or extends gating limits in the controller.
  • Lowering the Value: Decreases commanded threshold or restricts operational bounds.
