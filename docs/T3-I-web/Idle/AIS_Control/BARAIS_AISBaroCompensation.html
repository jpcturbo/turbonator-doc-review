1. Overview & Purpose

BARAIS is a 2D calibration curve that provides barometric pressure compensation for idle and air conditioning stepper motor adjustments. At higher altitudes, atmospheric pressure drops, reducing air density and oxygen mass per unit volume of intake air. To achieve the exact same mass airflow required to maintain target idle speed or absorb accessory loads, the idle air stepper motor must open further at high elevation than at sea level. BARAIS looks up an altitude scaling factor from barometric pressure that is stored in temporary memory and applied to downstream idle step calculations. In factory calibrations, BARAIS scales from a positive compensation multiplier at high altitudes (low barometric pressure) down to zero correction at sea level (14.7 psi absolute / 29.9 inHg).

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • High-Altitude Stall Prevention: * If operating at elevations above 4000 feet and experiencing accessory load idle sag, elevate the low-pressure cells in BARAIS to increase stepper motor compensation.
  • Sea-Level Baseline: * Factory calibrations flatten to zero compensation near standard sea-level atmospheric pressure (14.7 psi).
  • Table Verification: * Ensure points are monotonically decreasing as barometric pressure increases toward sea level.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect at high elevation, applying barometric altitude compensation to idle air stepper step targets.
