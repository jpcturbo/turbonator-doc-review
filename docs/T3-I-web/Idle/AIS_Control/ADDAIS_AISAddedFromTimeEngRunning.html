1. Overview & Purpose

ADDAIS: ADDAIS defines the additional idle air stepper motor (AIS) opening steps added based on cumulative engine running time. Following engine start, mechanical friction is high and oil pressure is building; ADDAIS holds the idle valve slightly more open to ensure smooth cold-start stabilization before decaying to normal target idle steps.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Calibration Strategy: Raising ADDAIS adds more idle air immediately after start, curing cold-start dip and stall on engines with heavy valvetrains. Lowering ADDAIS prevents excessive idle flares on hot engine restarts.
  • Raising the Value: Increases commanded threshold or extends gating limits in the controller.
  • Lowering the Value: Decreases commanded threshold or restricts operational bounds.
