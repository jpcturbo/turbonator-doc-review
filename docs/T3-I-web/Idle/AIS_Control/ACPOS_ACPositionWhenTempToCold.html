1. Overview & Purpose

ACPOS defines the fixed auxiliary step opening added to the idle air stepper motor when the air conditioning compressor engages while engine coolant temperature is cold. A cold air conditioning compressor pump contains thick, viscous refrigeration lubricant that imposes substantial mechanical drag when the magnetic clutch locks up. On a cold engine that is already dealing with high internal mechanical friction, this compressor engagement shock can easily cause a severe RPM plunge or cold stall. When the air conditioning clutch engages and coolant temperature is below the threshold set in ACTMP, the controller injects ACPOS as a dedicated fixed step adder. In factory calibrations, ACPOS commands an opening offset of 18 steps.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Cold A/C Stall Prevention: * Increase ACPOS from 18 to 22 to 26 steps if activating air conditioning or windshield defrost on cold mornings threatens to stall the engine.
  • Taming Cold A/C Flare: * If cold A/C engagement causes an annoying flare above 1600 RPM, reduce ACPOS steps.
  • Temperature Threshold Companion: * Active exclusively when coolant temperature is below ACTMP (factory 77 °F).

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during cold engine idle when the A/C is turned on, defining the baseline idle stepper motor opening when coolant temperature is low.
