1. Overview & Purpose

OCTAE defines the artificial manifold pressure delta step injected during off-idle throttle transitions. When the driver steps on the accelerator pedal and the throttle blade opens, intake manifold pressure rises rapidly. Because physical pressure sensor filtering introduces a brief mathematical lag in the running average manifold pressure variable, the engine controller subtracts OCTAE from instantaneous manifold pressure when leaving idle. This subtraction artificially suppresses the historical average pressure baseline, creating an instantaneous positive delta pressure jump that triggers immediate transient fuel enrichment and responsive ignition advance before the physical sensor filter catches up. Factory calibrations configure OCTAE to two counts, representing approximately zero point two three psi.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Tip-In Hesitation Cure: * Increasing OCTAE to three or four counts forces a sharper delta manifold pressure step, curing light off-idle lean stumbles.
  • Large Fuel Injector Tuning: * On engines with oversized injectors, lowering OCTAE softens off-idle transient fuel delivery to prevent rich bogging.
  • Clamped Lower Floor: * If the subtraction of OCTAE would reduce average pressure below minimum thresholds, MINAMP enforces a hard lower boundary.
  • Factory Setting: * Set to two counts (approx 0.23 psi) in factory 2.5L calibrations.
