1. Overview & Purpose

RVLSPD establishes the vehicle speed threshold that determines how the engine controller handles rev limiting when the throttle position sensor signal is invalid or failing. If the throttle position sensor produces a bad signal (for instance, an open wiper trace or erratic voltage), the controller cannot ascertain throttle blade position. If the vehicle is moving at road speeds, the controller presumes the driver is cruising and permits normal engine operation up to the standard rev limiter (REVLMT6). However, if the vehicle is stationary or moving at very low speeds, the controller enforces the restrictive plausibility rev limiter (REVLMT4 and REVLMT3) to prevent accidental runaway. In factory calibrations, RVLSPD is set to 20 counts, representing 10.0 mph on a 0 to 127.5 mph scale.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Fault Gating Threshold: * When b45_TpsBadSignal is active, speeds above RVLSPD allow normal engine rev limits (REVLMT6), while speeds below RVLSPD enforce safety rev limits (REVLMT4).
  • Raising for Paddock Safety: * Increasing RVLSPD to 25 mph prevents high-speed driving if the throttle sensor fails during competition.
  • Preserving Factory Setting: * The 10 mph factory threshold prevents stationary runaway while allowing normal road speeds.
