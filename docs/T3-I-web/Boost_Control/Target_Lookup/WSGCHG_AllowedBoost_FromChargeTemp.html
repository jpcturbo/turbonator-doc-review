1. Overview & Purpose

WSGCHG regulates maximum allowable turbocharger boost pressure according to intake manifold charge air temperature. Highly compressed intake air heats up significantly, and if intercooler heat soak or elevated ambient summer temperatures drive charge temperatures excessively high, cylinder knock and pre-ignition risks multiply dramatically. The engine controller continuously samples the charge temperature sensor and uses this table to scale back the boost target if intake air temperatures exceed safe thermal thresholds, preventing knock before ignition retard must intervene.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Charge Temperature Protection: * Scales boost target downward when intake manifold air temperatures become excessively hot. * Prevents high-load detonation caused by hot compressed air and intercooler thermal saturation.
  • Intercooler Upgrade Tuning: * Vehicles with large, efficient intercoolers can maintain full boost across wider temperature spans. * Keep a sharp reduction above 140 to 160 degF to guard against bad fuel or hot summer ambient conditions.
  • Sensor Voltage Scaling: * Table x-axis is indexed by charge temperature sensor voltage (0.0 to 5.0 Volts; lower voltage represents hotter air on NTC thermistors).
  • Minimum-Wins Priority: * Enforces the lower value into Temp4 whenever high intake temperature calls for boost de-rating.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect under elevated intake charge temperatures, lowering the target boost ceiling to prevent detonation when intake air is hot.
