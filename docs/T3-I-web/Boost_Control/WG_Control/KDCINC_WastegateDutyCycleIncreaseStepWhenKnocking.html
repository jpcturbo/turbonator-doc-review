1. Overview & Purpose

KDCINC establishes the incremental duty cycle reduction step added to the knock reduction penalty each time detonation is detected while operating under boost in T1 control. In T1 bleed-style wastegate systems, reducing solenoid duty cycle bleeds less air away from the actuator canister, allowing boost pressure to push the wastegate open and lower intake manifold pressure. When knocking occurs above 5 psi of boost, the controller adds KDCINC counts to the knock reduction penalty counter to lower duty cycle and reduce boost, providing emergency relief alongside ignition timing retard.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Knock Step Reduction Rate: * Sets the duty cycle decrement added to the knock reduction penalty per confirmed detonation event under boost. * Factory setting is 8 counts (4.0 percent duty cycle drop per knock cycle).
  • Pump Gasoline Protection: * Increasing KDCINC to 12 or 16 counts pulls boost down faster when bad fuel is encountered, protecting head gaskets.
  • False Knock Smoothing: * If aggressive forged pistons or valvetrain noise trigger false knock, lower KDCINC to 4 counts to avoid harsh power cutouts.
  • T1 Bleed Subtraction Mechanics: * The knock penalty value is subtracted from final WGDC at line 9746, reducing solenoid bleed and opening the gate.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect under boost when spark knock is detected, controlling the duty cycle reduction steps applied to the wastegate solenoid to bleed off boost.
