1. Overview & Purpose

RPMBST defines the minimum engine speed threshold required to advance the boost ramp countdown timer (Timer_BoostTimer) in T1 wastegate control. The boost ramp timer indexes the time-based allowed boost table (WSGTIM), allowing boost to ramp upward from base spring pressure to full target boost over a calibrated duration. To ensure that the boost timer does not prematurely advance while the engine is lugging at low RPM where the turbo cannot generate full boost, the controller requires engine speed to exceed RPMBST before incrementing the timer.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Spool Tracking RPM Threshold: * Prevents Timer_BoostTimer from counting up when engine RPM is too low to produce boost. * Factory setting is 102 counts (approximately 3264 RPM).
  • Matching Turbocharger Sizing: * For small, fast-spooling stock turbos, lower RPMBST to 2200 to 2500 RPM. * For large aftermarket turbos (e.g. Garrett GT30R, Holset HX35), raise RPMBST to 3600 to 4000 RPM.
  • T1 vs. T2 Separation: * RPMBST controls timer advancement in T1 mode; T2 mode uses its dedicated companion T2RPMBST.
