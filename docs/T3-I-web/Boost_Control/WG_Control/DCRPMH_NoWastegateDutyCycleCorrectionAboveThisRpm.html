1. Overview & Purpose

DCRPMH defines the upper engine speed threshold above which adaptive wastegate duty cycle learning is completely inhibited in T2 control. At high engine speeds approaching redline, exhaust gas pulsation and rapid changes in volumetric efficiency can introduce noise into closed-loop boost calculations. DCRPMH establishes an upper RPM boundary to freeze adaptive cell learning, ensuring that the controller relies on established feed-forward baseline tables and prevents high-RPM transients from corrupting learned adaptive trims.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • High-RPM Adaptive Lockout: * Engine speed threshold above which adaptive wastegate cell learning is frozen. * Factory setting is 188 counts (approximately 6016 RPM in template, 79 counts / 2528 RPM in legacy calibrations).
  • High-Revving Engines: * For engines with upgraded valvetrain revving to 6800 to 7200 RPM, raise DCRPMH to 6400 to 6800 RPM to maintain closed-loop adaptation up to redline.
  • Freezing Adaptives on Dyno: * Setting DCRPMH low locks out adaptive learning, allowing tuners to dial in open-loop base tables without adaptive interference.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect during boost control, setting the high engine RPM boundary above which adaptive wastegate corrections are disabled.
