1. Overview & Purpose

STGRON establishes the lower engine speed threshold where fuel injection and ignition spark are restored after being cut by launch control. When the staging rev limiter cuts spark at the upper ceiling, engine speed begins to decay. Once RPM falls below STGRON, the engine controller clears the cut flags and re-enables combustion. This two-threshold hysteresis design prevents rapid, erratic flutter by enforcing a controlled RPM cycle. The factory setting of 119 counts corresponds to approximately 3808 RPM, creating a 200 RPM staging window below the 4000 RPM cut ceiling.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Hysteresis Bandwidth: Set roughly 150 to 250 RPM below STGROF (factory gap is 192 RPM) for smooth, stable boost spooling.
  • Excessively Narrow Gap: Setting within 32-64 RPM of STGROF creates harsh high-frequency vibration and unstable wastegate operation.
  • Excessively Wide Gap: Setting more than 400 RPM below STGROF causes engine speed to sag noticeably, dropping boost pressure between cycles.
  • Calibration Ordering: Always ensure STGRON is strictly lower than STGROF.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect when launch control rev limiting is active, establishing the lower engine speed threshold where fuel injection and ignition spark are restored.
