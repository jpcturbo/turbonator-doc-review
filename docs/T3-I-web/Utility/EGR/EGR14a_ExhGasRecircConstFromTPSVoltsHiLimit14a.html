1. Overview & Purpose

EGR14A (ExhGasRecircConstFromTPSVoltsHiLimit) establishes the upper throttle position boundary for the Phase 1 Exhaust Gas Recirculation diagnostic baseline sampling window. When sampling baseline closed-loop oxygen sensor behavior, the engine controller must ensure that the vehicle is cruising steadily and not accelerating moderately or heavily. Evaluated at offset +1 from EGR14 during the boundary check at line 5434, EGR14A caps the maximum allowable throttle opening relative to the learned minimum closed throttle position. The factory setting of 43 counts corresponds to approximately 0.84 volts (roughly 17 percent throttle opening). If the driver depresses the accelerator beyond this threshold, Phase 1 immediately terminates and resets.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Raising EGR14A: Allows Phase 1 baseline sampling at larger throttle openings. May increase monitor completion frequency in hilly terrain where cruising requires more pedal input.
  • Lowering EGR14A: Restricts Phase 1 to very light throttle cruise. Useful on lightweight vehicles or sensitive configurations to ensure baseline data is completely free of transient enrichment.
  • Window Consistency: Must remain strictly higher than EGR14 (5 counts). Setting EGR14A below EGR14 permanently disables the EGR diagnostic routine.
  • Factory Ceiling: 43 counts (approx 0.84V / ~17 percent throttle) represents a well-tested threshold for stock vehicles.
