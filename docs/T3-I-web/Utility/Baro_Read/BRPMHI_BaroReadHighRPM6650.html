1. Overview & Purpose

BRPMHI defines the upper engine speed boundary for in-flight atmospheric barometric pressure sampling during vehicle deceleration. When the driver lifts off the throttle from high engine speeds, intense exhaust scavenging, valvetrain harmonics, and rapid manifold pressure transients occur. Additionally, the driver is more likely to re-apply throttle abruptly during aggressive driving. BRPMHI prevents the controller from actuating the baro solenoid above this RPM ceiling. In factory calibrations, BRPMHI is configured to 6650 RPM (48000 counts in word format, representing approximately 6000 to 6650 RPM). If engine speed exceeds this ceiling, barometric reading requests are locked out until engine speed settles.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • High-RPM Safeguard: * Keep BRPMHI below 6500 RPM to prevent venting the pressure sensor during aggressive high-RPM throttle lifts.
  • Transient Fueling Protection: * Ensures the pressure sensor is never disconnected from manifold vacuum when rapid wide-open throttle reapplication is likely.
  • Drag Racing Recommendations: * Leaving BRPMHI at factory settings prevents unwanted baro reads during high-RPM gear shifts.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect when the driver lifts off the throttle from high engine speeds, intense exhaust scavenging, valvetrain harmonics, and rapid manifold pressure transients occur.
