1. Overview & Purpose

RETALL: RETALL governs the multi-cylinder knock retard distribution strategy for the Lotus 16-valve Turbo III engine. When set, detecting knock on any individual cylinder causes the ECU to broadcast the knock retard angle across all four cylinders, protecting the entire Lotus DOHC valvetrain and high-compression pistons.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Calibration Strategy: Enabling RETALL ensures maximum valvetrain and piston safety by pulling timing across all cylinders when detonation is detected on any single runner. Disabling it confines knock retard strictly to the offending cylinder, preserving maximum power on unaffected cylinders.

  • Lowering the Value: Decreases commanded threshold or restricts operational bounds.
