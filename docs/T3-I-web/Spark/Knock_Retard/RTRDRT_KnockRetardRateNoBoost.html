1. Overview & Purpose

RTRDRT defines the step-size of knock retard applied to a knocking cylinder when knock is detected out of boost (under vacuum or light load below KNKOFS). When combustion knock occurs under light load or partial-throttle cruising, cylinder charge density is relatively low. Under these conditions, pulling a substantial, decisive step of ignition timing quickly extinguishes the knock without significantly disturbing vehicle momentum or causing driveline shudder. In factory calibrations, RTRDRT is set to 6 counts, corresponding to 3.0 degrees of retard applied to the active cylinder's individual retard variable upon knock detection.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Decisive Knock Suppression: * Factory setting of 3.0 degrees (6 counts) pulls enough timing on the first detection to snuff out light-load knock immediately.
  • Drivability vs. Response: * Raising RTRDRT above 4.0 degrees can cause noticeable hesitation on tip-in ping; lowering below 2.0 degrees risks repeat pinging.
  • Per-Cylinder Application: * Applied strictly to the specific cylinder that knocked, preserving full timing on the other three cylinders.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect when combustion knock occurs under light load or partial-throttle cruising, cylinder charge density is relatively low.
