1. Overview & Purpose

IDLADV: IDLADV defines the cold-engine idle spark advance curve versus coolant temperature for the Lotus 16-valve Turbo III engine. During cold-engine warm-up, extra ignition advance is introduced to compensate for slow cold-combustion flame speeds, stabilizing idle speed and preventing engine stalling before closed-loop idle air stepper control takes full authority.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration
