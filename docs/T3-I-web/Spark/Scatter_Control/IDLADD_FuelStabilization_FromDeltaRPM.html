1. Overview & Purpose

IDLADD defines the fuel pulsewidth stabilization correction curve indexed by idle engine speed error. In coordination with spark scatter ignition timing modulation, the engine controller applies high-speed fuel pulsewidth adjustments when engine speed deviates from target idle. When idle speed sags below target, IDLADD adds fuel pulsewidth to increase combustion charge energy; when idle speed flares above target, IDLADD trims fuel pulsewidth. This synchronized fuel and spark control stabilizes engine idle speed far faster than mechanical idle air stepper motor adjustments alone. In factory calibrations, IDLADD provides up to 50 microseconds of fuel pulsewidth adjustment.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Injector Scaling Proportionality: * When upgrading to larger fuel injectors, scale down IDLADD by the ratio of stock injector flow to new injector flow to prevent over-fueling during idle corrections.
  • Surge Prevention with Large Injectors: * If an engine develops a rich idle surge with large injectors, zeroing IDLADD often resolves the surging by leaving idle stabilization strictly to spark timing.
  • Synchronized Spark & Fuel Control: * Works in direct synergy with SPKADD and SPKRET to provide comprehensive idle speed dampening.
