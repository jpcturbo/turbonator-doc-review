1. Overview & Purpose

IDLTMP defines the minimum engine coolant temperature threshold required to activate IDLADD idle fuel stabilization. While the engine is cold, fuel puddle dynamics in the intake manifold runners are unstable and cold enrichment fuel multipliers are active. Modulating fuel pulsewidths based on idle speed fluctuations while the engine is cold could destabilize the fuel film and cause erratic fueling. In factory calibrations, IDLTMP is set to 138 counts, corresponding to approximately 176 degF, ensuring that fuel stabilization corrections are only permitted after the engine reaches fully warmed operating temperature.

2. Tuner's Guide & Real-World Calibration

  • Thermostat Compensation: * If running a 160 degF thermostat, lower IDLTMP to approximately 150 degF (125 counts) to allow idle fuel stabilization to engage.
  • Disabling Fuel Stabilization: * Setting IDLTMP to 255 disables IDLADD across the board, isolating idle speed control purely to ignition scatter and the idle air motor.
  • Cold Fuel Stability: * Keeping IDLTMP above 160 degF prevents fuel modulation while cold warm-up enrichment curves are still active.

3. Operating Conditions (When Does This Take Effect?)

Takes effect while the engine is cold, fuel puddle dynamics in the intake manifold runners are unstable and cold enrichment fuel multipliers are active.
